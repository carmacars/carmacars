What happens
------------------------

1. We stop new bookings after the Exit Date
   Bookings already confirmed before that date go ahead.
2. You send proof of your own insurance
   It must start on the Exit Date. We need it before we hand the car back.
3. On the Exit Date
   We remove the tracker, record the odometer and photos, and take the car off the fleet policy.
4. Registration goes back to you
   We complete our part within 10 business days after the Exit Date. We both sign Form E and you get the car, keys and permit.
5. Final account
   The last month's fees are pro-rated to the Exit Date. Your final balance is paid on the first payout date after the month of the Exit Date.
