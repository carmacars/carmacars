About the estimate
It is an estimate only and may vary. You pay ServiceOntario, the safety inspection and any appraisal directly, at the counter or to the shop. CarmaCars does not collect these amounts.

Your steps
------------------------

1. Get what your method needs
   {{owner_to_obtain}}
2. We prepare the forms and book the registry visit
   We fill in the government forms and send you the date, time and place. A safety certificate is valid for 36 days, so we book inside that window.
3. Registry visit
   You attend with one of our admins and pay the registry fees and any tax at the counter.
4. Tracker install
   Our field staff fit the tracker and we test it with the car parked. You get access to live location.
5. Fleet policy
   We add the car to the CarmaCars fleet policy and send you written confirmation. That date is your Enrollment Date.
