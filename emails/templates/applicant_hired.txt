Here is what happens next. You are not working a shift right away: this gives you access to the staff portal, where you do your training and claim your shifts online.

1. Set up your account
   You get an email from CarmaCars to set up your staff account: choose a password from the link in it.
2. Do your training
   Sign in and complete the CarmaCars Academy for your role (a few short lessons with a check at the end).
3. Claim your shifts
   Claim your shifts online in the portal. You pick the shifts that suit you; you do not have to take a particular first shift.
4. Say hello to the team
   Once you are hired, send a message in the team chat (the CarmaCars Team WhatsApp group, the link is in your portal). In each WhatsApp group, set your member tag as "Name - Position", for example "Maria - Sales".
