Before you begin

Start here

Who this guide is for

Anyone who rents a car from CarmaCars. You can send a request without an account. Your account keeps your trips, offers, agreements, receipts and documents in one place.

What you need

  • A G or G2 licence (licences from outside Ontario are reviewed by a person)
  • A credit card in your own name
  • Your email address and a mobile number
Sign in at
carmacars.ca/sign-in
You land on Trips, your list of bookings.

Your first sign-in

  1. 1
    Go to carmacars.ca/create-account. Enter your Full name, Email and a Password of 8 characters or more.
  2. 2
    Tick the box to agree to the terms, then press Create account.
  3. 3
    Open the email "Confirm your CarmaCars account" and press its link within 24 hours. It confirms your email and opens Trips.
  4. 4
    Next time, sign in at carmacars.ca/sign-in with your email and password. Link expired? Sign in and press Send a new confirmation link.
Good to know
Sent a request before you had an account? Once your email is confirmed, those requests are added to your account and Trips says "We found earlier requests made with your email".

Chapter 1

Find a car and send a request

Every booking starts as a request. A person checks the car and the price, then sends you an offer. Nothing is charged until you approve, sign and pay.

1RequestYou pick a car and dates
2OfferWe confirm the price
3SignThe rental agreement
4PayYour card is authorized
5VerifyLicence, selfie, coverage
6Pick upWe hand over the keys

Find a car

Start on carmacars.ca. You do not need to sign in to look around.

  1. 1
    Go to carmacars.ca and press Find a car.
  2. 2
    Add your pickup and return dates. The list then shows the cars that are free and what the whole trip costs.
  3. 3
    Narrow the list with Filters: type, seats, transmission and daily rate.
  4. 4
    Open a car to see its photos, rates, what is included and its own calendar. Its car code (like CC-7F3A) is under the price: quote it when you call or chat with us, and we know the car at once.

Choose your dates on the car page

  1. 1
    Under Dates and price, tap your pickup date, then your return date. Hatched days are already booked.
  2. 2
    Choose the Pickup time and the Return time.
  3. 3
    Want it brought to you? Tick Deliver it to me or Pick it up from me at the end. Open Add-ons to add a baby seat, flowers, a full tank and more.
  4. 4
    Check Your estimate, then press Request to book. The add-ons you chose come with you.
Watch out
A request does not hold the car. The car is booked only once you approve the offer, sign and pay.

Send your request

The request has five short steps. Your answers are saved as you go, so you can come back.

  1. 1
    Step 1, Dates and delivery: check the car and tap your dates in the calendar.
  2. 2
    Set the Pickup time and Return time. Any time, day or night, with at least 2 hours of notice.
  3. 3
    Your request, on the right, shows the estimated total as you go.
  1. 4
    Choose where you get the car: At our location, Deliver it to an address or At the airport. Then choose where you return it.
  2. 5
    Press Continue to driver details.
  1. 6
    Step 2, The driver: fill in your first and last name, email, mobile number and date of birth.
  2. 7
    Someone else will drive too? Add them under Additional drivers. Each needs a G2 or G licence and signs the agreement.
  3. 8
    Press Continue to licence.
  1. 9
    Step 3: choose your Licence class, for example Ontario G2.
  2. 10
    Choose how the car is covered: Option 1 (OPCF 27 on your own auto policy), Option 2 (rental coverage on your credit card) or Option 3 (the CarmaCars Protection Plan). You can change this any time before pickup.
  3. 11
    Choose the Booking type: Standard, or Non-refundable for 10% off with no refund.
  4. 12
    Add anything we should know in the notes, for example a flight number.
  5. 13
    Press Continue to add-ons. Step 4, Add-ons: tick what you want (see Add-ons below), then press Review request.
  1. 14
    Step 5, Check it and send: read every line. Use Edit beside a line to change it.
  2. 15
    Tick the box to confirm you are 18 or older and agree to the Terms of use and Privacy policy.
  3. 16
    Press Send request. Nothing is charged and no card is needed.
  1. 17
    The confirmation shows your request number, for example CC-10021. Keep it for any question.
  2. 18
    A person checks the calendar, your dates and your place, usually within 2 hours, day or night. Your offer arrives by email and in Trips.

Add-ons

Extras for your trip, such as a baby seat, a child or booster seat, flowers waiting in the car, a chauffeur, an umbrella, a phone charger or a full tank of gas.

  1. 1
    On the car page (under Add-ons) or in step 4 of the request, tick an add-on or use + and − for seats. The estimate shows the price at once.
  2. 2
    Each add-on shows its price: a trip, a day (with the most for a trip) or an hour. HST is added.
  3. 3
    Some need notice, for example flowers need 24 hours. Seats and other items show how many are left for your dates.
  4. 4
    A chauffeur is confirmed by our team in your offer.

With a full tank of gas (prepaid) you can return the car at any fuel level, with no fuel charge.

Ask in the chat

The chat button sits in the corner of every page.

  1. 1
    Press it and write your question: a car, your dates, the price, how booking works. The CarmaCars assistant answers right away with prices from our booking system.
  2. 2
    Want a person? Press Talk to a person. When our team is away, leave your name and email or phone with Send my details and tick "Email me a copy of this chat".
  3. 3
    Press End chat when you are done. If you gave your email, a copy of the chat arrives by email.

A price in the chat is for the dates you asked. Nothing is booked until you approve an offer, sign and pay.

Chapter 2

Your offer

Our offer is the confirmed price for your car and dates, with every fee and HST. It stays open for 24 hours. You approve it, ask for a change or decline it.

Open your offer

  1. 1
    We email you when the offer is ready. Sign in at carmacars.ca/sign-in and open Trips.
  2. 2
    Your next step is always at the top. Press Review offer.

Approve, ask for a change, or decline

  1. 1
    Check the price, the dates, the place and any message from our team. The black bar shows how long the offer stays open.
  2. 2
    All good? Press Approve and continue to sign and pay.
  3. 3
    Want something different? Press Ask for a change, write what you need, then Send request.
  4. 4
    Not going ahead? Press Decline offer, then Decline this offer.
Watch out
This offer does not hold the car. If someone else approves, signs and pays for the same car and dates first, we tell you and offer you another car or other dates.

Chapter 3

Sign and pay

After you approve, you sign the rental agreement on screen and pay. It takes a few minutes on a phone or a computer; the signing itself is three taps.

Sign the rental agreement

  1. 1
    Fill in your home address and licence details. You do this once; we keep them for next time.
  2. 2
    Press Save details. The agreement then shows your details.
  1. 3
    Check the key values at the top: the car, the dates, the deposit and the deductible. Open Read the full rental agreement to read it word for word, or press Download PDF to keep a copy.
  2. 4
    Under Sign, tap All four: it ticks the four statements, which stay on screen to read.
  3. 5
    Your full legal name is your signature. Prefer to sign with your finger or the mouse? Press Draw instead.
  4. 6
    Press Sign and continue to payment.

Pay to confirm

  1. 1
    Check What you pay now. The refundable deposit is taken at pickup, not today.
  2. 2
    Enter the Name on the card and the card details. Use a credit card in your own name.
  3. 3
    Press Pay $... (the button shows the amount).
Tip
Your card is authorized now and charged once the booking is confirmed. It becomes your Card on File for the deposit at pickup and any later charges you agreed to when you signed.
Watch out
See "This car was just taken"? Someone confirmed the same car first. The authorization on your card is released, and we offer you another car or other dates.

A rental we booked for you

Booked by phone, in person or through a partner? We add the rental and you sign it online.

  1. 1
    You get an email: "Read and sign your rental agreement; your card is charged when you sign."
  2. 2
    Open it. Check the car, the dates and the price, and read the agreement.
  3. 3
    Press Accept and sign. If we have your card on file, it is charged at that moment, not before, and the agreement is countersigned by CarmaCars as soon as you sign.
04
Chapter 4

Before pickup

Your booking is confirmed. A few things are left: your licence photos, a selfie and proof of coverage, a signature from each additional driver, and the deposit.

Your booking page

  1. 1
    Open Trips, then your booking. The status is at the top, for example Confirmed.
  2. 2
    The red card tells you what is still needed before pickup.
  3. 3
    Progress shows each stage, from Request sent to Statement.
  4. 4
    Need something else? holds Licence and coverage, Ask to change dates, Cancel booking and Extend.

Upload your licence, selfie and coverage

Do this at least 24 hours before pickup. It takes a few minutes with your phone.

  1. 1
    On your booking, press Upload documents (or Licence and coverage).
  2. 2
    Licence, front: lay the licence on a dark surface with all four corners visible. Press Take photo.
  3. 3
    Licence, back: do the same for the back.
  4. 4
    Selfie with your licence: hold the licence beside your face, then press Take selfie.
  5. 5
    Coverage for damage to the car: add what your option asks for, then press Upload proof.
Your optionWhat we need
Option 1, OPCF 27 on your own policyYour insurer, the policy number and its expiry (after your return date), and the policy or endorsement that shows OPCF 27, as a PDF or photos (up to 6 pages).
Option 2, your credit card's rental coveragePay with that card: it must be a credit card (debit and prepaid cards are refused) in your name. Tick that it covers this kind of car for this many days. The card's benefits document is optional.
Option 3, the CarmaCars Protection PlanAsk for it on your booking; we confirm it once it is active.
Tip
A person checks your proof and approves it, or tells you what to fix. The car is handed over only once it is approved. Changing your option replaces the earlier proof. We remind you 48 and 24 hours before pickup while it is missing. We delete licence and face photos 90 days after your rental ends.

Additional drivers

Each additional driver signs the agreement with their own link.

  1. 1
    When the agreement is ready, each driver with an email gets their own signing link.
  2. 2
    On your booking, press Copy their signing link to send it yourself, or Send a new link.
  3. 3
    Each driver adds their own licence photos and selfie, like you did.
Watch out
Signing links expire after 72 hours. A new link ends the older one, so always send the newest.

Add something to your trip

Until 24 hours before pickup (or the notice an add-on needs), add add-ons from your booking.

  1. 1
    On your booking, under Add-ons, press Add something to your trip.
  2. 2
    Tick the add-on you want. Its price shows below.
  3. 3
    Press Add and pay. It is charged at once on the card you paid with, and shows on your booking and receipt.
  4. 4
    A chauffeur needs our confirmation: press Ask for it. We confirm it and charge your card then.

No card on file for this booking? The add-on is added and waits for payment: press Pay for your add-ons under Add-ons, enter a card and press Pay. It is charged at once; that card is not kept.

Tip
If you cancel, add-ons are refunded like the rest of your booking. Flowers and a chauffeur are not refunded once their notice before pickup has started.

What to bring to pickup

  • Your original licence. A photo is not enough.
  • The card you paid with. The deposit is taken on it about an hour before pickup.
  • A few minutes to walk around the car with us. We photograph it together.

Chapter 5

During your trip

Plans change. You can ask for more days, ask to change dates, answer a change we suggest, or cancel, all from your booking.

Extend your rental

  1. 1
    On your booking, press Extend.
  2. 2
    Choose the new return date and time, then press See the price.
  3. 3
    Press Request extension. Once we approve it, press Pay for the extension before your current return time.
Watch out
Ask before your current return time. One rental can be at most 30 days.

Ask to change dates

  1. 1
    On your booking, press Ask to change dates.
  2. 2
    Set the new pickup and return, then press See the difference to see what you would pay or get back.
  3. 3
    Press Ask to change dates to send it. Nothing changes until you agree to our answer.

A change we suggest

Sometimes we need to change your booking, for example another car of the same class. You decide.

  1. 1
    You get an email, and your booking says "We suggest a change to your booking". Press Review and answer.
  2. 2
    Read what changes (before and after), the reason, the money and the time left to answer.
  3. 3
    To agree, press Accept and sign the amendment, sign, then press Accept and sign.
  4. 4
    Anything more to pay is charged to your Card on File. If the card is declined, press Pay and apply the change.
  5. 5
    To say no, press Decline this change. Your booking stays as it was.
Tip
You have 48 hours to answer, and we send one reminder. CarmaCars signs the amendment at the moment you sign it, and the signed copy is saved under Documents. If the change costs less, the refund goes back to your card within 10 business days after the amendment is signed.

Cancel a booking

  1. 1
    On your booking, press Cancel booking.
  2. 2
    Read What you get back. It shows the rule that applies and your refund.
  3. 3
    Type the booking number to confirm (for example CC-10021), then press Cancel booking.
Standard booking: when you cancelRefund of what you paid
Within 24 hours of booking, and 48 hours or more before pickup95% back
48 hours or more before pickup75% back
24 to 48 hours before pickup50% back
Less than 24 hours before pickupNo refund

A Non-refundable booking gets no refund. The deposit is taken at pickup, so none is held when you cancel before it.

Messages, tolls and tickets

  • Write to us about a booking at the bottom of its page: type in Your message and press Send.
  • Tolls and tickets are yours to pay, including Highway 407 ETR. If one reaches us, we pay it and charge the amount plus our fee to your Card on File, and email you a copy of the notice.
  • Running late? Ask to extend before your return time, or call us at 289-312-5991.

Chapter 6

After your trip

We check the car with you at the return. Your deposit balance comes back, and your receipts and agreement stay in your portal.

The return and your deposit

  • At the return we check the odometer, fuel or charge, keys and cleanliness, take photos and compare with pickup. You sign on our screen.
  • We release the deposit balance after you return the car. If a toll, ticket or claim is still open, we may hold back what we expect it to cost for a while.
  • Your statement appears under Documents.

Review your rental

  1. 1
    Open the trip under Past, then press Review your rental.
  2. 2
    Tap the stars. Add a title and a few words if you like. Both are optional.
  3. 3
    Tick Show my review on the site with my first name only if you agree to it being shown.
  4. 4
    Press Send my review.

Documents, notifications and profile

  1. 1
    Documents: your agreements, receipts, statements and uploads. Press View or Download.
  2. 2
    Notifications: notices from us. Use Mark as read or Mark all as read, and choose which optional emails you get.
  3. 3
    Profile: your contact details (Save), drivers, your Card on File and your marketing choice.
Tip
Your legal name is locked once you are verified. To change it or your email, call or message us.
