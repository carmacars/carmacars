Getting around Admin

Admin opens on To do: the day's counts at the top, then what needs you now, most urgent first. The menu groups every screen into seven groups: Today, Bookings, Cars, People, Marketing, Money and Settings. The long groups come in labelled parts (for example People: Owners and partners, then Renters, staff and investors). Search finds any booking, car or person, and Ctrl+K jumps to any page.

To do

Start every shift here. Each line says what to do and why, and its button opens a small pop-up that does it, already filled in. Nothing to remember and no menu to search.

  1. 1
    Each piece of work is its own line (work of one kind for several cars is one line, such as Add real photos (72 cars); its button opens the list), marked Now, Today or When you can: a car the insurer has not confirmed, a car not on the site, a renter's documents to check, a visitor waiting in live chat, a booking request, a change a renter asked for, an owner application, an owner invoice waiting for payment, the first and last month an owner paid after their invoices were voided, the terms agreed with an owner still on the standard grid, documents an owner has not signed, an official form to check, a plate to check, a car showing sample photos, and any task of the Admin.
  2. 2
    Press the line's button: Mark insured, Set rates and publish, Review documents, Record payment, Record what they paid, Enter the terms, Record paper signature, Checked: use for owners, Open the chat, and so on. A line you do not need has Not needed or These are correct. The pop-up is filled in; check it and confirm. The line disappears once the work is done.
  3. 3
    Under the lines, the counts with one button each (failed payments, agreements to countersign, tolls, listings, insurance, staff commissions, prices typed by hand, sales claims, Turo emails). A count with nothing waiting moves to the bottom, under "more with nothing waiting".
  4. 4
    While no assistant holds customer service, sales, finance or fleet (the live verification's test accounts do not count), their tasks come to To do too, and so do their emails, to rent@carmacars.ca.
  5. 5
    Beside the list (under it on a phone): today's pickups (ready or not ready), today's returns (due or late) and today's chauffeur bookings, by time.
  6. 6
    Press Overview for the money, fleet and queue figures, the alerts and the team's sales for the month.
  7. 7
    The dark band at the top counts the day, each count one tap from its page: requests waiting (with how long the oldest has waited, red past the reply target), pickups and returns today, payments to chase, deposits to settle, staff tasks open and alerts. A count that needs you now is red and says so.

People and access

Let people in, help them back in when they are locked out, and see exactly what they see. Every step is recorded in the audit log.

Portals and sign-in

Open People›Portals and view as. Every portal has a card.

  1. 1
    Each card links to Its sign-in page, the address to send that person.
  2. 2
    Choose a person in the list on the card.
  3. 3
    Press Open as this person to see that portal as they see it.

Invite a person

Invitations work for staff, field staff, owners and investors. The person gets our invitation email and chooses a password.

  1. 1
    Under Invite a person, choose the Role: Staff (assistant), Field staff, Owner on file or Investor on file. Admin is not offered: only rent@carmacars.ca can be an Admin.
  2. 2
    Fill in the Name and Email.
  3. 3
    For staff, pick the Main function, and tick Also covers for any other function they help with. For field staff, pick the Field function.
  4. 4
    Press Invite. The link in the email works for 24 hours.
Tip
Someone who already signs in gets the new role at once, with no email. Owners and investors must be on file first: People > Owners and People > Investors also have a one-click invite for a record without a sign-in.

Send a password link

For anyone who forgot their password or let their invitation expire.

  1. 1
    Under People who can sign in, search by name or email.
  2. 2
    On their row, press Send password link. They get "Reset your CarmaCars password", valid for 24 hours.
  3. 3
    View as (...) on the same row opens their portal, read only.

View as: see what someone sees

  1. 1
    A red banner says "Viewing as Name (email), read only." on every page.
  2. 2
    Click around their portal. Nothing can be changed: every save is refused.
  3. 3
    Press Exit to come back. A view also ends by itself after 30 minutes.
Watch out
Can't change anything in Admin? You may still be viewing as someone. Look for the red banner and press Exit.

The audit log

Settings›Audit log shows who changed what, and when.

  1. 1
    Changes lists every change with the person, the action, the table and the record. Data access lists lookups of private data.
  2. 2
    Every View as is recorded (view_as_start), with the person viewed.
  3. 3
    Search by table, record id or value.

Staff and roles

People›Staff opens Staff and roles.

  1. 1
    Invite staff here too: Role, Name, Email, Main function and Also covers, then Invite.
  2. 2
    Each row has Send password link, View as (...) and Limits (how much they may approve or refund).
  3. 3
    Press End access to close their staff portal at once. Restore undoes it.

Admin access

Settings›All settings, then Admin access. Who may hold the Admin role.

  1. 1
    The list holds rent@carmacars.ca, and verify-admin@carmacars.ca, the automated test account the site's nightly checks sign in with.
  2. 2
    Below it: everyone who holds the Admin role now. Each must be on the list; the database refuses the Admin role to any other address.
  3. 3
    Change the list only if the business's Admin address changes.

Staff scorecards

See how each assistant is doing against the targets.

  1. 1
    On a staff member's row in Staff and roles, press Scorecard.
  2. 2
    By week shows each measure (for example quote sent within 5 minutes) and the result for each week.
  3. 3
    A task meets its target when it is done by its due time. Daily checks count when done by 9:00 and 18:00.

Sales by staff member

See each person's commissions, quotes, offers, rentals added, bookings confirmed and booked revenue.

  1. 1
    On a staff member's row in Staff and roles, press Sales. Press By week or By month.
  2. 2
    The tiles show the current week or month. By week (or By month) lists each period: quotes created, offers sent, rentals added, bookings confirmed, conversion and booked revenue.
  3. 3
    Follow-ups due lists their open deals with the next step. Deals lists each booking they created, added or sent an offer on, with what it counts.
  4. 4
    A deal credited to the wrong person, or marked sourced when it was not? Press Reassign beside it, choose who, say whether they sourced the client, and write the reason.
  5. 5
    Commission rate gives this person their own flat rate (empty: the Program settings). Reverse beside a commission stops it, for example for a household rental found later.
  6. 6
    Staff enter their own home address (at their first sign-in, before they sign their agreement, or in their profile). It is needed before they add a rental or earn a commission, and spots a household rental. Home address here changes it if it is wrong.
  7. 7
    The whole team: Today›Overview, the Sales card at the bottom. Choose another month and press Show.

Chapter 3

Requests, offers and bookings

A booking starts as a request from the website, or as an offer you send yourself to a customer on the phone. You check it, adjust it and send the offer; an offer that is out is changed in place. After that, the booking page holds the money, documents, changes and history.

The request queue

Open Bookings›Requests. The oldest waiting request is at the top.

  1. 1
    Filter by New, Waiting on the renter, Pushed back, Offer out, Signing and payment, or Offer expired.
  2. 2
    Waiting shows how long the renter has waited; red means past the first reply target (5 minutes, set in Program settings). Open a booking number to answer it.
Good to know
A request whose pickup time passes without an offer expires by itself, with a note in its history. The renter is not emailed unless "Email the renter when their request expires that way" is on in Program settings.

Answer a request with an offer

  1. 1
    The Review and offer tab shows the request as the renter sent it.
  2. 2
    Adjust the car, the dates, the rate type, the places, the rates and fees, the deposit and extras.
  3. 3
    Price on the right updates as you change things.
  4. 4
    Add a message, choose how long the offer stays open, then press Send the offer.
  1. 5
    Changed fields but not ready to send? Press Save changes. The panel "The offer as the renter sees it" shows the result.
  2. 6
    Can't do it? Press Decline and fill in Reason and any alternative.
  3. 7
    Need an answer first? Press Ask the renter to send a question.

Send an offer without a request

Bookings›Send an offer, or Send an offer on Bookings and on Requests. For a customer on the phone, by text or at the counter: no request needed.

  1. 1
    The customer: type a name, phone or email and choose them. Not on file? Press Add a new customer: full legal name, mobile, email (the offer link goes there) and date of birth. A customer already on file with the same email or phone is shown with Use: one person, one record.
  2. 2
    The car and the dates: type the car code, plate or name, or choose from the list; each car says Free or Not free for the dates. Then the pickup and return places (our location, delivery with the address and distance, airport).
  3. 3
    The price: the Rate sheet, A total for the whole rental, or A rate per day (times the days). A price typed by hand needs its reason and can be plus tax or tax included. Set the kilometres, a promo code, the deposit, a discount and each fee (an Admin), and how damage is covered: with option 3 the Protection Plan price is the one the renter pays.
  4. 4
    How it is paid: the rental by card online (the usual way), e-Transfer, cash, cheque, bank transfer, platform payout or the insurer; the deposit by card hold, cash, e-Transfer or no deposit (an Admin).
  5. 5
    Who found this client, the message to the renter, how long the offer is open, and how the link goes: Email, Text, both or neither.
  6. 6
    The right side is the offer as the renter will see it, priced by the database as you type. Press Send the offer. Save without sending keeps it as a request for later.
Good to know
It makes the same request and offer the website makes: the renter approves, signs and pays (or you record the payment), and only then is the car on the calendar. No "request received" email goes to the renter for an offer you send. Staff commissions and statements count it like any other booking.

Change an offer that is out

The renter has the offer (Offered), or approved it and has not paid (Signing and payment). Press Edit the offer on the booking.

  1. 1
    Change anything: the car, the dates, the places, the price, fees, the deposit, add-ons, how it is paid. Each changed field is highlighted and the before and after are listed.
  2. 2
    Write what changed in Message to the renter and choose how long the offer is open.
  3. 3
    Press Save and send the update: the renter gets the new version by email or text (ticked), and the old version no longer counts. Or press Save without telling the renter and tell them yourself.
  4. 4
    A confirmed booking is changed with Change the booking (Propose a change, below).
Tip
No need to withdraw first. Withdraw the offer is only for taking it back for good; Send again, later expiry gives the renter more time, and Renter approved by phone records a yes given on a call.

Edit a booking that has started

On a confirmed, on-trip or returned booking (an Admin: a closed one too), Overview, Edit the booking, Edit.

  1. 1
    Change the pickup or the return (extend or shorten: the rental charge is worked out again at this booking's rates), the car (on trip: the car it leaves goes to Out of service for Fleet to check), the rental charge for the trip, each fee, the kilometres a day, the deposit (an Admin) and the note.
  2. 2
    Type why, if you like, and tick Email the renter what changed, or leave it off for a silent correction. Press Save the changes.
  3. 3
    What is open now shows at once: Email a pay link or Text a pay link for exactly that amount, Charge the saved card (the renter agreed), or record cash or an e-Transfer on the Money tab.
Good to know
Each edit is kept with who, when, why, before and after (Edits, under the form) and in the booking's history. A closed booking's owner split and the staff commission follow. To have the renter accept and sign first, use Propose a change instead.

Fees, a forgotten fee and the balance

On the booking's Money tab, Fees lists each fee. Press Change or add a fee.

  1. 1
    Type a new amount (for the whole trip, before HST) beside any fee, or choose one not on the booking (the young driver fee shows its price for these dates), or add a charge of its own with or without HST.
  2. 2
    Press Save the fees. The balance shows by part (rental, fees) with the pay link, the saved card and Record a payment beside it.
  3. 3
    The renter sees the balance on their booking with a Pay button, and pays by card on Stripe's form.
Good to know
Every change is recorded (Changes to the fees): the amount before and after, who and why. Customer service and sales may change fees while Program settings, Staff may change fees, is on.

Correct a renter's details

On the renter's page (People, Renters) or on any of their bookings, Edit renter.

  1. 1
    Correct the name, email, phone, date of birth, licence, home address (start typing to find it) or the staff note.
  2. 2
    A new email also becomes their sign-in at once (no confirmation needed for a typo you correct); tick Email the renter a link to set a password to send one there. Unsigned agreements and offers go to the new address.
  3. 3
    Press Save the renter. An email that another renter has is refused with their name; the same phone is a warning.
  4. 4
    The same person entered twice? Possible duplicates (same email, same phone, or same name and date of birth) shows under the renter. Merge into this record moves the other record's bookings, licences, cards, payments, documents and messages here and fills any detail missing here; the other record stays, blocked, with the merge in both histories.

Payments by part: the rental, the deposit and later charges

On the booking's Money tab, Money by part shows what is still open for the rental, the fees and later charges, the deposit and the card on file. Each part is paid its own way, in any mix: for example the deposit in cash and the rental by card.

  1. 1
    Record a payment: choose what it is for (the rental, fees, the deposit or a later charge) and how it was paid (e-Transfer, cash, cheque, bank transfer, platform payout, the insurer). An e-Transfer, cheque or transfer needs its reference. Press Record it. The renter gets a receipt; Print prints one to hand over.
  2. 2
    Collect a card: a payment (Everything open by default: the rental, the fees and any later charge in one), the deposit hold or saving the card. The renter types the card in Stripe's secure form: on this device (Open the card form, then hand it over) or by a link texted or emailed to them (Send the link). Staff never type a card number.
  3. 3
    A deposit hold lasts about 7 days and shows the days left; while the car is out it is collected by itself before it lapses. After the return, settle the deposit (below). Capture the hold for all of it keeps the money; a part capture or Release the hold waits for the settlement while the car is out or something is open (an Admin may do it with the reason).
  4. 4
    Charge the saved card (Finance or an Admin): extra kilometres, fuel, damage or a late return, with the reason the renter's receipt shows. For a rental over 7 days, save the card and charge the deposit this way.
  5. 5
    An offer paid another way: once the renter approved and signed and the money is recorded, the booking is confirmed (or press Confirm the booking).
  6. 6
    A deposit taken in cash or by e-Transfer is given back the same way after the return: Finance gets a task; press Returned with the reference or receipt number.
Good to know
Statements, reconciliation and the deposit settlement read each payment with its method: a cash deposit is never refunded to a card.

Settle the deposit

The deposit is never given back by itself. Finance or an Admin settles it after the return: on the booking's Money tab, under Deposit, press Settle the deposit. Issue the Check-in statement first.

  1. 1
    The form shows the deposit, what is still available, the charges owed from the statement and what is taken from the deposit for them. What the deposit does not cover is charged to the card on file; with no card (or a card declined), the renter gets a pay link by email and text, and Finance has a task until it is paid.
  2. 2
    Tolls and tickets can take up to 7 days to come in. Inside that window the whole rest of the deposit is filled in as the hold-back, until the window ends. To give it back sooner, tick Release now anyway: a toll that comes in later is charged to the card on file.
  3. 3
    Something still open keeps the whole deposit: an incident, a claim, new damage at the return not priced, damage found later not billed, a toll or ticket on the car during the rental not matched or not charged, a card dispute, or charges not on a statement yet. The form lists them. Only an Admin can release part of it, with the reason; it is recorded.
  4. 4
    Damage: Keep the whole deposit (damage) turns what is left of the deposit into a damage charge, with what was damaged and the photos; the renter is told. Bill damage adds a damage charge up to the $2,500 deductible (more goes through a claim): the deposit pays first, then the card on file.
  5. 5
    Press Settle. The money moves at Stripe at once: the charges are collected and what is not owed is refunded or the hold released. A step Stripe refuses is tried again by itself, and Finance gets a task if it keeps failing.
Good to know
A card hold lapses after about 7 days. While the car is out, or returned and not settled, the hold is collected in full a day and a half before it lapses: that keeps the money, and the settlement refunds what is not owed. A hold that lapses anyway is charged to the saved card, and Finance is told at once.

A booking

  1. 1
    Tabs: Overview, Money, Changes, History, and Drivers and documents.
  2. 2
    Overview shows the trip, the renter, what is still missing for the handover, and the price.
  3. 3
    Open tasks for this booking, with the queue and the due time.
  4. 4
    Top right: Stop blocking the calendar, Cancel for the renter and Cancel by CarmaCars.
  5. 5

Check a car out or in

From the booking, with your phone or a tablet: the same checklist field staff use.

  1. 1
    A confirmed booking shows Check out (pickup) under Check-out, check-in and signing; a booking on rent shows Check in (return). Today lists the check-outs and check-ins due, each with Check out or Check in.
  2. 2
    The checklist opens: the ready check, the renter and their licence and card, the odometer (km) and fuel or charge, the keys, the photo set (every side, the wheels, the interior, the odometer and fuel gauge, the plate), damage marks with close-ups, then the renter and you sign.
  3. 3
    Photos wait on the device without a connection and upload in the background. Each keeps its time, and the place when the device allows location.
  4. 4
    A field job of the booking is taken over (the history says so); with no job, one is made for you. When done, press See the photo report.
Tip
Fleet, Sales and Customer service check cars out and in the same way from their own booking page.

The photo report, late returns and the return charges

Under Check-out, check-in and signing, press Photo report.

  1. 1
    The report shows the due-back time, the actual check-out and check-in, Late by (or Overdue by while the car is out), the odometer and kilometres over the allowance, the fuel or charge out and in, every photo with its time and place, and the damage.
  2. 2
    At check-in the late return (after the grace period of the fee defaults), the mileage overage and the fuel or recharging are written to the return charges at once. A gas car with no pump receipt is priced at the refill price per eighth (Program settings) plus the markup until the receipt is entered.
  3. 3
    Not charging one? Press Waive beside it and give the reason: it stays waived if the charges are worked out again.
  4. 4
    The time was wrong? Press Correct the check-in time (or the check-out time) with the reason: lateness and the late charges follow, and the history keeps both times.
  5. 5
    The renter gets the same report (without these buttons) by email and in their Documents. Late by shows on the booking and in the Bookings list.

Signatures on the spot, or by link

The renter or an additional driver signs the rental agreement on your device, or on their own phone.

  1. 1
    While a signature is missing, the booking shows Sign now on this device, Email the signing link and Text the signing link.
  2. 2
    No agreement made yet (the renter has not saved their details at home)? Sign now on this device still shows: press Prepare the agreement to sign now and it is made on the spot.
  3. 3
    Sign now on this device: confirm the name matches their licence and that you saw the original licence (photograph it if none is on file), then press Hand the device to and their name.
  4. 4
    They read both agreements (CarmaCars' and Blind Spot's), tick the statements and sign. Then Back to the staff view for the next signer.
  5. 5
    The record is the same as signing online, and says it was signed in person, collected by you, on your device. It is printed on the signed copy's signature record page.

Rental agreements: every version, every field

Calendar sync with Turo and other platforms

A car rented on Turo, Getaround or another site must never be booked twice. Turo has no calendar link, so Turo works by email: Turo's trip emails block the car here, and you block our bookings on Turo by hand. Getaround, Google Calendar and other sites work through calendar links (iCal), both ways.

Set up Turo by email (once)

Turo emails rent@carmacars.ca for every trip booked, changed, extended or cancelled. One Gmail filter forwards those emails to trips@in.carmacars.ca, and each one blocks the car here within a minute.

  1. 1
    In Gmail (rent@carmacars.ca): Settings, See all settings, Forwarding and POP/IMAP. Press Add a forwarding address and type trips@in.carmacars.ca.
  2. 2
    Gmail sends a confirmation code to that address. Open Cars›Calendar sync: the code shows under "Turo by email" (and To do shows "Gmail forwarding to confirm"). Type it in Gmail and press Verify. Leave forwarding itself off: only the filter forwards.
  3. 3
    In Gmail, in the search box, show the search options. From: turo.com. Press Create filter, tick Forward it to: trips@in.carmacars.ca, and press Create filter.
  4. 4
    Nothing to set up per car. Every active car (not the sample cars) is matched by itself: see the next task.

How Turo emails find their car (automatic)

Each Turo email is matched to one of our cars by itself. You never type a car's name on Turo.

  • The reader looks at the Turo car number in the email's links and pictures, the VIN, the plate, the year, make and model (C-Class finds a C 300, Model3 a Model 3, 3 Series a 330i), trim and colour words, the trip already on a car, and whether a car is already busy at those dates.
  • A clear winner is applied at once. When two cars are close (two black 2021 Jettas), the assistant picks between them only if the email tells them apart; otherwise the email waits as "Which car is this?".
  • Every match teaches the car what Turo calls it (its Turo car number and the name in Turo's emails), so the next email for that car matches at once. Each car on Calendar sync shows what it learned; press Forget next to one that is wrong.
  • Rarely needed: Type its name on Turo (optional) on a car whose Turo name the reader cannot place. The Turo car number there is optional too.

Turo trips on the calendar

  1. 1
    A trip booked on Turo becomes an "External booking" block on the car, with the turnaround before and after it. The car cannot be requested, offered or booked here for those times.
  2. 2
    A changed or extended trip moves its block; a cancelled trip frees the car. An older email never undoes a newer one.
  3. 3
    Each car lists its Turo trips: the dates, the Turo trip number, the guest's name as Turo shows it and their phone when the email includes it (press Call). The car's calendar block shows them too. Press Cancelled if Turo cancelled a trip and its email did not come.
  4. 4
    Add a Turo trip: the trip start and end (Toronto time), and the Turo trip number if you have it. Use it for a trip whose email did not come.
  5. 5
    Only what the email says is kept: the car, the dates, the trip number, and the guest's name, phone and email when Turo shows them (a hidden or masked number is never taken). The email itself is never stored.
  6. 6
    Each guest gets one contact record (source Turo), so a Turo guest who calls our line or writes in Live chat is recognised by their phone. Contact them only about their trip: never add them to a marketing list or send promotions (Canada's anti-spam law and Turo's rules on taking guests off the platform). Nothing is sent to them by itself.

Which car is this? (Turo emails to check)

Only an email the reader cannot be sure about waits under "Turo emails to check", with a Fleet task and a line on To do.

  1. 1
    To do shows "Turo emails: which car is this?". Press Choose the car, or Which car is this? on the email in Calendar sync.
  2. 2
    The pop-up shows what Turo calls the car and the trip, then our best guesses first (with colour and the plate's last 3). Press the right car. Not among them? Choose it under Another car and press This car.
  3. 3
    The trip is added and blocks the car, the task closes, and the car remembers what Turo called it: the next email for it matches by itself. Other emails waiting with the same Turo car number are matched at once.
  4. 4
    Something else is missing (the dates or the trip number)? Check the trip on Turo, press Add as a Turo trip, choose the car, check the dates and press Add this trip. Not a trip (a request, a message)? Press Dismiss.

  2. 2
    Press Done on Turo. The booking leaves the list.
  3. 3

Getaround, Google Calendar and other calendar links

Cars›Calendar sync, for a platform that gives a calendar (iCal) link.

  1. 1
    Copy the car's calendar link on the platform (look for "Export calendar" or "iCal"). On Calendar sync, open Add a calendar link, choose Getaround, Google Calendar or Other, paste the link and press Add the link.
  2. 2

Double bookings

If a trip on Turo or another platform overlaps a confirmed CarmaCars booking of the same car, we tell you at once.

  • An urgent Fleet task "Double booking: on Turo and CC-1042", a line on To do and an email to Fleet and the Admins.
  • Calendar sync lists it at the top with both trips. Move, swap or cancel one of the two. The task closes by itself once they no longer overlap.
  • An offer or a booking awaiting payment over a trip on another platform raises a task for customer service: its payment would be refused, so withdraw the offer or change the dates.

A link that stops working

A platform answers an error when a link is reset or removed. After 3 failed reads in a row (a Program setting) Fleet gets a task and an email, and To do shows "Calendar links not working". The trips already read stay on the calendar. Copy the link from the platform again, remove the old one and add the new one.

Good to know
A block from another platform can be removed from the calendar like any block; calendar sync will not put it back. In Program settings, "Calendar sync" and "Turo by email": on or off, how often links are read, the inbound address, the mailboxes allowed to forward (rent@carmacars.ca) and how long the email log is kept (90 days).

Good to know

The rate on a rental

The signals

Each signal adds points to the booking likelihood. They come from what the visitor does on the site, never from what they type.

  • Intent: dates searched again and again, saw the full price, reached the booking form (and which step), came back for the same car on another day, comparing two or three cars, read the cancellation policy, read about the deposit, insurance or requirements, tapped to call, text or WhatsApp us or copied our number, checked delivery, looked through many photos or stayed long on one car.
  • Urgency: pickup within 72 hours, weekend or holiday dates, on a phone late at night, came from a city or model page or a "near me" search.
  • Value: a weekly or monthly rental, a premium car, SUV or van, adding a driver, asked about young drivers, a business email.
  • Hesitation: watching the price (sorted by lowest price, lowered the price limit), left after seeing the price, unsure about the deposit, trouble with a form, the car not free for their dates.
  • Context: came from an ad, a past renter, an offer or payment waiting, a game code not used yet, a popular car. The signals of known customers (offer opened, pay page left, change proposal, request stopped, returning visitor, viewing spree) count too.

Record a service in one step

From the overview, the car's Maintenance tab, or a To do line.

  1. 1
    Tick what was done. The items due are ticked already.
  2. 2

  3. 3
    Who pays: the owner (deducted on the owner's next statement, owner agreement 11.1; the default for an owner's car), CarmaCars (a company expense; the default for a company car), a claim (a repair cost on the claim), the renter (a charge at cost on the rental), or no charge.
  4. 4

Tires and the seasonal swap

On the car's Maintenance tab, under Tires.

  2. 2

  3. 3

Email

Settings›Email log

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4
    Suppressions: Suppress an address stops all mail to it; Lift undoes it.

Backups

  • The database is backed up every night, encrypted, kept for 30 days and test-restored on the spot.
  • Every Monday the newest backup is checked again: it must be under 30 hours old and restore cleanly.
  • A failed backup, or none for 30 hours, opens a site alert: an email (outside the quiet hours), a task and a line on To do. Tell your developer the same day.
  • Keep a copy of the backup key in the company password manager. Without it no backup can be read.

Site health and alerts

Today›To do, the Site health card at the bottom.

  1. 1
    Every 5 minutes the site checks itself: the home page, a car page, the sign-in page and the site's health check, and also emails, scheduled jobs, the nightly backup and Stripe payments.
  2. 2
    Each line shows Working, Problem, Waiting for the website deploy or Not checked yet, what was found and how many checks failed in the last 24 hours.
  3. 3
    Short problems, such as a single Error 1102, only show here and in your morning digest ("Site check: 6 failed checks in the last 24 hours, no alert email").
  4. 4
    An email goes to rent@carmacars.ca only for a real outage: a page failing every check for 15 minutes, emails failing for 30 minutes, the nightly backup failing, or Stripe failing for 30 minutes. It also raises an urgent task and "Site problems found by the site check" first on To do. A failed scheduled job is never emailed.
  5. 5
    At most 3 alert emails a day, and none from 11 pm to 7 am unless the whole site has been down for an hour. An outage held overnight is emailed at 7 am if it lasts. One "Recovered" email follows each alert, and the task closes by itself.
  6. 6
    "Waiting for the website deploy" on the health check means the database was updated before the website: nothing is wrong, and it turns to Working after the deploy.
Good to know
