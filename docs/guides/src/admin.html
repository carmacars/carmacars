Before you begin

Start here

Who this guide is for

CarmaCars Admins: the owner of the business and anyone an Admin invites with the Admin role. Admin sees and changes everything, and can open any other portal as one of its people.

What you need

  • Your Admin email address. The owner's account is rent@carmacars.ca
  • Access to that inbox (links work for one hour, once)
  • A computer for most work. Admin also works on a phone, with the menu as one scrolling strip
Sign in at
carmacars.ca/sign-in?next=/admin
You land on Today, the Admin dashboard.

Your first sign-in

  1. 1
    First time? Go to carmacars.ca/reset-password, enter your Admin email and press Send the link.
  2. 2
    Open the email "Reset your CarmaCars password" within one hour and press Choose a new password.
  3. 3
    Type a New password of 8 characters or more and press Save the new password. You land in Admin.
  4. 4
    Next time, sign in at carmacars.ca/sign-in with that email and password.
Good to know
A link that has expired or was already used opens "This link has expired" with Send me a new link. Invited by another Admin? The invitation email works the same way.

Getting around Admin

Admin opens on Today: what needs you now. The rail on the left groups every screen into Operate, Fleet, Money, Recover, People and Setup.

Today

Start every shift here.

  1. 1

  3. 3
    Requests waiting, pickups and returns today, late returns and money this month.
  4. 4
    Press Work the requests to go straight to the queue, or Calendar for the master calendar.

Chapter 2

People and access

Let people in, help them back in when they are locked out, and see exactly what they see. Every step is recorded in the audit log.

Portals and sign-in

Open People›Portals and sign-in. Every portal has a card.

  1. 1
    Each card links to Its sign-in page, the address to send that person.
  2. 2
    Choose a person in the list on the card.
  3. 3
    Press Open as this person to see that portal as they see it.

Invite a person

Invitations work for every role. The person gets our invitation email and chooses a password.

  1. 1
    Under Invite a person, choose the Role: Admin, Staff (assistant), Field staff, Owner on file or Investor on file.
  2. 2
    Fill in the Name and Email.
  3. 3
    For staff, pick the Main function, and tick Also covers for any other function they help with. For field staff, pick the Field function.
  4. 4
    Press Invite. The link in the email works for one hour.
Tip
Someone who already signs in gets the new role at once, with no email. Owners and investors must be on file first: Admin > Owners and Admin > Investors also have a one-click invite for a record without a sign-in.

Send a password link

For anyone who forgot their password or let their invitation expire.

  1. 1
    Under People who can sign in, search by name or email.
  2. 2
    On their row, press Send password link. They get "Reset your CarmaCars password", valid for one hour.
  3. 3
    View as (...) on the same row opens their portal, read only.

View as: see what someone sees

  1. 1
    A red banner says "Viewing as Name (email), read only." on every page.
  2. 2
    Click around their portal. Nothing can be changed: every save is refused.
  3. 3
    Press Exit to come back. A view also ends by itself after 30 minutes.
Watch out
Can't change anything in Admin? You may still be viewing as someone. Look for the red banner and press Exit.

The audit log

People›Audit log shows who changed what, and when.

  1. 1
    Changes lists every change with the person, the action, the table and the record. Data access lists lookups of private data.
  2. 2
    Every View as is recorded (view_as_start), with the person viewed.
  3. 3
    Search by table, record id or value.

Staff and roles

Settings›Staff and roles (also under People›Staff).

  1. 1
    Invite staff here too: Role, Name, Email, Main function and Also covers, then Invite.
  2. 2
    Each row has Send password link, View as (...) and Limits (how much they may approve or refund).
  3. 3
    Press End access to close their staff portal at once. Restore undoes it.

Staff scorecards

See how each assistant is doing against the targets.

  1. 1
    On a staff member's row in Staff and roles, press Scorecard.
  2. 2
    By week shows each measure (for example quote sent within 15 minutes) and the result for each week.
  3. 3
    A task meets its target when it is done by its due time. Daily checks count when done by 9:00 and 18:00.

Chapter 3

Requests, offers and bookings

Every booking starts as a request. You check it, adjust it and send the offer. After that, the booking page holds the money, documents, changes and history.

The request queue

Open Requests. The oldest waiting request is at the top.

  1. 1
    Filter by New, Waiting on the renter, Pushed back, Offer out, Signing and payment, or Offer expired.
  2. 2
    Waiting shows how long the renter has waited. Open a booking number to answer it.

Answer a request with an offer

  1. 1
    The Review and offer tab shows the request as the renter sent it.
  2. 2
    Adjust the car, the dates, the rate type, the places, the rates and fees, the deposit and extras.
  3. 3
    Price on the right updates as you change things.
  4. 4
    Add a message, choose how long the offer stays open, then press Send the offer.
  1. 5
    Changed fields but not ready to send? Press Save changes. The panel "The offer as the renter sees it" shows the result.
  2. 6
    Can't do it? Press Decline and fill in Reason and any alternative.
  3. 7
    Need an answer first? Press Ask the renter to send a question.
Tip
After the offer is out: Withdraw to edit takes it back, Send again, later expiry gives the renter more time, and Renter approved by phone records a yes given on a call.

A booking

Open Bookings, then a booking number.

  1. 1
    Tabs: Overview, Money, Changes, History, and Drivers and documents.
  2. 2
    Overview shows the trip, the renter, what is still missing for the handover, and the price.
  3. 3
    Open tasks for this booking, with the queue and the due time.
  4. 4
    Top right: Stop blocking the calendar, Cancel for the renter and Cancel by CarmaCars.

Propose a change to a confirmed booking

The renter gets an email and answers on their booking. The calendar moves only when they accept.

  1. 1
    Open the booking's Changes tab. Under Propose a change, set the new pickup, return or car.
  2. 2
    To price it by hand, type a New rental charge before tax. Leave it empty to use the car's rates.
  3. 3
    Write the Reason, for the renter. They read it.
  4. 4
    Press Preview the price, then Send to the renter.
Tip
The renter has 48 hours to accept (and sign the amendment) or decline, with one reminder. Any extra is charged to their Card on File; a lower price raises a refund for your approval.

Chapter 4

Calendars

The master calendar shows every car, booking, request and block. Each car also has its own calendar and turnaround time.

The master calendar

  1. 1
    Move through time with the arrows and Today, or pick a date.
  2. 2
    Choose the range: Today, Week, 2 weeks or Month. Agenda shows a list instead.
  3. 3
    The legend: confirmed, on rent, request, offer, not blocking, block and Turo.
  4. 4
    Each row is a car. Bars are bookings and blocks; the red line is now.

Move a booking, add or remove a block

  1. 1
    To move a booking, drag its bar to the new car or time, then press Confirm the move.
  2. 2
    To block a car, double-click its row where the block starts, then press Create the block. New block at the top does the same.
  3. 3
    To remove a block, open it and press Remove the block.
Tip
After every confirmed trip a cleaning block holds the car for its turnaround time. It follows changes and extensions and goes away with a cancellation. Remove it from the side panel if the car is ready sooner; that time is then free.

A car's own calendar

Vehicles›the car›Calendar

  1. 1
    The Calendar tab shows only this car, with its bookings and blocks.
  2. 2
    Turnaround between trips on this car shows the time used. Press Change to give this car its own time.

Chapter 5

Fleet and listings

Vehicles is the fleet. Listings is the pipeline that turns a car into a page on carmacars.ca: photos, copy, a duplicate check and a covered plate on every public photo.

Vehicles

  1. 1
    Filter by status and mode. Each row shows the plate, class, owner, mode, status, listing and daily rate.
  2. 2
    Search by plate, model or owner. Bulk rate change changes rates of many cars at once.
  3. 3
    Open a car for its tabs: Overview, Rates, Fees and coverage, Calendar, Listing, and Owner, documents, tracker.
Coming soon
The Tracker card on a car says "Coming soon": live location, the remote immobilize request and mileage from the tracker need the tracker company's connection first.

The listing pipeline

Open Listings. A draft waiting for review shows Needs review.

  1. 1
    Each row shows the car, plate, state, photos and the pipeline's progress.
  2. 2
    Press Start a listing to begin one for a car that has none.
  3. 3
    Open a car to review it.

Review a listing

  1. 1
    Pipeline shows each step done, such as photo sizes, copy draft and backgrounds.
  2. 2
    Similar cars on file lists possible duplicates. Press Not a duplicate or Same car. An open flag stops publishing.
  3. 3
    Check the copy: headline, description, features, questions, search title and description.
  4. 4
    Under Photos, each public photo shows its plate state: Plate not checked, Plate covered or No plate visible.

Cover the licence plate

On each public photo press Cover the plate. The editor opens.

  1. 1
    The editor suggests a box over the plate. Check it. Suggest the plate asks again.
  2. 2
    Drag over a plate to draw a box. Drag a box to move it, or its corner to resize it.
  3. 3
    Under Cover the plate with, choose A CarmaCars plate, Pixelation or Blur.
  4. 4
    Press Save the covered photo. Clear the boxes starts over.
  5. 5
    No plate in the shot? Press No plate visible on this photo.
Tip
The covered photo replaces the public one; the original is kept privately. Keyboard: N adds a box, the arrows move it, Shift and the arrows resize it, Delete removes it.

Publish

  1. 1
    When every public photo is covered or marked, press Approve and publish.
  2. 2
    Or press Approve, publish later, or Send back with what to fix.
Watch out
Publishing waits for a plate decision on every public photo ("Cover the licence plate or mark it not visible on every public photo first"). Listings already live show "Plate not checked" until someone checks them.

Owners and onboarding

Owners apply on the site. You approve them, set their terms, and check their forms. Their page shows everything about them in one place.

Owner applications

People›Owners

  1. 1
    Each owner shows their application, how many cars, and where onboarding stands.
  2. 2
    View as (owner portal) shows their portal. Send password link helps them sign in.
  3. 3
    Invite an owner on file: fill in Name and Email under Invite an owner, then press Send invitation.

Approve an application and set terms

  1. 1
    Open the application and press Approve and set terms (or Set terms (standard grid)).
  2. 2
    The owner signs the agreements and pays the upfront invoice in their portal.
  3. 3
    Paid offline? On the invoice, press Mark paid and fill in Reference, Date received and Amount ($).

An owner's page

Open an owner from Owners. The details are in the right-hand column.

  1. 1
    Upfront invoices: issue the first and last month's insurance fee for each car whose terms are set.
  2. 2
    Owner: address, sign-in, agreements, and what is still missing for the registry forms.
  3. 3
    Statements and payouts for this owner.
  4. 4
    Forms C and D, with a link to the review queue.
  5. 5
    Further down: View as (owner portal), Send password link and Send a sign-in link to the owner portal.

Forms C and D

People›Owner forms. Owners upload the scans signed in front of witnesses.

  1. 1
    Each row shows the owner, the form, the witnesses and when it was uploaded.
  2. 2
    Press Open to read the scan.
  3. 3
    Press Approve, or Send back with What to fix (the owner reads this).

Chapter 7

Owner money

Owner fee invoices are issued on the notice day and debited on the 15th. Statements are built, approved and issued each month, and payouts follow.

Owner invoices

Money›Owner invoices

  1. 1
    Run the monthly invoices issues the invoices for the 15th by hand, for a missed month. An invoice is never issued twice.
  2. 2
    Issue all tracker invoices bills the Tracker Install for each car waiting for its tracker.
  3. 3
    Filter by Open, Paid, Failed or In arrears. Failed invoices show the reason.
  4. 4
    Paid by e-Transfer or cheque? Open the invoice, press Mark paid, fill in Reference, Date received and Amount ($).

The automatic debit

The debit runs by itself. Your job is to watch for returned debits and owners without a mandate.

1Notice dayThe 5th: invoices issued, one notice per owner
2Debit dayThe 15th from 6:00: one debit per invoice
3ResultsCleared: paid. Returned: $50 Late Fee once
4RetryFrom the 18th: the second and last try
5ArrearsReturned again: a task and an email to you
  1. 1
    The tiles show the next debit day and notice day, scheduled, processing, cleared and returned debits.
  2. 2
    How it runs explains the policy in words, read from Program settings.
  3. 3
    Scheduled and processing lists each debit. Pause holds one (a reason is needed).
  4. 4
    Owners lists each mandate. Use Pause, Resume or Send a notice now.
  1. 5
    Results: each debit, cleared or returned, with the bank's reason.
  2. 6
    Run log: each notice and debit run, with its counts.
  3. 7
    Notices sent: who was told what, for which debit day.
Watch out
A debit is never more than its notice said. An owner with no mandate gets an email on the notice day and you get a task; nothing is debited for them. Change the days, retries and Late Fee under Debit settings (Program settings).

Owner statements

Money›Owner statements

  1. 1
    Press Build all for a month (or Build one).
  2. 2
    Open each draft. Check it, Rebuild after a correction, Preview PDF, then Approve.
  3. 3
    Press Issue to the owner. Reissue sends a corrected copy; CSV exports it.

An approved statement: the owner gets it by email with the PDF on the 10th, and payouts follow under Payouts.

Company statements

CarmaCars' own monthly statement, in twelve sections.

  1. 1
    Choose the month. The tiles show revenue, fees, refunds, expenses and the company net.
  2. 2
    Switch between Total, Per owner and Per vehicle.
  3. 3
    Download PDF, PDF by owner or PDF by vehicle, or press Email me the PDFs.
  4. 4
    Finalize freezes the month. Rebuild works until then.

Chapter 8

Tolls and tickets

Toll notices and tickets arrive by mail and email. Read them into the system, check each line against the notice, and the matching, charges and emails follow.

The work queue

Recover›Tolls and tickets

  1. 1
    Filter the queue: Unmatched, Needs review, Matched not charged, Owner not billed, Staff, Charged not notified, Disputed.
  2. 2
    Each toll shows its notice, kind, issuer, plate, when, amount and who it was matched to.
  3. 3
    Add one by one with Log one, then Save and match. Import a CSV takes a statement file.

Inside a toll

  1. 1
    Open a toll from the queue. Decide who pays: Charge the renter, Bill to the owner or CarmaCars pays.
  2. 2
    Wrong notice? Press Dispute. Finance can also press Match again.
  3. 3
    Reading profiles (top right on Read a notice) teach the reader an issuer's layout. Paste a notice's text under "Try it" to test one.

Read a notice

  1. 1
    Press Read a notice. Choose the file: a PDF, a photo (JPEG or PNG) or a CSV, under 15 MB.
  2. 2
    Leave Reading profile on finding it from the text, or choose the issuer.
  3. 3
    Press Read it. The file is stored first, then read in your browser.
  4. 4
    Notices read lists every read with its status and counts.

Check the lines and import

  1. 1
    The notice is on the left (Zoom, Open the file). The lines are on the right.
  2. 2
    Check each line beside the notice: kind, issuer, notice number, plate, date and time, amount, fees, due date.
  3. 3
    Fix what was read wrong, then press Checked against the notice. Leave out skips a line; Put it back returns it.
  4. 4
    Press Save as checked, then Confirm and import.
Tip
Renters matched with high confidence are charged at import, with the notice and the administration fee, when "Charge renters matched with high confidence now" is ticked. Other lines go to the work queue or stay as exceptions with the reason.

Chapter 9

Settings

Settings change the public site and the rules of the program at once. Program settings are kept as dated rows, so earlier values stay on record.

The Settings page

  1. 1
    Site setup lists what is still missing on the public site, with Open site settings.
  2. 2
    Site settings: contact details, hours, tagline, logo, socials, Google place id and featured cars.
  3. 3
    Program settings: every timing and policy value.
  4. 4
    Forms: the official ServiceOntario PDFs and what goes in each field.
  5. 5
    Insurers: insurance companies billed for bookings.

The other tiles: Site content (headings, FAQs, legal text), Fee defaults, Deposit tiers, Kilometre choices, Vehicle classes, Airports and delivery, Coverage prices, Email aliases and reply-to, Staff and roles, Promo codes and Quick replies.

Program settings

  1. 1
    Turnaround between trips: the time held between two trips. The cleaning block after a trip is this time.
  2. 2
    Settings come in groups such as Company, Booking requests and offers, Owner debits and statement emails, Deposits, Delivery, Owners and fleet, and Tolls and damage. Open one to see its fields.
  3. 3
    Change a value and press Save. It is saved as a new row from today; the rows before stay as they were.

Official ServiceOntario forms

Until a form's official PDF is mapped, owners get a data sheet with the values.

  1. 1
    Open Settings›Forms. Each form shows "Data sheet in use", "Mapping to check" or "Official PDF in use".
  2. 2
    Press Upload the official fillable PDF. Its fields are read in your browser.
  3. 3
    For each field, choose What goes in it (or "The owner signs here"), a Format, and Only when if it applies.
  4. 4
    Press Save the mapping, then Download a filled preview for one car and check it.
  5. 5
    Press Checked: use for owners. Every owner's form of that kind is filled again, except ones already signed or filed.

Insurers and the other lists

  1. 1
    Open Insurers. Each insurer on file opens to edit its billing contact, email, phone, address and notes.
  2. 2
    To add one, fill in the Add form (Name, Billing contact, Billing email and the rest) and press Add.
  3. 3
    Deposit tiers, Kilometre choices, Vehicle classes, Airports, Promo codes and Quick replies work the same way.

Chapter 10

Email, backups and site health

Keep an eye on email use against the daily limit, the nightly backup and the occasional error page.

Email

People›Email

  1. 1
    The banner shows today's emails against the daily limit. From 80, Admins get a notice; near the limit, digests and marketing wait first and booking and sign-in emails are held last.
  2. 2
    Filter the Outbox: All, Queued, Held, Sent, Failed, Suppressed or Skipped.
  3. 3
    Open an email to Resend or Cancel it.
  4. 4
    Suppressions: Suppress an address stops all mail to it; Lift undoes it.

Backups

  • The database is backed up every night, encrypted, kept for 30 days and test-restored on the spot.
  • Every Monday the newest backup is checked again: it must be under 30 hours old and restore cleanly.
  • Nothing is emailed. A failed backup shows as a failed scheduled job under Alerts on Today. Tell your developer the same day.
  • Keep a copy of the backup key in the company password manager. Without it no backup can be read.

When a page shows "Error 1102"

Our web host's free plan sometimes stops a page that takes too long. Reload the page; you are still signed in. Moving to the Workers Paid plan removes it.
