Before you begin

Who this guide is for

The CarmaCars Admin: the owner of the business, signed in as rent@carmacars.ca. Admin sees and changes everything, and can open any other portal as one of its people. Staff work in the staff portal instead.

What you need

  • The Admin email address: rent@carmacars.ca. Only that address can hold the Admin role (Admin > Settings > Admin access lists it)
  • Access to that inbox (links work for 24 hours, once)
  • A computer for most work. Admin also works on a phone, with the whole menu behind one Menu button
Sign in at
carmacars.ca/sign-in?next=/admin
You land on To do, the list of what needs you.

Your first sign-in

  1. 1
    First time? Go to carmacars.ca/reset-password, enter your Admin email and press Send the link.
  2. 2
    Open the email "Reset your CarmaCars password" within 24 hours and press Choose a new password.
  3. 3
    Type a New password of 8 characters or more and press Save the new password. You land in Admin.
  4. 4
    Next time, sign in at carmacars.ca/sign-in with that email and password.
Good to know
A link that has expired or was already used opens "This link has expired" with Send me a new link. Invited by another Admin? The invitation email works the same way.

Chapter 1

Getting around Admin

Admin opens on To do: what needs you now, most urgent first. The menu groups every screen into seven groups: Today, Bookings, Cars, Owners and partners, Money, People and Settings. Search finds any booking, car or person.

To do

Start every shift here. Each line counts what is waiting and has one button to deal with it.

  1. 1
    The list is in order of urgency: owner applications, booking requests, changes renters asked for, failed payments, agreements to countersign, tolls, listings, plates not checked, insurance, cars using sample photos, staff commissions to approve and to pay, prices typed by hand to review, and staff who say they sourced a website request.
  2. 2
    Press the button on a line to go straight to that work. A line with nothing waiting moves to the bottom, under "more with nothing waiting".
  3. 3
    Below the list: today's pickups (ready or not ready) and today's returns (due or late).
  4. 4
    Press Overview for the money, fleet and queue figures, the alerts and the team's sales for the month.

The menu

The current group is open, with its pages; press another group to open it. On a phone the whole menu sits behind one Menu button that says where you are.

Chapter 2

People and access

Let people in, help them back in when they are locked out, and see exactly what they see. Every step is recorded in the audit log.

Portals and sign-in

Open People›Portals and view as. Every portal has a card.

  1. 1
    Each card links to Its sign-in page, the address to send that person.
  2. 2
    Choose a person in the list on the card.
  3. 3
    Press Open as this person to see that portal as they see it.

Invite a person

Invitations work for staff, field staff, owners and investors. The person gets our invitation email and chooses a password.

  1. 1
    Under Invite a person, choose the Role: Staff (assistant), Field staff, Owner on file or Investor on file. Admin is not offered: only rent@carmacars.ca can be an Admin.
  2. 2
    Fill in the Name and Email.
  3. 3
    For staff, pick the Main function, and tick Also covers for any other function they help with. For field staff, pick the Field function.
  4. 4
    Press Invite. The link in the email works for 24 hours.
Tip
Someone who already signs in gets the new role at once, with no email. Owners and investors must be on file first: Owners and partners > Owners and People > Investors also have a one-click invite for a record without a sign-in.

Send a password link

For anyone who forgot their password or let their invitation expire.

  1. 1
    Under People who can sign in, search by name or email.
  2. 2
    On their row, press Send password link. They get "Reset your CarmaCars password", valid for 24 hours.
  3. 3
    View as (...) on the same row opens their portal, read only.

View as: see what someone sees

  1. 1
    A red banner says "Viewing as Name (email), read only." on every page.
  2. 2
    Click around their portal. Nothing can be changed: every save is refused.
  3. 3
    Press Exit to come back. A view also ends by itself after 30 minutes.
Watch out
Can't change anything in Admin? You may still be viewing as someone. Look for the red banner and press Exit.

The audit log

Settings›Audit log shows who changed what, and when.

  1. 1
    Changes lists every change with the person, the action, the table and the record. Data access lists lookups of private data.
  2. 2
    Every View as is recorded (view_as_start), with the person viewed.
  3. 3
    Search by table, record id or value.

Staff and roles

People›Staff opens Staff and roles.

  1. 1
    Invite staff here too: Role, Name, Email, Main function and Also covers, then Invite.
  2. 2
    Each row has Send password link, View as (...) and Limits (how much they may approve or refund).
  3. 3
    Press End access to close their staff portal at once. Restore undoes it.

Admin access

Settings›All settings, then Admin access. Who may hold the Admin role.

  1. 1
    The list holds rent@carmacars.ca, and verify-admin@carmacars.ca, the automated test account the site's nightly checks sign in with.
  2. 2
    Below it: everyone who holds the Admin role now. Each must be on the list; the database refuses the Admin role to any other address.
  3. 3
    Change the list only if the business's Admin address changes.

Staff scorecards

See how each assistant is doing against the targets.

  1. 1
    On a staff member's row in Staff and roles, press Scorecard.
  2. 2
    By week shows each measure (for example quote sent within 5 minutes) and the result for each week.
  3. 3
    A task meets its target when it is done by its due time. Daily checks count when done by 9:00 and 18:00.

Sales by staff member

See each person's commissions, quotes, offers, rentals added, bookings confirmed and booked revenue.

  1. 1
    On a staff member's row in Staff and roles, press Sales. Press By week or By month.
  2. 2
    The tiles show the current week or month. By week (or By month) lists each period: quotes created, offers sent, rentals added, bookings confirmed, conversion and booked revenue.
  3. 3
    Follow-ups due lists their open deals with the next step. Deals lists each booking they created, added or sent an offer on, with what it counts.
  4. 4
    A deal credited to the wrong person, or marked sourced when it was not? Press Reassign beside it, choose who, say whether they sourced the client, and write the reason.
  5. 5
    Commission rate gives this person their own flat rate (empty: the Program settings). Reverse beside a commission stops it, for example for a household rental found later.
  6. 6
    The whole team: Today›Overview, the Sales card at the bottom. Choose another month and press Show.
FigureHow it is counted
Credited toWhoever created the quote or added the rental; for a website request, whoever sent its first offer. An Admin can reassign it.
ConversionOf the deals credited in the period, the share confirmed so far.
Booked revenueThe rental charge before tax, after discounts, for bookings confirmed in the period. A cancelled booking counts only what was kept; refunds come off.

Chapter 3

Requests, offers and bookings

Every booking starts as a request. You check it, adjust it and send the offer. After that, the booking page holds the money, documents, changes and history.

The request queue

Open Bookings›Requests. The oldest waiting request is at the top.

  1. 1
    Filter by New, Waiting on the renter, Pushed back, Offer out, Signing and payment, or Offer expired.
  2. 2
    Waiting shows how long the renter has waited; red means past the first reply target (5 minutes, set in Program settings). Open a booking number to answer it.

Answer a request with an offer

  1. 1
    The Review and offer tab shows the request as the renter sent it.
  2. 2
    Adjust the car, the dates, the rate type, the places, the rates and fees, the deposit and extras.
  3. 3
    Price on the right updates as you change things.
  4. 4
    Add a message, choose how long the offer stays open, then press Send the offer.
  1. 5
    Changed fields but not ready to send? Press Save changes. The panel "The offer as the renter sees it" shows the result.
  2. 6
    Can't do it? Press Decline and fill in Reason and any alternative.
  3. 7
    Need an answer first? Press Ask the renter to send a question.
Tip
After the offer is out: Withdraw to edit takes it back, Send again, later expiry gives the renter more time, and Renter approved by phone records a yes given on a call.

A booking

Open Bookings›Bookings, then a booking number. Or type the number in the search box.

  1. 1
    Tabs: Overview, Money, Changes, History, and Drivers and documents.
  2. 2
    Overview shows the trip, the renter, what is still missing for the handover, and the price.
  3. 3
    Open tasks for this booking, with the queue and the due time.
  4. 4
    Top right: Stop blocking the calendar, Cancel for the renter and Cancel by CarmaCars.

Propose a change to a confirmed booking

The renter gets an email and answers on their booking. The calendar moves only when they accept.

  1. 1
    Open the booking's Changes tab. Under Propose a change, set the new pickup, return or car.
  2. 2
    To price it by hand, type a New rental charge before tax. Leave it empty to use the car's rates.
  3. 3
    Write the Reason, for the renter. They read it.
  4. 4
    Press Preview the price, then Send to the renter.

Who a booking is credited to

Overview (and Review and offer) shows the Sales credit: the staff member the booking counts for in their sales.

  1. 1
    A quote or a rental is credited to whoever created or added it. A request from the website is credited to whoever sent its first offer.
  2. 2
    To give it to someone else, press Reassign, choose who (or Nobody), say whether they sourced the client and write the reason. The booking's history and the audit log keep it, and the deal stays in the same week and month.
  3. 3
    Sourced means the staff member chose I sourced this client when they entered it, it came with a field staff referral link, or you credited it. Only a sourced booking earns a staff commission. A website request earns none unless you credit someone.
  4. 4
    Someone said they sourced a website request? The credit line shows the claim: press Credit them or Not theirs, with a reason.
  5. 5
    A price typed by hand shows beside the rate-sheet price, with the reason. A price under the floor or past the discount limit is flagged: press Mark reviewed once you have looked at it.
Tip
The renter has 48 hours to accept (and sign the amendment) or decline, with one reminder. Any extra is charged to their Card on File; a lower price raises a refund for your approval.

Chapter 4

Calendars

The master calendar shows every car, booking, request and block. Each car also has its own calendar and turnaround time.

The master calendar

  1. 1
    Move through time with the arrows and Today, or pick a date.
  2. 2
    Choose the range: Today, Week, 2 weeks or Month. Agenda shows a list instead.
  3. 3
    The legend: confirmed, on rent, request, offer, not blocking, block and Turo.
  4. 4
    Each row is a car. Bars are bookings and blocks; the red line is now.

Move a booking, add or remove a block

  1. 1
    To move a booking, drag its bar to the new car or time, then press Confirm the move.
  2. 2
    To block a car, double-click its row where the block starts, then press Create the block. New block at the top does the same.
  3. 3
    To remove a block, open it and press Remove the block.
Tip
After every confirmed trip a cleaning block holds the car for its turnaround time. It follows changes and extensions and goes away with a cancellation. Remove it from the side panel if the car is ready sooner; that time is then free.

A car's own calendar

Cars›Vehicles, then the car, then its Calendar tab.

  1. 1
    The Calendar tab shows only this car, with its bookings and blocks.
  2. 2
    Turnaround between trips on this car shows the time used. Press Change to give this car its own time.

Chapter 5

Fleet and listings

Vehicles is the fleet. Listings is the pipeline that turns a car into a page on carmacars.ca: photos, copy, a duplicate check and a covered plate on every public photo.

Vehicles

Cars›Vehicles

  1. 1
    Filter by status and mode. Each row shows the plate, class, owner, mode, status, listing and daily rate.
  2. 2
    Search by plate, model or owner. Cars›Rates for many cars changes the rates of many cars at once.
  3. 3
    Open a car for its tabs: Overview, Rates, Fees and coverage, Calendar, Listing, and Owner, documents, tracker.
Coming soon
The Tracker card on a car says "Coming soon": live location, the remote immobilize request and mileage from the tracker need the tracker company's connection first.

The listing pipeline

Open Cars›Listings. A draft waiting for review shows Needs review.

  1. 1
    Each row shows the car, plate, state, photos and the pipeline's progress.
  2. 2
    Press Start a listing to begin one for a car that has none.
  3. 3
    Open a car to review it.

Review a listing

  1. 1
    Pipeline shows each step done, such as photo sizes, copy draft and backgrounds.
  2. 2
    Similar cars on file lists possible duplicates. Press Not a duplicate or Same car. An open flag stops publishing.
  3. 3
    Check the copy: headline, description, features, questions, search title and description.
  4. 4
    Under Photos, each public photo shows its plate state: Plate not checked, Plate covered or No plate visible.

Cover the licence plate

On each public photo press Cover the plate. The editor opens.

  1. 1
    The editor suggests a box over the plate. Check it. Suggest the plate asks again.
  2. 2
    Drag over a plate to draw a box. Drag a box to move it, or its corner to resize it.
  3. 3
    Under Cover the plate with, choose A CarmaCars plate, Pixelation or Blur.
  4. 4
    Press Save the covered photo. Clear the boxes starts over.
  5. 5
    No plate in the shot? Press No plate visible on this photo.
Tip
The covered photo replaces the public one; the original is kept privately. Keyboard: N adds a box, the arrows move it, Shift and the arrows resize it, Delete removes it.

Publish

  1. 1
    When every public photo is covered or marked, press Approve and publish.
  2. 2
    Or press Approve, publish later, or Send back with what to fix.
Watch out
Publishing waits for a plate decision on every public photo ("Cover the licence plate or mark it not visible on every public photo first"). Listings already live show "Plate not checked" until someone checks them.

A listing without photos yet: sample photos

A car can be listed before its photos are taken. Until then the site shows a sample photo.

  1. 1
    Submit the listing without photos. The listing copy is still written for you from the car's details.
  2. 2
    The car page and the car's card show a sample photo, captioned "Sample photo", so renters know it is not this car. Staff see "Using sample photos" on the car and in Listings.
  3. 3
    Publishing does not wait for a plate check: a sample photo has no plate.
  4. 4
    Add the real photos when they are taken. Once real photos are approved they replace the sample at once; nothing else to do.

Sample photos

Settings›Sample photos. The pictures a car shows until its real photos are in.

  1. 1
    Every body type has a built-in picture (sedan, SUV, truck, van, coupe, hatchback, electric car).
  2. 2
    Add your own: choose the Body type, and a Make and Model if the photo is of that exact model, pick the Photo, and press Add the photo.
  3. 3
    The most specific photo wins: make and model, then make and body type, then body type. Turn off or Delete a photo you no longer want.
  4. 4
    Cars using sample photos lists the cars still waiting for real photos. It is also a line on To do.

Chapter 6

Partners, company cars and rentals added by hand

A car comes from one of three places: a program owner, CarmaCars itself, or a partner rental company. You add cars and rentals by hand, retire cars, and delete cars that were added by mistake.

Add a car

Cars›Vehicles, then Add a car. First choose who supplies it.

  1. 1
    A program owner's car: choose the owner. The car starts its onboarding (terms, agreements, registry) as if the owner had applied.
  2. 2
    CarmaCars-owned (company fleet): no owner. All of its revenue is CarmaCars' and it shows in the company statement as company fleet.
  3. 3
    A partner rental company's car: choose the partner. Leave the commission empty to use the partner's, or type one for this car.
  4. 4
    Type the VIN, the plate and the details, and the rates if you have them. Press Add the car.
Tip
The duplicate check runs as for every car: a VIN only once, a plate on one active car, and a likely twin waits for you in Similar cars before it is published.

On a car's page, Who supplies the car shows the supplier and the commission, and changes them. Vehicles filters by supply type.

Partners

Owners and partners›Partners. Partner rental companies whose cars we rent out on commission.

  1. 1
    Press Add a partner: business and legal name, contacts, HST number, commission and insurance with its expiry date.
  2. 2
    Open a partner for its cars, bookings, statements, payouts and documents. A warning shows when its insurance expires soon, and Tasks gets a reminder (Program settings sets how many days before).
  3. 3
    Banking (Admins only): Save the payout account for the account we pay the partner into. The account number is stored encrypted. Press Verify once checked.
  4. 4
    Portal: Invite to the owner portal gives the partner the owner portal with its cars, bookings, statements and the partner agreement, filled in and signed on screen in three taps.
  5. 5
    Signed on paper? Press Print the partner agreement, then record the signed scan under Record a paper signature.

Add a rental by hand

Bookings›Add a rental. For phone, walk-in, Turo, partner and insurer bookings.

  1. 1
    Choose the car and the dates. The check shows clashes with other bookings and the cleaning time after each trip. An Admin may book over a clash with a reason.
  2. 2
    Find the renter by name, email or phone, or add a new one: name, email, phone, date of birth and licence. Add any other drivers.
  3. 3
    Check the price: the car's rates, extras, coverage and deposit. An Admin may set the price by hand; assistants use the rate sheet and the discounts allowed.
  4. 4
    Choose Where it came from and how it is paid: Paid another way, Send a payment link, Charge the saved card when the renter signs or Unpaid: invoice later.
  5. 5
    Press Check the calendar and the price. Tick Do not email the renter if they should not get the confirmation. Press Add the rental.
Tip
The rental is on the calendars at once (a payment link waits for the payment). The renter signs the rental agreement in their portal, or on paper and you record it. With the saved card, nothing is charged when you add the rental: the card is charged when the renter presses Accept and sign, and until then the rental is awaiting signature. On the booking, Verified in person records a licence checked at the counter. A past rental is recorded as completed.

Retire or delete a car

Cars›Retire or delete, or the car's own page.

  1. 1
    Retire this car takes the car off the site and the calendars and keeps every record. It needs no future confirmed booking. Restore brings it back.
  2. 2
    Delete this car removes a car added by mistake, with its photos. Only a car with nothing tied to money (no booking, payment, statement, toll or damage). Type the plate or the name to confirm.
  3. 3
    On the same page, Delete all sample cars deletes every sample car that meets the rule at once. Type DELETE SAMPLE CARS to confirm; the ones kept show why.

Tolls when two rentals overlap

Bookings held for an insurer and other placeholders are never matched to a toll. When two real rentals overlap the toll time, nobody is charged automatically: the toll waits in the work queue under Two rentals, with both bookings, and you choose who pays.

Chapter 7

Owners and onboarding

Owners apply on the site with List your car. Every application waits for you in one place, Applications: approve it, ask the owner for something, or decline it. Approved owners sign and pay in their portal.

Applications

Owners and partners›Applications. Every owner application, newest first.

  1. 1
    The chips filter by status: New, Waiting on owner, Approved, Declined, or All. New applications also show on To do.
  2. 2
    Each row shows the application number, the owner, the car, how many of the four checks are done, and when they applied.
  3. 3
    Open an application to decide.

Decide on an application

One page holds everything: the owner, the car, the uploads, the four checks and the notes.

  1. 1
    Check the driver's licence, the ownership, the insurance and the lender or lessor. Mark each one Checked, fine, Problem or Not needed.
  2. 2
    Press Approve. The owner becomes active, the car gets its terms from the standard grid, the first and last month's invoices are issued, and the owner gets an email to sign and pay in the owner portal.
  3. 3
    Missing something? Press Ask for something, tick what you need (for example the ownership permit or the insurance slip), add a message if you like, and press Send to the owner. The owner gets an email and a task in their portal; the application shows Waiting on owner until they answer.
  4. 4
    Not a fit? Press Decline, write the Reason the owner reads, and press Decline the application. The owner is emailed for each car.
Tip
Notes on the page are for staff only: Add note keeps a line on the record. When the owner answers a request, their answer is in the notes and the application is New again.

Owners

Owners and partners›Owners. Every owner, with their cars and where onboarding stands.

  1. 1
    Each owner shows their application, how many cars, and where onboarding stands.
  2. 2
    View as (owner portal) shows their portal. Send password link helps them sign in.
  3. 3
    Invite an owner on file: fill in Name and Email under Invite an owner, then press Send invitation.

The owner signs everything at once in their portal (Check and sign: the agreements, schedules, Forms A and B and the official forms that are ready). The confirmations to tick, then one signature: CarmaCars countersigns at the same moment, under the signing officer's standing authorization. A car with a second owner on the ownership waits for that owner too: they get their own link by email and sign the same documents.

An owner's page

Open an owner from Owners. The details are in the right-hand column.

  1. 1
    Upfront invoices: issue the first and last month's insurance fee for each car whose terms are set.
  2. 2
    Owner: address, sign-in, agreements, and what is still missing for the registry forms.
  3. 3
    Statements and payouts for this owner.
  4. 4
    Forms C and D, with a link to the review queue.
  5. 5
    Further down: View as (owner portal), Send password link and Send a sign-in link to the owner portal.

Paid and signed

Owners and partners›Paid and signed. What each owner still has to pay or sign, and how each item was done.

  1. 1
    Each owner shows how many items are open and how many are done. Owners with nothing left are hidden; press Show every owner to see them.
  2. 2
    Open Checklist and quick actions. Each line says Signed in the portal, Signed on paper, Paid by e-Transfer, Not signed, and so on.
  3. 3
    Paid by e-Transfer, cheque, cash or bank transfer? Press Record payment. Choose Paid by, the Date received and the Reference (the cheque number for a cheque). Leave Amount empty for the full balance, or type less for a partial payment. Add a photo of the cheque or a screenshot if you have one.
  4. 4
    Signed on paper? Press Record paper signature. Choose the Document, check Signed by and Date signed, add the witnesses where the form needs them, and upload the scan of every signed page.
  5. 5
    The same checklist sits at the top of each owner's page.

A recorded payment marks the invoice paid (or partly paid), stops the automatic debit for that amount, sends the owner a receipt, and shows "Paid by e-Transfer on " in their portal. A paper signature counts as signed everywhere: the checklist, onboarding and the owner portal. The scan is kept privately with the owner's documents.

Watch out
Recorded the wrong thing, or a cheque bounced? Press Void beside it and give the reason. The invoice owes that amount again and the owner is told; a voided signature makes the item open again. Only an Admin can void.

Forms C and D

Owners and partners›Owner forms. Form C is signed on screen in the owner portal, with the witness on the same screen. Form D (and Form C on paper, if the owner prefers) is printed, signed in front of witnesses, and sent as a photo of each page.

  1. 1
    Each row shows the owner, the form, the witnesses and when it was uploaded.
  2. 2
    Press Open to read the photo or scan. Check each marked spot is signed or initialled.
  3. 3
    Press Approve, or Send back with What to fix (the owner reads this).

Chapter 8

Owner money

Owner fee invoices are issued on the notice day and debited on the 15th. Statements are built, approved and issued each month, and payouts follow.

Owner invoices

Money›Owner invoices

  1. 1
    Run the monthly invoices issues the invoices for the 15th by hand, for a missed month. An invoice is never issued twice.
  2. 2
    Issue all tracker invoices bills the Tracker Install for each car waiting for its tracker.
  3. 3
    Filter by Open, Paid, Failed or In arrears. Failed invoices show the reason.
  4. 4
    Paid by e-Transfer, cheque, cash or bank transfer? Press Record payment under the invoice. Leave Amount empty for the full balance.

The automatic debit

The debit runs by itself. Your job is to watch for returned debits and owners without a mandate.

1Notice dayInvoices issued, one notice per owner, 10 days before
2Debit dayThe 15th from 6:00: one debit per invoice
3ClearedThe invoice is paid
4Returned, insufficient fundsThe $50.00 Late Fee is added once; the same amount is tried again within 30 days
5Returned for another reasonNo retry and no Late Fee: the debit pauses and you get a task
  1. 1
    The tiles show the next debit day and notice day, scheduled, processing, cleared and returned debits.
  2. 2
    How it runs explains the policy in words, read from Program settings.
  3. 3
    Scheduled and processing lists each debit. Pause holds one (a reason is needed).
  4. 4
    Owners lists each mandate. Use Pause, Resume or Send a notice now.
  1. 5
    Results: each debit, cleared or returned, with the bank's reason.
  2. 6
    Run log: each notice and debit run, with its counts.
  3. 7
    Notices sent: who was told what, for which debit day.

Insufficient funds: the $50.00 Late Fee is added to that invoice once and collected with the owner's next debit, and the original amount is tried again within 30 days, as section 4.4 of the Enrollment Agreement sets out. The retry is never more than the original amount. Returned again: the invoice is in arrears and you get a task.

Watch out
Returned for any other reason (account closed, payment stopped, no authorization): there is no retry and no Late Fee. The owner's debit pauses, the owner gets an email asking them to fix the account or pay another way, and you get a task. A debit is never more than its notice said. An owner with no mandate gets an email on the notice day and you get a task; nothing is debited for them.

The guides keep up by themselves

After every update of the site that changes the portals, the guides are checked without anyone asking.

  1. 1
    Every screenshot is taken again on the new version of the site. One whose screen changed replaces the old one in the guide.
  2. 2

Email, backups and site health

Keep an eye on email use against the daily limit, the nightly backup and the occasional error page.

Email

Settings›Email log

  1. 1
    The banner shows today's emails against the daily limit. From 80, Admins get a notice; near the limit, digests and marketing wait first and booking and sign-in emails are held last.
  2. 2
    Filter the Outbox: All, Queued, Held, Sent, Failed, Suppressed or Skipped.
  3. 3
    Open an email to Resend or Cancel it.
  4. 4
    Suppressions: Suppress an address stops all mail to it; Lift undoes it.

Backups

  • The database is backed up every night, encrypted, kept for 30 days and test-restored on the spot.
  • Every Monday the newest backup is checked again: it must be under 30 hours old and restore cleanly.
  • Nothing is emailed. A failed backup shows as a failed scheduled job under Alerts on the Overview. Tell your developer the same day.
  • Keep a copy of the backup key in the company password manager. Without it no backup can be read.

When a page shows "Error 1102"

Our web host's free plan sometimes stops a page that takes too long. Reload the page; you are still signed in. Moving to the Workers Paid plan removes it.
