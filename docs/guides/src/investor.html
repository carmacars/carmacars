Chapter 1

Your investments

Investments shows each of your investments: what you put in, what it earns, and when it pays.

Read an investment

  1. 1
    The tabs: Investments, Statements, Documents to sign, Investor deck (while CarmaCars shares it) and Profile and notices. Guide and Sign out are at the top.
  2. 2
    Your agreement number and name, your Invested Amount, the Returns paid to date (or reinvested) and the next payment, with a chart of your account month by month.
  3. 3
    Each investment: its Invested Amount, how far the lockup period has run, the Yearly Percentage, how Returns are handled (Paid monthly or Reinvested) and the Next Payment Date. Principal, what was reinvested and redeemed to date, the Investment Date and the Lockup End Date are under "Principal, dates and amounts to date".
  4. 4
    Payment schedule: the last Returns on your statements and the next ones. An amount that is not on a statement yet is marked Estimated.
  5. 5
    Latest statement, with Open.
Tip
This area shows your records under your Investment Agreement. It is not an offer or a solicitation.

Sign your investment agreement

When CarmaCars sends your agreement, it is under Documents to sign.

  1. 1
    Press Documents to sign in the menu. The top shows what is signed ("0 of 1 signed").
  2. 2
    Check the key values: your name and address, the Invested Amount, the Yearly Percentage and the dates. The agreement is filled in from your record.
  3. 3
    Press Read the PDF and read it, with its schedules.
  4. 4
    Say whether you got independent legal advice, and tick the risk acknowledgment (Schedule C). Your initials are filled in.
  5. 5
    Press Sign. Your full legal name is your signature (or press Draw instead). The signed copy is on the same page.

Once you sign, the agreement shows "Awaiting countersignature by CarmaCars" under Signed. CarmaCars's signing officer countersigns it; the agreement binds CarmaCars once it is countersigned and your funds have cleared. You get the countersigned copy on the same page.

Tip
Signed on paper instead? Once we record it, it shows under Signed with the scan.

Chapter 2

How your returns work

Your return is worked out month by month on the Invested Amount, at the Yearly Percentage in your agreement. Here is the cycle and the words your statements use.

The monthly cycle

1Return MonthYour Invested Amount earns for each day of the month
2StatementThe return is worked out after the month ends
3The 15thPaid to your bank account, or reinvested
4EmailYour statement, with its PDF attached
  • No return is earned for the lockup period. Your first Return Month starts on your Lockup End Date.
  • Paid monthly: the return is paid by bank transfer on the 15th of the month after the Return Month (the Next Payment Date).
  • Reinvested: the return is added to your Invested Amount, so it earns from then on.
  • Each statement shows the Invested Amount at the start and the end of the month, so you can follow it over time.

The words on your statements

TermWhat it means
Invested AmountWhat is earning a return: your principal, plus returns reinvested, less amounts redeemed.
PrincipalWhat you invested.
Yearly PercentageThe yearly rate in your agreement.
Investment DateThe day your funds cleared.
Lockup End DateSix months after the Investment Date. No return is earned before it.
Return MonthThe calendar month a return is earned in.
Days earningHow many days of the month the amount earned, for example 31 of 31.
Next Payment DateWhen the next return is paid: the 15th of the month after the Return Month.

Notices from CarmaCars

Two notices can appear on Investments, under Needs your attention, above your investments:

NoticeWhat it means
Return on hold:A return is held, with the reason, for example tax information we still need. Send what it asks for and we release it.
Redemption notice:CarmaCars will repay an amount, with the Redemption Date.

Emails you get from us

  • Your statement is ready, with the PDF attached, each month.
  • Your return was paid, with the amount and date (not sent when it is reinvested).
  • We received your notice: an election change, a bank account change or a contact change.
Tip
Our investor emails are servicing notices about your existing investment. They are not an offer or a solicitation. CarmaCars gives no tax advice.

Texts or email

On Profile and notices, under Messages, choose Email, Text or Both. Texts need a phone you verified with a code and link to the page. Statements are always emailed. Reply STOP to any text to stop them.

Chapter 3

The investor deck

The investor deck tells the CarmaCars story in slides: the problem we solve, how the business makes money, every feature of the platform with its real screen, the investment offer, its risks, and how your Return builds.

Open the deck

  1. 1
    On Investments, press Open the deck, or press Investor deck in the menu.
  2. 2
    Above the slides, Watch the deck plays the whole deck, narrated, in a few minutes, with captions in the player (it is there once CarmaCars has published the video).
  3. 3
    Move with Previous and Next, the arrow keys, or a swipe left or right on a phone. The list at the top jumps to any slide.
  4. 4
    Press Download PDF to save or print it: each slide prints on its own landscape page.

The calculator

  1. 1
    Enter an amount (at least the minimum investment) and choose Paid monthly, Reinvested or Reinvest, then paid.
  2. 2
    Choose how many years to show and the Investment Date.
  3. 3
    You see when the lockup ends, your first payment date, what a full month pays, what is paid to you for the period, and your Invested Amount at the end, with a chart and a month-by-month table.

The calculator follows your agreement exactly: no Return for the lockup, then one twelfth of the Yearly Percentage on the Invested Amount each month, by days for a part month, paid on the 15th of the next month or added to the Invested Amount at month end.

Good to know
The examples use the yearly percentage of our current offer. Your own percentage is the one in your agreement. Every example and figure in the deck is an illustration, not a forecast or a promise, and is not part of any offering memorandum.

Chapter 4

Statements

One statement per month, as a page and a PDF. We also email each statement with its PDF attached.

Open a statement

  1. 1
    Statements lists each Return Month with the Invested Amount, days, the return and whether it was paid or reinvested.
  2. 2
    Press a month to open it, or PDF beside it.
  3. 3
    Download a year: all statements of a year (ZIP) and the summary table (CSV). Yearly summaries come as PDF.
  1. 4
    A statement shows the investor, the agreement, the amount at the start of the month, the Yearly Percentage, the days earning, the return for the month, when it is paid, and the amount at the end. Press Download PDF to keep it.

Chapter 5

Profile and notices

Your contact details, and the notices you can send us: how returns are handled, your bank account, and a transfer request.

Contact details

Contact details shows your name, email, phone, address and bank account on file. To update these, ask us; we confirm by phone.

Change how returns are handled

  1. 1
    Under How returns are handled, choose Paid monthly or Reinvested.
  2. 2
    Press Send the notice. The change applies from the first Return Month that starts at least 10 days after we receive it.

Bank account and transfers

  1. 1
    To change the account we pay, press Ask to change the bank account. We call you at the number on file to take the new details. A change applies 10 days after notice.
  2. 2
    To transfer an investment, choose it, fill in To (full name) and a Note, then press Send the request. A transfer needs CarmaCars' written consent.
  3. 3
    You see "Received. CarmaCars will confirm in writing." We confirm each request by email.

Two-step sign-in (recommended)

  1. 1
    At the bottom of Profile and notices, press Set up two-step sign-in.
  2. 2
    Scan the code with your authenticator app.
  3. 3
    Type the 6 digits and press Continue. From then on, every sign-in asks for a code.

Documents, further down the same page, lists your statements as files.

Chapter 6

Keeping your account safe

Your portal holds your investment records. A few habits keep it yours.

Good habits

HabitWhy
Sign out on shared computersSign out is at the top of every page. It signs you out on this device only.
Email links work onceInvitation and password links work for 24 hours, once. Never forward them.
Two-step sign-inWith it on, a password alone cannot open your portal. Set it up under Profile and notices.
We never ask for your passwordCarmaCars never asks for your password or a sign-in code by email or phone.
Bank changes are confirmed by phoneWe call the number on file before we change where returns are paid.

Signed in with the wrong account?

If a page says "You are signed in as ..." and that you have no access, you are signed in with another CarmaCars account (for example a renter account). Press Sign out, then sign in with the email your invitation was sent to.
