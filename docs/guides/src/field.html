Before you begin

Start here

Who this guide is for

CarmaCars field staff: drivers, detailers, and drivers who also detail. You hand cars over, take them back, move them and shoot listing photos. Everything is on your phone.

What you need

  • Your phone, charged, with a data connection (photos and signatures save as you take them)
  • The invitation email from CarmaCars (the link works for 24 hours)
  • A car charger and a cloth for the camera lens
Sign in at
carmacars.ca/sign-in?next=/field
You land on My jobs, today's jobs in order.

Your first sign-in

  1. 1
    Open the invitation email on your phone and press its link within 24 hours. Choose a password and press Save the new password.
  2. 2
    You land on My jobs. Next time, sign in at carmacars.ca/sign-in with your Email and Password.
  3. 3
    In your browser's menu, choose Add to Home Screen. The portal then opens full screen, like an app.
  4. 4
    Open Profile, add your mobile number and press Save my phone.
  5. 5
    At the top of the page, fill in your Home address and Postal code and press Save my home address. You need it before you add a rental or earn a commission.
Good to know
Recommended: on Profile, press Set up two-step sign-in and follow the steps with an authenticator app.

Chapter 1

My jobs

Your day, in order: handovers, returns, deliveries, moves and photo shoots. Each job card has the car, the plate, the place and the time.

Your job list

  1. 1
    The menu: My jobs, Report, Add a rental, My sales, Referrals and Profile. Sign out is top right.
  2. 2
    Switch between Today and Tomorrow. Jobs are in time order.
  3. 3
    The next job is the big card at the top: its time and how long until it, the car and its plate, the place with Open in maps and a button to call the renter, and what to put in the car. The rest of the day follows in time order.
  4. 4
    Press Open the job to start.
  5. 5
    Moving a car that has no job? Use Vehicle moves (see below).
Tip
Need help on a job? Call CarmaCars at 289-312-5991. The link is at the bottom of My jobs.

Open a job

  1. 1
    The card shows the kind, the time, the car, the plate and colour.
  2. 2
    The address, with Open in maps for directions, and a button to call the renter.
  3. 3
    For a handover: the booking, the renter's name and phone, the Card on File and the licence on file.
  4. 4
    Press Acknowledge so the office knows you have it. Then press Start when you are with the car.

Move a car

  1. 1
    Under Vehicle moves, press Start a move. Choose the Car and Why (for example Reposition).
  2. 2
    Fill in From and the Odometer (km), then press Start the move now.
  3. 3
    When you arrive, press End the move, fill in Now at and the odometer, then press End the move now.

Documents to sign

Your staff agreement, when CarmaCars sends it.

  1. 1
    My jobs shows "You have a document to sign". Press Read and sign.
  2. 2
    Check the key values at the top (your role, wage and start date), then press Read the PDF and read it.
  3. 3
    Press Sign. Your full legal name is your signature (or press Draw instead).

Chapter 2

A handover

You give the renter the keys only after every check passes. The steps guide you one by one: press Next after each, Back to go back.

Before you start: the ready check

The first step lists what must be done before the car can go: signed, paid, ID checked, deposit.

  1. 1
    Each line should show a green tick: identity verified, coverage confirmed, additional drivers accepted, the booking holds the calendar, deposit taken.
  2. 2
    All green? Press Next.
  3. 3
    Anything not ready? Press Stop: do not release the car and call the office.
Watch out
Never hand over keys if the ready check fails, the renter does not match the photo on file, or they do not have their original licence or the card on file. Stop and call us.

The steps, one by one

1
Car ready: fuel full or charged 80%, clean, extras in. Yes or No, with a note for Fleet.
2
Is this the renter? Compare the person with the name and photo on file.
3
Original licence: name, photo and expiry match. A photo of a licence is not enough.
4
Card on File: you have seen the physical card with the same last four digits, in the renter's name.
5
Odometer: type the reading in km.
6
Fuel or charge: tap the level on the gauge, in eighths from E to F.
7
Keys: how many keys and fobs you hand over. Use + and −.
8
Photo set: 19 photos in order. Press Take this photo for the next one; every photo is also in the list below, with Retake if needed.
9
Damage: tap the car diagram where it is, choose Where and Type, then Add the damage.
10
Tracker: it should say in place and reporting. Check again if not.
11
Renter reviews: the renter reads the summary of odometer, fuel, keys, photos and damage.
12
Signatures: the renter signs, then you. Use this signature for each.
  1. 13
    Press Hand over the keys. You see "Handed over. The rental has started."

Add-ons to put in the car

A booking may come with add-ons: a baby seat, a child or booster seat, flowers, an umbrella, a phone charger or a prepaid full tank.

  1. 1
    Open the job. Under Put in the car you see each add-on, how many, and what to do (for example "Put the bouquet on the front passenger seat").
  2. 2
    Put every item in the car before the handover. The Car ready step lists them again.
  3. 3
    Prepaid fuel: the renter may return the car at any fuel level. Do not charge for fuel at the return.
  4. 4
    A chauffeur on the booking: Fleet tells you who drives and when.
Tip
An item missing? Write it in the note for Fleet on the Car ready step and call the office before the handover.
Tip
The photo count shows "19 of 19 photos taken · all uploaded" when the set is complete. The first photo must show the plate. Open circles on the damage diagram were recorded before; add only what is new.
03
Chapter 3

A return

The return mirrors the handover. You record the car as it comes back and compare it with the pickup.

Take the car back

  1. 1
    Car and keys received: confirm you have the car and its keys.
  2. 2
    Odometer, then Fuel or charge, as at pickup.
  3. 3
    Keys and extras: count what came back.
  4. 4
    Cleanliness: inside and out.
  5. 5
    Photo set: the same shots, in the same order, as at pickup.
  6. 6
    Compare with pickup: for new damage, tap the diagram, fill in Where and What it is, press Add the damage and take a close-up photo.
  7. 7
    Belongings: check the seats, the trunk, the glovebox and the door pockets.
  8. 8
    Renter reviews and signs. Then you sign.
  9. 9
    Press Complete the return.
Watch out
New damage needs a close-up photo and a clear note. Finance uses your photos and notes to bill the renter, so take your time.
04
Chapter 4

Photo shoots

A photo shoot job gets a car ready for its listing. You shoot the list of photos with your phone and submit them for review.

Shoot the listing photos

  1. 1
    Open the photo shoot job and read the tips: daylight, a plain background, the phone held landscape. Press I have read this.
  2. 2
    The shot list: each photo with a short description. The bar shows how many required photos you have.
  3. 3
    Press Take photo for each shot (or From photos to pick one you took). Retake to redo one.
  4. 4
    An upload failed? Press Send again. Then press Submit for listing.
Tip
Keep other cars' plates out of the frame. The reviewer covers this car's plate before the listing goes live.

Chapter 5

Report, rentals, referrals and profile

Report anything that happens on the road, add a rental for a renter you found, share your referral link, and keep your details up to date.

Report something

  1. 1
    Open Report and choose the Car.
  2. 2
    Choose What happened: New damage found, Warning light, Collision while I was driving, Ticket or toll while I was driving, Lost property found or Something else.
  3. 3
    Write the Details: where, when and what you see.
  4. 4
    Press Take a photo for each photo, then Send the report.
Watch out
In a collision, make sure everyone is safe and call 911 if anyone is hurt. Then call CarmaCars at 289-312-5991 before you send the report.

Add a rental for a renter you found

Your home address must be in your profile first: until it is, the page asks for it.

  1. 1
    Open Add a rental. Choose the car, the dates and the places, then type the renter's details. A renter already on file is found by their email. Car already out with the renter? Enter the pickup as when it left: it is recorded as on trip.
  2. 2
    Press Check the calendar and the price. To charge another price, type it with the reason: a price far under the rate sheet is flagged for an Admin, not refused.
  3. 3
    Choose Send a payment link or Charge the saved card when the renter signs. The renter pays online: you take no payment for it yourself.
  4. 4
    Under Who found this client, choose I sourced this client or Entered for CarmaCars, then press Add the rental.

Commissions

  1. 1
    You earn 10% of the rental charge (before tax, after discounts, less refunds) of each rental you sourced: one you added and marked I sourced this client, or a booking made with your referral link.
  2. 2
    It is earned when the rental is completed and paid in full, then an Admin approves it and it is paid on your pay date. Your home address must be in your profile: until it is, a commission waits as not earned yet.
  3. 3
    My sales shows each commission: not earned yet, pending approval, approved, paid or reversed. A refund after payment comes off a later commission.

Referrals and profile

  1. 1
    Referrals: your own referral link. Press Copy the link, or let someone scan its QR code. A booking made with it counts as a rental you sourced.
  2. 2
    Profile: Save my phone, your home address (Change my home address), Set up two-step sign-in, Change my password and Sign out.

Sign in with your phone number

Quicker on a phone than a password: a 6-digit code by text.

  1. 1
    On Profile, under Sign-in, press Add and verify my phone number, enter your mobile number and press Text me a code.
  2. 2
    Type the code from the text (it usually fills itself in) and press Verify. Choose My phone number (a code by text) under Sign in with.
  3. 3
    Next time, the sign-in page opens on your phone number: press Text me a code and enter the code. Two-step sign-in still asks for your authenticator code.

Texts or email

On Profile, under Messages, choose Email, Text or Both for the messages we send you. Texts need the phone you verified above.
