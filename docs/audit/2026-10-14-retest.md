## 2. Error 1102 (the Cloudflare free plan's CPU limit), apart from real failures

Cloudflare's analytics from the verify run (38676127042): the last 24 hours 17,203 Worker invocations, 286 stopped with Error 1102 (exceeded resources; CPU p50 10.1 ms, p90 113 ms, p99 972 ms); the last hour (the deploy and the full verify run) 775 invocations, 0 Error 1102 (p50 9.3 ms, p99 839 ms). Every check of this retest that failed was a code defect (section 3); none failed on Error 1102. The 286 are the plan's limit, not a defect: the median request is at the free plan's 10 ms and a cold render of a portal page takes far more. Workers Paid (US$5 a month) removes the limit. CPU and 5xx per path still need the Cloudflare token's "Workers Observability: Read" and the zone's "Analytics: Read".

## 5. The email daily cap (Resend free plan, 100 a day)

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the outbox respect `notify_daily_cap` with priorities? | Yes. `app.outbox_claim` takes rows in priority order and lets a class send only while the day's count is under its limit: customer booking emails 97, owner and investor 95, immediate staff 90, digests and summaries 80, marketing 60 (and at most 15 marketing a day, only 10:00 to 16:00) |
| Are Supabase Auth's emails counted? | Yes, since 0043: the send times in `auth.users` of today (Toronto day) are added to the count. They cannot be held by the outbox (Supabase sends them itself), so the classes keep room for them |
| Is the Admin alerted, not by email? | Yes: at 80% one in-app notice to each Admin that day, an alert on Admin > Today, the use on Admin > Email. When something is held, the "Email limit reached for today" notice |
| Is anything dropped? | No: what cannot go is held until after midnight (booking emails first). Resend's 429 holds without spending an attempt. A final failure (an invalid address, or three real errors) tells the Admins in the portal with the reason, and Admin > Email can resend it |
| Live today | After the deploy: `app.email_usage()` on the live database: 15 of 100 today (15 from the outbox, 0 sign-in emails), warning at 80, classes 97, 95, 90, 80, 60, 0 held, 0 failed. The Auth count read the live `auth.users`: October 13 counts 2 (the invitation and the reset of `rent@carmacars.ca`) |
