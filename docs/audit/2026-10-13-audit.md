`docs/blueprint/99-traceability.md`, the links and buttons of every page for every role, every email, SEO, the
routes, and the live site. What was found was fixed in the same pass where it could be; what remains is listed at the
end, with what only the owner can provide.

Status words: **done, verified live** (checked on carmacars.ca by the verify workflow), **done, not verified live**
(built and covered by tests here, not exercised on the live site), **partial** (works, with a named gap), **missing**.

Evidence names: `supabase/tests/NN_*.sql` (the database suite, `scripts/test.sh`: 2,643 assertions, all passing),
`web/tests/e2e/*.spec.ts` (Playwright at 375 and 1440 with axe), `web/tests/lint/*.mjs` (the crawl, SEO, email, link
and copy checks), the CI run and the verify run named in section 9.

## 3. Links and buttons: the role crawl

`web/tests/lint/crawl-roles.mjs` (`npm run check:crawl`, and the CI job `audit`) renders every page reachable from the
home of each role, anonymous and signed in as a renter, an owner, an investor, an Admin, each assistant function
(customer service, Finance, Fleet, Sales) and field staff. It requests every link found (all of them) and renders up to
one or two pages of each shape (for example `/admin/bookings/:id`), and fails on: a page or link answering 404 or 5xx; a
GET form that fails; a button with no handler (React props on it or an ancestor, a DOM listener, or a form submit); a
toggle or dialog button that changes nothing or throws; a console error or a failed same-origin request; horizontal
overflow at 375 or 1440; a long dash, a misspelled brand, or (anonymous) the numbered company name. It never submits a
form that writes. `--fresh` crawls bare new accounts (what CI does on its fresh stack).

First full run (2 pages per shape: 1,843 pages, 3,693 links): 123 problems of 6 kinds, all fixed:

| Found | Where | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 500 on a document link | `/account/documents/[id]` for a document with no title | Title falls back to the kind |
| Staff saw every company file in the renter portal | `/account/documents` signed in as Admin or assistant | `public.my_documents` lists the caller's own (0034) |
| 404 links | `/va/calendar` linked cars to `/va/vehicles/[id]` for Finance and Sales | Links only for Fleet and customer service |
| Broken images, console errors | `/admin/listings/[id]`, `/va/listings/[id]`: unedited photos asked of the public bucket | Signed links to the private originals |
| Overflow 115 px at 1440 | `/owner/bookings` "Book my own car" | Card column fixed |
| Email links to 404 pages | `/admin/incidents/IN-1001`, `/owner/statements/ST-1029` (found by the email audit) | Both pages accept the number |

After the fixes: 0 problems, anonymous and every role (run of October 14, 1 page per shape: 948 pages, 2,240 links,
1,929 forms; and `--fresh` for six roles). No dead buttons were found; dialog buttons now say `aria-haspopup`.
