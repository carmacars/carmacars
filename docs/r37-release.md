## 8. Customer service and Fleet hold a lead while they handle its call (2320)
- The owner's rule: when customer service or Fleet handle a lead on a phone call, the lead is theirs for a limited time so Sales do not contact it meanwhile.
- Taken when a fully onboarded customer service or Fleet person answers or places a call with the lead (a Quo call by their Quo user, the site's Call button), adds a note (the lead, the caller pop-up) or uses Create lead on a call. The lead is found by the call's link, the number, or the renter's email or mobile. Admins and people not fully onboarded never hold.
- Lasts `lead_cs_hold_minutes` (Settings > Sales; empty: the Sales claim window of the lead's type, 15). Every new handled call extends it. Recorded in `lead_claim_log` as hold, hold_extend and hold_end with the role.
- Sales see "Being handled by <first name> (Customer service) until <time>" on the lead and in every list (Admin, staff portal, owner and game leads); they cannot claim, log a call, text or email, set an outcome, mark handled, press Call or text it from the inbox (a clear refusal); Next lead leaves it out. Other customer service and Fleet staff can still work it (their call or note takes the hold over).
- When it ends (ran out, released, closed, the holder stops being onboarded) the lead returns to whoever held a Sales claim on it, else the salesperson who brought or last had it, else the pool. An Admin (or the holder) can Release hold early.
- The hold is not a claim: a booking made from the lead keeps the existing credit rules and customer service is never credited.
- Tests: `99_r37_cs_lead_hold.sql`, `sales-leads/model.test.ts`.

## Customer service, Fleet and Finance: no sales credit or commission, but their help is acknowledged (2330)
The owner, November 11: "Customer service and fleet and finance gets no sales credit or commission ever. But they get 'credit' like acknowledgement."
- **The rule is in the database** (`app.credit_blocked`): a person whose functions are only customer service, Fleet and/or Finance is never the credited or commissioned person, by any path: a booking's sales credit and "sourced" flag (a quote, a rental added by hand, the first offer, a referral link, an Admin's Reassign, an accepted claim), the commission and its rate, a lead's sourced-by, an owner's recruited-by and application credit, an owner commission request, a Sales lead claim and the rebooking claim. Automatic paths leave the credit empty (the next Sales person to send an offer is credited); an Admin picking such a person is told no in words, and the pickers no longer list them. A person with Sales and customer service keeps credit for their work as Sales; Admins and field staff are unchanged.
- **Credit already on file is reported, not rewritten** (no payout changes): the migration prints a NOTICE and writes an `audit_log` row (`credit_rule_2330_report`) with the counts of bookings, sourced flags, claims, commissions (and their value, and how many are unpaid), leads, owners, applications and owner requests held by customer service, Fleet or Finance. Production counts were not available to this release; read them from that row after deploy and decide what to do with any unpaid ones.
- **Helped by**: on every booking (Admin and every staff portal) and every lead, read from the logs that already exist: offers sent, licences checked, renter questions asked and answered, extensions, tasks done, field jobs done, calls held, contacts logged. Not the person credited with the sale. A **Helped** line on each person's KPIs (their scorecard and the team KPI board): each booking or lead counted once, no money.
- **Guides**: the Staff guide's Commissions chapter and the Customer service, Finance and Fleet chapters say it in one line; the Admin guide's commission table drops the 10% for customer service, Fleet and Finance (field staff keep it). The "Who found this client?" choice carries the same note. The Thanks line in the staff digest was not added.
- Tests: `99_r37_no_credit_for_support.sql` (every path, Sales still credited, Helped by and the KPI). Suites 36, 37 and `99_r35g_staff_onboarding` were updated to the rule (the flat 10% is now tested on field staff; a lead is handed to Sales, not customer service). Note: `supabase/seed/2321_gen_guides.mjs --check` now reports out of date because the Staff guide changed again; `2330_gen_guides.mjs` is the one that carries these chapters.

## 11. SEO: no 503 for search engines, the place pages keep to our market, the AI crawl cut (2350)
- **Why pages answered 503**: under crawler load (about 50,000 Worker invocations an hour: AI crawlers 27k, AI search 15k, search engines 6.5k) renders of uncached place pages took over 4 s; five slow or failed renders in a minute switch busy mode on (r27b, 10 minutes, every Worker), and in busy mode a long-tail page with no kept copy got the 503 busy page, Googlebot included. Most of those pages were places we never serve: every US and Mexican region had its place pages on (0095 "all on by default"), hundreds of thousands of small-town pages.
- **Search engines never get the busy page**: a crawler Cloudflare verifies as a search engine (Googlebot, Bingbot) gets the kept copy or a render in busy mode (`scraper-guard.ts tailServing`, `verifiedSearchEngine`). Other agents keep the busy rule.
- **Our market**: the existing region switches (Admin > Settings > Search, `place_regions.is_enabled`) are set off for the United States (56) and Mexico (32); Canada stays on, Ontario first. Their place pages answer 404 (the database returns no hub) and leave every sitemap, the place directory and the place roots; the country pages for the US and Mexico go too. The count of places taken out is in `audit_log` (`place_market_canada`). A new region starts off; an Admin can switch any back on (the edge rule below then steps aside).
- **404 at the edge**: while every region outside Canada is off (the five-minute tick's `place_foreign`, tag store `carmacars/place-foreign`), a term-and-place address of a US or Mexican place (`/car-rental/<term>/<place>`, `/detailing/...`, `/body-shop/...`, `/buy/<sale term>/<place>`) is answered 404 before the app loads, kept a day by the caches. The one-segment `/car-rental/<slug>` stays with the database (a landing slug could end like a state code).
- **Place sitemap index**: lists only the files that hold pages (from the place directory's stored pages), not one file per 25,000 ids up to the last place: no empty sitemap files.
- **AI crawl cut**: the AI crawlers that crawl in bulk keep to the crawl scope (fixed pages, cars, landing pages, guides, vehicle tree to the trim): AI search crawlers (Applebot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, DuckAssistBot, Amzn-SearchBot) with no delay, the training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Meta, Amazonbot and others) with Crawl-delay 10; robots.txt says it and the edge answers 403 on the deeper pages (`bot-shield.ts AI_SCOPED`). Googlebot, Bingbot, DuckDuckBot, YandexBot and the fetchers a person triggers (ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User) are unchanged. The AI search crawlers keep every core page (docs/changes/2026-10-23-ai-search.md).
- **Languages keep their index through a release**: r37 added 8 template strings (37 to 45), which put every language at 82%, under the 90% line, so every language page would have gone noindex until the seo-i18n workflow ran. A string added or changed in the last `seo_i18n_grace_days` days (Program setting, 7) no longer counts against coverage; a page whose own strings are missing stays noindex (`localizedIndexable`). The translation workflow needs the Cloudflare API, so it was not run here.
- Search Console: the gsc workflow has no read-only mode (it submits sitemaps and IndexNow), so it was not dispatched; its per-page tables feed Marketing > SEO after deploy.
- Tests: `99_r37_seo_market.sql` (regions, 404 hubs, sitemaps, the index files, the tick flag and an Admin turning a region back on, the language grace), suite 61 updated to the new defaults; unit tests in `scraper-guard.test.ts`, `bot-shield.test.ts`, `families.test.ts`.
