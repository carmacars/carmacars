## Part 2 – Terms and Conditions

### 1. Definitions and structure

**1.1 Definitions.** Terms named in the Summary have the meaning shown there. In addition:

- **Charges**: every amount you owe under this Agreement.
- **Damage**: loss of or damage to the Vehicle, including theft, vandalism, glass, tire and interior damage, but not ordinary wear.
- **Owner**: the person who enrolled the Vehicle in our fleet.
- **Rental Period**: pickup until Check-in (section 4.3).
- **Tracker**: the GPS tracking and remote disabling device in the Vehicle.
- **Vehicle**: the vehicle in the Summary or its replacement, with its keys, accessories and Tracker.

**1.2 Rental only.** This Agreement is made when you accept it electronically and we confirm the booking. It is a rental only. The Vehicle stays ours or its Owner's. If the parts conflict, Part 5 prevails, then the Summary.

**1.3 Fees.** Fees vary by booking. The amounts in your Summary and in the Schedule given to you at booking apply. Each fixed fee is a reasonable estimate of our cost. Amounts are in Canadian dollars, before HST.

### 2. Drivers

**2.1 Age and licence.** Each driver must be 18 or older at pickup and must hold and carry, for the whole Rental Period, a valid Ontario class G2 or G licence or an equivalent we accept. The standard minimum age is 21. Drivers aged 18 to 20 are accepted only on vehicles we offer them.

**2.2 Identity verification.** Before pickup, each driver must pass our identity verification, by an identity check service or staff review as we choose. If a driver cannot be verified, we may refuse to release the Vehicle and will refund what you paid. Giving false, altered or another person's documents counts as cancelling at that time under section 7.

**2.3 Additional Drivers.** Only you and Additional Drivers may drive. Each Additional Driver is charged the additional driver fee in the Schedule. Each Additional Driver must meet this section and accept this Agreement, and is jointly and severally liable with you while they have the Vehicle. You are liable for the acts of anyone you let drive.

**2.4 Right to refuse.** We may refuse a booking, driver or vehicle if a person does not meet this section, gives false or incomplete information, appears unfit to drive, or does not meet our insurer's requirements.

### 3. Rental period

**3.1 Thirty-day limit.** Each rental is a single transaction of 30 days or less and never renews automatically. To keep a vehicle longer, you must make a new booking and sign a new agreement before the current rental ends. We may refuse a renewal or change the rate.

**3.2 Extensions and late return.** A day is 24 hours from the pickup time, and a part day counts as a day. An extension is valid only if we confirm it in writing and you pay for it before the return time. It reprices the whole booking at its new length under section 5.1, and you pay the difference. Returning early earns no refund. Keeping the Vehicle past the return time without a confirmed extension is a breach, and section 12 applies. If you return more than 30 minutes late, you owe the rental charge for each extra day or part day, at the booking's daily rate, plus the late return fee.

### 4. Pickup and return

**4.1 Handover.** We hand the Vehicle only to you, in person, with your original licence and the Card on File, once we have taken the Deposit.

**4.2 Condition Report.** The Condition Report is completed at pickup and at return. If no representative is present, you must take and upload the required photos. It is proof of the Vehicle's condition, odometer reading and fuel or charge level. Damage first recorded at return happened during the Rental Period, unless you show otherwise. We may add damage that could not reasonably be seen at return if we report it to you within 48 hours.

**4.3 Return.** You must return the Vehicle to the return place by the return time, in the same condition except for ordinary wear. Dents, paint scratches, glass chips, wheel damage, tears, burns, stains and odours are not ordinary wear. "Check-in" happens when you hand the Vehicle and keys to our representative or, for an unattended return we approved, when we inspect it, and no later than noon on our next business day. Leaving the Vehicle anywhere else is not a return.

### 5. Charges, Deposit and payment

**5.1 Rental charge and fees.** The rental charge uses the Vehicle's rates quoted at booking and the days billed. For 1 to 6 days, each day is charged at the daily rate, or at the weekend rate if it starts on a Friday or Saturday, up to the weekly rate. For 7 to 29 days, each day is charged at one-seventh of the weekly rate, up to the monthly rate. For 30 days, the monthly rate applies. The "booking's daily rate" is the rental charge divided by the days billed. The mileage allowance is the daily allowance in the Summary multiplied by the days billed. You must also pay each Schedule fee that applies. Distance is measured by the odometer or, if it fails or is tampered with, by the Tracker.

**5.2 Deposit.** The Deposit secures all Charges and does not limit what you owe. We take it at pickup, before handover, as a card hold or a refundable charge, as the Schedule says. A card hold lapses after about 7 days, so we may renew it or charge the Deposit instead if it would lapse before Check-in. At Check-in we capture or keep what you owe, and what we reasonably expect you to owe for a claim, toll or ticket still being assessed, and release the rest within 7 business days. We refund any amount held back and not owed within 30 days after Check-in. On a renewal, the Deposit carries over to the new agreement.

**5.3 Card authorization.** You must pay the total in the Summary at booking. The Card on File must be a valid credit card in your name. You authorize us to charge it, without asking you again, for every Charge, whenever it arises, including tolls, tickets, fees and Damage that become known only after return, until all Charges are paid. We will email you an itemized statement with the supporting record for anything charged after return or taken from the Deposit.

**5.4 Overdue amounts.** If a charge is declined, you owe the declined payment fee once for that charge, however often it is retried, and must pay the charge another way when we ask. An amount still unpaid 7 days after we send its statement bears interest at the Schedule rate from that day, and you owe our reasonable collection and legal costs.

### 6. Tolls, tickets and fines

You must pay all tolls (including Highway 407 ETR), parking charges, tickets, camera offences, fines, towing and impound charges incurred with the Vehicle during the Rental Period, unless our own failure caused them. We may pay any of these and charge you the amount plus the toll and ticket administration fee, or name you to the authority as the person who had the Vehicle. Section 12.2 applies. We need not contest anything.

### 7. Cancellation

**7.1 Refunds.** You may cancel online or by email. Notice runs from when we receive it to the pickup time in the Summary (the "start"). For a standard booking, the first row that fits applies:

| When we receive your cancellation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Within 24 hours of booking, and 48 hours or more before the start | 95% |
| 48 hours or more before the start | 75% |
| 24 hours or more, but less than 48 hours, before the start | 50% |
| Less than 24 hours before the start, or after it (including a no-show) | None |

**7.2 Non-refundable bookings.** A non-refundable booking earns a 10% discount on the rental charge. It is not refunded if you cancel, do not show up or return early. It is refunded only where section 2.2 (a driver cannot be verified through no fault of yours), 7.4, 7.5 or 11.3 gives a refund.

**7.3 How refunds work.** The percentage applies to the total you paid at booking. Refunds under this Agreement are paid within 10 business days. Section 5.2 governs the Deposit.

**7.4 If we cancel.** If we cannot supply the booked vehicle or a comparable one that you accept, we refund everything you paid, including for a non-refundable booking.

**7.5 Pricing errors.** If a price is obviously wrong, we may correct it before pickup. You may then cancel for a full refund.

### 8. Coverage

**8.1 Liability coverage included.** Third-party liability coverage, at the minimum limits Ontario law requires, is included in the rate. It does not cover Damage, your belongings or anything else you owe.

**8.2 Coverage Option required.** Before pickup and for the whole Rental Period, you must have one of these in place:

1. OPCF 27 rental vehicle coverage on an active auto insurance policy.
2. Rental car coverage on your credit card, with the rental paid on that card.
3. The CarmaCars Protection Plan, bought through CarmaCars. A third-party provider supplies it on its own terms, which we give you before you buy. It starts when we confirm activation. CarmaCars is not an insurer.

**8.3 Proof.** For option 1 or 2, you must give us satisfactory proof before pickup and check that it covers the Vehicle, the rental and every driver. We release the Vehicle only when a Coverage Option is confirmed. Having none at pickup counts as cancelling at that time under section 7. If your coverage ends, you must tell us and stop driving.

**8.4 Claims.** After any Damage you must promptly claim under your Coverage Option, cooperate with its provider and direct payment to us. You authorize us to deal with the provider directly. A Coverage Option is a source of payment and does not limit what you owe.

### 9. Damage

**9.1 Your responsibility.** You are responsible for all Damage during the Rental Period, whoever is at fault, except mechanical failure you did not cause and Damage caused by our negligence or breach.

**9.2 What you pay.** Damage that costs less than the Deductible is billed to you at cost, with the administration fee for a matter other than a claim. If Damage costs the Deductible or more, we claim on our fleet policy and you are billed the Deductible, with the claim administration fee. The rule applies to each incident. The cost of Damage is the estimate or invoice of a repair facility we choose, plus towing, storage and loss of use.

**9.3 When the limit does not apply.** To the extent the law allows, you owe the full cost of Damage where the insurer denies the claim because of your breach: an unapproved driver, impaired driving, a use prohibited by section 10.2, or false information.

**9.4 Indemnity.** You must repay us and the Owner for any claim, fine or cost, including reasonable legal fees, arising from use of the Vehicle during the Rental Period, to the extent insurance does not pay it and we did not cause it.

### 10. Use of the Vehicle

**10.1 Area.** You must keep the Vehicle in Ontario unless we consent in writing.

**10.2 Prohibited uses.** You must not use the Vehicle, or let anyone use it:

- when driven by anyone other than you or an Additional Driver, or by anyone impaired by alcohol, cannabis, drugs or medication;
- for an illegal purpose, racing, stunt driving, track use or driver training;
- to carry passengers or goods for payment, including ride-hailing and delivery work, or to re-rent it or list it on a car-sharing platform;
- to tow anything, off maintained roads, overloaded, or carrying dangerous goods; or
- to smoke or vape in it, or to carry a pet without our approval, except a service animal.

**10.3 Care.** You must keep the Vehicle locked and the keys with you. You must not alter it, copy a key, tamper with the odometer or the Tracker, or give anyone a right over it. If a key or fob is lost or not returned, you owe the lost key or fob fee.

### 11. Accidents, theft and breakdown

**11.1 Accidents and theft.** After an accident, theft or vandalism you must:

- call 911 if anyone is hurt, and report to the police or a Collision Reporting Centre where the law requires, and always for theft or vandalism;
- call our 24-hour line as soon as it is safe, and within 24 hours;
- collect the details of everyone involved, take photos, and not admit fault or settle with anyone;
- give us a written report, your photos and all keys within 48 hours; and
- cooperate with us, the Owner, the insurers and the police, and send us any legal document about the Vehicle.

**11.2 Roadside assistance.** Basic 24-hour roadside assistance for mechanical breakdown is included in the rate. If the Vehicle breaks down, call our 24-hour line.

**11.3 Breakdown that is not your fault.** If the Vehicle breaks down through no fault of yours, we will provide a replacement vehicle or refund the unused rental time.

**11.4 Call-outs you cause.** If you cause the call-out, you owe its cost plus the administration fee or, for a tow, the section 6 fee. This includes a lockout, running out of fuel or charge, a flat battery from lights left on, tire damage, getting stuck and an accident.

**11.5 No replacement or refund.** If the rental ends because of an accident, theft or your breach, we provide no replacement vehicle and no refund for unused time.

### 12. Tracking, disabling and repossession

**12.1 Consent.** As a condition of renting, you consent to GPS tracking of the Vehicle for the whole Rental Period and to remote disabling under this section, and you must tell every other driver. The Tracker records location, speed, distance, ignition and tampering, not sound or video. Where the Vehicle belongs to a third-party Owner, the Owner can also see its location, but the Owner's lock, alarm and shut-off controls are blocked during the Rental Period.

**12.2 Evidence.** Tracker data is proof of where the Vehicle was, how far and how it was driven, and who had it, unless you show it is wrong.

**12.3 Grounds.** We may end the rental by notice, disable the Vehicle and repossess it if:

1. the Vehicle is not returned within 2 hours after the return time;
2. a Charge stays unpaid 24 hours after we ask;
3. this Agreement is seriously breached, including by a breach of section 10 or the end of your Coverage Option;
4. we reasonably suspect theft, abandonment, fraud, or unsafe, impaired or unlicensed driving;
5. the Tracker reports tampering, or stops reporting and we cannot reach you; or
6. the police, a court or our insurer requires it.

**12.4 Disabling.** A moving vehicle is never disabled. We disable the Vehicle only when the Tracker shows it stationary with the ignition off, and disabling only prevents a restart. We will first try to reach you and give you at least 1 hour to fix the problem, unless grounds 4 to 6 apply or a warning would defeat recovery. If anyone is at risk where the Vehicle is disabled, call our 24-hour line and we will re-enable it to reach a safe place.

**12.5 Repossession.** When we end the rental, you must return the Vehicle immediately or tell us where it is. We or our agents may take it back peacefully wherever it is, including from your driveway or open parking area, without a court order where the law allows, but never by force or from a dwelling or locked garage. If the Vehicle is not returned within 24 hours after the return time and we cannot reach you, we may report it stolen. After a repossession caused by your breach, you owe our recovery costs, with no refund for unused time.

**14.2 Exclusions and cap.** We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, for belongings left in the Vehicle, for loss caused by tracking, disabling or repossession under section 12, or for events beyond our reasonable control.
