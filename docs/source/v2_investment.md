# CarmaCars Investment Agreement

**DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. NOT FOR SIGNATURE.**

This Agreement is made on the Investment Date between **18122211 Canada Inc.**, operating as CarmaCars (the "**Company**"), and the investor named in Schedule A (the "**Investor**").

## 1. Definitions

**1.1** In this Agreement:

- "**Invested Amount**": the amount the Investor paid to the Company as recorded in Schedule A, plus Returns reinvested under section 4, less amounts redeemed or bought under section 7.
- "**Investment**": all of the Investor's rights under this Agreement.
- "**Investment Date**": the date in Schedule A on which the Company received the Invested Amount in cleared funds.
- "**Lockup Period**": the six months from the Investment Date to the day before the "**Lockup End Date**" in Schedule A.
- "**Other Investor**": any other holder of an investment in the Company on substantially this form.
- "**Payment Date**": the 15th day of the month after a Return Month.
- "**Redemption Price**": the Invested Amount being redeemed plus unpaid Return earned on it before the date the Company pays it (the "**Redemption Date**").
- "**Return Month**": each calendar month, or part of one, from the Lockup End Date to the Redemption Date.
- "**Securities Laws**": the securities legislation of Ontario and of any other Canadian jurisdiction that applies to the Investor.
- "**Senior Obligations**": all amounts the Company owes at any time to secured lenders and lessors, governments, employees and contractors, vehicle owners under its owner program, renters, insurers and ordinary-course trade creditors.
- "**Transfer**": to sell, assign, pledge, charge or otherwise dispose of any part of the Investment, directly or indirectly.
- "**Yearly Percentage**": the yearly return percentage in Schedule A.

**1.2** Amounts are in Canadian dollars. Schedule A prevails on the Investor's amounts, percentage and dates.

## 2. The Investment

**2.1 Investment.** The Investor invests the Invested Amount in the Company on the Investment Date. The minimum initial investment is $20,000. An additional investment requires an updated Schedule A signed by both parties and has its own Investment Date and Lockup Period.

**2.2 Conditions.** The Company accepts no funds until the Investor has delivered signed Schedules C and D, every form Securities Laws require, and the identification the Company requests. The Company is bound only when it has received cleared funds and signed this Agreement. It may refuse any investment and will return unaccepted money without return.

**2.3 Investment contract only.** This Agreement is an investment contract. It is not a loan, deposit, promissory note or credit facility. The Investor receives no shares or right to shares, no ownership interest in the Company or its assets, no vote or part in management, and no share of profit, growth or sale proceeds beyond the Return and the Redemption Price. The Investor is not the Company's partner, co-owner, agent or employee. No one is the Investor's trustee or fiduciary.

**2.4 Use of funds.** The Company may use the Invested Amount for any purpose of its business as a whole. It is not tied to any vehicle or asset, is not held in trust, and may be mixed with other money.

**2.5 Other Investors.** The Company may raise any amount from Other Investors at any time, on different amounts, percentages and dates, without the Investor's consent. The Investor is not entitled to their terms.

**2.6 Register.** The Company's register of investors, Invested Amounts, reinvestments and Transfers is conclusive, absent obvious error.

## 3. Lockup and Return

**3.1 Lockup.** No return is earned for the Lockup Period, whether during it or afterwards.

**3.2 Return.** For each Return Month the Investment earns a return (the "**Return**") of one-twelfth of the Yearly Percentage multiplied by the Invested Amount at the start of that month, pro-rated by days for a part month. The Return is fixed: it does not vary with the Company's results and changes only by an updated Schedule A signed by both parties.

**3.3 Payment.** Unless a Return is reinvested under section 4, the Company will pay it on its Payment Date by electronic transfer to the Investor's bank account in Schedule A, or another account in the Investor's name notified at least 10 days before. That payment discharges the Company.

**3.4 Tax.** The Investor is responsible for all tax on the Investment, including on reinvested Returns. The Company gives no tax advice, may withhold what the law requires without gross-up, and may hold back payment until it has the tax information it needs.

**3.5 Yearly rate.** For the *Interest Act* (Canada), and without admitting that the Return is interest, the yearly rate equivalent to the monthly Return is the Yearly Percentage.

**3.6 Statements.** The Company will give the Investor a statement for each Return Month showing the Return, whether it was paid or reinvested, and the Invested Amount. The Investor has no other right to information or to inspect the Company's records, premises or vehicles.

## 4. Reinvestment

**4.1 Election.** The Investor may choose in Schedule A to have each Return added to the Invested Amount in place of being paid, and may change that choice by written notice. A change applies from the first Return Month that starts at least 10 days after the Company receives it.

**4.2 Effect.** A reinvested Return is added to the Invested Amount on the last day of its Return Month, has no new Lockup Period, and cannot be withdrawn.

**4.3 Suspension.** The Company may stop reinvestment going forward on 30 days' written notice, and will stop it if continuing would breach Securities Laws.

## 5. No Fixed Term; No Right to Repayment

**5.1 Term.** The Invested Amount stays invested with the Company. It is not repaid on any schedule, and no Return payment repays it. This Agreement has no fixed or maximum term.

**5.2 No demand.** The Investor has no right, at any time or for any reason, to demand repayment, redemption or withdrawal of any part of the Invested Amount.

**5.3 Exit.** The Investor may exit only by a Transfer under section 6 or a redemption or purchase under section 7.

## 6. Transfer

**6.1 Consent required.** A Transfer is void unless the Company consents in writing and the transferee delivers, in the form the Company requires, an agreement to be bound by this Agreement and signed Schedules A, C and D.

**6.2 Consent.** The Company will not unreasonably withhold consent to a sale or assignment. It may always refuse a pledge or charge; a transferee who does not qualify under Securities Laws, cannot be identified or lawfully dealt with, or competes with or is in a dispute with the Company; and any Transfer while the Investor is in breach of this Agreement.

**6.3 Terms.** The transferee may be an Other Investor or a new investor, and holds the Transferred part with the same Yearly Percentage, Investment Date and Lockup End Date from the effective date in the Company's consent. The price is a matter between the Investor and the transferee alone.

**6.4 Death or incapacity.** If an individual Investor dies or becomes incapable, the Investment passes to the estate or legal representative on evidence the Company reasonably requires, without consent.

**6.5 Resale restrictions.** The Investment is subject to resale restrictions under Securities Laws that may never expire, because the Company does not intend to become a reporting issuer. Complying with them on a Transfer is the Investor's responsibility.

## 7. Redemption and Purchase by the Company

**7.1 Redemption.** The Company may redeem all or part of the Investment at any time, including during the Lockup Period, on at least six months' written notice stating the amount and the Redemption Date, or on shorter notice agreed in writing. It may redeem the Investment without redeeming any Other Investor's investment, and the reverse.

**7.2 Payment.** On the Redemption Date the Company will pay the Redemption Price under section 3.3. Neither party pays any premium, penalty or break fee.

**7.3 Purchase on request.** After the Lockup Period the Investor may ask the Company in writing to buy any part of the Investment, at a price and date agreed in writing. The Company may refuse for any reason.

**7.4 Effect.** On payment, the redeemed or purchased part of the Investment is cancelled. The Investor will sign a receipt and release on request.

## 8. Ranking and Recourse

**8.1 Unsecured.** The Investment is unsecured. The Investor has no charge, lien or other claim over any vehicle or other asset of the Company, and will register nothing against the Company or its assets.

**8.2 Ranking.** The Investor's claims rank behind the Senior Obligations, equally and rateably with Other Investors, and ahead of distributions to the Company's shareholders and repayment of shareholder loans. A court or trustee may rank them differently.

**8.3 Further debt.** The Company may borrow, lease or finance vehicles and grant security over any of its assets without the Investor's consent. The Investor will sign any postponement a holder of Senior Obligations reasonably requires.

**8.4 No personal liability.** The Investor's only recourse is against the Company. No shareholder, director, officer, employee or affiliate of the Company, and no individual who controls any of them, guarantees or is personally liable for any obligation under this Agreement, except for that person's own fraud.

**8.5 Missed payment.** If the Company does not pay a Return when due, the Investor's only remedy is to sue the Company for that amount. A missed payment does not make the Invested Amount due, does not override section 5.2, and gives no right against the Company's assets or any person named in section 8.4.

**8.6 Winding up.** If the Company stops carrying on business, it will redeem the Investment, after the Senior Obligations and rateably with Other Investors, before any distribution to its shareholders.

## 9. Representations

**9.1 Investor's representations.** The Investor represents, on the Investment Date and on each additional investment, and will tell the Company promptly of any change, that the Investor:

1. has the capacity and authority to sign this Agreement and, if an individual, is at least 18;
2. resides, and is resident for tax purposes, where Schedule A says;
3. is investing the Investor's own money, which does not come from crime, as principal, for investment and not for resale or for anyone else;
4. fits the category initialled in Schedule D; and
5. has given the Company only true and complete information.

**9.2 Investor's acknowledgments.** The Investor acknowledges that:

1. the Investment is, or is likely to be treated as, a security, sold without a prospectus in reliance on section 9.1 and Schedule D;
2. no one has promised a resale, repurchase or refund of the Investment other than under this Agreement, or anything about its future value; and
3. the Investor has relied only on this Agreement, any offering memorandum the Company delivered, and the Investor's own advisers, and not on any advertisement, projection or oral statement.

**9.3 Indemnity and personal information.** The Investor will indemnify the Company for losses caused by an untrue representation of the Investor. The Investor consents to the Company using and disclosing the Investor's personal information to administer the Investment and meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations, including filings with securities regulators.

**9.4 Company.** The Company represents, on the Investment Date, that it validly exists, has authorized this Agreement, which binds it and breaches none of its articles, by-laws or shareholders' or material agreements, and is neither a reporting issuer nor insolvent. While the Investment is outstanding, the Company will carry on business lawfully with the licences and insurance a prudent operator would keep, keep proper books, make the filings Securities Laws require, and promptly tell the Investor of its insolvency or an agreed sale of substantially all of its business.

**9.5 No other representations.** The Company makes no other representation, including about its future results or ability to pay. Schedule B and any projection illustrate calculations only and promise nothing.

## 10. General

**10.1 Confidentiality.** Each party will keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and the other party's non-public information, except as required by law or a regulator, or to advisers, lenders, proposed transferees or buyers bound to keep it confidential.

**10.2 Maximum lawful rate.** The parties do not intend this Agreement to advance credit. If a court nonetheless finds any amount payable under it to be interest at a criminal rate under section 347 of the *Criminal Code* (Canada), that amount is reduced retroactively to the highest lawful amount, first by reducing the Yearly Percentage. Any excess received is applied against the Invested Amount or refunded, at the Company's choice.

**10.3 Set-off.** The Company may set off any amount the Investor owes it under this Agreement against any amount it owes the Investor.

**10.4 Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement about the Investment. It replaces all earlier discussions, term sheets, messages, advertisements and marketing material. Nothing in it limits a right under Securities Laws that cannot be waived.

**10.5 Changes and assignment.** An amendment or waiver must be in writing and signed by the party bound. A change to an Other Investor's terms does not amend this Agreement. The Company may assign this Agreement to a successor or a buyer of its business. This Agreement binds each party's heirs, successors and permitted assigns.

**10.6 Notices.** A notice must be in writing and sent by hand, courier or email to the address in Schedule A or one later notified.

**10.7 Law and courts.** Ontario law, and the federal laws of Canada that apply there, govern this Agreement. The courts of Ontario sitting in Toronto have exclusive jurisdiction.

**10.8 Other terms.** If any part of this Agreement is unenforceable, the rest continues in effect. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, special or punitive damages, except for fraud. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
