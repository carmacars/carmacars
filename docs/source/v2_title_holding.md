## 1. Definitions

1.1 Terms defined in the parties' CarmaCars Vehicle Enrollment Agreement (the **Enrollment Agreement**) mean the same here. This Agreement does not apply to a Turo-Only Vehicle. In addition:

- **Beneficial Ownership**: the whole economic and equitable interest in a Vehicle, including its value, its sale and total-loss proceeds and the right to have it back. Section 10.4 of the Enrollment Agreement governs claim money for repairable damage.
- **Registered Interest**: every registration of a Vehicle in CarmaCars' name at the Registry, as owner, plate registrant, co-registrant or lessee.
- **Registry**: the Ontario Ministry of Transportation and ServiceOntario.
- **Re-Transfer**: moving the Registered Interest back to the Owner, or to a person the Owner names in writing.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This Agreement governs registration, ownership, the Lease, the Owner's Lien, Finance Party matters, the power of attorney and Re-Transfer, and prevails on those subjects. The Enrollment Agreement governs everything else.

2.2 It applies to each Vehicle listed in Schedule 1 or in a signed Additional Vehicle Schedule (together the **Title Schedule**), from signing until its Re-Transfer. CarmaCars need not accept a Registered Interest until the Owner has paid the amounts due under section 4.2 of the Enrollment Agreement.

2.3 If the Insurer declines a Vehicle after its registration has changed, CarmaCars Re-Transfers it promptly and pays the Registry fees itself, unless the refusal results from something the Owner failed to disclose.

## 3. Onboarding Methods

3.1 The Title Schedule states one **Onboarding Method** for each Vehicle, from those open to it under Schedule B of the Enrollment Agreement.

| Method | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Lease** | A corporation owns the Vehicle, stays its owner and leases it to CarmaCars. An individual first sets up a corporation the individual owns and controls (an **Owner Corporation**) and moves the Vehicle into it, at the individual's own tax and legal risk. Both sign as Owner. |
| **Co-Registrant** | CarmaCars is added as a registrant beside the Owner, whose name stays on the ownership. |
| **Transfer** | The registration moves into CarmaCars' name. For a Leased Vehicle, only the Owner's lessee registration moves. |

3.2 Each party completes Registry and tax forms truthfully and describes the arrangement only as this Agreement states it.

## 4. Bare trust

4.1 Under every Onboarding Method, CarmaCars holds each Registered Interest as nominee and bare trustee for the Owner. Beneficial Ownership stays with the Owner at all times. For a Leased Vehicle, that is the Owner's interest as lessee.

4.2 CarmaCars has not bought any Vehicle, has paid nothing for it and has no equity in it. A value on a Registry form is for Registry or tax purposes only. A Vehicle is not available to CarmaCars' creditors.

4.3 CarmaCars' only duties as trustee are to hold the Registered Interest, deal with it only as the two agreements allow, and Re-Transfer it. It will not sell, pledge or borrow against a Vehicle, or rent it except through Rentals.

4.4 CarmaCars records each Vehicle in its books as held for the Owner and keeps every Vehicle out of any security it gives its own lenders.

4.5 If a lien, seizure, judgment or other claim attaches to a Vehicle through CarmaCars' own debts or conduct, CarmaCars has it removed at its own cost within 15 business days of learning of it.

4.6 The Owner may require immediate Re-Transfer and return of a Vehicle if CarmaCars becomes bankrupt or insolvent, makes a proposal to creditors, has a receiver appointed, is dissolved or stops carrying on its rental business.

4.7 If an individual Owner dies or loses capacity, CarmaCars acts on the written instructions of the legal representative, on reasonable proof of authority.

4.8 Form A, signed once, covers every Vehicle under the Co-Registrant or Transfer Method.

## 5. Lease (Lease Method)

5.1 The corporation that owns a Vehicle leases it to CarmaCars for the Program (the **Lease**), on the terms of Form B, which covers every Vehicle under the Lease Method. The lessor stays owner. CarmaCars has possession and use only, and no option to buy except the buyout in the Enrollment Agreement.

5.2 CarmaCars may use a leased Vehicle only for Rentals, including Owner Bookings, and related storage, cleaning and repair. The lessor consents to each Rental as a short-term sub-rental.

5.3 The lessor will keep its corporate filings and registrant identification number current, at the Owner's cost. The individual will keep control of an Owner Corporation. The Enrollment Agreement governs upkeep, insurance, risk, fees and revenue.

## 6. Owner's Lien

6.1 On a Vehicle that is Owned Outright under the Transfer Method, and on no other, the Owner may elect a lien in the Title Schedule. CarmaCars then grants the Owner a security interest in all of CarmaCars' right, title and interest in that Vehicle and its sale and total-loss proceeds, but not in claim money for repairable damage (the **Owner's Lien**). It secures CarmaCars' obligations to Re-Transfer and return the Vehicle and to pay over money it holds for the Owner.

6.2 The Owner may register it under the Personal Property Security Act (Ontario) against 18122211 Canada Inc. and the Vehicle's VIN, at the Owner's cost. The registration period is 5 years and the Owner may renew it. It does not make CarmaCars the owner.

6.3 The Owner may enforce it only if CarmaCars fails to Re-Transfer or return the Vehicle on time, and never by taking a Vehicle from a Renter without a court order.

6.4 The Owner discharges it within 10 business days after Re-Transfer, a buyout, a total-loss payout or the Vehicle becoming Financed. If not, CarmaCars may do so under Form D.

## 7. Financed and Leased Vehicles

7.1 The Owner must name every Finance Party in the Vehicle Schedule. Before any registration change the Owner must sign Form C, which contains the warning, and get the Finance Party's written permission. The permission should name the Vehicle, CarmaCars and the Onboarding Method and acknowledge rental to the public (**Finance Party Permission**).

7.2 The Owner alone bears every consequence if Finance Party Permission is missing, incomplete or withdrawn. CarmaCars may refuse to proceed without seeing it. Proceeding on Form C alone shifts no risk to CarmaCars.

7.3 CarmaCars does not contact a Finance Party for the Owner and does not take over or guarantee any loan or lease. A Re-Transfer and any total-loss settlement are subject to the Finance Party's registered interest.

7.4 The Owner must tell CarmaCars at once of any default notice, demand or threat to repossess, and before granting new security. If a Finance Party lawfully demands a Vehicle, CarmaCars may hand it over without liability, and the Owner reimburses CarmaCars' reasonable costs, including moving or compensating a Renter.

## 8. Registration

8.1 To the registration appointment the Owner brings a valid driver's licence, the original ownership permit, proof of current insurance, any Finance Party Permission and, for a corporation, proof of signing authority.

8.2 A safety certificate is valid for the Safety Certificate Period. If it expires first, the Owner pays for a new one. The Owner pays the Registry's charges, the inspection, any appraisal and any tax directly to the party charging them. CarmaCars collects none of these.

8.3 Plates registered to a business do not renew automatically. Annual renewal is at the Owner's cost. Where only CarmaCars can renew a plate, it does so and the Owner reimburses the Registry's charge.

## 9. Total loss and sale

9.1 On a total loss or unrecovered theft of a Vehicle, the Program ends for that Vehicle and its Fees stop on the date of the Insurer's decision. CarmaCars holds the settlement for the Owner and pays it in this order: to any Finance Party, what it is entitled to; to CarmaCars, any amount the Owner owes under the Enrollment Agreement; and the balance to the Owner within 10 business days of cleared funds.

9.2 If the Owner sells a Vehicle to someone else, CarmaCars Re-Transfers on the Owner's written direction to the buyer. The whole price is the Owner's.

9.3 On closing of a buyout under the Enrollment Agreement, the trust ends and CarmaCars owns the Vehicle outright.

## 10. Power of attorney

10.1 The Owner appoints CarmaCars as attorney for each Vehicle on the terms of Form D, and signs any other form the Registry, the Insurer or a Finance Party requires.

10.2 By Part 2 of Form D, CarmaCars appoints the Owner as its attorney for one purpose only: to sign and file in CarmaCars' name the documents needed to Re-Transfer any of the Owner's own Vehicles. The Owner may use it only where section 11.5 allows. CarmaCars cannot revoke it while any Vehicle is registered to CarmaCars.

## 11. Re-Transfer

11.1 CarmaCars must Re-Transfer a Vehicle when it leaves the Program, or when section 2.3 or 4.6 applies. The Owner's other Vehicles are not affected. It completes its part within 10 business days after the Exit Date, or immediately where section 4.6 applies.

| Method | CarmaCars |
| --- | --- |
| Lease | Returns the Vehicle and has its lessee record removed. The Lease ends. |
| Co-Registrant | Has itself removed as a registrant. |
| Transfer | Signs the registration back to the Owner or the person the Owner names. |

11.2 The Owner signs the Registry's forms, arranges the Owner's own plates and insurance, and collects the Vehicle promptly. From the Exit Date the Vehicle is at the Owner's risk and off the Fleet Policy.

11.3 Section 12.4 of the Enrollment Agreement says who pays the fees. A Form E for that Vehicle, signed by both parties, proves that its Re-Transfer is complete.

11.4 CarmaCars will not hold a Vehicle as security, or delay or refuse a Re-Transfer or the return of a Vehicle, because money is owed or in dispute. It recovers money owed to it under the Enrollment Agreement. The only conditions of Re-Transfer are that the Owner has insurance in place and that any Rental in progress has ended.

11.5 If CarmaCars has not completed its part within 5 business days after a written reminder sent once the time in section 11.1 has passed, or if section 4.6 applies, the Owner may complete the Re-Transfer using Part 2 of Form D, collect the Vehicle lawfully and peacefully, and recover the reasonable cost from CarmaCars. The Owner will not take a Vehicle from a Renter during a Rental without a court order.

## 12. General

12.1 Articles 14 to 16 of the Enrollment Agreement apply to this Agreement.

12.2 Money is not an adequate remedy for a failure to Re-Transfer or return a Vehicle. Either party may ask the Ontario Superior Court of Justice to order it.

12.3 Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. This Agreement may be assigned only with the Enrollment Agreement, and any successor to CarmaCars holds each Registered Interest on the same trust.

I appoint 18122211 Canada Inc., operating as CarmaCars, as my attorney for each Vehicle, only to:

- **Registry:** record the Onboarding Method, renew or replace the permit and plates, obtain vehicle records, and transfer the registration back to me or to a person I name in writing;
- **Insurance:** add the Vehicle to and remove it from CarmaCars' fleet policy, manage claims and repairs, sign releases and endorse insurer payments;
- **Tolls and tickets:** move a toll, ticket or offence notice to the person responsible; and
- **Lien:** register a lien in my favour, and discharge it only once the registration of that Vehicle is back in my name or in the name of a person I named in writing.

My attorney may not sell a Vehicle, borrow against it, transfer it to anyone else, or act for me in any other matter.

For each Vehicle, this power lasts until the registration is back in my name and every claim, toll and ticket is closed. Before then I will not revoke it without also giving notice to take the Vehicle back. This is not a continuing power of attorney for property and revokes no other.
