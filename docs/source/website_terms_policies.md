The first row that fits applies. The "start" is the pickup time on your booking.

- **How to cancel.** Cancel in your renter portal or email [●]. Notice counts from when we receive it.

- **Early return.** Returning the vehicle early earns no refund.
- **Identity check.** If we cannot verify a driver, we refund what you paid. False documents, or no confirmed Coverage Option at pickup, count as cancelling at that time.

# Legal review notes

Twelve points for counsel. Sources were opened on October 6, 2026. Ontario's e-Laws site could not be opened, so no Ontario statute or regulation text was read directly.

- **Which Act.** The Consumer Protection Act, 2002 still applies. The 2023 Act was reported not in force in [fall 2025](https://www.torys.com/our-latest-thinking/torys-quarterly/q4-2025/consumer-protection-in-canada) and [January 2026](https://www.inq.law/post/understanding-ontario-s-consumer-protection-act-2023-what-businesses-need-to-know). Today's status is unverified.
- **Internet agreements.** Part 1, section 3 tracks the 2002 rules: disclosure before payment, a chance to correct or decline, and a copy within 15 days. Only a [2006 secondary source](https://www.weirfoulds.com/online-protection) was read. Confirm the booking page shows every prescribed item.
- **Refund scale.** Confirm the 50% and nil tiers are defensible, and when the statutory 7-day and 30-day cancellation rights override Part 3.
- **Refund timing.** The Rental Agreement sets none. Part 3 uses 10 business days. [Ontario's guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) cites 15 days for statutory refunds.
- **Price errors.** Part 1 allows correction only before confirmation. The Rental Agreement has no clause for an error found afterwards.
- **Owner access to location.** Owners see live location; their controls are now blocked during rentals CarmaCars manages, so Enrollment Agreement 5.2 must change. Part 2 and consent 1 here now tell the renter. Rental Agreement sections 12 and 13 and Part 6 must be changed to match. The [consent guidelines](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/collecting-personal-information/consent/gl_omc_201805/) require naming recipients.
- **Face data.** It is sensitive and needs express consent. Rental Agreement 2.2 lets CarmaCars choose the method. Part 2 offers staff review on request. The provider's own retention is unchecked.
- **Retention.** Ninety days may be too short for late tickets and chargebacks. Licence details stay in the signed agreement for 7 years.
- **Cross-border.** Per [OPC guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/airports-and-borders/gl_dab_090127/), notice is enough for processing abroad. Countries are blank. Confirm where the tracker vendor's data sits.
- **Breaches and access.** Verified in [PIPEDA](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/page-3.html) sections 6.1, 8(3), 10.1 and 10.3 and the [breach regulations](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-64/FullText.html) (24-month records).
- **Anti-spam.** Verified in [CASL](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/E-1.6/page-1.html) and the [CRTC guidance](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm): unticked opt-in, sender identification, unsubscribe within 10 business days. The regulation prescribing the consent request's contents was not read.
- **Site promises.** Confirm the $100 cap, the 15-day change notice, and that the platform delivers every safeguard listed in Part 2, section 7 at launch.
