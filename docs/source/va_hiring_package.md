# CarmaCars VA Hiring Package

2026-10-06

## 1. Staffing plan

Hire three at launch: two full-time coordinators and a 20-hour finance coordinator. Fifty bookings a month is work for two, but 63 open hours a week on a 15-minute reply target is not, and money should sit with someone who neither sells nor dispatches.

| Role | Owns procedures | Supports | Shift, Toronto time | Hours a week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finance and Billing | 5, 11, 14, 20 | 3 (deposit), 6 (payment), 7 (refund), 9 (billing), 12 (upfront fees), 16 (final account), 19 (payout match) | Mon to Fri 9:00–13:00, plus the 1st, 10th and 15th | 20 |
| Fleet Operations | 8, 10, 16, 17, 19; opens 9 | 3, 4, 18 (dispatches Field, reviews photos); 12, 13 (after signature) | Fri to Tue 8:30–18:00 | 40 |
| Customer Service and Growth | 1, 2, 6, 7 (log), 12 (to signature), 13, 15 | 17 (publish), 19 (inbox) | Wed to Sun 9:00–18:00 | 40 |
| Sales (optional) | None; works procedure 1 steps 1 to 4 under CS | Quote follow-up, business and long-term outreach | Flexible: they choose (most enquiries come Thu to Sun afternoons) | Flexible |

- Assumes CarmaCars opens seven days. Toronto 9:00–18:00 is 21:00–06:00 in Manila until November 1, then 22:00–07:00.
- Cover: CS runs Fleet on Wednesday and Thursday; Fleet runs CS on Monday and Tuesday. Both work Friday to Sunday.
- Deposit at pickup outside Finance hours: the coordinator on shift places the card hold; Finance captures and releases.
- Incident line, 18:00 to 9:00: rings Abbas or Mahad. No VA shift covers it.
- Sales: add it when CS misses the 15-minute target two weeks running.

**If only two are hired**

| Person | Duties | Shift, Toronto time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | CS + Fleet | Tue to Sat 9:00–18:00 |
| B | Finance + cover for CS and Fleet | Sun to Thu 9:00–18:00 |

B is alone on Sunday and Monday and prices findings B also reported, so Admin approves every refund, deposit release and payout for good.

## 2. Pay

Budget US$2,250 to $2,950 a month for the three launch hires.

| Role | US$ an hour | Hours a week | US$ a month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Finance and Billing | 6.00–8.00 | 20 | 520–695 |
| Fleet Operations | 5.00–6.50 | 40 | 865–1,125 |
| Customer Service and Growth | 5.00–6.50 | 40 | 865–1,125 |
| Sales (optional) | None: commission only | Flexible | Commission only |

Month = hourly rate × weekly hours × 4.33, for the three hourly roles.

**Sales pay: commission only.** No salary, no hourly rate, no set days or hours. Sales earns a share of the rental charge (before tax, after discounts, less refunds) of each rental they sourced, once it is completed and paid in full: 10% under 50 rental days in the calendar month, 15% from 50 days, 20% from 100 days, the month's rate applying to all of that month's rentals. An Admin approves each commission before it is paid; a refund or chargeback after payment comes off the next payment. None on cancelled, refunded, unpaid or charged-back rentals, own or household rentals. The staff agreement's Schedule A says "Commission only" and section 3.4 sets the rule. Every other role also earns 10% on rentals they source, on top of their rate. Sales costs nothing until rentals close, but a commission-only role draws fewer and more sales-minded applicants: expect a longer search.

Basis, all read October 6, 2026: Filipino applicants asked a median of $714 a month full-time (about $4.10 an hour), $893 for bookkeeping and $800 for customer service and admin. Staffing firms quote $5 to $10 an hour for the same roles. The ranges sit above the asking median because the shifts are Manila nights and weekends and Finance carries trust; that premium is a judgement, not a sourced figure.

The sources are thin: three staffing firms that sell VAs and one firm's 1,385 applications. None is the hiring platform's own data, and sales has one source. The rates applicants name in question 5 are the real market.

## 3. Job posts

**Finance and Billing Coordinator, 20 hours a week, US$6–8 an hour**

CarmaCars is a Toronto car rental company launching October 15. You run our billing: price post-rental charges from inspection findings, release deposits, match tolls and tickets to bookings, bill car owners on the 15th, build owner statements and payouts for the 10th, and send a fleet report on the 1st.

Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00–13:00 Toronto time (night in Manila), plus the 1st, 10th and 15th.

Must have: two years of bookkeeping or billing for a foreign client; strong spreadsheets; arithmetic exact to the cent; clear written English; your own computer with backup internet and power; a current NBI clearance. Start your subject line with LEDGER. You may not work for another car rental business serving Ontario. Independent contractor, 30-day paid trial.

To apply: answer the five questions, give your hourly rate and two client references.

Hours: Friday to Tuesday, 8:30–18:00 Toronto time (night in Manila), weekends included.

Must have: two years in dispatch, logistics or operations for a foreign client; a sharp eye for detail in photos; calm on the phone in an emergency; clear spoken English; your own computer with backup internet and power. In answer 3, name the tenth photo in any checklist you have used. You may not work for another car rental business serving Ontario. Independent contractor, 30-day paid trial.

To apply: answer the five questions and give your hourly rate.

**Customer Service and Growth Coordinator, 40 hours a week, US$5–6.50 an hour**

CarmaCars is a Toronto car rental company launching October 15. You answer calls, email and WhatsApp within 15 minutes, quote from our rate sheet, send agreements and payment links, verify licences and coverage before pickup, handle extensions and cancellations, post our cars on classified sites and social media, and reach out to car owners who may list with us.

Hours: Wednesday to Sunday, 9:00–18:00 Toronto time (night in Manila), weekends included.

Must have: two years of phone and chat support for North American customers; neutral spoken English; accurate quoting under time pressure; you follow scripts and never invent a discount; your own computer, headset, backup internet and power. **Sales Coordinator, commission only, flexible hours**

CarmaCars is a Toronto car rental company launching October 15. You turn enquiries into bookings. You follow up every quote, answer price questions from our rate sheet, chase tentative holds before they expire, and contact businesses and long-term renters who need a car for weeks at a time. Write in words, not figures, how many rentals a month you expect to close. You may not work for another car rental business serving Ontario. Independent contractor, 30-day trial.

To apply: answer the five questions and send a two-minute voice recording selling a product you know.

**Application questions**

1. What hours do you work now, in Manila time? Can you cover this shift, weekends included? What is your backup for power and internet?
2. Describe one mistake you made with a customer's money or booking. How was it found, and what did you do?
3. Which three tools did you use most in your last role, and what did you do in each?
4. Which clients will you keep alongside us? Is any a vehicle rental business?
5. Your rate in US dollars an hour (Sales: the rentals a month you expect to close), your earliest start date, and two clients we may call.

Reject any application that missed the post's hidden instruction.

**Customer service: reply to three enquiries.** Rates: daily $100, weekend $120, weekly $560, 200 km a day, delivery and pickup $75 each, deposit $500.

1. "I need a car Thursday 10:00 to Sunday 10:00, delivered and collected. I'm 23." Good reply, as T1: rental $340, delivery and pickup $150, young driver $60, HST $71.50, total $621.50; 600 km included; $500 deposit at pickup; non-refundable saves 10% of the rental charge.
2. "I'm 19 and want the BMW X5 this weekend for Montreal, with my dog." Good reply: only cars flagged 18 to 20; $35 a day; G2 or G licence; Montreal and the dog go to Admin; nothing promised.
3. By WhatsApp: "Cancel booking 1050. Pickup is in 30 hours. I booked last week and paid $678." Good reply: asks for an email, whose time counts; 50%, $339, if received now; deposit returned in full; refund within 10 business days.

Scoring: for each reply, 4 points for accuracy and 2 for following the procedure (18); 2 for tone and English. An invented discount or promise fails the test.

## 5. Trust controls for the finance role

**Before hiring**

- Government ID matches the platform profile and payout name, and is shown live on video.
- NBI clearance issued in the last six months.
- Two former clients called and asked what money the candidate handled.
- Test score of 16 or more.
- Written list of other clients; none a vehicle rental business serving Ontario.
- Agreement, Schedule A and the franchisor's NDA signed before any login.

**First month**

- Schedule A: Single Limit $0, Dual Approval Threshold $0, Approver Abbas or Mahad. Finance prepares; Admin releases every refund, deposit release and payout, in one daily batch.
- No bank login. Payment processor: own user, with no right to edit payout accounts or payee bank details.
- Only Admin changes a payee's bank details.
- Daily: processor payouts matched to the Bookings log; Admin sees anything unmatched.
- Weekly: Admin traces five entries back to their documents.
- Month two, after a clean scorecard: Single Limit CA$250, Dual Approval Threshold CA$500. Payout runs stay Admin-approved for good.

## 8. Access and removal

**New hire, after signature**

- [ ] Agreement, Schedule A and franchisor NDA filed
- [ ] Company email and password manager; two-step verification everywhere; no shared logins
- [ ] Ops chat group and Admin escalation channel
- [ ] Time and task tracker
- [ ] Bookings log, calendar, booking folders, templates, rate sheet
- [ ] E-signature: send only. Admin countersigns
- [ ] Finance: payment processor (limited user), Charges log, Owner ledger, Toll and Ticket log
- [ ] Fleet: Tracker (disable only on Admin approval), Fleet register, Incident log, Turo account
- [ ] CS and Sales: phone line, WhatsApp, shared inbox, listing and social accounts

**Leaver, the hour notice is given**

- [ ] Suspend every login; end open sessions; change any password they knew
- [ ] Remove from chat groups, phone line, WhatsApp, e-signature, Tracker, processor, Turo and social pages
- [ ] Move file and account ownership to Admin; forward their email
- [ ] Finance: review 30 days of refunds, releases, payouts and payee changes
- [ ] Get written confirmation that Confidential Information is returned or deleted
- [ ] Approve and pay final hours and the commissions earned (Sales: commissions only)
- [ ] Remind in writing: confidentiality continues, and non-solicitation runs 12 months
