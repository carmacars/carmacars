# Flat-Rate Vehicle Enrollment Agreement

Status: approved by the lawyer (Release 21). Program settings, "Flat-rate agreements awaiting lawyer OK", is off.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

- **Company**: 18122211 Canada Inc.
- **Owner**: each person named as Owner, jointly and severally.
- **Vehicle**: each vehicle listed in Schedule A or in a signed Additional Vehicle Schedule (together the **Vehicle Schedule**).
- **Monthly Amount**: the fixed amount per month the Vehicle Schedule sets for a Vehicle.
- **Payout Date**: the 10th of each month.
- **Enrollment Date**: the date the Company confirms in writing that a Vehicle is on the Fleet Policy. **Exit Date**: the date it leaves the Program.
- **Fleet Policy**: the Company's commercial fleet insurance policy. **Insurer**: its insurer.
- **Deductible**: $2,500 per claim.
- **Rental**: a rental of a Vehicle to a **Renter** on the Company's **Rental Agreement**.
- **Title Agreement**: the parties' Vehicle Title-Holding and Lease Agreement, with its Forms A to D.
- **Tracker**: the Company's GPS device with killswitch.
- **Notice Period**: 30 days.

1.2 Amounts are in Canadian dollars. HST is extra where it applies. On a conflict the order is: the Title Agreement on its subjects, the Vehicle Schedule, this Agreement.

## 2. The Program

2.1 The Company runs an Ontario car rental business. The Owner places each Vehicle on the Fleet Policy under the Title Agreement and the Company pays the Owner the Monthly Amount (the **Program**).

2.2 The Owner keeps beneficial ownership and all value of each Vehicle.

2.3 The parties are independent contractors. Apart from the Title Agreement trust, the Company is not the Owner's trustee or fiduciary.

2.4 This Agreement covers all the Owner's Vehicles. A Vehicle is added by an Additional Vehicle Schedule signed by both parties.

## 3. Eligibility and onboarding

3.1 A vehicle with a rebuilt or salvage title is not eligible. The Company or the Insurer may decline any vehicle.

3.2 The Owner pays ServiceOntario, the safety inspection and any other third-party onboarding cost directly. The Company prepares the paperwork.

3.3 The Owner keeps the Owner's own insurance on each Vehicle until the Company confirms in writing that it is on the Fleet Policy. Loss during a gap is the Owner's alone.

3.4 No registration of a financed or leased Vehicle changes until the Owner signs Form C of the Title Agreement.

## 4. The Monthly Amount

4.1 The Company pays the Owner each Vehicle's Monthly Amount for each month from its Enrollment Date to its Exit Date, on the Payout Date after the month, whether or not the Vehicle is rented.

4.2 A part month, including the month a Vehicle is removed, is paid by the day: the Monthly Amount times the days in the Program over the days in the month.

4.3 There is no revenue split. The Company keeps all rental revenue and owes the Owner no rental income, statement of rentals or revenue report. The Owner pays no Insurance Fee, Tracker Fee or Install Fee.

4.4 HST is added to the Monthly Amount when the Owner gives the Company an HST number.

4.5 **Set-off.** The Company may deduct any amount the Owner owes under this Agreement or the Title Agreement from any Monthly Amount.

4.6 The Monthly Amount changes only in writing signed by both parties. Moving a Vehicle to a revenue share needs the Company's standard Vehicle Enrollment Agreement, signed again.

## 5. Tracker

5.1 A working Tracker is installed on every Vehicle at the Company's cost. The Owner will not remove or tamper with it.

5.2 The Company may use its location and controls to run rentals, recover a Vehicle and meet the Insurer's requirements. No one uses the shut-off on a moving Vehicle.

## 6. Rentals

6.1 The Company decides the rentals, rates and Renters of each Vehicle and manages every Rental.

6.2 The Company may block or end a Rental, or take an unsafe Vehicle out of service. Kilometres, ordinary wear and loss in value are not compensated.

## 7. Owner use

7.1 The Owner may drive the Owner's own Vehicle when it is not on a Renter's booking, by booking it and signing the Rental Agreement (an **Owner Booking**). The Owner pays no rental charge, fee or deposit.

7.2 If the Owner damages the Vehicle during an Owner Booking and a claim is made, the Owner pays the Deductible. A Renter's confirmed booking has priority.

## 8. Insurance, claims and repairs

8.1 From its Enrollment Date to its Exit Date each Vehicle is on the Fleet Policy, on the policy's terms. The Company is not an insurer.

8.2 The Owner reports any accident, theft, damage or claim to the Company within 24 hours. The Company manages every claim. The Owner will not contact the Insurer, admit fault or settle without the Company's written consent.

8.3 Damage is repaired through the claim, only at shops the Company approves. Claim money is applied to the repair to pre-loss condition and the costs of the claim; any balance belongs to the Company. A total loss is paid as section 9.1 of the Title Agreement sets out.

8.4 The Owner is not paid for downtime, but the Monthly Amount continues while a Vehicle is repaired. A loss the Fleet Policy does not pay stays with the Owner, except to the extent the Company's negligence, wilful misconduct or breach caused it.

## 9. Upkeep, tolls and tickets

9.1 The Owner pays for maintenance and routine wear, including tires, brakes and servicing, and keeps each Vehicle safe and serviced to the manufacturer's schedule.

9.2 Tolls and tickets are paid by the Renter who caused them, by the Owner during an Owner Booking and outside the Program, and by the Company while its staff have the Vehicle.

## 10. Term and exit

10.1 There is no minimum term. Either party may remove a Vehicle on written notice of the Notice Period. Its Exit Date is the last day of that period or, if later, the end of a Rental then in progress.

10.2 The Company may remove a Vehicle at once if the Insurer declines or cancels cover, or on the Owner's serious breach.

10.3 On exit the registration moves back under the Title Agreement. The Company pays the Monthly Amount owing on the first Payout Date after the month of the Exit Date. Sections 4.5, 8 and 11 to 13 survive.

## 11. Owner's promises

11.1 The Owner confirms that the Owner has authority to enrol each Vehicle, owns or leases it free of any claim except a lender named in the Vehicle Schedule, and has told the Company the truth about it.

11.2 The Owner will pay every lender on time and tell the Company within 2 business days of any change.

11.3 The Owner has had the chance to get independent legal, tax and insurance advice.

## 12. Liability

12.1 The Company is liable to the Owner only for loss caused by its negligence or wilful misconduct, the Monthly Amounts it owes and its Title Agreement obligations.

12.2 The Owner indemnifies the Company against uninsured loss and third-party claims arising from the Owner's breach, a Vehicle's condition, the Owner's own driving, or a claim by a lender, co-owner or creditor.

12.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit. The Company's total liability for one Vehicle in any 12 months is capped at the Monthly Amounts paid for it in the previous 12 months, except for money it owes, the return of the Vehicle and its registration, fraud or wilful misconduct.

## 13. Privacy and general

13.1 The Owner consents to the Company collecting and using Tracker data, including while the Owner drives, and the Owner's personal information, to run the Program, handle claims, tolls and tickets and comply with the law.

13.2 Ontario law governs. This Agreement, the Vehicle Schedule and the Title Agreement are the whole agreement about the Program. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

13.3 Notices are in writing, to the address or email on the cover summary. Electronic signatures are valid.
