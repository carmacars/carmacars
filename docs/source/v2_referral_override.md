## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

- **Company**: 18122211 Canada Inc.
- **Referrer**: the person named as Referrer on the cover summary.
- **Referred Owner**: the owner named on the cover summary, who enrolled vehicles with the Company after the Referrer's introduction.
- **Referred Vehicle**: each vehicle of the Referred Owner in the Company's program while this Agreement is in force.
- **Owner Revenue**: for a rental of a Referred Vehicle, the rental charge plus kilometre overage, less refunds, chargebacks and kickbacks, before taxes, as the Referred Owner's agreement with the Company defines it.
- **Override Rate**: the points of Owner Revenue on the cover summary.
- **Override**: the Override Rate times the Owner Revenue of a rental.
- **Start Date**: the date on the cover summary. **Payout Date**: the 10th of each month.

1.2 Amounts are in Canadian dollars. HST is extra where it applies.

## 2. The Override

2.1 The Company pays the Referrer the Override on each rental of a Referred Vehicle that counts in a month from the Start Date. A rental counts in the month it ends, once collected.

2.2 The Override comes out of the Company's own share of each rental. It never reduces the Referred Owner's payout, and the Referred Owner does not pay it.

2.3 The Company pays the Override on the Payout Date after the month, with a statement showing each Referred Vehicle, its Owner Revenue and the Override. It may deduct any amount the Referrer owes the Company under any agreement.

2.4 A refund or chargeback on a rental after its Override was paid is adjusted on the next statement.

2.5 HST is added when the Referrer gives the Company an HST number. The Referrer is responsible for its own taxes. [LAWYER: confirm whether the Override must be disclosed to the Referred Owner as a referral fee.]

## 3. Seeing the Referred Vehicles

3.1 When the cover summary says so, the Referrer may see in the owner portal, read only, the performance of each Referred Vehicle: its bookings, days booked, revenue and Override. The Referrer never sees the Referred Owner's personal or banking information or any renter's information.

3.2 The Referrer keeps what it sees confidential and uses it only to follow the Override.

3.3 The Company shows the Referred Vehicles only with the Referred Owner's written consent. [LAWYER: the Referred Owner's consent under PIPEDA; we suggest a consent line in the Referred Owner's Vehicle Schedule special terms before the view is turned on.]

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement starts on the Start Date and continues while the Referred Owner has a Referred Vehicle in the program.

4.2 Either party may end it by 30 days' written notice. Overrides on rentals that counted before the end are still paid. [LAWYER: confirm whether the Company may end the Override while the Referred Owner stays, or whether it should last as long as the Referred Vehicles do.]

4.3 The Company may end it at once if the Referrer breaches section 3.2.

## 5. General

5.1 The Referrer is not the Company's partner, agent or employee and has no authority to bind the Company or to promise terms to any owner.

5.2 The Company's liability under this Agreement is limited to the Overrides it owes.

5.3 Ontario law governs. This is the whole agreement about the Override. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Notices are in writing, to the email on the cover summary. Electronic signatures are valid.
