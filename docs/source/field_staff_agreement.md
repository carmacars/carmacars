# CarmaCars Field Staff Agreement

This agreement is between 18122211 Canada Inc., operating as CarmaCars ("CarmaCars"), and the employee named in Schedule A ("you"). It starts on the Start Date in Schedule A.

## 1. Employment

1.1 You are an employee of CarmaCars in the role in Schedule A. The Employment Standards Act, 2000 ("ESA") applies, and where it gives you more than this agreement, the ESA governs.

1.2 Your hours vary with bookings and none are guaranteed. CarmaCars schedules your shifts.

1.3 You cannot sign for CarmaCars or bind it, except to complete handover documents as the Procedures require.

## 2. Duties

2.1 You perform the jobs CarmaCars assigns by WhatsApp or its app (each a "Job") for the role in Schedule A. You follow CarmaCars' written procedures, as updated from time to time ("Procedures"). A "Vehicle" is any vehicle CarmaCars asks you to handle. Vehicles belong to third-party owners.

2.2 Your Jobs include:

- **Delivery and pickup.** Move the Vehicle to or from the customer at the assigned time and place, and tell CarmaCars at once if you will be late.
- **Identity check.** Before you release a Vehicle, check each driver's original licence against the person and the booking, and photograph it as the Procedures require. Do not release the Vehicle if the licence is expired or does not match, or if the person seems impaired. Call CarmaCars instead.
- **Handover.** Have the customer sign the handover record.
- **Photo inspection.** At every handover and return, take time-stamped photos from the fixed angles in the Procedures and upload them before you leave. The minimum is each corner, each side, each wheel, the windshield, the roof, the front and rear interior, the trunk and the dashboard.
- **Fuel and odometer.** Record the fuel or charge level and the odometer reading in the app at every handover and return.
- **Detailing.** Clean the Vehicle to the standard in the Procedures and hand any lost property to CarmaCars.
- **Damage reports.** Report new damage, warning lights, missing items, smoke odour or signs of misuse, with photos, before the Vehicle is moved again.

## 3. Licence and driving record

3.1 You confirm that you hold a full Ontario Class G licence, have held it for the period in Schedule A, may legally work in Canada, and have a driving record that CarmaCars' insurer accepts.

3.2 You give CarmaCars a current driver's abstract before you start and whenever it asks. You consent to CarmaCars and its insurer checking your licence and record.

3.3 You must tell CarmaCars immediately, and before you drive a Vehicle again, about:

- any suspension, restriction or expiry of your licence, including a roadside suspension;
- any driving-related charge or conviction, and any criminal charge;
- any collision in which you were the driver; and
- anything that makes you unfit to drive safely.

3.4 You do not drive a Vehicle while your licence is not valid, while you are unfit, or after CarmaCars' insurer declines to cover you.

## 4. Vehicle rules

4.1 You drive a Vehicle only for an assigned Job, by a direct route, and only if it is a type authorized in Schedule A. You never use a Vehicle for personal trips or errands, and you never let anyone else drive it.

4.2 While a Vehicle is in your charge, you:

- obey all traffic and parking laws;
- have no alcohol, cannabis or other impairing drug in your body;
- do not hold or use a phone while driving, set navigation before you move, and answer messages only when parked;
- do not smoke or vape in or beside the Vehicle;
- carry no passengers, pets or personal cargo unless CarmaCars approves in writing;
- never leave it unlocked, running or with the key inside; and
- give the key only to the named renter or to CarmaCars.

4.3 Every Vehicle has a GPS tracker that records location, speed, route and driving events at all times. You consent to CarmaCars collecting this information, and the photos and messages you send, to manage Jobs, investigate incidents, deal with insurers, owners and authorities, and enforce this agreement. You do not tamper with a tracker.

## 5. Tickets, accidents and damage

5.1 **Tickets.** You are responsible for every traffic, parking, toll and camera fine incurred while a Vehicle is in your charge, unless it results from CarmaCars' instruction. You pay or dispute each fine before it is due. If CarmaCars pays it, you owe CarmaCars that amount.

5.2 **Accidents.** After any collision, theft or injury, you stop, make the scene safe, call 911 where anyone is hurt or the law requires, and call CarmaCars at once. You take photos and the other party's details, and you do not admit fault, settle or sign anything. You give CarmaCars a written report within 24 hours and cooperate with the insurer and the police.

5.3 **Damage.** You are responsible for loss or damage, including the insurance deductible and uninsured costs, caused by your gross negligence, wilful misconduct, impaired driving, unauthorized use or breach of section 3 or 4. You are not responsible for an ordinary accident that happens while you follow this agreement.

5.4 **Recovery.** CarmaCars does not deduct tickets, fines or Vehicle loss or damage from your wages. An amount you owe under this section 5 is a debt, which CarmaCars may recover only where the law allows.

## 6. Customers and confidentiality

6.1 With customers you are polite and professional. You do not argue, harass or discriminate, and you contact a customer only about a Job. You refer questions about price, insurance or fault to CarmaCars. You take no cash or payment from a customer, and you never offer a private rental or side service.

6.2 "Confidential Information" means everything not public that you learn through your work. It includes customer and owner names, addresses, identification and licence details, Vehicle locations, pricing, Procedures, and material belonging to CarmaCars' franchisor.

6.3 You use Confidential Information only for a Job and disclose it to no one, during and after this agreement. You delete identification photos and customer details from your phone once they are uploaded and keep no copies. You post nothing online about customers or Vehicles. You report a lost phone or any leak to CarmaCars within 24 hours. You sign the franchisor's non-disclosure agreement if asked.

6.4 For 12 months after this agreement ends, you do not solicit any customer or owner you dealt with to rent or list a vehicle elsewhere.

## 7. Referral commission

7.1 You earn a referral fee of 20% of the Rental Charge on the first completed rental of a renter you referred. Later rentals by that renter earn nothing. The fee is a commission and part of your wages.

7.2 The "Rental Charge" is the rental rate CarmaCars collects and keeps for that rental, after discounts and refunds and before tax. It excludes taxes, deposits, protection products, delivery charges and charges for fuel, kilometres, tolls, tickets and damage.

7.3 A referral counts only if:

- the renter has never booked or enquired with CarmaCars before;
- your referral code or name is entered on the booking when it is made; and
- no one else was recorded as the referrer first.

CarmaCars' booking record decides who referred a renter, unless it contains an obvious error.

7.4 The fee is earned when the rental is completed and paid in full, and is paid on the next Pay Date. It is not earned on a booking that is cancelled, refunded, unpaid or charged back, on your own rental or one by a member of your household, or on a booking made after this agreement ends.

7.5 You promote CarmaCars truthfully and only as the Procedures allow. CarmaCars may change or end the referral fee on 30 days' written notice, for bookings made after that notice period.

## 8. Pay and hours

8.1 **Wage.** CarmaCars pays the Hourly Wage in Schedule A for all hours you work, on the Pay Dates, less the deductions the law requires for income tax, Canada Pension Plan and Employment Insurance. Your pay is never less than the ESA minimum wage.

8.2 **Vacation pay.** You agree that CarmaCars pays your vacation pay, at 4% of your wages, on each Pay Date and shows it separately on your pay statement. You may take the vacation time the ESA gives you.

8.3 **Hours.** You record your start and end times and your Jobs in the app. Your hours of work, rest and eating periods, public holidays and leaves follow the ESA. Overtime needs CarmaCars' approval beforehand. It is paid at 1.5 times your Hourly Wage for each hour over 44 in a week.

8.4 **Expenses.** CarmaCars repays expenses it approved beforehand, such as fuel, parking and transit, when you give it the receipt.

## 9. Ending your employment

9.1 You may resign on 7 days' written notice.

9.2 CarmaCars may end your employment by giving you the notice or termination pay, and any other entitlement, that the ESA requires, and nothing more. CarmaCars gives no notice or pay only where the ESA permits that.

9.3 CarmaCars may stop assigning you driving Jobs at any time for safety or insurance reasons.

9.4 When your employment ends, you return every key, fob and item of equipment at once and delete all Confidential Information. CarmaCars pays all wages you earned to the end date, including referral fees and vacation pay, within the time the ESA requires. Sections 5, 6 and 10 continue.

## 10. General

10.1 The laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there govern this agreement. Disputes go to the courts of Ontario.

10.2 This is the whole agreement on its subject. Changes must be in writing and accepted by both parties, except that CarmaCars may update the Procedures by notice.

10.3 If part of this agreement cannot be enforced, the rest stands. This agreement is signed through an electronic signature service, and electronic signatures are valid.

10.4 You confirm that you understood this agreement and had the chance to get advice.
