# CarmaCars Virtual Assistant Contractor Agreement

This agreement is between 18122211 Canada Inc., operating as CarmaCars ("CarmaCars"), and the contractor named in Schedule A ("you"). It starts on the Start Date in Schedule A.

## 1. Status

1.1 You are an independent contractor. You are not an employee, partner or agent of CarmaCars and cannot sign for it or bind it.

1.2 You supply your own equipment, internet and workspace, and you pay your own taxes and insurance and follow the laws where you live.

1.3 You do the work yourself. You may not subcontract it or let anyone else use your access.

1.4 You may work for other clients, but not for another vehicle rental business that serves Ontario while this agreement runs.

## 2. Services and standards

2.1 You provide the services for your role in Schedule A (the "Services") during the Coverage Hours.

2.2 You will:

- follow CarmaCars' written procedures and instructions, as updated from time to time ("Procedures");
- work accurately, honestly and on time;
- deal with customers, vehicle owners and suppliers only through CarmaCars' accounts, phone numbers and templates;
- make no promise, discount or statement about price, insurance or coverage that the Procedures do not allow; and
- tell CarmaCars promptly about any error, complaint or missed deadline.

2.3 You record your time and tasks in the system CarmaCars names. CarmaCars may monitor and record everything done in its systems, including calls and messages.

## 3. Pay

3.1 CarmaCars pays the Rate in Schedule A. A monthly Rate covers the Coverage Hours of each month: a part month, and Coverage Hours missed without CarmaCars' approval, are paid in proportion. An hourly Rate is paid for time you worked, recorded and CarmaCars approved. Payment is made by the Payment Method, at the times in Schedule A.

3.2 The Rate is your whole payment. There are no benefits, overtime, vacation pay, commissions or expenses unless CarmaCars agrees in writing first.

3.3 CarmaCars may deduct from any payment an amount you owe it under this agreement.

## 4. Confidentiality and data

4.1 "Confidential Information" means everything not public that you learn through the Services. It includes personal information about customers and owners ("Personal Data"), pricing, Procedures, financial records, supplier and insurance terms, and material belonging to CarmaCars' franchisor.

4.2 You will use Confidential Information only to perform the Services and disclose it to no one outside CarmaCars. This duty continues after this agreement ends. If a law forces disclosure, you will tell CarmaCars first where the law allows.

4.3 You will:

- reach Personal Data only through the systems and accounts CarmaCars gives you ("Systems"), and only as a task requires;
- not copy, download, export, screenshot, photograph or forward any customer or owner list or Personal Data to a personal device, a personal account or any other person;
- never write down or store payment card numbers anywhere, and enter them only into CarmaCars' payment system;
- work only on a password-locked device with current software, never on a public or shared computer;
- use a unique strong password and two-step verification on every account, and share logins with no one; and
- report any actual or suspected loss, unauthorized access or misuse of data, an account or a device (a "Data Incident") to CarmaCars within 24 hours of discovering it, and help contain it.

4.4 You will sign the franchisor's non-disclosure agreement if asked. The franchisor may enforce this section 4 directly for its own material.

## 5. Money controls

5.1 This section applies whenever your role touches CarmaCars' money. CarmaCars' money and accounts are used only for its business and only as the Procedures allow.

5.2 You will not make or approve any payout, refund, credit, deposit release or other payment that is outside the Procedures or above the Single Limit in Schedule A. A payment above the Dual Approval Threshold in Schedule A needs written approval from the Approver before it is made.

5.3 You will not:

- change a payee's banking details without the Approver's written approval;
- pay yourself or anyone connected to you, other than your approved pay under section 3;
- move money to an account that is not CarmaCars'; or
- take money personally from a customer, owner or supplier.

5.4 Every entry must be accurate and backed by a document. You report any discrepancy or suspected fraud the same day. CarmaCars may audit your work at any time.

## 6. Work product, referrals and non-solicitation

6.1 Everything you create or collect in the Services belongs to CarmaCars from the moment it exists. This includes records, content, listings, leads, contact lists, templates and any account or profile opened for the Services. You assign all rights in it to CarmaCars and waive your moral rights.

6.2 You earn no referral fee, kickback or commission on any renter, owner or booking. You accept no gift or payment from a customer, owner or supplier.

6.3 While this agreement runs and for 12 months after it ends, you will not, for yourself or anyone else:

- solicit or divert any customer or owner you dealt with or learned of through the Services to rent or list a vehicle elsewhere; or
- solicit any CarmaCars staff member or contractor to stop working with CarmaCars.

## 7. Ending this agreement

7.1 Either party may end this agreement on 7 days' written notice. During the Trial Period, either party may end it at once.

7.2 CarmaCars may end it at once, without notice, if you breach section 4, 5 or 6, act dishonestly, repeatedly miss Coverage Hours or commit any other serious breach.

7.3 CarmaCars may suspend your access to the Systems at any time.

7.4 When this agreement ends, you stop using the Systems, hand over every password and account, return or delete all Confidential Information and confirm in writing that you have done so. CarmaCars pays for approved time up to the end date and owes nothing more.

7.5 Sections 4, 6 and 8 continue after this agreement ends.

## 8. General

8.1 The laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there govern this agreement. Disputes go to the courts of Ontario. CarmaCars may also ask any court for an urgent order to stop a breach of section 4, 5 or 6.

8.2 This is the whole agreement on its subject. Between you and CarmaCars it prevails over the hiring platform's terms where they permit. Changes must be in writing and accepted by both parties, except that CarmaCars may update the Procedures by notice.

8.3 If part of this agreement cannot be enforced, the rest stands. You may not transfer this agreement. CarmaCars may.

8.4 This agreement is signed through an electronic signature service, and electronic signatures are valid. Notices go by email to the addresses in Schedule A.

8.5 You confirm that you have read and understood this agreement and had the chance to get advice.
