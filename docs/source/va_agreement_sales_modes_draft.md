## 2. A new block after "Duties by role"

**Sales work modes** (draft of October 26, 2026, pending legal review; not part of any signed agreement until the lawyer approves it)

In the Sales role, Schedule A names one of two work modes. Both are commission only (section 3.1) and use the rule of section 3.4, except as set out here.

- **In person (Toronto).** The rates in section 3.4: under 50 rental days in the calendar month, 10%; 50 to 99 days, 15%; 100 days or more, 20%. A rental counts as sourced by you when you found the renter, closed a walk-in or a handover, or brought it in from a partner visit, and CarmaCars' booking records credit it to you.
- **Remote (you work from anywhere, as an independent contractor).** The rates are five points lower on every tier: under 50 rental days, 5%; 50 to 99 days, 10%; 100 days or more, 15%. Only rentals you source count: a rental, quote or offer you entered and marked "I sourced this client", or a website request you answered with CarmaCars' first offer during one of your submitted availability blocks. Walk-ins and partner deals do not count.
  - *Coverage.* You submit your availability each week and cover at least 20 hours a week of Thursday to Sunday afternoons, Toronto time, replying within 15 minutes to the chats, texts and calls assigned to you during those hours.
  - *Daily lead generation.* Reposting on Marketplace and classified sites, replying to social media messages, three follow-ups on every quote, and outreach to long-term and business customers.
  - *Monthly activity minimum.* At least 400 logged touches (follow-ups, reposts, message replies, outreach calls, emails or texts, and car owner outreach) in the calendar month, counted from CarmaCars' systems. If you log fewer, that month's rate is one tier lower (15% becomes 10%, 10% becomes 5%) and the lowest tier becomes 2.5%. CarmaCars may change the minimum, the number of tiers dropped and the lowest rate on 30 days' written notice, for months that start after the notice period.
- **Locked months.** Once CarmaCars approves a month's commissions, the work mode, rates and activity count of that month do not change. A later refund or chargeback still lowers the tier as section 3.4 says.

## 3. Points for the lawyer

- A remote rep is an independent contractor working from another country: confirm the contractor status, the governing law and
  the payment terms (section 1, section 8) still fit someone outside Canada.
- The activity minimum lowers the commission rate; confirm it reads as a rate rule and not a penalty, and that the 30 days'
  notice for changes (as in section 3.4) is enough.
- The credit rule for remote reps (a website request answered during their own submitted blocks counts as sourced) widens
  section 3.4's definition of a sourced rental for remote reps only.
- Section 3.4's "Rate" bullet names only the in person tiers. Either point it to Schedule A for the Sales role or restate both modes there.
