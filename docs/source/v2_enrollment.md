## 1. Definitions

1.1 These terms also apply in the Title Agreement.

- **Enrollment Date**: the date CarmaCars confirms in writing that a Vehicle is on the Fleet Policy or live on Turo. **Exit Date**: the date it leaves the Program.
- **Fees**: the monthly Insurance Fee and Tracker Fee.
- **Finance Party**: a lender or lessor with an interest in a Vehicle, which is then **Financed** or **Leased**. Otherwise it is **Owned Outright**.
- **Fleet Policy**: CarmaCars' commercial fleet insurance policy. **Insurer**: its insurer.
- **Owner**: each person named as Owner, jointly and severally.
- **Rental**: a rental of a Vehicle, and each renewal, to a **Renter** on CarmaCars' **Rental Agreement**. **Rental Charge**: its time-based charge before tax.
- **Title Agreement**: the parties' CarmaCars Vehicle Title-Holding and Lease Agreement, which defines the Onboarding Methods.
- **Tracker**: CarmaCars' GPS device with killswitch.
- **Vehicle**: each vehicle listed in Schedule A or in a signed Additional Vehicle Schedule (together the **Vehicle Schedule**).
- Schedule B, Part 1 terms have the values given there.

1.2 Amounts are in Canadian dollars. HST is extra. On a conflict the order is: the Title Agreement on its subjects, the Vehicle Schedule, this Agreement, the cover summary.

## 2. The Program

2.1 CarmaCars runs an Ontario car rental business. The Owner places each Vehicle on the Fleet Policy by moving its registration to CarmaCars or leasing it to CarmaCars under the Title Agreement, pays the Fees, and earns income when it is rented (the **Program**).

2.2 The Owner keeps beneficial ownership and all value of each Vehicle.

2.3 CarmaCars is not responsible for a Vehicle before its Enrollment Date and promises no bookings or income. The parties are independent contractors. Apart from the Title Agreement trust and its duty to account for revenue, CarmaCars is not the Owner's trustee or fiduciary.

2.4 This Agreement covers all the Owner's Vehicles. Each has its own entry in the Vehicle Schedule and its own terms. A Vehicle is added by an Additional Vehicle Schedule signed by both parties. Nothing else is re-signed.

## 3. Eligibility and onboarding

3.1 A vehicle with a rebuilt or salvage title is not eligible. CarmaCars or the Insurer may decline any vehicle.

3.2 CarmaCars collects no registry or other third-party onboarding cost. It gives the Owner information and the rough estimates in Schedule B, Part 3, helps prepare the paperwork and attends the registry visit. The Owner pays ServiceOntario, the safety inspection, the appraisal, any notary, any corporation registration and any tax directly to the party charging them. Amounts may vary.

3.3 The Owner must keep the Owner's own insurance on each Vehicle until CarmaCars confirms in writing that it is on the Fleet Policy. Loss during a gap is the Owner's alone.

3.4 No registration of a Financed or Leased Vehicle changes until the Owner signs Form C of the Title Agreement (lender permission warning).

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 The Vehicle Schedule sets each Vehicle's Fees, Revenue Share, Management Mode and Onboarding Method. The Schedule B grid is only a starting point.

4.2 Before a Vehicle's Enrollment Date the Owner pays CarmaCars the Install Fee and the first and last month's Insurance Fee for that Vehicle. There is no security deposit. The prepaid amounts are applied only to that Vehicle's first and final months, and any unused part is credited or refunded.

4.3 CarmaCars charges each Vehicle's Fees on each Billing Date, from its Enrollment Date to its Exit Date, whether or not the Vehicle is rented, in repair or off the road. The Fees for a Vehicle's first and last month are pro-rated by day.

4.4 If an automatic pre-authorized debit of an invoice is returned for insufficient funds, CarmaCars adds the Late Fee to that invoice once and may re-present the debit once, for the original amount, within 30 days. The Late Fee is collected with the Owner's next debit or deducted from the Owner's next payout.

4.5 **Set-off.** CarmaCars may deduct any amount the Owner owes under this Agreement from any payout due to the Owner for any Vehicle.

4.6 **Arrears.** If an invoice remains unpaid 30 days after a failed re-presentment, CarmaCars may, on written notice, remove the Vehicle from the program and transfer its registration back to the Owner at the Owner's cost. Return of the Vehicle is not withheld for unpaid fees.

4.7 Any balance left at the next Payout Date is a debt payable on demand. CarmaCars may deduct it from amounts payable for any of the Owner's Vehicles.

4.8 CarmaCars may change the Fees on written notice of the Notice Period. The Owner may exit instead. A Revenue Share changes only in writing signed by both parties.

## 5. Tracker and killswitch

5.1 A working Tracker is mandatory on every Vehicle on the Fleet Policy. The Owner pays the Install Fee and will not remove or tamper with it.

5.2 CarmaCars has live location, lock and unlock, alarm and engine shut-off. It may use the controls and data to run rentals, recover an overdue, stolen or misused Vehicle, enforce a Rental Agreement, bill tolls and tickets, and meet Insurer requirements. Neither party uses the shut-off on a moving Vehicle.

5.3 The Owner has live location of the Owner's own Vehicles at all times. The Owner also has the lock, alarm and shut-off controls, but CarmaCars blocks them during every Rental it manages, other than an Owner Booking. The Owner will use access only to protect the Owner's own Vehicle, never to follow, contact or monitor a Renter.

## 6. Management Modes

| | Managed | Self-Managed | Turo-Only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rentals run by | CarmaCars | The Owner, who finds Renters and sets the price. CarmaCars collects the money | CarmaCars, on Turo only, under its Turo account |
| Revenue Share | Of Owner Revenue | None: the Owner is paid 100% of Owner Revenue | Of the Turo Payout |
| Title Agreement, Fleet Policy, Fees, Tracker | Apply | Apply | Do not apply |

6.1 **Managed.** The Owner will not rent, lend or list the Vehicle elsewhere, or take payment from a Renter.

6.2 **Self-Managed.** Every rental must go through CarmaCars' booking system and checkout, on the Rental Agreement, to a Renter who meets section 7.2. CarmaCars collects all of the Renter's money; the Owner takes none. The killswitch keeps the Vehicle disabled outside booked rentals. CarmaCars may decline to enable it while any amount is overdue.

6.3 **Turo-Only.** Ownership and registration stay with the Owner. The Owner must keep valid comprehensive insurance active at all times, keep the ownership in good standing, and confirms that its insurer permits rental on Turo. Turo's terms govern trips, in place of Articles 7, 9 and 10. The Owner may drive the Vehicle on the Owner's own insurance once CarmaCars has blocked the dates on Turo.

6.4 The **Turo Payout** is Turo's payout for a trip's price and distance fees, after Turo's fees. Turo's payments for delivery and extras stay with CarmaCars. Its payments for damage to the Vehicle go to the Owner.

## 7. Rentals and Renters

7.1 CarmaCars sets the Rental Agreement, Managed Vehicle rates and all rental rules, and may refuse any booking.

7.2 Every Renter must hold a G2 or full G licence, be at least the Minimum Renter Age, and have OPCF 27 coverage on an active automobile policy, credit card rental coverage or the CarmaCars Protection Plan.

7.3 Damage under the Deductible is billed to the Renter at cost. Above it, a claim is made and the Renter is billed the Deductible. Each Rental, and each renewal, is a separate transaction within the Maximum Rental Length.

## 8. Revenue and payouts

8.1 **Owner Revenue** for a booking is the Rental Charge plus mileage overage fees, less every Referral Kickback on that booking. A Renter who returns late is charged the Rental Charge for each extra day, which is Owner Revenue.

8.2 Late return, delivery and pickup, fuel, cleaning and young driver fees, the Protection Plan sold to Renters, and toll and ticket admin fees belong to CarmaCars alone, in every Management Mode.

8.3 A **Referral Kickback** is the Kickback Rate on the Rental Charge of a new Renter's first Rental, earned by an Owner or a CarmaCars field staff member who referred that Renter before booking. Every kickback is deducted from that booking before Owner Revenue is calculated, even on another Owner's Vehicle. An Owner's is paid with that Owner's next payout. An Owner earns none on the Owner's own Vehicles. None applies to a renewal or a Self-Managed rental.

8.4 For a Managed Vehicle, CarmaCars keeps its Revenue Share of Owner Revenue and pays the Owner the rest. For a Self-Managed Vehicle it pays the Owner all of Owner Revenue. Example for a Managed Vehicle at a 25% Revenue Share:

| Rental Charge | Referral Kickback (20%) | Owner Revenue | CarmaCars keeps | Owner is paid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $1,000 | $200 | $800 | $200 | $600 |

8.5 On each Payout Date CarmaCars pays the Owner for the previous month, with a statement for each Vehicle of each Rental, Referral Kickback, Revenue Share and deduction. A Rental counts in the month it ends, once collected, net of refunds and chargebacks.

8.6 CarmaCars may deduct only unpaid Fees and Late Fees, Owner costs it has paid or been billed, the Owner's share of a refund or chargeback, and amounts the Owner agrees to in writing.

## 9. Owner use

9.1 An Owner may drive the Owner's own Vehicle whenever it is not on an active booking, by booking it like any Renter and signing the Rental Agreement (an **Owner Booking**). The Owner need not buy coverage.

9.2 The Owner pays no Rental Charge, fee or deposit. The one exception: if the Owner damages the Vehicle and a claim has to be made, the Owner is billed the Deductible. A Renter's confirmed booking has priority. An Owner Booking earns no Owner Revenue and reduces no Fee.

## 10. Insurance, claims and repairs

10.1 From its Enrollment Date to its Exit Date each Vehicle is listed on the Fleet Policy, on the policy's terms. CarmaCars is not an insurer and does not promise that any claim will be paid.

10.2 The Owner must report any accident, theft, damage or claim to CarmaCars within 24 hours. CarmaCars manages every claim. The Owner will cooperate, will not contact the Insurer, admit fault or settle without CarmaCars' written consent, and will do nothing that could void or increase the cost of the Fleet Policy.

10.3 Repairs are done only at shops approved by CarmaCars. Owners are not paid for downtime: no income, Fee credit or compensation while a Vehicle is being repaired or off the road.

10.4 **Claim money.** Money paid on a claim for damage to a Vehicle is applied to repair the Vehicle to its pre-loss condition at an approved shop and to the costs of the claim. Any balance remaining belongs to CarmaCars. The Owner receives the repaired Vehicle and no share of claim money. A total loss is different: its settlement is paid in the order set out in section 9.1 of the Title Agreement.

10.5 A loss the Fleet Policy does not pay stays with the Owner, except to the extent CarmaCars' negligence, wilful misconduct or breach caused it.

## 11. Upkeep, tolls and tickets

11.1 The Owner pays for all maintenance and routine wear, including tires, brakes and servicing. The Owner keeps each Vehicle safe, roadworthy and serviced to the manufacturer's schedule.

11.2 CarmaCars may take an unsafe or unfit Vehicle out of service. Kilometres, ordinary wear and loss in value are not compensated.

11.3 Tolls and tickets are paid by the Renter who caused them; by the Owner during an Owner Booking and outside the Program; and by CarmaCars while its staff have the Vehicle.

## 12. Term, exit and buyout

12.1 There is no minimum term. The Owner may take back or sell any Vehicle on written notice of the Notice Period. Its Exit Date is the last day of that period or, if later, the end of a Rental then in progress. The Owner's other Vehicles stay in the Program.

12.2 CarmaCars may remove a Vehicle for any reason on the same notice.

12.3 CarmaCars may remove a Vehicle immediately if the Insurer declines or cancels cover, and any or all Vehicles on Owner default: a breach not fixed within 10 business days of a request; a serious breach, such as a false statement, Tracker tampering or a rental outside section 6.2; an amount unpaid 30 days after a failed retry; action by a Finance Party; or insolvency.

12.4 After the Free Re-Transfer Period, CarmaCars covers the fees to transfer the Vehicle back. Before that, the Owner pays them. Covered fees are registry charges and any required safety inspection only. On Owner default the Owner always pays.

12.5 **Buyout.** CarmaCars may, at its sole option, buy a Vehicle the Owner offers to it in writing. The Owner cannot require a sale. The price is Canadian Black Book value plus the Buyout Premium, plus any sales tax. A Leased Vehicle can be sold only if its lessor agrees and is paid out. Canadian Black Book value means its average trade-in value for a matching vehicle on the offer date. The sale closes within 30 days, as is, with clear title, and any Finance Party is paid from the price first.

12.6 CarmaCars pays any final balance for a Vehicle on the first Payout Date after the month of its Exit Date. This Agreement ends when the Owner's last Vehicle has left. Amounts owing and Articles 8, 10 and 14 to 16 survive.

## 13. Owner's promises

13.1 The Owner confirms, on each Enrollment Date, that the Owner has authority to enrol each Vehicle; owns it, or is its lessee, free of any claim except a Finance Party named in the Vehicle Schedule; is not in default under any loan or lease; and has told CarmaCars the truth about each Vehicle, which is safe, has an accurate odometer, no known open recall, no undisclosed structural, flood or fire damage, and has never had a rebuilt, salvage or irreparable brand.

13.2 While a Vehicle is in the Program the Owner will pay every Finance Party on time, tell CarmaCars within 2 business days of any change to section 13.1, and not sell, pledge, re-register or re-plate the Vehicle except as the two agreements allow.

13.3 The Owner has had the chance to get independent legal, tax and insurance advice. CarmaCars has given none.

## 14. Liability and indemnity

14.1 CarmaCars is liable to the Owner only for loss caused by its negligence or wilful misconduct, revenue it collected and owes the Owner, and its Title Agreement obligations.

14.2 The Owner indemnifies CarmaCars against all uninsured loss and third-party claims, with defence costs, arising from the Owner's breach or untrue statement; a Vehicle's condition; the Owner's own driving and Self-Managed rentals; any claim by a Finance Party, co-owner, spouse, creditor, estate or tax authority, including from proceeding without a Finance Party's permission; tax or penalties on a registration change or re-transfer; and, for a Turo-Only Vehicle, lapsed or refused Owner insurance.

14.3 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit, rental income, use or value. CarmaCars is not liable for the acts of a Renter, the Insurer, Turo or any other third party, or for events outside its reasonable control.

14.4 CarmaCars' total liability for one Vehicle in any 12 months is capped at the greater of the Liability Floor and the Revenue Share it kept on that Vehicle in the previous 12 months.

14.5 Sections 14.3 and 14.4 do not limit CarmaCars' duty to pay over money it holds for the Owner, transfer back the registration and return the Vehicle, or liability for fraud or wilful misconduct.

## 15. Privacy

15.1 The Owner consents to CarmaCars collecting, using and storing Tracker data, including while the Owner drives, and the Owner's personal information, and disclosing them as needed to run the Program, handle claims, tolls and tickets and comply with the law. Withdrawing consent to tracking means removing the Vehicle.

15.2 Location data during a Rental is the Renter's personal information. The Owner keeps it confidential.

## 16. General

16.1 Ontario law governs. Its courts in Toronto have exclusive jurisdiction.

16.2 This Agreement, the Vehicle Schedule and the Title Agreement are the whole agreement about the Program and replace anything earlier, including under an earlier trade name. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties, except under section 4.8. A change to rental terms is not a change to this Agreement.

16.3 The Owner may not assign this Agreement without CarmaCars' written consent. CarmaCars may assign it to a buyer of its business. Each party keeps the other's Vehicle Schedule numbers confidential.

16.4 Notices must be in writing, to the address or email on the cover summary. An unenforceable term does not affect the rest. Non-waivable consumer rights are unaffected. Counterparts and electronic signatures are valid.
