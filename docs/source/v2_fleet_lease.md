## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

- **Company**: 18122211 Canada Inc.
- **Supplier**: the corporation named as Supplier on the cover summary.
- **Vehicle**: each vehicle listed in Schedule A or in a signed Additional Vehicle Schedule (together the **Vehicle Schedule**).
- **Delivery Date**: the date the Supplier delivers a Vehicle to the Company, as the Vehicle Schedule or the Company's written record of the delivery states.
- **Exit Date**: the date a Vehicle leaves this Agreement under section 5.5 or 9.
- **Tier**: Regular or Troubled, as the Vehicle Schedule sets for each Vehicle. A **Troubled** Vehicle is one that needs work before it can be rented reliably.
- **Lease Rates**: the amount a month per Vehicle for each Tier: the **Regular Rate** and the **Troubled Rate**.
- **Lease Terms**: the table on the cover summary: the Lease Rates, the Claim Profit Share, the Early Write-Off Share, the Write-Off Period, the Later Write-Off Share and the Notice Period.
- **Upkeep Table**: the table on the cover summary that names, for each type of upkeep cost, the party who pays it.
- **Period**: a calendar month. **Payout Date**: the 10th of each month.
- **Fleet Policy**: the Company's commercial fleet insurance policy. **Insurer**: its insurer.
- **Claim**: a claim under the Fleet Policy or against another person for loss of or damage to a Vehicle.
- **Claim Profit**: the money received on a Claim, less the repair, any policy deductible and the claim costs (towing, storage, appraisal).
- **Write-Off**: a Vehicle the Insurer declares a total loss, or a stolen Vehicle not recovered.
- **In-Service Months**: the whole months from a Vehicle's Delivery Date to the date of its loss.
- **Lender**: a lender or lessor with an interest in a Vehicle, named in the Vehicle Schedule.
- **Title Agreement**: the parties' Vehicle Title-Holding and Lease Agreement, with its Forms A to D.

1.2 Amounts are in Canadian dollars. HST is extra where it applies. On a conflict the order is: the Title Agreement on its subjects, the Vehicle Schedule, the Lease Terms, this Agreement.

## 2. The lease

2.1 The Supplier leases each Vehicle to the Company from its Delivery Date to its Exit Date for the Company's Ontario car rental business. The Company decides the rentals, rates and renters of each Vehicle and keeps all rental revenue.

2.2 The Supplier keeps ownership of each Vehicle. The parties are independent contractors.

2.3 This Agreement covers all the Supplier's Vehicles. A Vehicle is added by an Additional Vehicle Schedule signed by both parties and removed under section 9.

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 A vehicle with a rebuilt or salvage title is not eligible. The Company or the Insurer may decline any vehicle.

3.2 The Supplier keeps its own insurance on each Vehicle until the Company confirms in writing that the Vehicle is on the Fleet Policy.

## 4. Lease payments

4.1 The Company pays the Supplier, for each Vehicle, the Lease Rate of its Tier for each Period from its Delivery Date to its Exit Date, whether or not the Vehicle is rented, in repair or in a Claim.

4.2 Payment is in arrears. The first payment for a Vehicle is made on the first Payout Date that falls at least one full month after its Delivery Date, and covers every Period up to then. After that, each Period is paid on the Payout Date after it, with the Company's owner payouts.

4.3 A part Period is paid by the day: the Lease Rate times the days the Vehicle was leased in the Period, divided by the days in the Period. The statement shows the calculation.

4.4 Each payment comes with a statement listing each Vehicle, its Tier, the days paid, the amount and any deduction.

4.5 HST is added to a payment when the Supplier gives the Company an HST number.

4.6 **Tier.** Each Vehicle's Tier is set in the Vehicle Schedule when the Vehicle is delivered and does not change for as long as the Vehicle is leased. If a Tier was recorded in error, the Company corrects it by written notice to the Supplier; a correction never changes a payment already made.

4.7 The Lease Rates change only in writing signed by both parties.

4.8 **No fees.** The Supplier pays the Company no fee of any kind: no insurance fee, management fee, tracker fee, install fee or first and last month's payment, and no share of rental revenue. The only amounts the Supplier bears are the upkeep costs the Upkeep Table gives to the Supplier, which the Company deducts under section 4.9.

4.9 **Set-off.** The Company may deduct from any payment any amount the Supplier owes under this Agreement or the Title Agreement, including an upkeep cost under section 6.2 that the Company paid.

## 5. Claims and write-offs

5.1 The Company manages every Claim. The Supplier reports any damage it learns of within 24 hours and does not contact the Insurer, admit fault or settle without the Company's written consent.

5.2 Lease payments continue unchanged while a Vehicle is in a Claim or being repaired.

5.3 When a Claim on a Vehicle that is not a Write-Off is settled, the Company pays the Supplier the Claim Profit Share of any Claim Profit, with the next payment. A shortfall on a Claim is the Company's.

5.4 On a Write-Off, the settlement first pays any amount owing to a Lender on that Vehicle. Of the balance, the Supplier receives the Early Write-Off Share if the Vehicle had fewer In-Service Months than the Write-Off Period, and the Later Write-Off Share otherwise. The Company keeps the rest. The Supplier's part is paid with the next payment after the settlement funds clear.

5.5 A Write-Off's Exit Date is the date of the Insurer's decision. Lease payments end on it.

## 6. Upkeep

6.1 Each type of upkeep cost is paid by the party the Upkeep Table names.

6.2 Where the Supplier pays a cost, the Company may arrange the work at a shop it approves and deduct the cost under section 4.9, with the receipt.

6.3 Work caused by an event that is a Claim is paid through the Claim. A cost of a type not in the Upkeep Table is paid by the party the parties agree in writing before the work.

6.4 While the Company has a Vehicle it keeps it serviced to the manufacturer's schedule, at the cost of the party the Upkeep Table names.

## 7. Tracker

7.1 The Company may install a GPS tracker on a Vehicle at its own cost, with no tracker fee. The Supplier will not remove or tamper with it. The Company uses it to run rentals, recover a Vehicle and meet the Insurer's requirements.

## 8. Tolls and tickets

8.1 Tolls and tickets are paid by the renter who caused them, by the Company while its staff have the Vehicle, and by the Supplier before the Delivery Date and after the Exit Date.

## 9. Term and exit

9.1 This Agreement has no end date. It continues until it is ended under this section.

9.2 Either party may remove a Vehicle, or end this Agreement, by written notice of the Notice Period. A Vehicle's Exit Date is the last day of the notice or, if later, the end of a rental then in progress.

9.3 The Company may remove a Vehicle at once if the Insurer declines or cancels cover, or if the Supplier seriously breaches this Agreement.

9.4 On exit the Company returns the Vehicle and its registration under the Title Agreement, in the condition it was delivered in, except ordinary wear and the work the Supplier pays for under the Upkeep Table. The Company makes the last payment on the first Payout Date after the Period of the Exit Date. Sections 4.9, 5 and 10 survive.

## 10. Promises and liability

10.1 The Supplier confirms that it has authority to lease each Vehicle, owns it free of any claim except a Lender named in the Vehicle Schedule, has each Lender's permission where it is needed, and has told the Company the truth about each Vehicle.

10.2 The Supplier pays each Lender on time and tells the Company within 2 business days of any change.

10.3 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit. The Company's total liability for one Vehicle in any 12 months is capped at the lease payments made for it in the previous 12 months, except for money it owes, the return of the Vehicle and its registration, fraud or wilful misconduct.

10.4 The Supplier indemnifies the Company against claims by a Lender, co-owner or creditor, and against loss from a Vehicle's condition that the Supplier knew of and did not disclose.

## 11. General

11.1 Ontario law governs. This Agreement, the Vehicle Schedule and the Title Agreement are the whole agreement about the Vehicles. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11.2 Notices are in writing, to the address or email on the cover summary. Electronic signatures are valid. Each party has had the chance to get independent legal, tax and insurance advice.
