## Renters

- Add-ons such as child seats and extra drivers, bookable with a rental
- Calendar sync, reminders to finish an unfinished booking, and automatic site monitoring

## Owners

- Owner terms and invoices, and staff can add a car on an owner's behalf

## Staff tools

- Guides that update themselves as the platform changes
- Rates and included kilometres set in one step when listing a car
- Bookings from other rental marketplaces read from email, and quieter site alerts

## Website & search

- Discount games that reward website visitors with a promo code
- Live visitors, buying signals and live chat on the website
- Sample cars and photos shown while real listings are being prepared
- Car descriptions written automatically, landing pages, guides and faster search engine notification
