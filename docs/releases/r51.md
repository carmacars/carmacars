## Staff tools

- Welcome is one checklist for every new staff member: a big button for each step left, done steps ticked, and the portal opens when the list is done
- **Sign my agreement now** makes the staff agreement at once and opens it to sign; if something is missing, the page says what and an Admin is told
- Every academy question says to answer it yourself; the quiz notices browser automation, copy and paste and switching away, and a try that shows them does not count
- After a quiz under the pass mark, fix the wrong answers at once; the wait after a failed fix is 1 minute, and Try again starts the next try in one press
- Each staff member can have their own extension on the CarmaCars phone menu that rings their portal and the app
- Managers see the hours, training, pay owed and field jobs of the staff assigned to them, read only
- A car is available again as soon as it comes back clean; only a return that needs cleaning waits for the detail job
- An email an insurer's server refuses is caught: the invoice shows it bounced, it goes again to the insurer's general claims address, and an Admin gets the request to send them
- Managers get follow-ups for their team (academy, onboarding, missed shifts or jobs, KPIs) with call, text and a note, a weekly summary, and a Managers section in the academy
