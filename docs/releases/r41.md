## Staff tools

- Bookings, a booking's page, requests and offers, leads, messages, the calendar, Today, field jobs and shifts show changes made elsewhere within about two seconds, without a reload
- The alerts bell and its pop-up react as soon as an alert arrives
- What someone is typing is kept: an update waits until they leave the field
- Marketing > Email campaigns: the audience, what is waiting, queued and sent, who unsubscribed, and each campaign; Sales sees it read only
- Lists show the newest first, in the order they came in: bookings, hiring applications, leads (Sort still offers Priority and Oldest), investors and every other list; queues ordered by urgency or time stay as they are
- Each campaign has its own switch and timing; marketing email volume rises automatically to the free capacity left after booking emails, never on a paid plan
- Staff change how damage to the car is covered with one button on the booking: the three options with their prices, a required reason, and the new price applied at once without moving money by itself
- Every listing photo has its licence plate checked automatically: a plate found is covered, a photo with no plate is marked "No plate visible", and a photo the check is not sure of is left for staff, each with one-click undo
- "Cover the plate" opens the editor right under its photo and saves the CARMA plate again
- Photos already on file are checked the same way, within the day's free AI allowance
- Townhall reminders to every staff member and Admin: the day before, the morning of, an hour before and 15 minutes before with the join link
- A townhall card on every portal home from the day before: the time, a countdown, Join / check in, Add to calendar, and a warning after a missed townhall
- Interview call reminders for Admins (the morning of, an hour before the first call, 10 minutes before each) and Interviews today on Admin Today
- AI interviews decide by themselves at a higher bar: a pass is hired into the academy (field: invited to a live call), a fail gets the decline or Sales email once; only a booked live call is a To-do
- A harder AI interview: reliability, a typed customer message, an objection for Sales, and more probing follow-ups

## Renters

- A trip's status, payment, agreement and checklist update on the page by themselves
- A weekly email with the cars ready to rent and the deals running, and an email each time a new car joins the fleet
- One click unsubscribes from every marketing email, at once; booking, payment and safety emails always go
- The privacy policy (updated November 12, 2026) says who gets marketing emails, why (CASL implied consent), who sends them and how to unsubscribe
- Renters change their own protection on their booking page: the Protection Plan at once, their own policy or card coverage before pickup, and leaving the Protection Plan after our team checks their proof
- Credit card coverage is chosen only with a card from our list that covers the car and a promise to pay with it; otherwise the renter's own auto policy with a promise to upload the policy; otherwise the Protection Plan
- No more "send me deals" box on the booking request, the car pages or the discount game

## Owners

- The owner's dashboard, bookings and money update by themselves

## Reliability & security

- Live updates read a tiny version number straight from the database while a tab is open and visible, and nothing while it is hidden; a faster push channel switches on when the server's live service is enabled
- Emails can go through Amazon SES first, a high-volume sender in Canada, with the existing senders taking over on their own when it cannot send
- Booking and system emails keep a free backup sender for when the main one cannot send, and marketing emails use a free sender only, so no email goes through a paid plan beyond the main sender
