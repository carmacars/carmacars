## Renters

- Renters can request to book a car online, with dates, price and extras worked out on the spot
- The booking application: renters apply once and their details carry into every rental
- Emails reach the right reply address and carry clear contact details
- The renter portal: bookings, documents, payments and messages in one place

## Owners

- Owner onboarding: owner records, agreements, forms and car listings
- Owner questions answered in the owner area, with clear agreement terms
- The owner portal: owner agreements, statements and invoice payments online

## Staff tools

- Booking, payment and pickup notifications for renters, owners and staff
- Day-to-day operations tools: handovers, returns, cleaning and car availability
- Tolls, tickets and damage charges tracked per booking, and monthly statements
- The Admin portal, with access checked for every action
- Listings written and photographed automatically, documents generated as PDFs, and secure file links

## Partners

- Staff and investor portals, each person seeing only what their role allows

## Website & search

- The public website: car pages, search and booking pages

## Reliability & security

- Reliability improvements to how the booking, payment and operations parts work together
- Reliability improvements across bookings and payments
- Reliability improvements to the staff and investor portals
