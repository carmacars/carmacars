## Staff tools

- A car can go out with the deposit still owed, with the reason: every booking page shows **Deposit pending**, the saved card is tried each morning and the renter is sent the deposit link; after 48 hours an Admin gets a To do
- Each message type that can go by text shows its texts of the last 30 days and its channel; once email is ready for production, only urgent messages keep their texts and the rest go by email
- **Approve all (n)** counts only what it can approve, and the get-ready line says which page the renter still has to send
- Open pay links that asked for more than is owed were cancelled at release; Claude's connector can cancel one pay link (an Admin, previewed first, no money moves)
- The offer screen is one clear form with one main button, and an offer that is out is changed in place: what staff save stays saved
- The fleet calendar is easier to read on a computer and a phone: full car names, the renter and booking number on each bar, clear colours by status and today marked
- Every car on the fleet calendar says whether it is free now, taken days are tinted so white is free, and **Only cars free for these dates** shows the cars you can offer
- Every alert opens the exact thing it is about: the booking's messages, the conversation, the visitor, the field job or shift on the board, the person's Academy page; end of day alerts open that role's To do list
- An offer is open 12 hours and closes 1 hour before the pickup at the latest; once accepted, the car is held 2 hours for the payment, then freed, and the renter gets a fresh link to pay while the car is still free
- Texts and emails go out within a second or two of being queued, whoever queued them; the To do lists, Today, Alerts and the booking lists update by themselves
- A booking can be a long-term rental paid weekly: the car stays out on the calendar until the renter gives notice, and each weekly payment moves the return 7 days
- The academy teaches the guides: worked examples for every role, and the path The renter's side for every staff member
- Calls through the site open the caller pop-up and its Call helper within a second, for the calls you place and the ones that ring you; every call keeps its recording, transcript and summary on the renter
- A booking on a weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly plan is confirmed once its first payment is paid, and a plan never shows an empty last payment
- A booking partly paid but not yet confirmed can move to another car, or other dates, with **Edit**; its price and its payments stay
- A payment of the whole total pays every line of the booking (rental, short notice, young driver fee, Protection Plan) and confirms the booking when nothing is open; bookings stuck that way were repaired
- A deposit by e-Transfer or cash is twice the car's deposit (Program settings); record a part, a top-up or more than the deposit (given back with it), shown as **under, still owed** or **over, to return**
- Changing the deposit updates the Money tab and **Collect $X deposit** at once, also while the booking waits for payment; a voided deposit is **Not collected** again everywhere and can be recorded again
- Tips: record a cash or e-Transfer tip on the Money tab; half to the salesperson, half to the staff member at the handover (CarmaCars keeps the half with no staff member); your tips on **My sales** and **My pay**; every tip on **Money > Tips**
- **Approve** on Documents names what it approves; one driver reads **No additional drivers**; **Licence and selfie verified** ticks as soon as every image is approved
- Every greyed-out button says what to do first

## Renters

- The booking page says when the deposit is still owed and how to place it
- Signing no longer says "your signature does not match": the signature you drew is the one recorded, even if you press Sign quickly or sign on two devices
- Coming back? Initial all the key terms and sign with your saved signature, one tap each
- The details form is filled in with what you gave us last time: check it and save
- A card in your name written a little differently (a middle name, a short first name, accents) no longer stops your booking
- A renter's checklist says exactly which page of their own policy is still missing, and its button opens that upload, with example pictures of a pink slip and an OPCF 27 page
- A pay link asks only for what is owed: after a change, the difference; a link no longer needed is cancelled by itself and never charged
- CarmaCars Rewards emails renters: welcome, points earned, a new tier, the free day, reminders before points end, a monthly statement, and nudges they can unsubscribe from
- From Platinum up, a rental near the renter's birthday or on a holiday weekend is 10% (Platinum) or 20% (Black) off the rental charge, shown as its own line; Gold is off until an Admin gives it a percent

## For admins

- A car can go out with the deposit still owed, with the reason: the booking shows **Deposit pending $X** for every role, the saved card is tried each morning, the renter gets the deposit link, and after 48 hours you get a To do that opens the Money tab
- Program settings shows each message type that can go by text with its texts in the last 30 days; once **Email ready for production** is on, only urgent messages keep their texts and the rest go by email
- Claude's connector records a pickup with the deposit owed (`deposit_pending: true` with the reason), previewed first
- A card whose name still differs goes to a To do, **Check the name on the card**; press **Accept this card** with a short reason (staff who verify bookings can too). Program setting **Card in the renter's name** turns the rule off
- A typed signature whose name differs is accepted and listed as **Check a typed signature**
- **Approve all (n)** counts only what it can approve; a proof missing a page says **Waiting for the renter** with the page named
- A pay link for a booking defaults to and is capped at what is owed; a booking paid in full gets no balance link
- The guides lay out by the room they have: no more notes or steps squeezed into a thin column in the field portal or beside a menu
- Editing an offer that is out keeps what you saved: after **Send the update** or **Save without sending**, the screen and a reload show the saved car, dates, price and fees
- The offer screen is one form with one main button: **Send the offer** for a request, **Send the update** for an offer that is out, with **Save without sending** beside it; how long it is open, Email or Text, the rate card, discounts, insurer billing and the internal note are under **More options**
- **Send the offer** under Next step takes you straight to that button
- The fleet calendar is easier to read on a computer and a phone: full car names, the renter and booking number on each bar, clear colours by status, today marked, and the cars and dates stay in place while you scroll
- Each car on the Calendar says if it is free now (**Available now**) or who has it and until when; white days are free
- Alerts no longer land on a general page: each opens its booking (in the right section), conversation, visit, field job, shift, staff member or car
- Offers and accepted unpaid bookings stop holding the car on time; the Calendar shows them as a light hatched hold until they end. The times are in **Program settings** (offers open 12 hours, 1 hour before pickup at the latest, 2 hours to pay)
- Logs > Emails shows how long each email took from queued to sent, and the last day's average for emails, texts and phone alerts
- **Long-term, paid weekly** on a booking: the car stays out until notice, **End rental on** sets the last day, and 48 hours before the return with the next week unpaid the renter gets the pay link and you a To do (Program settings sets the hours); the connector's `change_booking` takes `rolling_weekly` and `rolling_end_on`
- Admin > Academy: every role's path gains worked examples from its guide, and every staff member (and the Admin path) the new path **The renter's side**, nine steps of the renter guide with the renter's screen and its page; new hires pass it before their portal opens, staff already working have 7 days, and lesson scenes can show a guide screenshot
- Calls placed from the site and calls that ring the browsers open the caller pop-up within a second, for every office role it rings; the renter's Activity log shows each call with Play, the transcript and the summary, and calls of the last 60 days missing them are looked up at Twilio and Quo
- Settings > Rewards lists every Rewards email with its 30-day counts, **Preview** and **Turn off**, and the birthday and holiday rewards by tier with the holidays
- **Edit** on a booking waiting for payment that already has a payment: another car, the dates, the places, with the price and payments kept; the connector's `change_booking` does the same
- Claude's connector lets a main driver keep a licence from outside Ontario on one booking (`licence_let_go`, your **Let it go** with the reason), previewed first
- The two pages of an OPCF 27 proof have one name on every screen: the pink slip and the OPCF 27 endorsement page

- On a car, **Studio photos** shows each made view beside the car's own photos: **Make again**, **Take off the site**, **Put on the site**, and the car's scene (highway, brick building and the other scenes)
- **Photo standard** shows the fleet's progress and about how many days are left at Gemini's daily limit
- Studio photos with a blurred box where a plate is or would have been are made again by themselves; the photo check now refuses a blur or box where no plate is, or one larger than the plate
- The marketing set is made from the studio photos, with up to three lifestyle shots marked AI; tick the photos you want in the ZIP for a post
- Gemini calls refused for where they came from are sent through the company server, and a failed call waits and tries again instead of blocking the queue

## For staff

- Let a car go with the deposit owed from the check-in, Record the pickup now or the field job, with the reason; you do nothing more once it is taken
- Customer service and Sales get the same offer screen: change an offer that is out and press **Send the update**, no need to withdraw it first
- The Calendar is easier to read: full car names, the renter and booking number on each bar, today marked, and the cars and dates stay in place while you scroll
- Each alert opens the exact booking, conversation, visitor or job it is about
- A renter paying week by week: turn on **Long-term, paid weekly** on the booking and record each week's payment; the return moves by itself
- Your academy has worked examples from your guide and the new path **The renter's side**: what renters see at each step, with the renter guide at **Renter guide**; staff already working have 7 days, shown on the academy page
- **Ask the guide first**: the Assistant names the guide chapter of the page you are on
- A call you place from the site, or one that rings your browser, opens the caller pop-up with the Call helper; on the renter, choose Calls to play a call or read its transcript
- Every staff member sees where the cars are: **Car map** on Find a car (read only), and **Where the car is** on each car and booking page, or **No tracker yet**
- Dates and times you type on the offer screen, Change car or dates and the calendar are Toronto time, wherever your computer is
- **Propose a change**: one more day costs exactly what a day of the booking costs (rental, fees and coverage), or type the new total; the preview shows the price a day

## For owners

- Each car's page shows **Where your car is** and its trips of the last 30 days, or **No tracker yet**; you see the location only, never the renter or the booking

## For renters

- The privacy policy and the rental agreement say that CarmaCars staff and the car's owner can see where the car is

- Your booking page says when the deposit is still owed and how to place it
- Your booking's checklist says exactly which insurance page is still missing, and its button opens that upload
- Example pictures of a pink slip and an OPCF 27 endorsement page, with the parts that must show circled, and how to take the photo
- A pay link asks only for what you owe; one you no longer need is cancelled by itself and never charged
- We email you about your Rewards points, your tier and your free day, and a reminder before points end
- From Platinum up, a rental near your birthday or on a holiday weekend costs less; add your birthday on the Rewards page
- On a payment plan, your booking is confirmed as soon as your first payment is in
- Paying the deposit by e-Transfer, cash or debit? Your offer and pay page show that amount (twice the card deposit) before you pay
- Add a tip for the team when you pay, on your booking after the trip or from the link in the Returned email: 10, 15 or 20%, or any amount

## Website & search

- Words are no longer squeezed into slivers or broken letter by letter on a phone: labels, car names, buttons and notes keep their room, and a wide table scrolls instead of crushing its columns
- Form labels such as "Where you are (city or neighbourhood)" fit a small phone
- Every car's photos look taken in one studio: the same nine views, the same angle and framing, the covers all facing the same way, in the dark dealership with the CarmaCars sign (the model's name behind the car on the first photo)
- A view a car has no photo of is made from its other photos; a car with no photo at all gets its outside views from its model's stock photo, in its own colour
- The home page, the cards, the car page, partner feeds and the sitemap show the same photos, the front three-quarter first
- Where the site showed a sample photo (the home page's big photo, example cars and cars without photos yet), real cars of the same type now show in turn, all in the same studio angle
- A car with no front plate gets no blur or box on its front bumper; the studio photos have the plate taken off cleanly, and where a blur is still used it covers only the plate, with a soft edge

## Reliability & security

- The photo queue no longer stalls on steps waiting with no end
