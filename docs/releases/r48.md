## Staff tools

- A car can go out with the deposit still owed, with the reason: every booking page shows **Deposit pending**, the saved card is tried each morning and the renter is sent the deposit link; after 48 hours an Admin gets a To do
- Each message type that can go by text shows its texts of the last 30 days and its channel; once email is ready for production, only urgent messages keep their texts and the rest go by email
- **Approve all (n)** counts only what it can approve, and the get-ready line says which page the renter still has to send
- Open pay links that asked for more than is owed were cancelled at release; Claude's connector can cancel one pay link (an Admin, previewed first, no money moves)
- The offer screen is one clear form with one main button, and an offer that is out is changed in place: what staff save stays saved
- The fleet calendar is easier to read on a computer and a phone: full car names, the renter and booking number on each bar, clear colours by status and today marked
- Every car on the fleet calendar says whether it is free now, taken days are tinted so white is free, and **Only cars free for these dates** shows the cars you can offer
- Every alert opens the exact thing it is about: the booking's messages, the conversation, the visitor, the field job or shift on the board, the person's Academy page; end of day alerts open that role's To do list
- An offer is open 12 hours and closes 1 hour before the pickup at the latest; once accepted, the car is held 2 hours for the payment, then freed, and the renter gets a fresh link to pay while the car is still free

## Renters

- The booking page says when the deposit is still owed and how to place it
- Signing no longer says "your signature does not match": the signature you drew is the one recorded, even if you press Sign quickly or sign on two devices
- Coming back? Initial all the key terms and sign with your saved signature, one tap each
- The details form is filled in with what you gave us last time: check it and save
- A card in your name written a little differently (a middle name, a short first name, accents) no longer stops your booking
- A renter's checklist says exactly which page of their own policy is still missing, and its button opens that upload, with example pictures of a pink slip and an OPCF 27 page
- A pay link asks only for what is owed: after a change, the difference; a link no longer needed is cancelled by itself and never charged

## For admins

- A car can go out with the deposit still owed, with the reason: the booking shows **Deposit pending $X** for every role, the saved card is tried each morning, the renter gets the deposit link, and after 48 hours you get a To do that opens the Money tab
- Program settings shows each message type that can go by text with its texts in the last 30 days; once **Email ready for production** is on, only urgent messages keep their texts and the rest go by email
- Claude's connector records a pickup with the deposit owed (`deposit_pending: true` with the reason), previewed first
- A card whose name still differs goes to a To do, **Check the name on the card**; press **Accept this card** with a short reason (staff who verify bookings can too). Program setting **Card in the renter's name** turns the rule off
- A typed signature whose name differs is accepted and listed as **Check a typed signature**
- **Approve all (n)** counts only what it can approve; a proof missing a page says **Waiting for the renter** with the page named
- A pay link for a booking defaults to and is capped at what is owed; a booking paid in full gets no balance link
- The guides lay out by the room they have: no more notes or steps squeezed into a thin column in the field portal or beside a menu
- Editing an offer that is out keeps what you saved: after **Send the update** or **Save without sending**, the screen and a reload show the saved car, dates, price and fees
- The offer screen is one form with one main button: **Send the offer** for a request, **Send the update** for an offer that is out, with **Save without sending** beside it; how long it is open, Email or Text, the rate card, discounts, insurer billing and the internal note are under **More options**
- **Send the offer** under Next step takes you straight to that button
- The fleet calendar is easier to read on a computer and a phone: full car names, the renter and booking number on each bar, clear colours by status, today marked, and the cars and dates stay in place while you scroll
- Each car on the Calendar says if it is free now (**Available now**) or who has it and until when; white days are free
- Alerts no longer land on a general page: each opens its booking (in the right section), conversation, visit, field job, shift, staff member or car
- Offers and accepted unpaid bookings stop holding the car on time; the Calendar shows them as a light hatched hold until they end. The times are in **Program settings** (offers open 12 hours, 1 hour before pickup at the latest, 2 hours to pay)

## For staff

- Let a car go with the deposit owed from the check-in, Record the pickup now or the field job, with the reason; you do nothing more once it is taken
- Customer service and Sales get the same offer screen: change an offer that is out and press **Send the update**, no need to withdraw it first
- The Calendar is easier to read: full car names, the renter and booking number on each bar, today marked, and the cars and dates stay in place while you scroll
- Each alert opens the exact booking, conversation, visitor or job it is about

## For renters

- Your booking page says when the deposit is still owed and how to place it
- Your booking's checklist says exactly which insurance page is still missing, and its button opens that upload
- Example pictures of a pink slip and an OPCF 27 endorsement page, with the parts that must show circled, and how to take the photo
- A pay link asks only for what you owe; one you no longer need is cancelled by itself and never charged

## Website & search

- Words are no longer squeezed into slivers or broken letter by letter on a phone: labels, car names, buttons and notes keep their room, and a wide table scrolls instead of crushing its columns
- Form labels such as "Where you are (city or neighbourhood)" fit a small phone
