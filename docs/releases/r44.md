## Staff tools

- A Sales person who closed a sale with a customer keeps that customer's conversation and activity on every booking, the later ones included
- The automatic AI answer waits when a person replied to that customer in the last 30 minutes, by text (from the site or the phone app) or by email; the minutes are a setting in Program settings, AI auto-replies
