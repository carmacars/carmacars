## Staff tools

- Staff make and take calls in the browser of the staff and Admin portals on the business number, from anywhere, without using their own phone number
- A call to the business number rings the staff who are online by role, and a call nobody answers becomes a missed call with its follow-up task
- The calendar shows, for any pickup and return, which cars are available, why the others are not and when they are free next, with one click to an offer for those dates
- The AI interview decides by itself again by default, and new hires are welcomed with their training, shift claiming and the team chat
- A booking's total and balance follow every change of a line, and an Admin can set a total directly
- Paperwork for insurer-billed rentals matches the insurer's own numbers exactly, and an ended rental can be signed for the file
- Payment records show only money actually received, and a new data check lists any booking where they disagree
- The automatic answers wait while a person is talking with the customer, also in the live chat
- Field staff can add the photos and readings of past check-ins and check-outs, from a list of the ones still missing

## Reliability & security

- Every request from the phone service is checked against its signature before anything is done
- A person who works in Sales only can read only their own leads

## For staff

- Calls are made and taken in the browser, in the staff portal, on the business number: your own phone number is never used or shown, wherever you work from
- A call to the business number rings the browsers of customer service and Sales who have a staff page open, with Answer and Decline; nobody answering gives the caller a short message and a missed call with its task
- During a call: Mute, a keypad and Hang up, with the call's timer
- At the top of Calendar, pick the customer's dates: every car says Available or Not available with the reason and the next free date, and Offer these dates starts the offer
- The calendar's colours now say on rent, confirmed, offer or hold, maintenance and block
- Once hired: training first and claiming shifts online, then a hello in the team chat and your member tag "Name - Position" in each WhatsApp group
- Changing the amount of a line on the Money tab now changes the booking's total and balance with it, so no balance appears that nobody owes
- A failed card attempt says what became of it: settled by a payment received another way, nothing owing any more, or a button to record the payment
- A Sales person who closed a sale keeps that customer's chats, calls and conversations on every later booking
- The automatic AI answer waits when a person replied to that customer in the last 30 minutes, also when the reply went to their booking or account, and in the live chat
- The Protection Plan can be offered on a car with no value on file, on every screen that offers it
- Field staff see **Missing check-in and check-out** on Today: past rentals they handled or were given, each with **Add the photos and readings** (odometer, fuel, damage photos), including closed bookings and pickups recorded with the details later
- Fleet sees every missing one on the Field jobs page and gives each to a field staff member

## For admins

- Staff who live in Canada get Canadian terms on their agreement (Canada, CAD, paid by Interac e-Transfer or direct deposit) and never read as being in the Philippines
- The same phone bar on every Admin page: Admins call and answer in the browser too
- The same Which cars are free view on the Admin calendar, with Offer these dates
- New settings: calls in the browser, whose browser rings, how long it rings, a forward number when nobody answers (empty by default) and the message callers hear
- The AI interview decides by itself again by default: only Sales passes are hired; every other role books an interview
- The welcome email gives no start date, shows each step in full, and has a button to the staff portal
- A person who works in Sales only reads only their own leads from their own session
- **Change the total** on the Money tab sets a booking's total cleanly; the difference goes on the rental charge
- An ended rental can get its rental agreement for the insurer's file: **Make the agreements again** or **Sign on this device** prepares it, and details not on file print blank
- The replacement rental agreement shows the insurer's authorized days and daily rate, with totals that match the insurer invoice exactly
- Insurer invoices carry a cheque section beside the e-Transfer details, never show the CarmaCars name, and stop saying Draft once issued
- **Take instant booking away** is on the renter's page as well as the booking page
- Data Health lists bookings whose deposit reads as charged with no deposit money recorded
- **Missing check-in and check-out** on Field jobs lists every past rental without its photos and readings, with **Give to field staff**
- An insurer invoice can be sent or marked sent from the booking; Send by email goes to the adjuster's email on the booking, else the insurer's billing email

## For renters

- Calls from CarmaCars always show the business number
- A deposit that was never paid no longer reads as charged on the booking
- **I've returned the car** and photos for **Report a problem** send from a phone browser opened by a text message link

## Website & search

- Every outside photo has the same CarmaCars showroom behind it: when a car cannot be cut out cleanly, Gemini replaces the background from the original photo instead of leaving the place it was taken in
- The plate is only ever on the car's plate: no plate text on the wall sign, the model name or anywhere else on the photo
- Inside photos are sharp across the whole cabin, never blurred; the blurred ones are made again
- The first photo of every car is its outside front three-quarter: outside photos filed as details are found and moved, so Redo photos with Gemini no longer answers that there is nothing to redo
- Redo every car with Gemini brings the whole fleet to the current photo standard, and the rest follows by itself within Gemini's daily allowance

## Owners

- A cancelled or no-show booking is on the owner's statement only for the charge actually kept: nothing paid, no line

## Renters

- Telling us the car is back, and sending photos of a problem, now work from a phone browser opened by a text message link
