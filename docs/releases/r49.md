## Staff tools

- A Finance dashboard: the cash in the bank and with the card processor, money in and out, HST, what renters and insurers owe, and every bank line from the bank's download matched to its payout, payment or expense

## For admins

- **Finance** (Company, first tab): cash in the bank and at Stripe with the payouts on the way, free cash after deposits, payouts, refunds and HST, money in and out of the month, HST for the quarter, renter balances overdue, insurer invoices by age and twelve months of money in and out
- **Bank lines**: drop the RBC download (CSV or OFX); each line is kept once, matched by itself to its card processor payout, payment, payout or expense when one record fits, and the rest becomes a To do for Finance

## For staff

- Finance has the same Finance page under Money, with the bank import and the matching of bank lines
