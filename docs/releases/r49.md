## For renters

- Give a CarmaCars gift card or a gifted rental from the footer's **Gift cards**: any amount, emailed with your message on the day you choose, and it never expires. Got one? Add the code under Rewards, **Gift cards**; on Sign and pay the balance is used before your card.

## Staff tools

- A Finance dashboard: the cash in the bank and with the card processor, money in and out, HST, what renters and insurers owe, and every bank line from the bank's download matched to its payout, payment or expense
- Sales are told when a website request has had no reply for 30 minutes, and the Admins at 2 hours
- Finance gets a daily list of overdue renter balances with booking links, a To do for the Admins per balance overdue 3 days, and a notice for every refund paid or failed
- A booking's documents list no longer shows documents that were removed or replaced, so none opens an error page

## For admins

- **Finance** (Company, first tab): cash in the bank and at Stripe with the payouts on the way, free cash after deposits, payouts, refunds and HST, money in and out of the month, HST for the quarter, renter balances overdue, insurer invoices by age and twelve months of money in and out
- **Bank lines**: drop the RBC download (CSV or OFX); each line is kept once, matched by itself to its card processor payout, payment, payout or expense when one record fits, and the rest becomes a To do for Finance
- Money > **Gift cards**: what is owed on gift cards, every card and its history; issue a voucher (an amount or a percentage), void with a reason, resend.

## For staff

- Finance has the same Finance page under Money, with the bank import and the matching of bank lines
- Finance: Money > **Gift cards**: the liability outstanding and every card's balance history.

## For owners

- Owners are told once when their car is 2 hours late coming back

## Reliability & security

- An automatic check on every change makes sure each event reaches every portal it touches, with its email template in place
- The user guides and the academy are checked against the app on every change: a page, button or setting that no longer exists fails the build
- An installable Android test build can be made on demand, without any store
