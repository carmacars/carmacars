## Owners

- Owners can be shown the repair costs and partner credits on their own cars
- Every debit email explains each amount: the car, the fee, the month and how it was worked out

## Staff tools

- Staff alerts by email come together in one digest an hour, urgent ones on their own
- The booking money screen in two panels, rental charges and security deposit, each collected on its own
- The deposit can be held on the card on file in one step
- Staff emails about owner payments say what happened, how much and what to do, with one button
- A debit preview before each debit day, with a hold per owner, and a check of each month's owner invoices against the signed terms
- Repair costs recorded per car and per claim, with receipts
- Partner credits and repair costs shown per car, per owner and for the business, with a date range and export

## Partners

- An Updates page in the investor portal: every release of the platform and what it added, with search and a filter by area

## Reliability & security

- Reliability improvements: emails go out at a steady pace within one daily limit
- Job applicants can record their interview answers out loud again
