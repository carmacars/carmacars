## Staff tools

- The caller pop-up has a Call helper: a greeting to read out with the staff member's own first name and the caller's, a button for each kind of call (new booking, change a booking, late return, damage or accident, payment or deposit, owner calling, job applicant, other) with what to say and the next action, and a search over quick answers
- The greetings, the steps and the quick answers are written by an Admin in Settings, Call helper, and change on the next call; the amounts, days and percentages in them are read from the settings, never typed
- The caller pop-up minimizes to a small bar with the caller's name and the call's timer, and opens again from it; a click outside the panel or Esc minimizes it instead of closing it, and it follows the device's dark mode
