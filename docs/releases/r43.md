## Staff tools

- A Sales person who closed a sale with a customer keeps that customer's conversation and activity on every booking, the later ones included
- The automatic AI answer waits when a person replied to that customer in the last 30 minutes, by text (from the site or the phone app) or by email; the minutes are a setting in Program settings, AI auto-replies
- Only a Sales applicant who passes the AI interview is hired by itself; every other role that passes is invited to book an interview date
- An Admin can press **Redo the AI interview**: the old interview is kept as history and the applicant gets a new link
- **Primary photo is not exterior** flags a car whose first photo is an inside one, and **Redo photos with Gemini** makes its outside photos again
- Switching a booking to the Protection Plan uses the car's own plan price instead of being refused (included through the release 42 hotfix)
- Check-in and check-out are one screen on the phone instead of ten to fourteen steps: each part shows a tick when it is done, and the button at the bottom says what is still needed
- The odometer, fuel and keys are filled in from the tracker or the car's last report, photos are one tap each, and anything optional is folded away; an interrupted handover opens where it was left
- A late return is no longer charged by itself: staff choose on the return screen or on the booking between an extension, extra days, the late return fee, a custom amount with a reason, or waiving it with a reason
- The same one-screen handover opens for Admins, the assistants and field staff
- The caller pop-up has a Call helper: a greeting to read out with the staff member's own first name and the caller's, a button for each kind of call (new booking, change a booking, late return, damage or accident, payment or deposit, owner calling, job applicant, other) with what to say and the next action, and a search over quick answers
- The greetings, the steps and the quick answers are written by an Admin in Settings, Call helper, and change on the next call; the amounts, days and percentages in them are read from the settings, never typed
- The caller pop-up minimizes to a small bar with the caller's name and the call's timer, and opens again from it; a click outside the panel or Esc minimizes it instead of closing it, and it follows the device's dark mode
- Every list of people and records has a sort: Newest first, Oldest first, Recently active and A to Z, and people lists open newest first
- Hiring and onboarding are one pipeline, one stage per person, with the next action on each line
- Waiting for your approval is the first thing on Staff and roles and on Today, with Approve and Reject
- After every release, each portal shows a What's new pop-up once with what changed for that person, and **What's new** in the menu opens it again
- Owners see each car's current kilometres and its next service (what, in how many km or by which date) on their dashboard and car page
- Add a rental: the renter's email is optional for a rental that already ended, is billed to an insurer, or is marked not to email the renter

## Renters

- Sending "I've returned the car" no longer fails on a server hiccup: it is tried again by itself, and an error shows its code instead of only asking to call
- The first photo of every car on the site is its exterior at the front angle

## Reliability & security

- Every late return choice is recorded with who made it and why, the renter gets a message that says what was chosen, and an amount above a person's limit or a waiver still waits for an Admin
- The AI interview takes video answers only, scores every answer out of 10, and passes only answers averaging 7 or more with none under 5 and good or fluent English

## For staff

- When you close a sale with a customer, you keep their conversation on every later booking too
- The AI does not answer a customer for 30 minutes after a person replied to them
- Check-in and check-out are one screen instead of ten to fourteen steps, with the odometer, fuel and keys filled in for you and a tick on each part when it is done
- A late return is never charged by itself: on the return screen or the booking you choose an extension, extra days, the late return fee, a custom amount or a waiver, each with a reason where needed
- The caller pop-up has a Call helper with a greeting, a button for each kind of call and quick answers; it minimizes to a small bar when you click outside it
- Every list of people and records can be sorted newest first, and opens newest first
- Hiring and onboarding are one pipeline with the next action on each line
- After each release a What's new pop-up shows what changed

## For admins

- Only Sales applicants are hired by the AI interview by themselves; every other role that passes books an interview date
- The AI interview takes video answers only, with two minutes per question, scores each answer out of 10 and is stricter; **Redo the AI interview** sends a new link
- Approvals waiting for you are first on Staff and roles and on Today
- **Redo photos with Gemini** remakes a car's outside photos, and every car's first photo is its front exterior
- Switching a booking to the Protection Plan uses the car's plan price
- Add a rental: the renter's email is optional for past rentals, insurer-billed rentals, or when marked not to email

## For owners

- Your dashboard and car pages show each car's current kilometres and its next service, in km or by date

## For renters

- If sending "I've returned the car" hits a server hiccup it is tried again by itself
- Every car's first photo is its exterior from the front
- Returning the car yourself always sends your report: if a photo or reading is missing, staff confirm the return instead of the form refusing

## For investors

- Hiring is stricter: video-only AI interviews scored per answer, and only Sales is hired without a person
- Check-in and check-out are faster and late returns are charged by a staff decision, never automatically
