## Staff tools

- Only a Sales applicant who passes the AI interview is hired by itself; every other role that passes is invited to book an interview date
- An Admin can press **Redo the AI interview**: the old interview is kept as history and the applicant gets a new link
- **Primary photo is not exterior** flags a car whose first photo is an inside one, and **Redo photos with Gemini** makes its outside photos again
- Switching a booking to the Protection Plan uses the car's own plan price instead of being refused (included through the release 42 hotfix)
- Placeholder: check-in, check-out and late return choices
- The caller pop-up has a Call helper: a greeting to read out with the staff member's own first name and the caller's, a button for each kind of call (new booking, change a booking, late return, damage or accident, payment or deposit, owner calling, job applicant, other) with what to say and the next action, and a search over quick answers
- The greetings, the steps and the quick answers are written by an Admin in Settings, Call helper, and change on the next call; the amounts, days and percentages in them are read from the settings, never typed
- The caller pop-up minimizes to a small bar with the caller's name and the call's timer, and opens again from it; a click outside the panel or Esc minimizes it instead of closing it, and it follows the device's dark mode
- Every list of people and records has a sort: Newest first, Oldest first, Recently active and A to Z, and people lists open newest first
- Hiring and onboarding are one pipeline, one stage per person, with the next action on each line
- Waiting for your approval is the first thing on Staff and roles and on Today, with Approve and Reject

## Reliability & security

- The AI interview takes video answers only, scores every answer out of 10, and passes only answers averaging 7 or more with none under 5 and good or fluent English
