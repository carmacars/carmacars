## Renters

- Common questions by text, email, chat and social messages are answered at once from our FAQ, and a person answers anything else
- No automatic text between 1 am and 5 am: an answer asked for then arrives at 5 am
- Address fields suggest Canadian addresses from our own address search, faster and as you type
- Before pickup and sign and pay show how far along you are, and a confirmed booking celebrates once
- Every search page shows cars ready to book and a Request this exact car form, never an empty list
- Car pages show the car's price when new, production years and plants, dimensions, recall campaigns, the parts owners report most, charging, equipment and ratings out of 10, each with its source
- Place, event and airport pages list the parking lots, garages and streets with parking nearby
- A page for each major Toronto and GTA event, with the cars, getting there and parking near the venue
- Airport pages: the car delivered to the terminal at the standard delivery price
- Renting vs owning pages compare the monthly cost of owning a model, line by line with sources, with renting it by the month
- The search pages in 18 languages spoken across the GTA
- Real reviews on every car and search page, each with where it was received

## Staff tools

- Each listing chooses its first photo on the site and in the Marketplace kit: the plain car, a car-show photo with a model, or a flyer with the live price, the offer and the call to action
- The flyer is made again by itself when the price or its words change
- The flyer's words and the model's description are settings, never fixed in the site
- Each channel's automatic answers switch on or off, with the quiet hours, the daily limit per person and how sure the answer must be
- The last automatic answers and the questions left for a person show in settings and under renter messages; a question it cannot answer becomes a To do
- Add a customer review that arrived by text, call, email or another platform, exactly as received, with a screenshot
- Event pages: add an event or set its dates once announced, for Admin and sales
- The most requested car is shown lower in the fleet order, and its page points to similar cars, so requests spread across the fleet
- A GPS tracker page and section from Program settings

## Reliability & security

- Automatic answers stay inside the free daily AI allowance and stop when it is used
- Canadian addresses loaded province by province, Ontario first, with a size cap
- Deploy secrets can live encrypted in the repository, opened with one key
