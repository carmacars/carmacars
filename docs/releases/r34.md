## Renters

- Change your name, password and phone from your profile; a legal name change is a request an Admin approves

## Owners

- One onboarding checklist: each step shows what is done, who it waits on and the one thing to do next
- Replace or remove any onboarding photo yourself
- Add many cars at once from a list of VINs
- Government registration forms are made automatically and listed with your documents

## Staff tools

- Customer service and Admins see who is calling, live: their booking, what they owe, the deposit, open requests and notes
- Notes on each renter, kept with the author and time, from the call pop-up or the renter's page
- New hires land on a welcome page and must pass their Academy path before their work pages open; an Admin can waive it with a reason. Staff already working are never held
- New Academy sections with quizzes cover the newest workflows, and a section is flagged for review when its guide changes
- A printable card for every car with the steps to follow after an accident and the coverage details
- Book it now from the AI assistant, with a preview and a confirmation
- A booking that replaced a duplicate request links to it, both ways
- A visitor who comes back for the same car raises an alert on its own
- Staff and Admins can replace onboarding photos, make the government forms again and add cars from a VIN list

## Reliability & security

- Alerts to many staff are sent several times faster
- A government form is no longer made again when nothing on it changed
