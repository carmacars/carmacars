## Owners

- One onboarding checklist for each car: what is done, who each step waits on, and the one thing to do next
- A wrong onboarding photo is replaced or removed in the portal, and checked again
- Government registration forms are filled in by themselves and kept up to date, with the date each was made
- Add several cars at once from a list of VINs

## Staff tools

- Staff and Admins see the same onboarding checklist on every owner's page
- Staff and Admins replace, remove, approve or reject an owner's onboarding photos
- Every car's government forms on the owner page and the car page, with Make again for an Admin
- Add cars for an owner from a pasted or uploaded list of VINs

## Reliability & security

- A car moved to a new owner keeps only that owner's terms, so edits on the terms tab show at once
