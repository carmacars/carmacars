## Website & search

- On a phone, the menu opened partway down a page keeps the header (logo and Close) at the top
- The cars list for chosen dates loads again: each car is priced once, about seven times faster, and a busy moment is retried quietly
- A car's own photos stay on the site while their new Gemini versions are made; one that is refused stays as it was and is flagged
- The first outside photo leads the gallery and the cards wherever it sits, so no car shows a drawn sample while it has photos
- The cars that rent least come first in the fleet, with a partner's fleet after our own cars
- Every car shows nine views generated from its own photos in a photorealistic showroom, the front three-quarter first; a view that fails the check leaves the car's real photo in its place

## Reliability & security

- Website error alerts count only real errors, once a page a day with how many people met it; dropped connections, stale tabs and browser translation are kept on record without an alert
- Pages no longer fail when the browser translates them, or when a busy moment delays a page's title

## For admins

- Every booking shows its deposit beside the status for every role: **Deposit held $500 on the card**, **No deposit hold yet** or **Deposit hold failed**
- The To do list is shorter: one **New staff this week** To do with what each hire still waits on, and one **Pay details to verify**
- An insurer rental with no known end date asks you to extend or close it instead of starting late-return notices
- A payment captured twice at the same moment is no longer reported as failed
- Claude's connector reads the photo standard per car and for the fleet, with Gemini's use today
- Claude's connector reads a booking's payments live from Stripe beside what the site recorded, and the Stripe account's balance, payouts and open requirements; a payment Stripe took that the site missed can be recorded, previewed first, nothing charged

- A refused remake is flagged on Photo standard for **Redo this car now**; the photo stays on the site meanwhile
- Photo standard shows each car's **Generated set**; a car with too few photos uses its check-in and check-out photos as references (never shown)
- **Showroom background plates** in Program settings: upload a real showroom photo to replace the background of every outside view
