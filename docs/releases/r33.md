## Renters

- One Sign and pay page: the agreement, the signature and the card form together, with exactly what is left and the amount
- A signature for an older version of an offer opens the current agreement to sign instead of a dead end
- A debit or prepaid deposit shows its card processing fee before paying; the refund returns the full deposit
- Anyone can change their own sign-in email, confirmed by a link

## Owners

- Fleet suppliers apply, choose the fleet route, sign the lease and the power of attorney in their portal and set each car's tier
- Every owner route fills its government registration forms automatically
- Paper signatures are gone: every agreement is signed electronically
- Updated terms are accepted once in the portal before carrying on

## Staff tools

- An Admin can override any check blocking a pickup or return, with a reason; staff ask and an Admin approves in one tap
- Record a past trip: both times in one form, then settle the deposit
- Book it now: an offer becomes a booking at once, the renter signs and pays on one page; staff offers wait for an Admin's approval
- Charge the renter after a return in one step: choose the reason, the amount, and the platform takes it from the deposit, the card on file or a pay link
- Deposit holds renew by themselves before they expire; an expired hold is shown as such with the card on file offered instead
- Reasons for keeping part of a deposit come from one list, shown on the renter's receipt
- Listing photos import from a shared cloud folder link, one car or a whole folder of cars at once
- Hiring in one step: role, start date and pay, the account, the welcome and the agreement
- Requests and offers: every summary box filters, a new Accepted, not paid filter, old expired offers become leads and duplicates are hidden
- Pickup and return wording is the same everywhere: check-in is the pickup, check-out the return
- Owner invoices can be issued without an email and marked as already paid outside the platform
- Approve all in the document review approves exactly what it shows

## Partners

- The AI assistant connector can now act as the Admin, with a preview and a confirmation for every change

## Reliability & security

- The listing studio letters the model name on the cover and every outside angle, with a manual cut-out editor
- The photo import key is never shown in full or written to the audit log
