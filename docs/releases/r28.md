## Renters

- A booking shows as booked as soon as it is accepted, with payment and pickup shown as separate steps
- Renters can do their own check-in and checkout from the booking page in one screen
- Pickup and return photos uploaded many at a time from the camera roll, with a suggested-angles checklist
- Deposits held on the card instead of charged, and released by staff when the trip is settled
- Agreements update with booking changes without signing again

## Owners

- Pickup and return records with photos when we collect a car from its owner or bring it back
- Owner agreement terms clarified
- Owners can see the cars of owners they referred, without personal details

## Staff tools

- Check-in and checkout collapsed into one screen for every staff role
- The offer preview shown right under the send button
- A cleaning fee set in Admin and billable from the booking
- The partner rental agreement filled in completely, with an editable view
- Scan a VIN with the camera or read it from a photo wherever a VIN is entered
- API access and a connector for the founders to update bookings, owners and requests, with every change logged

## Partners

- An invoice builder for body shop, towing and storage partners, with sending, reminders and payment tracking

## Website & search

- The CarmaCars logo on every page, email, document and app icon
- One search box on the website and in every portal
- Real cars rotating in the home page photos
- Place pages with more local detail, and search engines told about changes right away
- Listing photos in any order, up to 30 at once, framed on the studio background with a light touch-up

## Reliability & security

- Reliability improvements: the platform learns from past fixes and applies them by itself
