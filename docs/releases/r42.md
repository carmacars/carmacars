## Staff tools

- When a booking that was left out of the owner's revenue starts counting (it blocks the calendar, is shown to the owner or is billed to the renter), the owner gets the new booking email once, at that moment, never twice

## Renters

- When a rental is covered by the renter's credit card, the pay page says "Pay with the card that covers this rental" with the card's network, and stops a card of another network before it is charged

## Owners

- The owner and admin guides now say which bookings an owner statement carries: bookings paid by the renter always, and not a booking billed to an insurer that is hidden from the owner or that does not block the calendar
- A booking that starts counting later reaches the owner with the new booking email

## Reliability & security

- A card of another network than the one that covers the rental is refused on the server before the booking is confirmed, with a plain message and nothing charged
- An address whose mailbox does not exist, or that reports one of our emails as unwanted, is blocked automatically and not emailed again
- The faster push channel for live updates switches on with the server's next update
