# Release 36b: missing angles, short videos, social scheduling

Migrations 2200 to 2249. Owner requests: messages of 2026-11-08 (AI photos and social content) and 2026-10-23 (social media automation).

## 1. Make the missing angle (2200)
- On the listing page (Admin, Fleet) and in the Marketplace kit (Admin, Sales), **Make the missing angle** lists every required angle the car has no photo of. One press asks the image model (Gemini, the showroom's gate: Program settings "Showroom image model", its free daily limit, an hour's pause after a quota answer) to make that view from up to three of the car's approved photos. No paid use: when the free allowance is used up the view says so and waits.
- Every result is stored as generated (`listing_angle_makes.generated`, always true) and waits for a person: Approve or Reject (Admin, Fleet or Sales). Only approved views show, after the real photos, labelled "AI view".
- Never the cover and never evidence: generated views are not vehicle photos, so the cover, the made covers (flyer, model), handover photo sets, condition reports and claims never use them. A real photo of the angle replaces the view at once.
- The words given to the image model are Program settings `listing_angle_prompt` ({angle}, {car}).
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r36b_missing_angle.sql`; worker unit test in `web/src/features/listing/worker.test.ts`.
