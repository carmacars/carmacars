# Release 36b: missing angles, short videos, social scheduling

Migrations 2200 to 2249. Owner requests: messages of 2026-11-08 (AI photos and social content) and 2026-10-23 (social media automation).

## 1. Make the missing angle (2200)
- On the listing page (Admin, Fleet) and in the Marketplace kit (Admin, Sales), **Make the missing angle** lists every required angle the car has no photo of. One press asks the image model (Gemini, the showroom's gate: Program settings "Showroom image model", its free daily limit, an hour's pause after a quota answer) to make that view from up to three of the car's approved photos. No paid use: when the free allowance is used up the view says so and waits.
- Every result is stored as generated (`listing_angle_makes.generated`, always true) and waits for a person: Approve or Reject (Admin, Fleet or Sales). Only approved views show, after the real photos, labelled "AI view".
- Never the cover and never evidence: generated views are not vehicle photos, so the cover, the made covers (flyer, model), handover photo sets, condition reports and claims never use them. A real photo of the angle replaces the view at once.
- The words given to the image model are Program settings `listing_angle_prompt` ({angle}, {car}).
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r36b_missing_angle.sql`; worker unit test in `web/src/features/listing/worker.test.ts`.

## 2. Short video for social media (2210)
- **Make a short video** on the listing page (Admin, Fleet, customer service) and in the Marketplace kit (Admin, Sales): a 9:16 video (720x1280) of the car's real approved photos, each with a slow zoom and pan and a fade to the next, the brand at the top and the car, the price a day and the call to action at the bottom.
- Made in the browser (canvas recorded with MediaRecorder; MP4 where the browser records it, WebM otherwise). No paid service and no server rendering. Keep the tab in front while it records (it records in real time, about 20 seconds for 8 photos).
- Saved to the public bucket (`vehicles/<car>/social/<id>.mp4|webm`, under 14 MB), listed with a preview, **Download** and **Remove**, and offered to the post scheduler (item 3).
- Never made from a generated view, a made cover (flyer, model) or a private photo. Words and timing are Program settings: `social_video_cta` ({price} is the live price a day), `social_video_brand`, `social_video_seconds_per_photo`, `social_video_max_photos`.
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r36b_social_video.sql`; `web/src/features/social/video.test.ts`.

## 3. Social post scheduler (2220)
- **Scheduled posts** on Admin > Marketing > Social drafts and on Sales > Marketing: queue a post for a date and time on the Facebook Page, Instagram or TikTok, with a photo (a draft's photo) or a short video (item 2), and the caption from a social draft (with its link) or typed. Admin, Fleet, customer service and Sales may schedule (the same rule as the marketing photos).
- A pg_cron job (`carmacars-social-queue`, every 5 minutes) marks posts whose time has come as due. When Meta publishing is connected (Admin > Settings > Meta publishing: the Page id, the Instagram business account id and the Page token kept in Vault; empty by default), the Worker's ads job posts the due Facebook and Instagram posts through the Graph API: photos, Facebook Page videos and Instagram reels (MP4 only; the reel is published once Meta has processed it). Each result is logged (`social_post_log`): three tries, then Failed with Meta's answer, with Try again.
- Without Meta connected the queue shows **Connect Meta** and works as a list: each post is due at its time with the caption to copy, the photo or video to download and **Posted by hand** (with the post's address).
- **TikTok** is always posted by hand: the Content Posting API needs TikTok's app review before it can post for us. A due TikTok post shows the download-and-post checklist.
- **Facebook Marketplace** has no posting API: the Marketplace kit and the session flow stay as they are (now with Missing angles and Short video in the kit).
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r36b_social_scheduler.sql`; `web/src/integrations/meta-video.test.ts`.
