# Release 37: gaps closed, lesson videos, Google Meet, the Channel API

Migrations 2300 to 2304 (SCHEMA_MARKER 2304). Items the owner asked for that the audit found only partly done, plus two new asks.

## Owner action needed once
- **Admin > Hiring > Connect Google Calendar**: an Admin clicks it once and allows access (the company Google account). Until then video interview calls keep Jitsi links. The Google client's authorized redirect URIs must include `https://carmacars.ca/api/google/calendar/callback` (the reviews client is used unless `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_CLIENT_ID`/`SECRET` are set).
- **Translations**: seven new strings for the vehicle language pages; run the seo-i18n workflow once after deploy (it runs weekly otherwise). Until it runs, a language under its coverage setting is noindex.

## 1. Vehicle pages in 18 languages, only where real cars back them (2302)
- `/<lang>/cars/<make>/<model>/<year>/<trim>`, `.../<place>` and `.../<place>/<colour>`, and the per-car service pages `/<lang>/t/<term>/<make>/<model>/<year>/<trim>` (every detailing term, and the DUI, suspension and bad-record rentals the search terms serve).
- Made only for a real, listed car (never a sample): its trim, the largest places (Program setting `seo_i18n_vehicle_places`, 12), and the colour of the real car. Any other path is a 404. The place, colour and term switches of the vehicle pages apply.
- In the language sitemap with hreflang alternates; edge cached like the other programmatic pages; translated through the existing seo-i18n path.
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r37_vehicle_languages.sql`; `web/src/lib/edge/page-cache.test.ts`.

## 2. Insurer claim card: already done
- The full claim and adjuster card shows only on insurer-billed bookings, in the Admin and staff booking pages (r35 gaps, commit 5812f22f); a renter-billed booking keeps only "Bill an insurer instead". Nothing changed.

## 3. Contact capture: sign in on the spot, not bookable until verified (2300)
- After a form saves contact details, the note under it offers **Sign in now** at once (new account or existing one).
- A sent request on "Your request" (the unverified session) shows **Not bookable until your licence is verified**, with a sign-in link to add the licence; the office's Requests queue (Admin and staff) says the same on the licence chip.
- The state is one database rule (`app.booking_licence_state`, the licence chip's rule). Tests: `99_r37_unverified_requests.sql`, `capture/fields.test.ts`.

## 4. Academy lessons as real videos (2301)
- Every lesson has **Watch as video** (Admin and every staff portal): the first press records the lesson in the browser (canvas and MediaRecorder: MP4 where the browser records it, WebM otherwise), free, and saves it to private storage for everyone; then it plays and **Download** gives the file.
- Admin > Academy > **Render videos** makes every missing one in a row.
- A changed lesson makes its video stale; the next press records it again. The browser voice cannot be recorded, so the video carries the words as captions.
- Tests: `99_r37_academy_videos.sql`, `academy/video.test.ts`.

## 5. Interview calls on Google Meet (2303)
- With Google Calendar connected, each booked video call gets a Calendar event with its own Google Meet link; the link replaces Jitsi in the confirmation email (held up to `meet_hold_minutes`, 10, for it), the candidate's page, the reminders and Admin > Hiring > Calls. Moving the call moves the event; cancelling deletes it.
- Any Google failure keeps the Jitsi link, so a candidate always has a working link; the error shows in Admin > Hiring.
- The refresh token is kept in the vault, never in a table or the repository. Hiring is Admin-only, so there is no staff portal page to mirror.
- Tests: `99_r37_google_meet.sql`, `meet/event.test.ts`.

## 6. Channel API for rental distribution partners (2304)
- Admin > Settings > **Channel partners**: partners with markup, commission, allowed classes and calls a minute; keys shown once (SHA-256 kept), scopes availability and rates by default, bookings off unless ticked; revoke at once; pause a partner; the call log.
- `/api/channel/v1`: locations, vehicles, availability with prices (the website's quote plus the partner's markup), OTA XML (`OTA_VehAvailRateRQ` to `RS`), and booking requests that go through the normal flow (the renter still verifies their licence).
- Never renter, owner, plate, VIN, cost, utilization or another partner's data. Program setting `channel_api_enabled` switches it off. What to send partners: `docs/channel-api.md`.
- Tests: `99_r37_channel_api.sql`, `channel/ota.test.ts`.

## 7. Insurer-billed rentals that do not block the calendar need no renter contact (2310)
- Add a rental (Admin, customer service/Sales, field): billed to an insurer AND not blocking the calendar, the renter needs only a name and date of birth (the driver details): no account, phone or email. Email and phone show as optional with a note; every other rental still asks for both. Enforced in the database (`app.manual_rental_create`), not only the form.
- Nothing is sent to a renter with no account, email or phone: no email row (no empty address), no text row, no error. Statements, the insurer invoice and owner statements are unchanged.
- Tests: `99_r37_insurer_no_contact.sql`, `supply/renter-contact.test.ts`.
