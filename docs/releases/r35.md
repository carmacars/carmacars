## Owners

- A corrected government form replaces the earlier one in your documents
- A debit that was missed comes off your next monthly statement, shown with its invoice number

## Staff tools

- Field jobs are given only to field staff who passed the academy and hold a shift that day; Fleet and Admin publish the shifts and field staff take them on their portal
- Work lands only on days with shifts, a warning shows on any booking whose handover or return falls on a day with none, and the Admin can be given a job and opens the field jobs board
- A job can be taken off a person without telling them
- An Admin account is only an Admin: it has every staff page, and no staff role can be added to it
- Clocking in and out records where the phone is at those two moments only; Fleet and Admin see a map link, the distance, and a flag when it was over 500 m away or refused
- One activity log for every renter and booking: emails, texts, live chat, calls with their summaries, notes, booking events, documents and website visits, newest first
- Small filters for each kind of activity, a search, and older entries a page at a time
- Reply, call and text right from the log, on the renter's page in every staff portal
- Admins and fleet change a value on a government form with a reason; each change is a new version and earlier versions stay downloadable
- The forms' mapping is previewed on a sample car, every save is kept and can be loaded again, and fleet can map the forms too
- Making a car's forms again keeps the values changed by hand unless you choose to discard them

- The field portal is built around the shift: four tabs (Today, My shifts, My pay, Training) and one ordered list of the day, each car and time one card with its clean check, add-ons and handover
- The pickup waits until each add-on is ticked as in the car, or an Admin overrides
- One Add button for money spent (with the receipt, approved and paid back by Finance), a service record (on the car, with its expense, Fleet and Finance told), photos and a problem with a car
- Clocking out with work still open asks a reason for each; Fleet sees each person's list and how much is done
- Field staff give up a shift with a reason, it is offered to everyone free then and the first to claim takes it; swaps between two people; late give-ups are flagged; Fleet is alerted when nobody claims
- Field staff take a payment or the deposit on the spot by link, QR or the card reader, check the licence and get the agreement signed; refunds stay with Finance
- Fleet gives field staff the onboarding prep of a new car; finished items tick the owner's onboarding checklist
- Pay details before the first pay: legal name, SIN (encrypted, only Finance and Admin see it, every view recorded) and a void cheque; Finance sees who is missing them
- Field staff can ask to do sales too: the Sales pages open once the Sales training is passed

## Website & search

- The investor presentation for 2026 is live in the investor portal as web slides that fit any screen
- Every investor page states the same terms as the presentation

## Reliability & security

- The daily email limit is shown as it is set, everywhere it is mentioned
- Government forms are only ever the official government PDF with its boxes filled; anything else gives the data sheet
- No missed owner debit is left waiting for staff: the next statement recovers it
