## Renters

- If your credit card does not cover the car, our team can move your booking to the Protection Plan, on an offer or a booking you have paid, and back; you get an email with what changed and the new total, and your booking page shows it
- Any extra comes as a secure payment link, and you no longer send a card coverage document once you are on the plan
- An accident card for each booking with the steps after an accident and the coverage that booking uses, ready to print
- Report an accident from the phone in one form, with photos and videos, and our team alerted at once
- The km and fuel at the return compared with pickup on the return receipt, with any extra km or fuel charge
- Two accounts of the same person become one by themselves: sign in with either email, or with your phone number and a code
- One short note tells you your accounts are now one
- The accident card shows your own protection plan policy number and the plan's claim page when you bought the plan with the rental

## Owners

- No HST is charged on the monthly, first and last month coverage fee or on registration; invoices already paid show what you actually paid
- One email and one text for what happens on your booking at the same moment, such as the confirmation, the receipt and the agreement together
- No pickup reminder right after the confirmation that already gives the date and time
- A documents reminder only for what you still have to send, and it names exactly that
- No new agreement version just because you paid
- A short grace after your return time before any overdue reminder, and none once you are on your way back
- One email a day listing every invoice we cancelled, instead of one email each
- A corrected government form replaces the earlier one in your documents
- You sign each government form your car needs on screen during onboarding: you see the official form filled in, check it and sign it, and Documents shows which forms are signed
- If a value on a form changes after you signed it, you get one To do and one reminder email to sign it again
- A debit that was missed comes off your next monthly statement, shown with its invoice number

## Reliability & security

- The same message is not sent twice to the same person for the same booking within 30 minutes; receipts, signed copies and staff messages always go
- The daily email limit counts every email service that is sending, and every page says plainly which ones are
- The daily email limit is shown as it is set, everywhere it is mentioned
- Government forms are only ever the official government PDF with its boxes filled; anything else gives the data sheet
- An owner's on-screen signature is a record kept beside the form with the date, IP address and device; it is never put on the official form
- No missed owner debit is left waiting for staff: the next statement recovers it
- The server check email names the failing scheduled job, its last error and whether its next run was ok, and only emails when a job keeps failing
- Showroom photos are made by the site itself when the photo service is busy or past its daily limit, so no photo waits and nothing is paid past the free use
