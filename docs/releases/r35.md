## Owners

- No HST is charged on the monthly, first and last month coverage fee or on registration; invoices already paid show what you actually paid
- A corrected government form replaces the earlier one in your documents
- You sign each government form your car needs on screen during onboarding: you see the official form filled in, check it and sign it, and Documents shows which forms are signed
- If a value on a form changes after you signed it, you get one To do and one reminder email to sign it again
- A debit that was missed comes off your next monthly statement, shown with its invoice number

## Staff tools

- One activity log for every renter and booking: emails, texts, live chat, calls with their summaries, notes, booking events, documents and website visits, newest first
- Small filters for each kind of activity, a search, and older entries a page at a time
- Reply, call and text right from the log, on the renter's page in every staff portal
- Admins and fleet change a value on a government form with a reason; each change is a new version and earlier versions stay downloadable
- The forms' mapping is previewed on a sample car, every save is kept and can be loaded again, and fleet can map the forms too
- Making a car's forms again keeps the values changed by hand unless you choose to discard them
- Each government form shows the owner's signature beside it (who signed, when and which version) and a print-ready form without any signature; a form changed after signing is marked and the owner is asked to sign again

## Website & search

- The investor presentation for 2026 is live in the investor portal as web slides that fit any screen
- Every investor page states the same terms as the presentation

## Reliability & security

- The daily email limit is shown as it is set, everywhere it is mentioned
- Government forms are only ever the official government PDF with its boxes filled; anything else gives the data sheet
- An owner's on-screen signature is a record kept beside the form with the date, IP address and device; it is never put on the official form
- No missed owner debit is left waiting for staff: the next statement recovers it
