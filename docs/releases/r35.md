## Renters

- If your credit card does not cover the car, our team can move your booking to the Protection Plan, on an offer or a booking you have paid, and back; you get an email with what changed and the new total, and your booking page shows it
- Any extra comes as a secure payment link, and you no longer send a card coverage document once you are on the plan
- An accident card for each booking with the steps after an accident and the coverage that booking uses, ready to print
- Report an accident from the phone in one form, with photos and videos, and our team alerted at once
- The km and fuel at the return compared with pickup on the return receipt, with any extra km or fuel charge
- Two accounts of the same person become one by themselves: sign in with either email, or with your phone number and a code
- One short note tells you your accounts are now one
- The accident card shows your own protection plan policy number and the plan's claim page when you bought the plan with the rental
- One printed card in every car with the steps after an accident and what to show for each coverage option; its QR code opens your own card on your phone with your booking's coverage and proof, the drivers and the rental agreement, and Report a problem
- Drivers named on a booking see the same card on their phone and can report an accident too
- Change your date of birth and name in your profile until your licence is verified; after that, ask for a correction and our team checks it against your licence
- Rented with us before? Book an available car instantly: sign and pay at once, no waiting for an offer
- Add an optional secondary contact we can call if we cannot reach you, on your profile or for one trip
- Driving for Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart or another platform: your booking page makes the documents they ask for (rental agreement copy, coverage letter, vehicle letter)
- New reward points for a photo with your review and for every rental after your first; every way to earn is on your Rewards page
- An extension costs what you already pay, averaged per day: your booking's total with its fees and HST, divided by its days, for each added day, with the price a day shown before you pay
- A longer stay never costs less than a shorter one, and an offer costs exactly what the booking form quotes for the same car, dates and extras
- The Protection Plan can be priced from a real quote for your dates and age, and is offered for cars within its coverage limit
- Every request and booking that is not complete starts with what we still need from you: each item, its state, the reason when something was not accepted, and one button
- Find your credit card in a list and see at once whether it covers your car: its value limit, the kinds of car it leaves out and the most days; if it does not, the page says why and offers OPCF 27 or the Protection Plan
- On your own auto policy (OPCF 27) you send the pink slip and the endorsement page before you pay
- Delivery is priced by distance, with the first kilometres included, shown in your estimate and your offer
- A fuel shortfall at the return is priced from the car's tank and the local pump price, plus a refuelling fee, and checked by our team before any charge

## Owners

- No HST is charged on the monthly, first and last month coverage fee or on registration; invoices already paid show what you actually paid
- One email and one text for what happens on your booking at the same moment, such as the confirmation, the receipt and the agreement together
- No pickup reminder right after the confirmation that already gives the date and time
- A documents reminder only for what you still have to send, and it names exactly that
- No new agreement version just because you paid
- A short grace after your return time before any overdue reminder, and none once you are on your way back
- One email a day listing every invoice we cancelled, instead of one email each
- A corrected government form replaces the earlier one in your documents
- You sign each government form your car needs on screen during onboarding: you see the official form filled in, check it and sign it, and Documents shows which forms are signed
- If a value on a form changes after you signed it, you get one To do and one reminder email to sign it again
- A debit that was missed comes off your next monthly statement, shown with its invoice number
- Set the lowest and highest daily rate your cars are rented at, for every car or one car: short-notice and booked-ahead pricing, promo codes, discounts and our offers stay between them, and only an Admin can make a recorded exception on one booking
- Statements cut off on the 7th: the payout on the 10th covers what your car earned through the 7th of that month, and each statement shows the dates it covers
- A rental that runs past the cutoff, and a long weekly or biweekly rental, is paid for the days inside each period, never twice and never left out
- Insurer-billed rentals and trips recorded after they happened count on your statement like any other rental
- An invoice deducted from payouts can start from a chosen payout date

## Reliability & security

- Security audit: a form or request sent to carmacars.ca from another website is refused before the site runs, for every page and action
- Every table, including the internal ones, has row-level security; no database function that runs with extra rights can be called by anyone it was not given to by name
- The list of database functions a visitor or a signed-in person can call is fixed in a test, so a new one cannot appear by accident
- The image resizer the site never uses is closed (it carries a critical Next.js advisory); upgrading Next.js is waiting for the owner's go-ahead
- A partner's feed or photo address can no longer redirect the server to an internal address
- Uploaded photos are checked by their bytes in one place for every upload
- 538 database indexes on the links between tables, so purges and the portals' lists no longer scan whole tables
- Engineering rules written down (docs/engineering-rules.md), with checks: a database change already applied can never be edited, no debug output or code-from-text in the app, no focused tests
- The same message is not sent twice to the same person for the same booking within 30 minutes; receipts, signed copies and staff messages always go
- The daily email limit counts every email service that is sending, and every page says plainly which ones are
- The live visitors map loads in under a second again on a busy site, and a part that cannot be read says so while the rest is drawn
- The daily email limit is shown as it is set, everywhere it is mentioned
- Government forms are only ever the official government PDF with its boxes filled; anything else gives the data sheet
- An owner's on-screen signature is a record kept beside the form with the date, IP address and device; it is never put on the official form
- No missed owner debit is left waiting for staff: the next statement recovers it
- The server check email names the failing scheduled job, its last error and whether its next run was ok, and only emails when a job keeps failing
- Showroom photos are made by the site itself when the photo service is busy or past its daily limit, so no photo waits and nothing is paid past the free use
- Search engines are told about changed pages in smaller batches, waiting longer after each server error, and the index sample counts pages the way Search Console does
- No daily email counter on the booking email box, Admin home, email log or alert banner; Settings > Usage shows the real daily capacity (all our sending services together) and, when a service is not sending, why
- Listing photos are sized and finished by the site itself; the photo service is asked only to cut the car out, and when it is busy the photo goes on the site from the original and is made again later
