## Owners

- Owner agreements: a new fleet lease for suppliers who lease many cars to us: a fixed monthly lease per car by tier, paid in arrears on the 10th with the owner payouts
- Each leased car's tier is set once when it is onboarded; part months are paid by the day, with the calculation shown on the statement
- Suppliers pay us no fees, and the platform never invoices or debits them; claim profit shares and write-off payouts follow their agreement
- Upkeep costs are billed to the party the agreement names, and come off the supplier's lease payments when they are the supplier's
- A Referral Override Agreement for owners who refer other owners, signed in the owner portal
- The standard owner agreement now covers onboarding costs taken from payouts and shareholders' own cars
- A new Agreements checklist in Admin shows each owner's required agreements, whether each is sent or signed, with one-click Send
- Open legal points are marked for the lawyer's review before the new agreements are final
