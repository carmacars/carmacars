## Renters

- Licence photos are read the moment they are uploaded: a blurry or cut-off photo is retaken straight away, and the licence details are filled in for the renter to confirm
- When the main driver's licence is not from Ontario, the renter adds a main driver with an Ontario licence in one step and stays on the booking as an extra driver

## Staff tools

- Each driver's licence shows what was read and one pass or flag line per check: expiry, class, age, name and date of birth, the Ontario number, front and back
- Staff still approve licences; an Admin setting can approve them automatically when every check passes (off by default)
- A booking whose main driver lacks an Ontario licence cannot go out until it is fixed; only an Admin can undo the change or let it go with a reason
