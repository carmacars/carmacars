# Release 31

Release 31 is one release with two parts (the planned r32 "Simple" was folded into it):

1. **Simple** (this branch, `r31`): fewer pages, tabs and filters. Change record: `docs/changes/2026-11-07-simple-portals.md`.
2. **Licence reader** (branch `r31-lic`, merged before the release): the D2 reader and the Ontario primary rule.

## Simple: what changed so far

- **Filters, one bar for every list** (`features/list-filters`): one search box across fields, one row of at most five
  chips for what needs action, a date range or a sort only where useful. No "More filters" panel, no "More" chip.
  Done on Bookings, Vehicles, Owners, Hiring and the staff To do queue. The other money and log lists were cut to five
  chips. Old query parameters still filter when a link carries them, **Clear** removes them, and the last choice is
  remembered on the device.
- **The booking page in six sections** for Admin and staff: Overview (the next step; the review and offer while it is
  arranged), Money, Drivers & documents, Car & handovers, Messages & history, Claims & extras. Old `?tab=` links open
  the section that holds them now.
- **The vehicle page in six tabs**: Rates and Fees are one tab (an old `?tab=fees` opens it); Owner & documents.
- **Owners**: a search and three action chips (terms to approve, invoices open, not invited yet).
- **Every other list** now uses the same bar (money, owner money, insurer invoices, partner ledger, emails, tolls,
  tasks, applications, marketing, staff money and messages, requests, game leads), each cut to five action chips.
  The three period pickers (reviews, hiring calls, the sales board) stay as they are: they are date choices.
- **Settings** were already one page with a search over every section (r27); kept.
- **Today homes** already exist for every role (Admin and staff To do, field My jobs, owner Home, renter Trips,
  investor Home); kept.
- Guides: three chapters updated (migration 0600, guide text only).

## Shared pages

- **One booking page for Admin and staff** (`features/booking-page`): `/admin/bookings/[number]` and
  `/staff/bookings/[number]` render the same component. One loader picks the door from the viewer (an Admin through
  admin_read, staff through va_read in the page's function, the shared ops_read as before) and reads only what the
  viewer's sections use. Every section and button shows by one table (`features/shared-pages/viewer.ts`,
  `bookingCan`): each staff function keeps exactly what its own copy showed (Fleet: no Money; Claims & extras for
  customer service only; refunds asked by Finance; schedule a field job for Fleet), an Admin keeps everything.
- **One vehicle page** (`features/vehicle-page`): the same component, loader and tabs at both addresses. An Admin
  gets the six tabs; Fleet Overview, Rates & fees, Listing, Maintenance and Documents; customer service Overview,
  Listing and Documents; Finance Maintenance. Old `#maintenance` links open the Maintenance tab.
- **One requests queue, Bookings page, Renters page and Add a car for an owner** (`features/requests-page`,
  `bookings-page`, `renters-page`, `add-owner-car-page`): the same page at both addresses, each role's access as
  before (staff still find renters and bookings with a logged search).
- **Fleet alert link fixed** (0620): "a listing waits for review" opened a 404; it now opens the car's Listing tab, and
  the links already sent are corrected.
- **One listing review** (`features/listing-page`): the same page; Retry, Unlist and the sample photos note stay with
  an Admin, Fleet keeps the link to the car.
- The staff addresses still work: they render the shared page (no redirect, so the staff layout keeps every button
  on the staff door with the function's limits). `STAFF_SHARED` lists them, with a unit test that both addresses
  render the shared component through the right door.
- The handover job, photo report and sign-now pages were already one shared component each.
- Checks: `shared-pages/viewer.test.ts` (what each function gets), `supabase/tests/99_shared_pages.sql` (what each
  function's door answers on these pages), `tests/e2e/shared-pages.spec.ts` (every role on the same booking in each
  section, the car and the listing, at 375 and 1440). Guides: admin `requests-offers-and-bookings`, va
  `shared-tools` (0600).
