## Staff tools

- Every list has one filter bar: one search box across fields and at most five chips for what needs action
- The booking page is grouped into six sections: Overview, Money, Drivers & documents, Car & handovers, Messages & history, Claims & extras
- The vehicle page has fewer tabs, with rates and fees together
- Admin and staff now share one booking page, vehicle page, listing review, requests queue, bookings list, renters list and Add a car page, so a fix lands for everyone at once while each role still sees only what it uses
- The alert for a listing waiting for review now opens the car's listing instead of a missing page
- Each driver's licence shows what was read and one pass or flag line per check: expiry, class, age, name and date of birth, the Ontario number, front and back
- Staff still approve licences; an Admin setting can approve them automatically when every check passes (off by default)
- A booking whose main driver lacks an Ontario licence cannot go out until it is fixed; only an Admin can undo the change or let it go with a reason
- A higher fee on a car can now be saved with its reason before the owner has signed the car's terms

## Renters

- Licence photos are read the moment they are uploaded: a blurry or cut-off photo is retaken straight away, and the licence details are filled in for the renter to confirm
- When the main driver's licence is not from Ontario, the renter adds a main driver with an Ontario licence in one step and stays on the booking as an extra driver
- Listing photos are framed the same way on every car: centred, filling the frame, with the wheels on the floor line

## Owners

- Owner agreements: a new fleet lease for suppliers who lease many cars to us: a fixed monthly lease per car by tier, paid in arrears on the 10th with the owner payouts
- Each leased car's tier is set once when it is onboarded; part months are paid by the day, with the calculation shown on the statement
- Suppliers pay us no fees, and the platform never invoices or debits them; claim profit shares and write-off payouts follow their agreement
- Upkeep costs are billed to the party the agreement names, and come off the supplier's lease payments when they are the supplier's
- A Referral Override Agreement for owners who refer other owners, signed in the owner portal
- The standard owner agreement now covers onboarding costs taken from payouts and shareholders' own cars
- A new Agreements checklist in Admin shows each owner's required agreements, whether each is sent or signed, with one-click Send
- Open legal points are marked for the lawyer's review before the new agreements are final

## Reliability & security

- Plate covering on listing photos finds the plate on the reworked photo, never on an older version
