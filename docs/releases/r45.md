## Staff tools

- A changed booking can be put back to any earlier version after a preview, without touching a payment
- Offers and rentals entered by staff show the renter's licence, address and drivers already on file
- Field shifts open by themselves ahead, with a shift for every handover and return
- A townhall that did not happen no longer counts anyone absent
- Staff agreements can be redone with the current terms, keeping the signed one on file

## Renters

- A signed-in renter's booking request fills itself from their profile and flags anything out of date

## For renters

- Signed in, a booking request fills itself from your profile: your name, date of birth, licence class and how we reach you, all editable
- A licence that has expired or an old address on file is flagged for you to update, and drivers you named before are one tap away

## For staff

- Picking a renter on Send an offer or Add a rental shows their licence, home address and drivers named before, with anything out of date flagged
- **Restore this version** puts a confirmed booking back to an earlier version's dates, car, rental charge and fees, after a preview, without touching a payment
- Field shifts are published by themselves, one shift every second day from 10:00 to 17:00, and every handover or return gets a shift to claim
- A townhall that did not happen counts nobody absent, and a cancelled one sends no reminders
- The guides and the academy cover the new flows of Releases 44 and 45

## For admins

- **Send a new agreement** redoes a staff agreement already sent or signed with the current terms; a signed one stays on file as superseded
- A To do lists the staff in Canada whose agreement still carries the old terms
- **This townhall did not happen** and **Cancel this townhall** on the Townhall page
- The shift template is a Program setting, and shifts open only forward from the latest one already published
