## Website & search

- The place pages cover Ontario only: every city, town and neighbourhood in Ontario keeps its pages; the other provinces, the United States and Mexico are switched off (kept, not deleted) and answer not found
- The pages of a whole province, territory or country that is off (Quebec, British Columbia, the United States, Mexico ...) answer not found and leave the /car-rental hub; the Canada page says we operate in Ontario
- Vehicle pages cover model years from 2005 on; older years have no page and are in no sitemap or list of years
- One page per service in each place and for each make, model and year: car hire, cars for hire and vehicle rental lead to the car rental page, auto body repair to collision repair, auto detailing to car detailing; each page lists the other names it is searched by ("Also searched as: ...")

## Staff tools

- Welcome tells a new staff member what their agreement is waiting for (home address, driver's licence number) with a link to each place, what only CarmaCars can set, and that it arrives within about 5 minutes once the profile is complete; filling in a missing value now makes the agreement at the next run instead of the next day
- Every form on the website, when it cannot be sent, lists what is missing or wrong near the send button, outlines each such field in red with a short message and moves the cursor to the first one
- Sales calls and texts every lead and request through the site again while customer numbers stay hidden; the first call asks for the salesperson's own mobile once and saves it without a texted code, and a call that cannot start says what to do
- Settings > Search: Ontario is the only region on; any region can be switched back on, and the place pages, sitemaps and the list of provinces follow at once
- Settings > Vehicle pages: the oldest model year with pages (2005), a setting
- The other names of a service send visitors to the service's page with a permanent redirect and are in no sitemap; different services (interior and exterior detailing, ceramic coating, window tint, PPF, financing, leasing, lease takeover, the replacement rental while a car is repaired, each mechanical service) keep their own pages
- Pages of the regions that are off and of older model years are answered at the edge, before the site renders anything
