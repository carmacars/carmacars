## Renters

- Each booking message is texted once, never twice
- The non-refundable discount only when booked a week or more ahead
- Renters can pay again after a declined or cancelled card
- A car's weekend rate is always above its daily rate
- A higher deposit on a debit or prepaid card
- The deposit hold placed on the morning of pickup
- A payment link paid in full confirms the booking

## Owners

- Owners can start on their own, and a car can be added before its VIN or plate
- Company statements tie owner invoices correctly

## Staff tools

- Admin keeps your place on the page
- Hiring call screens fit any screen; make, model and colour filters on the car list
- The handover essentials first
- The Live map of cars and bookings
- Send an offer as one flow, with charges following the choices made

## Website & search

- A phone number box for renters on car pages
