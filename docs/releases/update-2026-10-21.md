## Renters

- Show password, a simpler cookie banner and quieter site checks
- Sign in with a phone number

## Owners

- Listing a car with no roadblocks, proof of coverage, and smoother animations
- Owners can open an account before adding a car
- Owner signing that always completes, with the first and last month invoice optional

## Staff tools

- Sensible default settings for every new car
- Guided next steps on Admin's Today page for insurer confirmations and renter documents
- A maintenance and service tracker for every car
- The business phone line built in: calls and texts inside the platform
- Rentals already underway can be added by hand
- Marketplace booking emails matched to the right car automatically
- A usage guard for paid services and an assistant for staff
- Adding a car starts from its VIN, with the details filled in automatically

## Partners

- The investor deck, available in the investor portal

## Website & search

- Contact details captured from every form, so no enquiry is lost
- Discount games appear sooner, can be replayed and can be used in the booking form
- Live visitor signals: who is likely to book, why, and what to say to them
- Real car listings look as polished as the sample cars
- Pages for every make, model, year and trim, by place, colour and search term

## Reliability & security

- Security hardening
- Reliability improvements to website speed and storage
