## Staff tools

- Alerts, texts and urgent emails go out the moment something happens instead of on a timer
- A car coming back is recorded in one tap from To do, Bookings and the booking page, with the time it actually came back
- A cleaner menu with one search box that finds both pages and records
- Booking and vehicle pages show one main action per card, with the rest in a More menu
- Staff uploads open the photo library as well as the camera

## Renters

- The rental and fees are paid in one card charge with one receipt
- Renters choose between taking a photo and picking one from their phone when they upload a licence or proof of coverage
- The renter home links straight to uploading the licence and proof of coverage, and to the credit card coverage checker
- Request and offer emails link straight to the licence upload while it is missing

## Website & search

- Local area pages are kept ready to serve, so they load faster and cost far less to run
- The CarmaCars mark appears on public car photos, loading screens and documents
