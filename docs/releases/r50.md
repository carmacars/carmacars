## Owners

- Owners sign their agreement with CarmaCars before any car is handed over, guided step by step in their portal
- A car is approved, delivered or rented only once its owner has signed for it
- Long rentals show what each owner statement carried, and signed forms show the CarmaCars countersignature in its place

## Website & search

- Every car is shown the same way: nine studio views in one room, the same size and angle for every car, on the car page, the cards, the home page and the feeds; a car still being photographed shows one "Photos coming soon" studio photo
- Booking and paying keep working when card payments are off: renters pay by Interac e-Transfer instead

## Staff tools

- Staff see what each owner still has to sign and send a reminder in one tap
- A rental whose pickup was not recorded keeps its car off new offers until it is recorded
- Field staff choose **Whose money?** on every expense: **My own money (pay me back)** or **Company card/cash**. My shifts shows what is **Owed to you**.
- Finance is told of each expense, approves or declines it with the reason (the person is told), and pays back what is owed from **Reimbursements owed** on Payroll and Field spending.
- Studio photos are made again across the fleet: a photo step that keeps failing is flagged with its reason after three tries instead of blocking every other car
- Imported photos the sorter could not read are sorted again by themselves, so a car's outside photos become its cover
- A studio grid shows every car's nine views side by side, so a photo that does not match the others is easy to spot

## For renters

- Card payments now open the card processor's secure card window (Sign and pay, pay links, add-ons, gift cards). CarmaCars never sees the card number.
- For a rental longer than 7 days the deposit is charged and refunded after the rental instead of held; the pay page says so.
- While card payments are paused, Sign and pay shows how to pay by Interac e-Transfer (the email, your booking number as the message and the exact amounts) and you upload the screenshot; we confirm it and email you.
- Every car's photos now look the same: the same studio, the same angles, the car the same size in every photo

## For owners

- You sign your agreement first, filled in from your profile, before any car comes over. Your portal shows six steps (profile, agreement, cars, photos, approval, delivery) with one button for the next thing to do
- Each car you add goes on Schedule A of your agreement; you confirm new cars with one signature. Fleet suppliers can add cars from a list of VINs with each car's tier and plate
- A long rental shows the rental charge for all its days and what your statements carried so far
- A rental whose pickup was not recorded shows as out, not available
- Signed copies show the CarmaCars countersignature in its place; corrected copies are filed and you are told

## For admins

- Cards run on the new processor: set its keys as in `docs/ops/helcim-setup.md`. Until then cards are off and renters pay by e-Transfer.
- **Card payments** in Program settings: one switch (and the card processor's own answer, checked by itself) turns every card form into e-Transfer instructions with a screenshot upload; you are alerted once when the card processor stops taking payments
- While card payments are paused, Collect a card, card links and deposit holds are off; the Money tab says **Ask the renter to e-Transfer** with the message ready to copy
- The connector reads the new processor (its account, its payments, and records one a booking missed); `add_expense` and `list_expenses` know whose money paid.
- **Agreements before cars** on an owner's page and application: exactly what is missing and who it waits on, with **Send reminder**
- A car is approved, delivered or rented only once its owner signed; an approval made before goes through by itself when they sign
- **Change the method**: correct a car's Onboarding Method before its Vehicle Schedule is signed
- **Document check** (Owners): anything inconsistent on an owner's agreements, schedules or forms, by owner, with what fixes it
- A car whose pickup was not recorded takes no new offer until the handover, the return or a no-show is recorded
- The connector adds a car for an owner from its VIN, approves an owner's cars with their terms (a fleet supplier's car goes on the lease with its agreement and forms to sign), puts a car on the fleet lease, and reads and corrects owner agreements and documents
- Studio renders are made again across the fleet: a photo step that timed out or was refused three times is flagged on **Photo standard** with the reason instead of being tried for ever, so the other cars' renders go ahead
- Photos filed as "Other detail" (imports the sorter could not read) are sorted again by themselves, so a car's outside photos become its cover
- Connector: `photo_standard` shows a rendered cover as `studio`, and per car the detail photos still to sort
- Studio renders made before the new standard stay on the site while each is checked in place; only those the check refuses are made again, the most viewed and bookable cars first
- Only studio views that passed the consistency check are on the site; **Photo standard** > **Studio grid** shows each car's nine views side by side with why any was refused

## For staff

- Fleet: **Photo standard** > **Studio grid** shows every car's nine views side by side; the site shows only views marked Live
- Fleet and customer service see **Agreements before cars** on the owner page and can send the reminder
- Fleet gets a To do for every confirmed rental whose pickup was not recorded
- Field staff choose whose money paid each expense; what is owed to you is on My shifts and paid back on the payroll
