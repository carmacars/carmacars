## Website & search

- Every car page shows photos: a car whose own photos are all inside shots leads with a representative photo of its model, then its own photos

## Staff tools

- Studio photos are made again across the fleet: a photo step that keeps failing is flagged with its reason after three tries instead of blocking every other car
- Imported photos the sorter could not read are sorted again by themselves, so a car's outside photos become its cover

## For renters

- Every car page shows photos: a car whose own photos are all inside shots shows a representative photo of its model first, then its own photos

## For admins

- Studio renders are made again across the fleet: a photo step that timed out or was refused three times is flagged on **Photo standard** with the reason instead of being tried for ever, so the other cars' renders go ahead
- Photos filed as "Other detail" (imports the sorter could not read) are sorted again by themselves, so a car's outside photos become its cover
- Connector: `photo_standard` shows a rendered cover as `studio`, and per car the detail photos still to sort
