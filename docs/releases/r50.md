## Owners

- Owners sign their agreement with CarmaCars before any car is handed over, guided step by step in their portal
- A car is approved, delivered or rented only once its owner has signed for it
- Long rentals show what each owner statement carried, and signed forms show the CarmaCars countersignature in its place
- Owner onboarding is one guided pipeline per car: application, terms, agreements, documents, the ownership transfer, the new registration, the fleet policy, inspection and listing, with one next step at a time

## Website & search

- Our location is now 1940 Eglinton Ave E, Toronto, ON M1L 4R1, on the site, in search results and for delivery distances
- Every button, form and list answers a press on every page and portal: a tick and "Saved" or "Sent" on the button, a short shake on a field to fix, "Copied" on copy buttons, totals that roll when they change, rows that fold away, sheets you drag down to close on a phone, and the phone's haptics in the app
- The earnings calculator now counts the car's year: cars of about the same year when we have them, otherwise the model's average moved for an older or newer car
- In the CarmaCars app and the site added to a home screen, drag down from the top of any page to refresh it
- The discount game now shows to signed-in people too: a renter taps once and the code goes to their account (staff see a preview and get no code)
- Every page follows the owner's design rules with no exceptions: every move 300 ms or less with the kit's eases, soft see-through borders, big enough buttons and fields on phones, and copy buttons whose icon turns into a check
- Every car is shown the same way: nine studio views in one room, the same size and angle for every car, on the car page, the cards, the home page and the feeds; a car still being photographed shows one "Photos coming soon" studio photo
- Booking and paying keep working when card payments are off: renters pay by Interac e-Transfer instead
- Renters choose **Book instantly** (pay now, confirmed) or **Request to book** (no charge until we approve) on the car page and in the booking form

## Staff tools

- **Self-healing**: the site finds its own problems (site checks, errors, Search Console, scheduled jobs, stuck emails and texts, the server), applies the matching fix from its fix library, checks it worked and tells the Admins once only when it cannot fix it safely; Admins save what fixed a problem as a new fix in Site health > Self-healing
- Booking pages and other staff and Admin pages show every date and time in Toronto time on the server and in the browser alike, so they load without the "page error" flash; booking changes read the times as Toronto time from any browser; the Admin home loads its site health again; a staff or renter policy expiry that is not a real date is said before the upload instead of failing it
- The weekly townhall's Platform updates slide lists the week's releases and the staff bug reports fixed that week, each with its reporter named as a shout-out, and the count of bugs still open
- Admins add and replace the business documents investors see under Investors > Documents (business plan, financial forecast, pitch deck, feature decks, or a new section); every version is kept and investors are told
- Staff see what each owner still has to sign and send a reminder in one tap
- A rental whose pickup was not recorded keeps its car off new offers until it is recorded
- Field staff choose **Whose money?** on every expense: **My own money (pay me back)** or **Company card/cash**. My shifts shows what is **Owed to you**.
- Finance is told of each expense, approves or declines it with the reason (the person is told), and pays back what is owed from **Reimbursements owed** on Payroll and Field spending.
- Studio photos are made again across the fleet: a photo step that keeps failing is flagged with its reason after three tries instead of blocking every other car
- Imported photos the sorter could not read are sorted again by themselves, so a car's outside photos become its cover
- A studio grid shows every car's nine views side by side, so a photo that does not match the others is easy to spot
- The Academy shows pictures in every section: real screens with numbered pins, good and bad example photos, flow diagrams and do and do not pairs; and about one quiz question in three has a funny wrong answer written for that very question
- **Guide me** in every staff portal: an optional coach that walks you through a real job step by step while you do it, on the live screens (send an offer, answer a request, record a payment, a pickup, a return and deposit review, approve an owner's car, a field expense)
- **Check-in visits**: long rentals get a free monthly check-in visit; staff work one queue (booked, due, overdue, done) and a short checklist with photos, the odometer, damage, tires, fluids and warning lights. What needs looking after becomes a To do, damage goes to the Admins, and the reading feeds the car's maintenance

## For renters

- **Guide me**, at the top of your account and on the sign-up and booking pages: pick what you want to do (create your account, book a car, add your licence, sign and pay, pay by e-Transfer, pickup day, extend, return, your deposit, rewards) and a small card shows the next step. It is optional and never in the way.

- On the car page and in the booking form you choose: **Request to book** (no charge; we check and send you a pay link or an offer) or **Book instantly** (Gold Rewards members: pay now and the car is confirmed). With card payments paused, Book instantly is paid by Interac e-Transfer.
- Card payments now open the card processor's secure card window (Sign and pay, pay links, add-ons, gift cards). CarmaCars never sees the card number.
- For a rental longer than 7 days the deposit is charged and refunded after the rental instead of held; the pay page says so.
- While card payments are paused, Sign and pay shows how to pay by Interac e-Transfer (the email, your booking number as the message and the exact amounts) and you upload the screenshot; we confirm it and email you.
- Every car's photos now look the same: the same studio, the same angles, the car the same size in every photo
- On a rental of about a month or longer, your trips page shows **Next check-in visit: around** a date: a quick, free visit to keep the car in top shape and catch maintenance early. Nothing to pay. A few days before, we remind you; **Pick a time** at our office or **By request**.
- **Returning the car is never blocked**: documents still being checked, the deposit, the agreement or money owed never stop a return, even when your pickup was never recorded (it is then recorded at your booked time). A licence approved on an earlier booking counts on every booking until it expires, and your booking asks only for what is really still missing

## For owners

- **What you could earn** now depends on your car's year as well as its make and model: an older car is estimated lower, a newer one higher
- **Guide me**, at the top of the owner portal and on the application page: step by step through applying, your profile, signing, adding a car, statements and payouts, invoices and documents

- **Your next step**, on Home and each car's page: every step of your car's onboarding (Transfer, Co-Registrant or Lease), the one thing to do now with its button, what to bring, where to go (the ServiceOntario centre finder) and how long it takes. You are emailed and texted when a step is yours
- Upload your licence, the lender's letter, corporate documents, the UVIP, the safety certificate and the new ownership permit on the car's page; book your ServiceOntario visit and press Transfer done after it
- Need a corporation for the lease route? **Incorporate now** opens our online incorporation partner

- You sign your agreement first, filled in from your profile, before any car comes over. Your portal shows six steps (profile, agreement, cars, photos, approval, delivery) with one button for the next thing to do
- Each car you add goes on Schedule A of your agreement; you confirm new cars with one signature. Fleet suppliers can add cars from a list of VINs with each car's tier and plate
- A long rental shows the rental charge for all its days and what your statements carried so far
- A rental whose pickup was not recorded shows as out, not available
- Signed copies show the CarmaCars countersignature in its place; corrected copies are filed and you are told
- Your car page lists the monthly check-in visits of long rentals: the date, the kilometres, the tire tread and the car's condition as we found it

## For admins

- **Resignations**: staff resign in their portal; you and Finance get an alert and an Offboarding To do (final pay, keys and equipment, jobs and shifts to reassign, what to hand back). Staff and roles shows **Resigning** with the last day and the reason; change the last day, withdraw it, end access now, or **Record a resignation** for someone who resigned by email. Access ends by itself after the last day; `list_staff` shows it
- **Calls inside the site, on our own server**: the weekly townhall and video interviews no longer need an outside link. You present the townhall: your slide is everyone's, and People shows who is in, who left and who has not joined; attendance is recorded by itself. Interview candidates join from the link in their email (it works in one browser only) and you see their details, the rubric and **No show** / **Not hired** beside the video. If the call server is ever down, **Switch to the outside link** on the Townhall page.
- **Calendar invites go out by themselves**: every staff member and Admin has the weekly townhall in their calendar (sent to the whole team now, then to each new hire); changing the day or time sends the update, a townhall you cancel ahead is taken off everyone's calendar. Interview candidates get the call in their calendar when they book, moved when they move it, removed when it is cancelled.
- Renters choose **Book instantly** or **Request to book**; each booking records the choice. A Book instantly that could not go through lands in your requests with the chip **Asked to book instantly** and the reason: answer it first.
- **Report a bug** works while a pop-up, drawer or sheet is open: the button shows at the bottom left, and the report's picture and details show the pop-up.
- Cards run on the new processor: set its keys as in `docs/ops/helcim-setup.md`. Until then cards are off and renters pay by e-Transfer.
- **Card payments** in Program settings: one switch (and the card processor's own answer, checked by itself) turns every card form into e-Transfer instructions with a screenshot upload; you are alerted once when the card processor stops taking payments
- While card payments are paused, Collect a card, card links and deposit holds are off; the Money tab says **Ask the renter to e-Transfer** with the message ready to copy
- The connector reads the new processor (its account, its payments, and records one a booking missed); `add_expense` and `list_expenses` know whose money paid.
- **Agreements before cars** on an owner's page and application: exactly what is missing and who it waits on, with **Send reminder**
- A car is approved, delivered or rented only once its owner signed; an approval made before goes through by itself when they sign
- **Change the method**: correct a car's Onboarding Method before its Vehicle Schedule is signed
- **Document check** (Owners): anything inconsistent on an owner's agreements, schedules or forms, by owner, with what fixes it
- A car whose pickup was not recorded takes no new offer until the handover, the return or a no-show is recorded
- The connector adds a car for an owner from its VIN, approves an owner's cars with their terms (a fleet supplier's car goes on the lease with its agreement and forms to sign), puts a car on the fleet lease, and reads and corrects owner agreements and documents
- The connector runs the weekly townhall: `townhall_status` (next meeting, call link, this week's deck, which staff are on it and which are missing, reminders, field-call slots), `townhall_set_call_url` and `townhall_rebuild_deck` (rebuilds this week's deck from live data and keeps the notes), each change previewed, then confirmed.
- The townhall deck adds, from live data: **Website visitors** (a day this week against last week, and the 4-week trend), the **Top incident of the week** (picked automatically from tracker alerts, incidents, claims, damage and late returns; **Choose another** to pick or describe it yourself), **Disputes and chargebacks** (this week and the month to date, open, won and lost; the amounts on an Admin-only slide), **Birthdays** (this week and next), **KPIs hit this week** (a line for every active staff member), and the cars added and removed by name on the fleet slide. Still 30 minutes. Add a staff member's birthday from the staff list.
- Studio renders are made again across the fleet: a photo step that timed out or was refused three times is flagged on **Photo standard** with the reason instead of being tried for ever, so the other cars' renders go ahead
- Photos filed as "Other detail" (imports the sorter could not read) are sorted again by themselves, so a car's outside photos become its cover
- Connector: `site_errors` (the site's server and browser errors by page and message), `site_health` (site checks and data health problems) and `search_console_issues` (pages Google sees with 5xx, 404 and other errors, sitemap problems), all read only; the server's own errors are now recorded too
- Connector: `photo_standard` shows a rendered cover as `studio`, and per car the detail photos still to sort
- Connector, for dispute evidence (read only): `list_comms` (every call, text, chat and email of a renter or booking, card and ID numbers taken out), `get_call_recording` (transcript and a 10-minute audio link), `get_id_check` (ID checks with licence and selfie links), `get_booking_invoice` (base rate, add-ons, HST), full chat threads in `list_chats`, and signed links in `get_agreement` (with signer IP and time), `list_booking_photos` and `photo_standard`; recording and ID access is in the audit log; `stripe_find_payments` and `stripe_booking` name the payer (receipt, billing and Customer email, name and phone, card fingerprint) and `matched_renter` with candidate bookings and their open amounts
- Studio renders made before the new standard stay on the site while each is checked in place; only those the check refuses are made again, the most viewed and bookable cars first
- Only studio views that passed the consistency check are on the site; **Photo standard** > **Studio grid** shows each car's nine views side by side with why any was refused
- **Academy**: Guide me walkthroughs by staff member (started, completed, stopped) and the step people stop at most in each workflow
- **Check-in visits** (Cars): long rentals (28 days or more, long-term weekly, rideshare drivers) get a free monthly visit, on by default. Program settings: on or off, the length, the days between visits (30), the reminder lead (5 days), the office slots. Each booking has its own switch on its booking page; visits show on the booking timeline and the owner's car page.

- **Onboarding steps** on the owner page: each car's step and whose turn it is; **Advance this step** or **Override a step** (kept in the audit log). **Owner pipeline** (Marketing) shows every car by step
- **Settings > Incorporation referral**: paste the affiliate link (off until then), see who was referred and record the commission
