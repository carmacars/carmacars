## Reliability & security

- Four pages made sturdier: a time written the same by the server and every browser, a leads page that still opens when one of its lists cannot load, the pay page under the browser's own translation, and a page left open across an update that reloads by itself
- Every pop-up and the portals' top row fit a phone at 375 and 320 pixels wide, checked by a test

## Staff tools

- An Admin shows a lead's full phone number to its salesperson, lead by lead, and hides it again; every other lead stays hidden
- Sales asks for a lead's number in one tap, and the request reaches an Admin as a To do
- The To do to send staff in Canada a new agreement leaves out test accounts

## For admins

- **Show the phone to** on a lead (its page or its card) shows its full number to the lead's salesperson, or to a Sales person you choose, with an optional reason kept in the audit log
- **Hide it again** hides it; reassigning the lead hides it by itself, so the new salesperson needs it shown again
- A salesperson's ask arrives as a To do with **Show the phone**
- The same buttons are on every Admin lead view, the expired offers on Renters > Leads included
- On a phone, What's new, Report a bug and the other buttons of the top row keep their words inside the button, and every pop-up fits the screen
- Admin > Field jobs no longer fails to load
- The To do to send staff in Canada a new agreement lists real staff only, never a test account

## For staff

- Sales: **Ask an Admin to show the number** on a lead whose number is hidden; once shown, that lead says **Number shown by an Admin** and has the full number with a call link and **Copy**
- An alert tells you when an Admin shows you a number, and opens the lead
- The number is shown for that lead only, and hidden again if the lead goes to another salesperson
- On a phone, What's new, Report a bug and every pop-up fit the screen; long words and links wrap
- Game leads no longer fails to load

## For renters

- After the site is updated, a page left open reloads once by itself instead of showing an error
- The pay page works with Chrome's translation on
