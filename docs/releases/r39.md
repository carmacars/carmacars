## Staff tools

- Outside listing photos stand in a real photographed dealership room when one is stored: the car about 70% of the width, its wheels on the floor, a soft contact shadow and a faint reflection, its light matched to the room; no sign or lettering on a real wall
- The rooms are real photos licensed for commercial use (CC0, public domain or CC BY), fetched once from Wikimedia Commons with their author, licence and source kept; until one is stored the drawn showroom stays
- A cut-out that is not clean enough (pieces, a blurry or ragged edge) is never placed in a room: the real photo is used, its plate covered as usual, and the photo says why
- Program settings > Listings: the photographed rooms switch (on), the car's width in the room (0.70) and the cut-out quality floor (0.60)

## Reliability & security

- After every deploy the site checks itself: a key page or the sign-in page failing (5xx, Error 1101) fails the deploy; listed cars on the stand-in while they have a real photo, stand-in lettering that overflows, covers made from a poor cut-out, empty sitemaps and pages wrongly marked noindex send the Admins an alert (Site self-check) and are kept as a report
