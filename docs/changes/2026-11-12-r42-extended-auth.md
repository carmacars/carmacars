# Deposit holds last about 30 days: Stripe Extended Authorization is on (Release 42: 2650, 2651)

Branch `r42-extauth`. Migrations `2650_r42_extended_auth.sql` (the setting) and `2651_r42_extended_auth_guides.sql` (the
guide chapters, generated by `supabase/seed/2651_gen_guides.mjs`). SCHEMA_MARKER 2651. No table, column or function added,
so the database types are unchanged. DB test `supabase/tests/99_r42_extended_auth.sql` (and the two assertions of
`99_r28_operations.sql` that said the setting starts off); unit tests in `web/src/integrations/payments/stripe.test.ts` and
`web/src/server/pay-card.test.ts`.

Stripe approved Extended Authorization on the live account: a card hold can last up to about 30 days on Visa, Mastercard,
Amex and Discover cards (not Interac). On 2026-10-30 the account was not eligible and the request was taken out
(`2026-10-30-card-hold-no-extended-authorization.md`). It is back, on deposit holds only.

| Part | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Setting | Program settings, **Extended deposit holds (up to about 30 days)** (`program_settings.deposit_extended_holds`, 0541) is on for every dated row, and on by default for a new install |
| Pay link and staff device | `createStaffIntent` takes `extendedHold` and, for a hold (manual capture), sends `payment_method_options[card][request_extended_authorization]=if_available` under the idempotency key `<key>:ext`. Passed, from `server_deposit_extended_holds`, by Collect a card > The deposit hold (`actions/offers.ts`), the deposit links sent by themselves (`deposit-guard.ts`), and the hold placed again on the same link after a debit or prepaid card (`paylink.ts`, both places) |
| Not on a charge | A payment, the balance, an extension and charges after the rental never pass it, and the adapter ignores it when the capture is not manual. The booking total (`createBookingIntent`) does not ask for it: it is captured when the booking is confirmed (the comment says so) |
| Refused when the intent is made | The same intent is made again without the option (`extendedRefused`, the pattern of `chargeOffSession`); any other error is thrown as before |
| Refused when the renter confirms | The refusal of 2026-10-30 came as the card was confirmed in the browser. For a hold, the pay page hands Stripe's message to `payLinkStandardHold`; only when it is that refusal (`extendedRefusedText`), the link is open, it is a hold and the intent is not confirmed, the same intent is updated to `request_extended_authorization=never` (`standardHold`) and the form confirms once more. Same intent and client secret, no second hold, and an ordinary decline changes nothing |
| Already in place | The saved card on the pickup day and Re-authorize (0541) and the card reader (r35) already asked; they now do because the setting is on. The hold's real expiry is Stripe's `capture_before`, recorded on the deposit, and a hold is still re-authorized before it lapses |
| Pay page | A deposit hold's page says "A hold lasts about 7 days, or up to about 30 days on most credit cards" |
| Settings help | The setting's help text says it is on and what it does |

Not changed: `card-tools.ts` has no `createStaffIntent`; its holds are on the reader, which asks already.

## Guides

- `admin/settings`: Extended deposit holds is on, and what it covers
