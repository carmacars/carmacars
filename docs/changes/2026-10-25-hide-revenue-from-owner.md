# Hide a booking's revenue from the owner, and owner deductions on a booking, October 25, 2026

The owner: "I just want to make sure that you implemented the thing where we can hide the revenue from a booking from a car
owner and they don't see it right away". It did not exist. Now staff can hide one booking's money from the car's owner until
it is revealed, while the owner's statement always carries every booking at its true amounts (payouts are never affected),
and they can record a documented owner deduction on a booking that the owner always sees, itemized, once the booking is
visible. Nothing here shows an owner a lower amount than their statement pays.

## 1. What the owner sees of a booking

`bookings.owner_visibility`:

| Value | The owner sees |
| --- | --- |
| `shown` | The booking and its amounts (as before) |
| `until_return` | Booked, with the dates and status, no amount, until the car is returned (or the booking is cancelled) |
| `until_statement` | Booked, with the dates and status, no amount, until the monthly owner statement covering it is approved |
| `never` | Only that the car is unavailable for those dates: no booking number, no amount, no renter. Only for a booking with no owner revenue (an insurance placeholder, a shareholder's or the owner's own use at no charge); refused otherwise. If such a booking later carries owner revenue it is treated as hidden until the statement |

A new booking takes the Program setting `owner_revenue_visibility` (Admin > Settings > Program, Owners and fleet: "Owners
see a booking's revenue": right away (default), after the return, when the statement is approved). The owner's own booking is
always shown. Staff override it per booking: an Admin, customer service or fleet (`app.may_set_owner_visibility`), on Add a
rental (section 6, The car's owner; the Placeholder preset picks Not shown), on Send an offer, and on the booking page (the
card What the owner sees above the tabs: **Hide from the owner**, **Show to the owner**, **Change how it is hidden**, an
optional reason). Every change is in `booking_owner_visibility_log` (append-only, audited) and the booking history
(`owner_visibility`). A booking on an approved statement cannot be hidden again.

Reveal: by staff, by the return (`until_return`), and automatically when the owner statement covering the booking is approved
(`app.approve_owner_statement` wrapped: every hidden booking with a line on it is set to `shown`, logged as the statement's).

## 2. Enforced in the database

`app.booking_owner_view` (full | booked | unavailable) decides, and every owner read uses it:

- `owner_portal_bookings` (a new column `owner_view`; the amount null unless full; no number and no renter when unavailable).
- The `owner_bookings` view (same rule, plus the split columns), and the owners' row policies on `booking_splits` and
  `owner_deductions`.
- The month to date (0151): the provisional build runs with the hidden bookings' splits set aside inside its own savepoint
  (`app.compute_booking_split` wrapped, `app.owner_mtd_hide`), so its totals, lines and payout carry only what the owner may
  see; `held_back` counts the finished rentals still to come; an unavailable rental under way is listed without its number.
  Nothing is kept, as before.
- Emails: `owner_new_booking` and `owner_booking_changed` get `show_amounts` (the rental charge and the shares are left out
  while hidden); their preheaders (also the in-app notice) carry no amount now. The owner booking notices of an unavailable
  booking are not sent (`app_events` marked processed, "not shown to the owner (0156)"). The texts never carried amounts.
- The owner portal shows a hidden booking as **Booked** with its dates ("Amounts on your statement once approved") in Bookings,
  the car page and Home's Coming up; it counts nowhere in the figures (rental charges, days, bookings), and the months panel
  says how many are still to come. An unavailable booking reads **Unavailable**; the car's live status says Unavailable while
  one covers now. The calendar stays truthful.

The statement is never changed by visibility: `owner_portal_statement` and the PDF show every booking. Investor views are
unaffected.

## 3. Documented owner deductions on a booking

On the booking page's Money tab (card Owner deductions on this booking) and on Add a rental (The car's owner, Owner deductions):

- **Add a booking expense**: cleaning, repair, fuel, platform fee or other; the amount; the reason the owner reads (5
  characters or more); a receipt number or link. It comes off before Owner Revenue (`booking_splits.owner_expense_cents`; the
  split's check now reads rental + mileage - kickbacks - partner markup - expenses). **Remove** voids it with a reason.
- **Set the Revenue Share for this booking** (the commission on a partner's car): within the car's signed terms as stored on
  the booking (0135); above them only an Admin, with "Above the signed terms" and the reason. **Back to the car's terms** undoes
  it (`booking_owner_shares`).

Who: an Admin, or customer service and sales while Program settings let them change fees (`app.may_edit_fees`). Not on a
booking already on an approved statement (an owner cost on the owner's account instead). Each is audited and in the booking
history (`owner_expense`, `owner_share`). The split is worked out again at once when the booking has one not paid out.

The owner sees them: the statement's rental line has the expense amount, the booking's share, and a description listing each
expense with its reason (the owner portal statement and the PDF show them); the owner's booking row lists each expense and
the share once the booking is visible.
