# Release 5 of October 24: offers, handover and card coverage together

Release 5 is origin/offers (0129, `2026-10-24-offers-and-rentals.md`), with origin/handover (0130, check-out and check-in by
staff, signing on the spot, the licence with the request, return charges) and origin/cardcover (0131, which credit cards
include rental car coverage) merged in that order. Schema marker `0131`.

## How the three work together

- **The wrappers compose.** `app.door` is 0131's, calling 0130's (`door_pre0131`), calling 0129's (`door_pre0130`), calling the
  older chain (`door_pre0129`); each handles its own names and passes the rest on. `app.site_setup_problems` is wrapped by
  0131 only. 0129's `app.approve_offer` is 0016's, gated by 0130 (`approve_offer_pre0130`): an offer staff send without a
  request needs the renter's licence (front, back, selfie, expiry) before it is accepted, like any offer. The renter is
  asked for it on the offer page (0130); staff can also photograph it on the spot. A rental added by hand is not gated.
- **The whole chain.** Staff send an offer (0129) → the renter uploads the licence and accepts (0130's gate) → pays online,
  by a pay link, or by e-Transfer recorded by part (0129) → signs online or on the spot (0130) → check-out by any staff
  with the photo report, the deposit taken by a card hold on the staff phone, in cash or by e-Transfer (0129, 0130) →
  check-in with the return charges (0130) → the statement and the deposit settlement, a cash or e-Transfer deposit given
  back the same way (0129).
- **The deposit at check-out (field).** A started pickup job whose deposit is not taken shows the card Deposit: **Place the
  deposit hold on this phone** opens the pay page on the phone for the renter (Stripe's form; field staff never take cash
  or type a card number), with **Back to the job** once it is placed. A rental longer than the hold limit shows **Save the
  card (long rental)**; Finance charges the deposit to it. The ready check reads the deposit as taken.

## Fixes found by running the three together

- **Sales at check-out and check-in.** 0130 lets any staff with the handover permission check a car out or in, but
  `app.va_booking_0031` kept Sales to requests and offers, so Sales could not open the booking. 0130 now also opens to
  Sales (with the handover permission) a confirmed booking picking up by tomorrow, a rental on rent returning by tomorrow,
  or a booking with a field job of theirs; other confirmed bookings stay closed (suite 31, suite 82 section 7).
- **Location for the photo report.** The Permissions-Policy header said `geolocation=()`, which blocked 0130's photo
  locations; it is now `geolocation=(self)` (`web/src/lib/security/csp.ts`, `web/scripts/serve.mjs`).
- **The check-out completion screen.** Completing a check-out or check-in from the booking page re-renders the page (the
  action revalidates), which swapped the checklist and its "Checked out" screen for a bare "Done" line. The finished job
  now shows the same completion screen, with See the photo report (`web/src/features/handover/HandoverRun.tsx`).
- **The renter's photo report.** An old email-link redirect (`/account/bookings/:n/condition-report` to the booking)
  ran before 0130's page of that address, so the report from Documents and the "Returned" email opened the booking
  instead. The redirect is removed (`web/next.config.ts`).
- **0130's end-to-end spec.** The late check-in sets the allowance per day with the total (the check
  `km_allowance_total = km_allowance_per_day × billable_days`; the sample cars allow 200 km a day); the waiver reason is
  typed in the open dialog (every charge has one); the fuel step fills a new value (the same value fires no change), and the profile address
  test puts the renter on file first (the contact form shows only for a renter on file).
