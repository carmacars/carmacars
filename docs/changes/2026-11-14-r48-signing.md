# Signing never wrongly refuses a renter, a returning renter signs in one tap, and a card in a slightly different name never blocks a booking (Release 48: 2910, 2911, 2912)

Migrations `2910_r48_signature_match.sql`, `2911_r48_signing_saved.sql`, `2912_r48_card_name.sql`, `2913_r48_signing_guides.sql`.

Renters were told "The signature does not match the one drawn: sign again". Root causes:

- The signature box stored the drawing at the end of every stroke, and each stored drawing became the signing session's. A renter who drew two or three strokes and pressed Sign at once sent an earlier stroke while the last one was being stored; the database compared the two exactly and refused (0154, 0193).
- The same renter signing on a phone and a computer within two hours shares one session: the drawing of the other device made the first one fail the same way.
- A typed name was compared with the legal name ignoring only case and spaces: accents, hyphens, a middle name, the order of the names or an initial refused a real person.

Bookings also broke on "The card must be in the renter's name": the card form (`PayForm.tsx`) refused the name before paying, the payment step (`payment.ts`) compared the cardholder name with the legal name ignoring only case and spaces and voided the card hold, and credit card coverage was refused the same way (0071, `features/coverage/card.ts`).

What changed:

- The signature recorded is always the session's own latest drawing: any drawing of the same session (an earlier stroke, the other device) or none signs with it. A drawing from another signing is still refused, with "Reload the page and sign once more". The signer is found from the signed-in renter or the driver's link, never from the page.
- The signature box stores the drawing once the renter stops drawing, and the Sign button waits for it ("Saving your signature...").
- Names are compared the way people write them (`app.person_names_match`, `web/src/features/signing/names.ts`): case, accents, apostrophes, hyphens, extra spaces, middle names, the order of the names and an initial for the first name do not matter. With the signing session on (Program settings, the default), a renter's typed name that still differs is accepted and staff get the To do **Check a typed signature** (without the session the name is all there is, so it is still refused).
- A renter's drawn signature and initials are saved on their profile when they sign. Next time the signing page offers **Initial all with your saved initials** and **Sign with your saved signature**: one tap each, recorded in the certificate as steps marked saved. Only for the signed-in renter's own booking, never for an additional driver and never on a staff member's device.
- "Complete your details" is filled in with what the renter already gave us (`public.renter_prefill`): date of birth, home address, mobile, licence, and the insurer and policy or the card of their last booking. The renter checks it and saves; answers are saved back to the profile. Read on the server for the renter's own booking only; nothing goes in a link.
- The card's name is compared tolerantly (`app.card_name_matches`: the rules above plus a shortened first name, "Alex" for "Alexander"). A card that still differs never blocks: the payment goes through, card coverage is accepted, and staff get the To do **Check the name on the card**. An Admin, or staff who verify bookings, press **Accept this card** with a short reason (logged on the booking's history; the To do closes). Program setting **Card in the renter's name** (on by default) turns the rule off entirely.
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r48_signing.sql` (names, the stroke race, a foreign drawing, typed names, the saved signature and initials, the prefill and who may read it, the card name, Accept this card, the setting), `web/src/features/signing/names.test.ts`, `renter-details.test.ts` (prefill), `coverage/card.test.ts`, `50_coverage_proof.sql` and `tests/e2e/portal.spec.ts` updated (a card in another name is no longer refused), `tests/e2e/returning-renter-sign.spec.ts` (a returning renter initials and signs with one tap each).

## Guides

Migration `2913_r48_signing_guides.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/2913_gen_guides.mjs`) publishes:

- `renter/sign-and-pay`: a returning renter presses **Initial all with your saved initials** and **Sign with your saved signature**; the details form shows what they gave last time to check and save.
- `va/customer-service`: the name on the card. A To do **Check the name on the card** or **Check a typed signature** means the booking went ahead; check the card and the licence, then press **Accept this card** with a short reason. An Admin can turn the rule off in Program settings, **Card in the renter's name** (the setting carries its own hint).
