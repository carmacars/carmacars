# Insurer rentals at the desk: documents, adjuster, signing, editing the agreement (migration 0183)

Feedback from the desk (Mahad, October 28) on an insurer-billed rental:

- **Upload a renter's documents.** Admin > booking > Renter documents > Upload a document (and on the assistant's booking
  page): the licence front or back (per driver), the insurance policy or pink card, an insurer's letter, or another
  document, as a photo or a PDF. A policy or pink card is linked to the booking's coverage record, made when there is
  none: nothing is refused for a missing coverage option. Review it as before with Review documents.
- **Insurance claim and adjuster, at any status.** A new card on the booking's overview: bill to, the insurer, the claim
  number, the adjuster's name, phone and email, the policy, the authorized rate, days and date. It works on a booking
  already confirmed or on the road. Switching who pays prices the booking again; each save is in the audit log.
- **Signing without confirming the account.** A renter signs the rental agreement (and an amendment) without first
  confirming their email or phone, and uploads documents without it too. The signature still needs the one-time code,
  the initials, the consents and the drawn signature. Accepting an offer and paying still wait for the confirmation.
- **Edit the agreement.** The Agreement card has Edit the agreement: every printed field with its value (renter, each
  driver's licence, the car, the coverage, the insurer claim), not only what is missing. Only what changed is saved; a
  material change after signing makes a new version to sign, as before. The renter's legal name is an Admin's correction.
