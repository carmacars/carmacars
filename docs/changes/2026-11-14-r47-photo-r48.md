# Every view of every car generated from its photos: photo standard r48.1 (Release 47: 2900 to 2902)

Migrations `2900_r47_photo_r48.sql`, `2901_r47_photo_r48_guides.sql`, `2902_r47_photo_r48_academy.sql`. Web: `features/listing/r48.ts`, `worker.ts` (`makeR48`), `PhotoStandard.tsx`, `ShowroomPlates.tsx`, `scripts/r48-references.mjs`.

The owner: the showroom behind the cars still looked fake, and every car should get every standard angle generated by Gemini from its real photos, the sample photos and the background, with each angle described.

- **Generated set.** Every car gets nine views (front three-quarter first, front, side profile, rear three-quarter, rear, dashboard, front seats, rear seats, trunk), each a `vehicle_photos` row with `r48` and `ai_generated`, made by job `photo_r45` in mode `r48`.
- **References.** Up to 6 of the car's real photos (the same angle first). With fewer than 3, or none of that side, its newest check-in, check-out and field job photos (`condition_report_photos`) fill the list: read by the worker only, never a listing photo, never on the site. Then the composition guide of the angle for the body type (the sample cars' drawing, camera position only), the stock photo of the model, and for an outside view the background plate of its family. Each angle has written framing (camera height, lens look, crop, centring, 45 degrees for a three-quarter). Plates blurred, no text, no logo added.
- **Background plates.** `listing_r48_plates` (front, side, rear): made once by Gemini with real-photography words, used only when a second Gemini look says it looks like a real photo with no text, logo, car or person (`scripts/r48-references.mjs`, or the worker on first need). An Admin replaces them with a real showroom photo in Program settings, **Showroom background plates**.
- **The check.** A second Gemini look compares the view with the real photos (colour, model, wheels, parts, warping, render look) and with the framing. Refused: made again twice, told the reasons; then flagged and kept off the site, and the car's real photo of that angle stays on the site (2890's rule). No real photo's `is_public` is ever changed.
- **Gallery.** A passed r48 view of an angle is shown in place of the car's other photos of that angle; the r48 front three-quarter is the cover. Real photos stay in the record and the Admin view.
- **The run.** `app.job_photo_r48` every 5 minutes (`listing_photo_r48_auto`, on): the front three-quarter of every car first, newest listed first, then the other angles, within the Gemini daily limit less the reserve (2000 a day by default: about 900 views plus retries fits in about a day; not raised). The r45 fleet remake (`listing_photo_r45_auto`) is off; new uploads still go through it.
- **Photo standard card.** Each car's generated set (made of nine, being made, flagged, from check-in photos); Redo this car now and Redo every car make the generated views again too.
- Tests: `web/src/features/listing/r48.test.ts`, `supabase/tests/99_r47_photo_r48.sql`.
