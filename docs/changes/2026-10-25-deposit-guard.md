The owner, October 25, 2026: "tickets and tolls may take up to seven days to come in. And if a car is damaged, then I have
to keep the entire deposit. Just make sure there's no situation where we get screwed out of money. The deposit is not being
released automatically, but instead it is very important to allow us to bill tickets tolls gas late return additional usage
and damages."

## 1. The audit

Every way a deposit can be given back or lapse, and every way money owed is billed, per deposit method, before this change.

### How a deposit was returned or lapsed

| Path | Before | Gap | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Settle the deposit (0020 `app.settle_deposit`, the screen of 0032) | A person presses Settle; Finance within the single limit, an Admin above it or with a hold-back | Nothing warned inside the toll window; an open incident, claim, unmatched toll or dispute did not stop a release | The window warning and the pre-filled hold-back; "Release now anyway" recorded; what is open blocks any release unless an Admin gives the reason (`deposit_overrides`) |
| A toll, ticket or damage found later charged (0021 `app.charge_after_rental`, from `charge_toll_ticket` and `bill_damage`) | Called `app.settle_deposit` with an empty plan | **Gap a: it released the rest of a deposit not settled yet, by itself** | "Apply only": the charge comes from the deposit, the rest stays held with its reason; never a release |
| The money a settlement decides (capture, card charge, hold release, refund) | Recorded as "pending" payments with `server_actions` for a server | **Gap b: no code ever ran them.** A captured hold was never captured at Stripe and lapsed (the money was lost); the balance on the card was never charged; refunds and releases were never sent | The runner (`server/deposit-guard.ts`) runs each step at Stripe at once from the Settle screen, and the deposit job retries what did not go through (`payments.server_action`, five tries, then a Finance task) |
| A card hold lapsing (Stripe cancels an authorization after about 7 days) | `deposits.hold_expires_at` was set and shown | **Gap c: nothing acted before it lapsed; a lapse was never recorded** (the deposit still read "held"; the webhook ignored `payment_intent.canceled`) | The job captures the whole hold 36 hours before it lapses while the car is out, returned and not settled, or closed with anything held (protection, not a release: the settlement refunds the rest). A lapse (the webhook, or a capture refused as canceled) is recorded and the deposit charged to the saved card, with an urgent Finance task. A hold lapsing before the pickup: a task to place a new one |
| Release the hold, Capture the hold in part (0129 `hold_action_begin`) | Finance within the single limit, any time | **Gap d: a release (or a part capture, which releases the rest) past an open incident, toll or dispute, during the rental or the toll window** | While the car is out, inside the toll window or with anything open: an Admin with the reason (recorded); before the pickup as before |
| A deposit in cash or by e-Transfer | Settled as a charged deposit; what is released becomes a Finance task to give it back | None (a person gives it back) | Same, under the same window and blockers |
| A cancellation or no-show before the pickup | The deposit returned through the cancellation's refund, by a person | None (no rental, nothing to owe) | Unchanged |
| A renewal of a long rental | The deposit carried forward to the next period | None | Unchanged |
| Scheduled jobs (deposit due, hold-back reaching 30 days) | Tasks only | None: they never moved money | Unchanged |

### How money owed is billed, and what happened past the deposit

| Charge | How it is billed | Gap | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Late return, extra kilometres, fuel | At check-in on the return statement (0130 `booking_return_auto_charges`), applied from the deposit at settlement | The rest was charged to the card as a pending payment never run | Run at once; declined: pay link, then a Finance task |
| Cleaning, smoking, other charges | Lines added by Finance, on a statement | Same | Same |
| Tolls, tickets and their administration fees | 0021 matching and `charge_toll_ticket`, after the return through `charge_after_rental` | Gap a (it released the deposit); the card charge was never run | Apply only; the card charge runs from the job |
| A toll or ticket weeks after closing | `charge_after_rental` on the closed booking: card on file | Pending charge never run; **a pay link for a later charge was refused on a closed booking** | The job runs the charge; a pay link is allowed for a later charge on a closed booking |
| Damage under the deductible, at cost, and the deductible on a claim (0020 assessment, 0021 `bill_damage`) | Lines from the incident's costs; the deposit first, then the card | Same as above | Same, plus Keep the whole deposit (damage) and a damage charge of its own (below) |
| Damage above the deposit, up to the $2,500 deductible | The card on file for the rest | The card charge never ran | Runs; declined: pay link, then a Finance task |
| Direct-billed damage (no incident) | None by hand except a line typed into the table | No damage line with its reason and photos; the renter was not told | `app.damage_charge_add` (an Admin): amount up to the deductible, what was damaged, the photos or estimate; the renter is told (`renter_extra_charge`, kind damage) |
| Any balance with no card on file (cash or e-Transfer deposit, no deposit) | | **Gap e: the settlement failed** ("no Card on File to charge the balance") | The balance stays on its lines; the follow-up sends a pay link by email and text and opens a Finance task until it is paid |
| A later charge paid by pay link | `server_payreq_settle` ties the lines | **Gap e: the lines were tied only when the whole part was paid first, so a paid link left them open** | `app.tie_later_lines`: oldest first, while the payment covers them |
| A long rental that saves a card instead of a hold | Charge the saved card (deposit) by hand | The Settle button only showed when a deposit existed, so a booking with no deposit had no settlement on screen | The Settle button shows on every returned booking; with no deposit taken and a saved card, the screen offers **Charge the deposit to the saved card** first |

### By deposit method

| Method | Protected by |
| --- | --- |
| Card hold (7 days or less) | Captured in full before it lapses while out or unsettled; the settlement captures and the runner runs it; a lapse is charged to the saved card |
| Card captured (a hold captured, or a deposit charged to the card) | Refunds only through Settle (or an Admin); the runner sends them |
| Saved card, no hold (long rentals) | Charge the deposit at the return from the Settle screen; every balance goes to the saved card first |
| Cash, e-Transfer | Given back only through a Finance task after Settle; balances: pay link and Finance task (no card needed) |
| None (an Admin waived it) | Every charge goes to the card on file, else a pay link, else a Finance task |

## 2. No automatic release

Nothing releases or refunds a deposit without a person pressing Settle (or an Admin's explicit action with the reason):
`charge_after_rental` applies only; the deposit job and the webhook capture or charge (they keep money) and never create a
release or refund (suite 97 checks the sweep releases nothing); the scheduled SQL jobs only raise tasks.

## 3. The card hold before it lapses

`program_settings.deposit_hold_capture_hours` (36) and `deposit_auto_capture` (on). The daily job (`/api/jobs/deposits`, the
Worker's five-minute tick when `public.server_jobs_due()` says `deposits`: a hold to capture, a money step waiting, or the day's
sweep not done) asks `public.server_deposit_guard_sweep()`, captures each hold (`server_deposit_capture_begin`, the payment marked
`protect_capture`), records it as a captured deposit (`deposit_events` "Captured in full before the card hold lapsed"), so the
settlement later refunds only what is not owed (`refund_deposit`). A refused capture whose intent is canceled is a lapse
(`app.deposit_hold_lapsed`): the deposit is charged to the saved card (`deposit_charge`) and Finance gets an urgent task.

### Nothing old is run by itself

`program_settings.deposit_auto_max_age_days` (14). The job never runs a money step older than that (a marked payment left
pending, `public.server_money_action` gives the server nothing for it), never charges or chases a balance whose newest statement
with an open line is older than that and that the guard did not start in time (`balance_followups.manual`), never captures a
hold already past its expiry, and never charges the saved card for a lapse found more than 14 days after the hold expired. Each
gets a Finance task to review (`deposit_money_review`) instead. Payments left "pending" by settlements before 0155 carry no
`server_action`, so the job never runs them at all.

## 4. The Settle screen

`program_settings.deposit_toll_window_days` (7; the hold-back limit stays `deposit_holdback_days`, 30). `app.deposit_guard_facts`
(the window, the blockers, the suggested hold-back) is on the preview. Inside the window the whole rest is pre-filled as the
hold-back until the window ends; releasing needs **Release now anyway** (recorded as `release_in_window`). `app.deposit_blockers`
in words: an incident open, a claim not settled (at-cost damage already billed excepted), new damage at the return not priced,
damage found later not billed, a toll or ticket on the car during the rental not matched, not charged or disputed, a card
dispute, charges not on a statement yet. With any of them the whole deposit stays; an Admin releases part of it with the reason
(`release_past_blockers`, the blockers kept on the row and in the booking history). **Keep the whole deposit (damage)** (an Admin,
`app.deposit_keep_whole`) turns what is left after the other charges into a damage line with the reason and the photos, on a
statement, applied; nothing is released. **Bill damage** (`app.damage_charge_add`) bills up to the deductible.

## 5. Billing beyond the deposit

The settlement's card charge, then `public.server_balance_next`: the card on file (once; not again within 3 days of a decline),
else a pay link (`server_balance_payreq`, `server_balance_payreq_send`: the booking's message by email and a text, 7 days), else
a Finance task `collect_balance` that names what was tried and closes when nothing is owed (`app.booking_balance_open`).
`balance_followups` keeps the last step per booking; the job follows up once a day.

## 6. Screens

Plain words everywhere (`features/money/deposit-words.ts`): the deposit's method and status on the booking pages, the blockers,
the window. The booking's Deposit card says the deposit is never released by itself. The field check-in tells the field staff
member the deposit is not given back at the return. Release the hold asks for the reason (an Admin) and says why it waits.

## Not done

- The renter is not emailed when a hold is captured before it lapses (the booking history records it; the settlement email
  tells them what was kept and refunded).
- A damage charge's photos are references typed by the Admin (the condition report photo numbers or file names); picking them
  from the return report is not built.
