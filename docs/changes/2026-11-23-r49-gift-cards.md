# Gift cards, vouchers and gifted rentals (Release 49: 3600)

Migration `3600_r49gift_gift_cards.sql`. The owner's backlog of November 20: "Gift cards, vouchers and gifted rentals."

## What it does

- **Buy a gift card** on `/gift-cards` (linked from every page's footer and the sitemap): a preset amount ($50, $100, $250, $500) or a typed one in whole dollars ($25 to $2,000), the recipient's name and email, a message (500 characters) and an optional send date (today to a year ahead). Signed in to pay: a visitor fills the form, signs in and comes back to it (kept in the tab). The card is charged at once in Stripe's own frame (`createGiftIntent`, metadata `purpose=gift_card`, the card not saved). The browser and the Stripe webhook both settle through `public.server_gift_order_paid`, which checks the intent's id, amount, currency and status against the order; a refused payment is refunded at once.
- **Gifted rental**: a car on the site and 1 to 14 days, priced with the site's own quote (`app.price_quote`, a week ahead at 10 am: the rental, its fees and the HST as the site charges them; insurance never carries HST). It becomes a credit for that amount tied to the recipient's email: only the account with that address may add or use it, on that car or another.
- **HST**: none on the sale of a gift card (a gift certificate is not a supply, Excise Tax Act s. 181.2); the HST is in the rental the balance later pays, as the booking already charges it.
- **Never expires**: the table refuses an end date on a card sold for money (Ontario, O. Reg. 17/05 s. 25.3). Only a voucher given free may end.
- **Emails**: the buyer's receipt (`renter_gift_card_receipt`), the recipient's gift with the code, the message, the redeem link and the printable card (`renter_gift_card_delivered`, at once or on the send date by the hourly job `app.job_gift_cards_send`, pg_cron `carmacars-gift-cards-send`), the buyer's notice that a scheduled gift went (`renter_gift_card_sent`), the holder's notice when a booking used the card (`renter_gift_card_used`) and when a refund put it back (`renter_gift_card_refunded`), Finance and Admins' digest line for a card sold or a voucher issued (`staff_gift_card_sold`, with the liability outstanding) and the immediate notice of a void (`staff_gift_card_voided`). Every renter one is in the in-app notices and push (topic payments), preference type Receipts (always sent: money); the staff ones are Money. No texts (the recipient gave no phone).
- **The printable card**: `/gift-cards/card/<token>` (the card's 64-character secret, never indexed): the card, the message and how to use it; Print or save as PDF.
- **Account > Gift cards** (`/account/gift-cards`, under Rewards, also linked from the Rewards header): the balance, Add a code (the email's link fills it; 10 tries an hour), each card with its state and balance history, and the gifts the person sent.
- **Sign and pay**: a **Gift cards** box above the card form lists the renter's cards with **Use $X**, an applied card with **Remove**, and **Have a gift card code?** (add and use in one go). The balance is used before the card; a part leaves the rest on the card; the card form then asks only for what is left and "What you pay now" shows the gift cards and the card part. When the gift cards pay all of it, **Confirm with gift cards** confirms the booking with no card charge. Never the deposit: a gift card pays only what is due at booking. A renter with no saved card pays at least $1.00 by card (`gift_card_min_card_cents`) so the card for the deposit is saved. Credit card coverage (option 2) needs the rental paid on that card: the box says so and applies nothing.
- **The ledger** (`gift_card_ledger`): issue, redeem, release, refund, void and adjust, in cents, insert-only (update and delete refused), the balance kept by its trigger, never below zero and never above the card's amount (checks on both tables; the balance column refuses any other write).
- **Confirmation**: `app.finalize_booking` adds the applied gift cards to the card amount for the check against what is due; on success the card payment is the card part and a second payment (method `gift_card`) the gift card part. A booking that leaves "waiting for payment" without being confirmed (declined, expired, cancelled, pushed back), or whose amount due changes, puts the balance back at once.
- **Refunds**: a refund of a booking paid in part by gift card returns the gift card part first: the refund row splits into the card part and a gift card part (`refunds.gift_split_of`), approved together (the cancellation table's rule counts the two as one), and the gift card part is paid back to the balance as soon as it is approved. The card part goes through the processor as before. The card-refund email is not sent for the gift card part.
- **Admin and Finance**: Money > **Gift cards** (`/admin/money?tab=gift`, `/staff/money?tab=gift` for Finance): the liability (what CarmaCars owes in rentals on cards sold for money), voucher credit given, sold and used in the last 30 days, gifts waiting for their send date; every card with search (code, last four, name, email) and status; one card's balance history. Admins **Issue a voucher** (an amount: a credit that keeps its remainder, up to $1,000 per voucher, optional end date, optional email it is sent to; or a percentage: a promo code with its most uses and end date, `source=admin_voucher`), **Void** with the reason (an applied balance goes back first, then all of it is taken off) and **Resend**. Every issue and void is in the audit log with who and why.
- **Settings** (Program settings): `gift_cards_on`, `gift_card_min_cents`, `gift_card_max_cents`, `gift_card_presets_cents`, `gift_rental_max_days`, `gift_card_min_card_cents`, `gift_voucher_max_cents`.
- **Security**: every function is for the server (service_role); nothing new for the browser roles or the door allow-list. The tables have row-level security (`admin_all` read). Orders are limited to 8 an hour per account and code tries to 10 an hour.

## What's new

- For renters: Give a CarmaCars gift card or a gifted rental from the footer's **Gift cards**: any amount, emailed with your message on the day you choose, and it never expires. Got one? Add the code under Rewards, **Gift cards**; on Sign and pay the balance is used before your card.
- For admins: Money > **Gift cards**: what is owed on gift cards, every card and its history; issue a voucher (an amount or a percentage), void with a reason, resend.
- For staff (Finance): Money > **Gift cards**: the liability outstanding and every card's balance history.

## Guides

- `renter/sign-and-pay`: the Gift cards box, Use, Remove, Confirm with gift cards, never the deposit.
- `renter/rewards`: Account > Gift cards: add a code, balances, gifts sent; buying one on /gift-cards.
- `admin/requests-offers-and-bookings`: Money > Gift cards: the liability, Issue a voucher, Void, Resend; a refund of a gift-card booking.
- `va/finance`: Money > Gift cards for Finance, and the gift card part of a refund.
