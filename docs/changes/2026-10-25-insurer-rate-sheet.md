# The insurance-replacement rate sheet and each insurer's billing history, October 25, 2026

The owner's request (October 23): "build an insurance-replacement rate sheet with billing history per car class per
insurance company". The example: an insurer pays $65 a day to replace a car with a 2024 Jetta (a sedan).

Migration `supabase/migrations/0163_insurer_rate_sheet.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0163`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0163_gen_guides.mjs` (`GUIDES_BASE=origin/main`). Tests: `supabase/tests/99_insurer_rates.sql` (database),
`web/src/features/insurers/rates.test.ts` (unit), `web/tests/e2e/insurer-rates.spec.ts` (end to end).

What was there: `insurers` (contacts only, 0016), the insurer fields of a booking (0016 and Add a rental's Insurance
replacement, 0148: the insurer, claim, adjuster, days and daily rate authorized, provisional end, billed to the insurer or
split), the insurer's share (`app.booking_insurer_share`, 0016), insurer invoices (`app.draft_insurer_invoice`,
`app.record_insurer_payment`, Admin > Insurer invoices). All of it is kept; nothing applied is edited.

## 1. The sheet

`insurer_rates`: one row per insurer, class and start date.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `insurer_id`, `class_code` | the insurer and a class of `vehicle_classes` (the existing list: Economy, Standard, Sedan, SUV and the rest) |
| `effective_from` | the first day it applies (Toronto) |
| `daily_rate_cents` | what the insurer pays a day; empty ends the rate from that day |
| `weekly_cap_cents`, `monthly_cap_cents` | optional: the most for each full 7 days and each full 30 days (a part week costs no more than a week, the rest of a month no more than a month) |
| `delivery_allowance_cents` | optional: the most it pays for delivery and pickup on one rental |
| `km_per_day` | optional: the kilometres a day it authorizes (shown to staff; the booking keeps its own allowance) |
| `taxes_note` | optional: printed on its invoices |
| `note`, `created_by`, `created_at` | why it changed, who, when |

- **History, never an overwrite.** A trigger refuses any update or delete ("A rate on the sheet is kept as it was saved").
  A change is a new row from its date. The rate on a day is the row with the latest start on or before it (the latest
  saved when two start the same day, shown as "Replaced the same day"). `app.insurer_rate_at`.
- **Saving** (`app.insurer_rate_set`, Admin only, door `admin_do` `insurer_rate_set`): checks the insurer and class, a daily
  rate above $0 (or `remove`), caps at least the daily rate (and the month at least the week), 0 to 5,000 km, a start no more
  than a year back and two years ahead, and refuses a row identical to the rate on that day ("Nothing changed").
- RLS on, `admin_all` and `staff_read` (select), writes by the server only, audited (`app.audit_row`).

## 2. A car's rate

`app.insurer_rate_for(insurer, car, day)`: the car's pricing class (`vehicles.class`) first, then its other classes
(`vehicle_class_links`) in the list's order; the first class with a rate that day wins. The Jetta (pricing class standard,
also a sedan) gets the insurer's sedan rate unless the insurer has a standard rate.

## 3. Bookings billed to an insurer

A trigger on `bookings` (`bookings_insurer_rate`, before insert or update of the bill-to, insurer, car, pickup, rate or
booking type) keeps three new columns: `insurer_rate_id` (the row used), `insurer_sheet_daily_rate_cents` (the sheet's
rate that day) and `insurer_rate_reason`.

- No rate given: the sheet's rate is taken (`insurer_authorized_daily_rate_cents`).
- A rate typed that differs from the sheet: refused on a change without a reason ("The daily rate authorized ($72.00)
  differs from X's rate sheet for Sedan ($65.00): give the reason for the different rate, or leave it empty to take the
  sheet's"); with a reason it is kept and flagged. The reason comes as `insurance.rate_reason` with Add a rental
  (`app.manual_rental_insurance` wrapped) and as `changes.insurer_rate_reason` with a booking's changes (the review screen,
  an offer changed in place): the door keeps it for the trigger (`app.insurer_rate_reason`, this transaction only) and
  takes it out of the changes, so `app.adjust_booking` (0016) is unchanged.
- A booking whose insurer, car or pickup day changes follows the sheet when its rate came from the sheet (or the insurer is
  new on it); a rate set with a reason stays. A booking with a rate typed before its insurer had a sheet keeps that rate
  until its insurer changes.
- No sheet for the car: nothing changes from 0148 (the rate typed, no reason asked).
- `app.manual_rental_quote` (Add a rental's check) also returns `insurer_rates`: each active insurer's rate for the car on
  the pickup day (`app.insurer_rates_for_car`).

## 4. The insurer's share and its invoices

`app.booking_insurer_share` is replaced with the same signature and the same result when there is no sheet row:

- The rate is the booking's, else (a booking with none) the sheet's for the car on the pickup day.
- The rental part is the lower of the rental charge and `app.insurer_rate_amount(rate, weekly cap, monthly cap, days)`.
- With a delivery and pickup allowance: split (the insurer pays the rental only), the insurer also pays delivery and pickup
  up to the allowance; billed wholly to the insurer, delivery and pickup above it are the renter's. HST in proportion.

`app.draft_insurer_invoice` is wrapped: 0016's draft (amounts, less what was invoiced, one draft per booking), then the
first line says the rate and its source ("Rental charge, booking CC-10001, 10 day(s) authorized at $65.00 a day (X rate
sheet, Sedan), at most $390.00 a week", or "(rate agreed for this claim)" when typed with a reason), the fees line names
the allowance, and the invoice keeps `daily_rate_cents`, `rate_source` (sheet, override, booking, none) and
`insurer_rate_id`; the taxes note goes in `notes`. `app.pdf_facts` is wrapped to give the PDF the note, which the invoice
PDF prints (`web/src/features/documents/pdf.ts`). Sending, payments (`app.record_insurer_payment`) and write-offs are
unchanged.

## 5. Admin screens

- **Money > Insurer rates** (`/admin/insurers`, new): the grid, one row per active insurer and one column per class; each
  cell the rate today, its caps, the next rate saved, **Set** or **Change** (a dialog: daily rate, caps, allowance, km,
  taxes note, From, why) and **End**. On a phone the table's rows are cards (the admin table's card layout). Below, what each
  insurer was billed and still owes.
- **The insurer's page** (`/admin/insurers/<id>`): tiles (bookings, days used and authorized, billed, paid, outstanding,
  average daily rate weighted by the days covered, days to pay from sent to paid); rates by class with Set, Change and End;
  the history of every rate (from, to, status, why, who); billing by class; the bookings (class, claim, days authorized and
  used, rate and its source, Sheet and the reason when it differs, billed, paid, outstanding, days to pay, each invoice with
  its PDF); a period (picked up from and to); **Download CSV** (`/admin/insurers/<id>/export`, Admin only; a cell that
  starts like a formula is quoted so a spreadsheet never runs it). Reads: door `admin_read` `insurer_rate_sheet`,
  `insurer_billing`.
- **The booking**: an Insurance replacement card on the Overview and Money tabs (door `admin_read` `booking_insurer`):
  insurer and claim, the rate and where it comes from, Differs with the reason, the sheet row, the taxes note, the days
  covered, what the insurer pays, its invoices (PDF, Insurer invoices).
- **Add a rental** (Insurance replacement): once the price is checked and the insurer chosen, the daily rate authorized is
  filled from the sheet (it follows another insurer chosen unless staff typed one); the hint gives the sheet's rate and caps
  and the taxes note; a different rate shows "Differs from the rate sheet" and needs the reason before the rental is added.
- **The review screen** (request or offer, Bill to insurer): the same prefill and reason; an empty rate now means "the
  sheet's" (it was sent as $0).
- Settings > Insurers keeps the contacts; its line points to Money > Insurer rates.
