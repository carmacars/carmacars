# Marketing emails to everyone who gave us their email, weekly and new car campaigns (Release 41: 2520, 2521)

Branch `r41-marketing`. Migrations `2520_r41_marketing_everyone.sql` (new `marketing_contacts`, `marketing_campaigns`,
`marketing_campaign_sends`; wraps `app.has_marketing_consent`, `app.unsubscribe` and `app.notification_recipients` as
`_pre2520`; patches the claim's marketing maximum; replaces `public.my_profile`; the catalogue rows of the two new emails,
generated by `supabase/seed/2520_gen_catalogue.mjs`) and `2521_r41_guides.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/2521_gen_guides.mjs`).
No applied migration is edited. Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r41_marketing.sql`.

The owner's instructions (November 12, 2026, approved by his lawyer): "If they unsubscribe then do not email them. However, if
they have put their email anywhere on our website, then that is grounds for us to be able to email them." "Do not include send
me deals checkbox. I'm only asking you to adjust the email campaigns." "Email addresses typed into forms that were not submitted
do count." "Make sure that you're increasing the quantity of emails that you're sending for marketing automatically for
everybody." "Every single person should also be emailed every single time we add a new vehicle to the fleet."

| Part | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | `marketing_contacts`: every distinct address from the customer sources (renters, customer accounts, renter leads from the site, forms left unfinished), with its sources and the consent basis `provided_on_site`. Never staff, owners as owners, investors, Turo guests, leads typed in by staff, owner leads or owner applications. Rebuilt hourly, so an address whose sources were deleted leaves |
| Consent | `marketing_everyone_enabled` (on): every marketing email (the existing drips too) may go to anyone who has not unsubscribed. Unsubscribing (one-click link and List-Unsubscribe header, as before) suppresses the address at once and now also marks the account with that address; waiting campaign emails are dropped. Bounces and complaints stay out |
| No deals box | The "send me deals" email boxes are gone from the booking request, the car page lead forms and the discount game. Account > Notifications shows the offers box ticked unless the person unsubscribed; unticking unsubscribes |
| Weekly email | `marketing_weekly` (`marketing_weekly_enabled`, `marketing_weekly_dow` 4, `marketing_weekly_hour` 10, `marketing_weekly_cars` 6): the cars listed now with their prices and the public promo codes running now. A new week replaces last week's emails still waiting |
| New car email | `marketing_new_vehicle` (`marketing_new_vehicle_enabled`): once per car when its listing first goes live, never a sample car; cars listed before 2520 are not announced; someone still waiting when the car stops being listed is not sent |
| More email, free only | `marketing_auto_volume` (on): the day's marketing allowance is the day's free capacity (`app.email_day_cap`: Cloudflare's budget, then Resend's free plan) less `marketing_transactional_reserve` (60, or twice the recent daily average), never below `notify_marketing_daily_max`. Campaign emails are queued at priority 5 (every other email first), in the marketing hours, paced across the window, one campaign email a person a day; the rest wait for the next day. No paid send |
| Pages | Admin > Marketing > Email campaigns (audience, waiting, queued, sent, unsubscribed, campaigns, the switches); Sales > Marketing > Email campaigns, read only |
| Emails | Marketing emails to renters say "You are receiving this because you gave us your email address on carmacars.ca" and "Unsubscribe at any time. It takes effect at once." |
