# Close past trips: no trip left open after its return (Release 35, part W: 0910, 0911)

Branch `r35-w`. Migration `0910_r35w_booking_ops.sql` (part 3). Test: `supabase/tests/99_r35w_booking_ops.sql` (section 5).

The owner, November 8: past trips are never left open. Admin and Fleet close them at the booked times in one tap, with an edit
when it was late or early; no renter emails for a back-dated close; the deposit then settles; a Fleet To do and a digest line.

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| List | `past_trips` (door `ops_read`, Admin or Fleet): bookings confirmed or on rent whose return time passed, oldest first. Pages `/admin/bookings/past-trips` and `/staff/past-trips` (Field jobs tab); a line on Bookings now and on Field jobs while any are open |
| Close one | `past_trip_close`: a confirmed booking gets the past-trip override of 0770 (pickup and return at the booked times, or the times entered), one on rent a return override at its booked return; Admin or Fleet apply it at once (no approval step), the checks it passed kept on the override |
| Close all | `past_trips_close_all`: each trip on its own, the ones that could not close listed with the reason |
| Renter | No email or text for a back-dated close: the renter events and texts made by the close are set aside |
| To do, digest | Every quarter-hour (`app.past_trips_sweep` from the jobs tick): a Fleet To do `past_trip_open` per trip open 2 hours after its return (done when the trip closes), and one morning digest line for Admins and Fleet with the count |
