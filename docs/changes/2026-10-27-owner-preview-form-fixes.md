# The owner's agreements before approval, and the ServiceOntario form fixes (0181)

Two requests of October 27, 2026. The owner chose: show the two owner agreements right after sign-up, filled in from the
owner's profile and cars, read only and marked "Terms pending approval", the fees pending, never invented; signing stays
off until CarmaCars approves the terms, exactly as before. And the ServiceOntario forms previewed for real owners' cars
on the live site filled wrongly in four ways (below).

Migration `supabase/migrations/0181_owner_preview_form_fixes.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0181`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0181_gen_guides.mjs`. No applied migration is edited; no form template is marked checked (an Admin checks
each one in Admin > Settings > Forms after the deploy).

## 1. The agreements before approval
- Before: `/owner/sign` said "We are preparing the terms for your vehicles" until an Admin approved the application and
  set each car's terms. `app.owner_agreement_facts` (0029) reads the owner agreements and the current Vehicle Schedule
  entries; a car still in intake has neither, so the packet was empty.
- Now: `public.server_owner_agreement_preview_facts` (`app.owner_agreement_preview_facts`) returns the same facts (same
  access check: the owner or staff) plus one entry for each car still in intake, filled from the application: the car,
  how it is held, its title status and lender, the management mode and onboarding method proposed. The Insurance Fee,
  Tracker Fee, Install Fee, Revenue Share, schedule and agreement numbers and dates are null; the missing agreements
  appear with status `preview` and no number; `preview: true`.
- `/owner/sign` with nothing to sign and a car in intake shows "Your agreements, filled in": the alert "Terms pending
  approval: you can sign once CarmaCars approves your terms.", the values filled in with their Fix links, and the
  Vehicle Enrollment Agreement and the Title-Holding and Lease Agreement (when a car is not Turo-Only) word for word, each
  headed "PREVIEW, NOT FOR SIGNATURE". Fees and the Revenue Share read "Pending approval"; the agreement and schedule
  numbers read "Given when CarmaCars approves your terms". The Sign button is shown disabled ("Sign after approval").
- The signing gate is unchanged: nothing is prepared or opened from the preview, and `app.owner_open_agreements` still
  refuses until an Admin's terms create the agreements ("The owner has no enrollment agreement yet: set the terms first").
- Pointers: Home's next step is "Your agreements are filled in: terms pending approval" with **Read your agreements**;
  the owner's signing checklist (`app.my_signing_checklist`) says of each agreement not prepared yet "Filled in for you
  to read now. You sign once CarmaCars approves your terms." and links to the preview; Documents has "Read your
  agreements, filled in"; each car's Onboarding checklist links its "Agreements signed" step to `/owner/sign`.

## 2. The ServiceOntario forms
| Seen on the live site | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SR-LV-006 "Street Number and Name" printed a district ("Scarborough"), City "Toronto": the street lost | The owner's address went to the boxes as stored, and `app.split_ca_address` (0122) took the first comma part as the street and the last as the city with no idea of a district, a unit inside the street line ("123 Example St Unit 5"), a province with no comma ("Toronto ON M5V1A1" read as the province), an address with no commas, or a street that starts like a unit word ("University Ave", "Steeles Ave") | `app.address_parts` reads the owner's separate columns first; a second line that is a unit is the unit, one that is the street (the first line a building or district) is the street, a district goes in no box; a whole address typed in the street line is parsed. `app.split_ca_address` finds the street by its civic number, the city after it (a district between is dropped), a unit word or `#` anywhere, `5-123` as unit 5, and the city after the street type when there are no commas (`app.street_and_unit`, `app.unit_only`, `app.ca_province_code`, `app.ca_postal_code`) |
| Last Name / First Name boxes empty, 9 boxes filled (two owners) | The registry context took the name parts as stored: owners whose first and last names were never stored (added by staff, or a corporation whose legal name is the corporation's) had none; ON00453's person boxes and the registry name fell back to the bare legal name or nothing | Missing parts come from the legal name (`app.person_name_parts`): an individual always gets "LAST, FIRST M". A corporation owner's person is its signing officer; the corporation's name is its own, else a corporation owner's legal name (`app.looks_like_company`), else the name proposed for a new Owner Corporation on the application; CarmaCars' registry name falls back to its trade name when no legal name is set |
| 0 text boxes filled on SR-LV-006 (one owner) | The car's onboarding method was on no schedule, onboarding or vehicle (only its ownership situation had it), so the method was "none" and every registrant field of section 1 was empty | The method is also read from the situation; with no method at all the owner (or the owner's corporation) is the vehicle's registrant, so section 1 is never empty, for Lease, Co-Registrant, Transfer and none alike |
| 013-1155 Recipient blank for an individual owner | Correct only when the form is not needed: an onboarding's form list could outlive a change of method | `app.vehicle_registry_forms` lists the forms by the car's current method and situation (013-1155 and 013-0169 only for a Lease through a new Owner Corporation; none for a Transfer of a car owned outright or financed). `app.fill_vehicle_government_forms` keeps to it, corrects the onboarding's list and supersedes an open form the method does not need (a signed or filed one stays on record), so the owner's checklist and Documents no longer show it as needed |

On deploy: the checked mapping of each official form (0122's, from the publisher's download) goes back on the current
template where no Admin has saved one and the template has every mapped field; an Admin's mapping is left as it is (an
Admin who saved a mapping with "Only when" conditions that skip section 1 changes it in the UI). Every car's form list is
corrected, open forms its method does not need are superseded, and every form in use that is not signed or filed is
filled again from the fixed context. No template is marked checked.
