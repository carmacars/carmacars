# Search growth: positive pages, demand steering, car data, parking, events, Turo searches, the tracker, airports, renting vs owning, languages, reviews, sitemaps (Release 36, SEO: 2000 to 2005)

Branch `r36-seo`. Migrations `2000_r36s_positive_copy_demand_steer.sql` (replaces `app.job_fleet_trending` and
`app.public_fleet_trending`), `2001_r36s_car_data.sql` (replaces `app.server_seo_data_load`, `app.server_seo_data_targets`,
`public.place_pois_near`; new constraint lists on `vehicle_specs` and `place_pois`), `2002_r36s_reviews_everywhere.sql`
(new `outside_reviews`, `public.review_summary`; wraps `app.door` as `door_pre2002`), `2003_r36s_page_families.sql`
(new `seo_events`, `ownership_costs`, the Turo landing pages, the tracker and renting vs owning settings; wraps `app.door`
as `door_pre2003`), `2004_r36s_languages_sitemaps.sql` (new `seo_languages`, `seo_string_sources`, `seo_strings`, the
families and languages sitemaps; template epoch `r36-1`) and `2005_r36s_seo_guides.sql` (generated by
`supabase/seed/2005_gen_guides.mjs`). No applied migration is edited. Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r36s_seo.sql`,
`web/src/features/seo/copy-lint.test.ts`, `car-data.test.ts`, `families.test.ts`, `web/src/lib/edge/page-cache.test.ts`.

The owner's goal: 5 million Google clicks a month, page one for every car, place and service page by relevance.

## Not done

- The first seo-data and seo-i18n runs fill the new data: until then the sections are left out and the language pages stay noindex.
- The Transport Canada recall API address is taken from its published API; the first run's notices say if it failed.
- No insurance or parking averages are loaded yet: the owner or a VA loads them with their source (the curated CSV, kind ownership, or Admin).
- Localized slugs (a French word in the address) are not used: the language is the first segment, with the English path after it.
- Google ignores sitemap priority and changefreq, so none is written; lastmod stays each row's own date.
