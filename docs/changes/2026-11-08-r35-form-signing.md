# Owners sign each government form on screen; staff see it with the signature beside it (Release 35, part Q: 1000, 1001)

Branch `r35-q`. Migrations `1000_r35q_form_signing.sql` and `1001_r35q_form_signing_guides.sql` (generated by
`supabase/seed/1001_gen_guides.mjs`). SCHEMA_MARKER 1001. No applied migration is edited. Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r35q_form_signing.sql`,
`web/src/features/onboarding/sign-state.test.ts`, `web/src/features/onboarding/steps.test.ts`.

The owner: "Car owners need to sign each government form that is applicable to them on the onboarding. And then on the admin
side and the fleet side we should see the same form as an editable form field by field and print ready with the car
owner's signature removed, but still displayed somewhere, off the form."

The rule that stays: the government forms are the exact official PDFs with only their boxes filled. The on-screen signature
is never stamped on the PDF.

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| 1. The owner signs each form | `/owner/sign/forms/<car>` (Documents > Sign, Home > Check and sign, the Onboarding checklist step) lists each government form the car needs. **Show the form** displays the official PDF with its boxes filled (or the data sheet while the form is not in use as a PDF); the owner checks each form and then signs with the signing steps of every signature on the site (the reading, the consents, the drawn signature; a typed name only where Program settings allow it). One signature record per form: signer, drawn or typed, date, IP address, device, the form's version, the values signed and the signing session with its trail (`esign_sessions`, `esign_events`). The session covers the forms by their SHA-256 (`app.government_form_values_sha`); a form that changed after it was opened is refused. A form with a value missing, or signed in ink already, is not offered |
| 2. Admin and Fleet | The form's page (`/admin/government-forms/<id>`, `/staff/government-forms/<id>`) keeps the field by field edit and the versions of release 35 part S, and shows **Download the print-ready form** with no signature on it. **Owner's signature** sits beside the form, off it: the drawn signature or typed name, who signed, when, IP address and device, the version signed, every earlier signature. The signature image is served to staff only (`/admin/government-forms/<id>/signature`) and is not in any PDF. Fleet has the same page in the staff portal |
| 3. Changed since signed | A form whose values change after it was signed (a value changed by hand, the records, a new template) is **Changed since signed**: the page names the boxes that changed, the owner's government forms step opens again (one To do, on the checklist and as Home's Next step) and the earlier signature stays on record. The state is compared by SHA-256 against the values signed (`app.government_form_sign_state`: unsigned, signed, changed, ink) |
| 4. Documents list | Each form shows Signed, Not signed, Changed since signed or Signed in ink, with **Sign** or **Sign again** and the date. Staff lists show the same state |
| 5. The checklist step | The government forms step of the Onboarding checklist is done only when every form that applies is signed (a form signed in ink counts): `app.onboarding_checklist` and `app.onboarding_overview` (signed, to sign, changed). Steps after it wait for it, as before |
| 6. One reminder email | The daily job (`app.government_forms_sign_nudge`, from `app.notify_daily_checks`) queues one email, the signing email `owner_agreement_to_sign` with the registration paperwork of the car and the link to its forms, when a complete form has waited a day for the signature or a form changed after it was signed. One per state of the car's forms (the dedupe key names the forms and who signed last); a reminder still waiting is replaced; only owners with an account and an email; the sender's daily limit and holds apply as for every email |

## Guides

- `owner/documents-and-forms`: sign each form on screen, Show the form, Sign again, the signature is a record that is never on the form.
- `owner/apply-and-get-your-car-listed`: sign each form on screen under Documents, then in ink.
- `admin/owners-and-onboarding`: Owner's signature beside the form, the print-ready form, Changed since signed, one To do and one reminder email.
- `va/fleet`: the same for fleet.
