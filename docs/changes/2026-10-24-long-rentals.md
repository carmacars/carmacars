# Long rentals, every fee editable, editing a booking that has started, October 24, 2026

The owner, entering a rental picked up the day before: "It won't let me put in the dates cuz the maximum days are 31 or
something. In reality people should be able to book more than a month but a new rental agreement should be generated for
each month automatically. That being said the monthly discount is not being applied. And then I can adjust the monthly
rate but cannot adjust the young driver fee or protection plan price. I'm trying to input how it was paid and I can only
write out one payment method per booking when the guy actually paid his deposit in e-transfer and his rental in cash.
This whole process just isn't smooth." Then: "we can't edit a booking after it already started", "There's no way to adjust
a renter's details. We accidentally entered someone's email wrong", (booking CC-10012) "we forgot to charge a young
drivers fee and there's no way to add it in rn. And also when changes are made they need to be updated of their
outstanding balance and we need to be able to process the payment", and, with a picture of the price on Add a rental,
"each field needs to be editable when uploading a rental or sending an offer back" with "little i's".

Migration `supabase/migrations/0132_long_rentals.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0132`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0132_gen_guides.mjs`. Tests: `supabase/tests/85_long_rentals.sql` (77 assertions; 01, 03, 06 and 08 follow
the new longest booking), `web/src/features/offers/model.test.ts`, `web/src/server/stripe-events.test.ts`,
`web/tests/e2e/long-rentals.spec.ts` (the owner's case end to end at 375 and 1440).

This is the first checkpoint. Not in it yet: the monthly periods' own agreements and invoices (below).

## What changed

### 1. No 31-day limit

The pricing engine (`public.calc_rental_charge`) took 1 to 30 days and every booking path refused more with "Longer stays
renew with a new booking", a renewal nothing ever made; the table itself (`bookings_billable_days_check`) stopped at 30.
Now the renter's request takes up to Program settings, Longest booking (`max_rental_days`, 365 by default); staff (Add a
rental, offers, quotes, edits) up to the longer of that and `staff_max_rental_days` (730). The staff door marks the session
(`app.staff_pricing`), so `app.max_rental_days_now()` gives each the right limit. A rental already underway (pickup in the
past) was already taken (0074); the form now says so beside the dates. The public pages say "30 days" where they meant the
monthly rate and the longest booking where they meant that.

### 2. The monthly rate

30 days or more is priced at the car's monthly rate for each 30 days (the monthly rate divided by 30, times the days),
everywhere the engine prices: the renter's quote and request, the offer screen and its preview, Add a rental, an edit.
The per-day fees with a cap per rental (young driver, additional driver, winter tire, baby seat) apply the cap per period
of 30 days, and a Protection Plan priced per rental counts once per period (`app.rental_periods`, `app.calc_capped_periods`).
Add a rental and the offer screen list the monthly periods with each one's share.

### 3. Every line of the price editable where it is entered

A fee line set by hand for one booking is `booking_line_overrides` (the line's amount for the trip, before HST, by key: the
charge type or `addon:<code>`); the engine uses it in place of the computed amount (`app.apply_line_overrides`), so HST and
the total follow, and a key with no line yet (a Protection Plan not priced, a young driver fee forgotten) is added. Lines of
their own are `custom_fee` lines (a label, an amount, HST or not). Who may: an Admin, and customer service and sales while
Program settings, Staff may change fees (`staff_may_edit_fees`, on), is on (`app.may_edit_fees`). Each change is in
`booking_fee_changes` (who, when, before, after, the reason, the status then) and the booking's history.

- **Add a rental**: the price table is the form. Each line takes a new amount in place: the rental charge (for the trip, or
  a rate a day with the other shown), the young driver fee, the Protection Plan, delivery, each add-on, the deposit (an
  Admin) and the kilometres a day. Add a line from the list or a charge of its own; Remove takes a fee off. An (i) beside
  each line says what it is and how it is worked out (`web/src/features/forms/Info.tsx`, a real button, works on a tap).
  One optional reason covers every change.
- **Send an offer** and **the review screen** (an offer sent back): each fee for the trip, the Protection Plan, charges of
  their own, "A total for the trip" or "A rate a day". The review screen sent a fee as a rate a day (typing $50 for three
  days gave $150); it now sends the amount for the trip.
- **An existing booking**: Money tab, Fees, Change or add a fee (the young driver fee shows its price for the dates), or a
  charge of its own.

### 4. Paid in parts, the deposit its own way

Add a rental: the rent is paid by one way with its amount, then Add another payment for each part (cash and e-Transfer, for
example); the deposit after it, its own way with its reference and date. Each part pays the rental first, then the fees
(one that straddles both is recorded as two). A payment of the whole booking (the form's first payment, the website's card
payment, the saved card charged by staff) now pays the rental first and then the fees: it used to close every part for good,
so a fee added after a card payment never showed as owing. Before you save sums up the renter, car, days, total, what was
received, what is still to collect, the deposit and the agreement.

### 5. Editing a booking that has started (or ended)

Overview, Edit the booking: the pickup and the return (extend or shorten; repriced at the booking's own rates, or the price
typed by hand the same a day), the car (on trip: the swap of record, the car it leaves to Out of service), the rental charge,
each fee, the kilometres, the deposit (an Admin) and the note, on a confirmed, on-trip or returned booking (an Admin also a
closed one). `app.booking_staff_edit` records each edit in `booking_staff_edits` (who, when, status, reason, each change,
the totals); a returned or closed booking's owner split is computed again (a split already on a sent statement leaves Finance
a task) and the commission synced. Tick Email the renter what changed: they get the "booking updated" email of an applied
change, saying the new total; leave it off for a silent correction. To have the renter accept and sign first, Propose a
change stays.

### 6. The balance and its pay link

After any change the booking shows what is open, by part, with: Email a pay link or Text a pay link for exactly that amount
(a payment request of `covers = 'balance'`, paying the rental first, then the fees), Charge the saved card, or Record a
payment (For: the rental and fees, the new default). The renter's booking page shows Balance due with Pay, which opens
Stripe's form for exactly the balance (`public.renter_balance_payreq`).

### 7. Correcting a renter

Edit renter on the renter's page and on every booking (Admin, customer service, sales): name, email, phone, date of birth,
licence, home address with the lookup, and a staff note (`renters.staff_note`). A new email becomes the sign-in's at once
(`adminSetEmail` in the auth adapters: Supabase's admin API, no confirmation email for a typo staff correct; optionally a
link to set a password there); unsigned agreements' signers and the bookings not over follow. Another renter with that email
is refused by name; the same phone is a warning. Each correction is in `renter_edits` and the audit log.

### 8. The Stripe events the site did not listen to

The live endpoint took 8 events. `scripts/deploy/supabase.sh` now subscribes it to `payment_intent.requires_action`,
`charge.refunded`, `refund.created`, `refund.updated` and `charge.dispute.created`, `.updated`, `.closed` too
(`web/src/server/stripe-events.ts` keeps the same list; a unit test compares them). A refund made from the site settles or
fails (`public.server_stripe_refund_event`); one made in Stripe's dashboard is recorded on its payment with a Finance task;
a dispute is recorded in `disputes` with tasks for the Admin and Finance (`public.server_stripe_dispute_event`); a saved-card
charge the bank wants confirmed gives Finance a task to send a pay link. Each is recorded once (the webhook's claim, and the
refund or dispute id).

### 9. Every confirmed booking gets its agreement

Booking CC-10013 (added by hand, confirmed, with an additional driver) had no rental agreement: a rental added by hand made
its agreement only when the renter first opened the signing page, so the renter was never asked to sign and the additional
driver never got a link. Now the site makes it for every confirmed or on-trip booking that has none (unless signed on paper):
right after Add a rental, and on the five-minute job (`/api/jobs/agreements`, run when `server_jobs_due` says
`agreements`). It makes the rental agreement and Blind Spot's, sends the renter "Sign your rental agreement" (email and text
by the renter's channel) and each additional driver their own link; the booking's signing panel (0130) shows each signer with
Resend and Sign now. Each try is recorded (`agreement_auto_runs`); three failures stop and give staff a task. The migration
logs every confirmed or on-trip booking without an agreement (`audit_log`, `agreement_repair_queued`): the first job run makes
and sends them.

### 10. Addresses

The offer screen's delivery and collection addresses and the booking edit's pickup and return places use the address
lookup like the rest of the site; Edit renter and Add a rental fill the home address from the lookup field by field.

## The flows walked as the owner

At 390 and 1440, Add a rental, Send an offer, Edit the booking, Record a payment:

- Add a rental: the intro cut to the point; "Pickup" twice (a time and a method) is now "Pickup date and time" and "How the
  renter gets the car"; the delivery and pickup addresses show only when chosen; the licence class, expiry and home address
  fold under one line (the agreement asks the renter); non-refundable and promo code fold; add-ons fold; "Check the
  calendar and the price" only before the first check; the rental charge is typed in its own line, not in a separate box
  "typed by hand"; the reason for a price is optional (a default is kept); the rent's payment, then the deposit's, instead
  of the two interleaved; a stale price check (a date of birth typed while it was out) checks again instead of keeping
  the price without the young driver fee.
- Send an offer: "A total for the trip", "A rental rate a day, before fees and tax", each fee "for the trip", (i)s.
- Record a payment: For defaults to "The rental and fees" (one payment, split for you).

## Privileges

Every new function is the service role's only, reached through the staff door: ops reads `booking_fees`,
`booking_edit_form`, `renter_edit_form`; ops actions `booking_fees_change`, `booking_staff_edit`, `booking_custom_fee`,
`renter_edit`. The renter's `renter_balance` and `renter_balance_payreq` check the booking's renter. The new tables have the
admin policy and the audit trigger.
