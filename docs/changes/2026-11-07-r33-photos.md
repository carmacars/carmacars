# Release 33, part D: listing photos (0700, 0701)

The owner's three asks of November 7 on listing photos.

## 1. The model's name behind the car

- The listing photo worker's preset is now `studio_v5` (`web/src/features/listing/worker.ts`). The model's name is
  lettered behind the car again, as on the sample photos: on the cover and on the eight named outside views (the
  samples letter the front, side, rear and three-quarter views). It is not lettered behind the extra outside photos or
  any inside photo. Release 31 had it off (`LETTER_REAL_PHOTOS = false`) because the car was then small in the frame;
  since `studio_v4` the car is framed big and stands in front of the word, as on the samples.
- The word comes from the car's model (`letteringName`, `web/src/features/car/studio.ts`): a Mercedes class of one
  letter keeps the word ("C-Class" is C CLASS, "A-Class" is A CLASS), a longer one is its letters alone ("CLA-Class" is
  CLA, not "CLA CLASS"), anything else as written (AMG GT, CTS, F-150). The stand-in drawings of cars without photos use
  the same word. The bands are new files (`site/backdrop/lettering-v2-*.png`), made once per model.
- The job context says whether a photo is the cover (`photo.is_cover`). Photos already on the site get the lettering
  only when staff press **Frame the outside photos again** (it now makes again every outside photo not framed with
  `studio_v5`); nothing is processed again by itself. A photo chosen as the cover later gets its lettering at its next
  framing.

## 2. Any number of extra photos

- "More outside photos" (`exterior_more`, studio background) and "Other detail" (`other`, touched up) take any number
  (1000; was 30). The capture screen has **Extra outside photos** and **Extra inside and detail photos**, each taking many
  files in one go; **Add photos** has no 30-photo cap any more. Extras go through the same processing and plate check.
- An extra photo is ordered after every standard shot (`sort_order` from 100000), so the car's page shows the standard
  shots first. In the review, **Extra photos, in their order on the car page** lets staff drag them by a handle (mouse,
  finger or pen) or use Earlier and Later, then **Save the order** (`app.listing_photos_order`).
- The capture screen's count is now the database's (`app.listing_capture`: `counted`, `min_photos`): a photo with a mark
  on it, or a rejected one, no longer counts toward the 10 on screen while the database does not count it.

## 3. The cut-out editor

- The worker keeps the segmenter's cut-out as it came (`vehicles/<car>/work/<photo>-mask.png`, private; its alpha is the
  mask) and where it placed the car (`vehicle_photos.cutout`: `auto_mask`, `place`; then `mask` and `adjust` after an
  edit).
- **Edit the cut-out** under an outside photo in the listing review (`web/src/features/listing/CutoutEditor.tsx`):
  - Cut-out: the car over the photo as taken (faint); **Erase** and **Restore** brushes, size, zoom and pan, Undo.
  - On the studio: the car on the studio background with the model's name, as the worker draws it; drag to move,
    **Size** 40% to 160%, arrows and + and - on a keyboard.
  - **Reset to the automatic cut-out**, then **Save as a new version**.
  Pointer events throughout: a phone (touch), a pen or a mouse.
- Save (`/api/listing/cutout`, `app.photo_cutout_edit`): the edited cut-out (the original's colours with the edited mask)
  goes to the private bucket; the photo is made again from it by the worker (no segmenter call), with the same framing,
  shadow, lettering and touch-up; the new files have new names (`-v<n>`) and the version before is kept
  (`vehicle_photo_versions`), as is the photo as taken. The photo stays as it was on the site until the new version lands;
  an edit that fails leaves it as it was.
- The plate check carries over only if the car did not move (its place on the frame within 1%): "no plate visible" stays
  and the photo stays approved; a covered plate is covered again on the new file by the review when it is open
  (`PlateCarry.tsx`, the saved boxes and style, through the plate route). If the car moved, the plate is asked again.
  0661 still applies: on a published listing the new version goes back on the site once its plate is checked.
- A photo made before masks were kept shows **Make the cut-out**: the worker makes the mask only (one transformation;
  the photo is left as it is) and the editor opens by itself when it is ready.
