# Owner onboarding as one guided pipeline, and the incorporation referral (Release 50: 4510 to 4513)

Migrations `4510_r50op_document_kinds.sql` (five document kinds, alone so 4511 can use them), `4511_r50op_owner_pipeline.sql` and `4512_r50op_notices.sql` (catalogue block generated by `supabase/seed/4512_gen_catalogue.mjs`). SCHEMA_MARKER 4513. The owner (Nov 24): owners do not just get "accepted"; the car's title moves into the company's name (the Title-Holding Agreement and Forms A to E), or a corporation leases it. Make it a clear, guided pipeline.

## What it does

- **Built on what exists, nothing duplicated.** The stage tracker (`app.owner_stage_track`) reads the records the platform already keeps: the Onboarding checklist (0022 and its wrappers: signatures, government forms, Forms C and D, listing photos, listing), `app.owner_sign_missing` (3800: the master agreement and Schedule A), the onboarding method of the Vehicle Schedule (or the application's), the onboarding photos and their review (0821), the registry visit, permit and fleet policy dates of `vehicle_onboardings`, the safety certificate (0237), `app.confirm_fleet_policy` (0078, the Insurer confirmed button). Only what no record held is new: `owner_pipeline` (one row a car: route, stage and since when, who was told, the visit and its fees, whether a UVIP or certificate is needed, the inspection, an Admin's overrides).
- **Routes** (`app.owner_stage_route`): Transfer (title to CarmaCars: 9 stages), Co-Registrant (still supported by the code: the same 9), Lease of an existing corporation (no title transfer: corporate documents and the lease instead; 7 stages) and Lease through a new Owner Corporation (Incorporate first; 8).
- **Stages** (`owner_stage_defs`, wording an Admin may change): Application, Terms approved, Set up your corporation, Agreements signed, Documents uploaded (licence, ownership permit, insurance, the lender's payout letter or permission when financed; corporate documents for a lease), Ownership transfer (the filled Form A and transfer documents, the ServiceOntario appointment or visit, the UVIP and the safety certificate when needed, the fees paid by whoever the terms say: the owner at the counter under section 8.2, or CarmaCars on the upfront invoice when the Vehicle Schedule lists it), New registration uploaded, Plate and insurance on the fleet policy, Inspected and photographed, Listed.
- **One next action** per current stage for the right person (owner, Sales, Admin, Fleet), with the button, what to bring, where to go (the ServiceOntario centre finder, Program settings `owner_stage_finder_url`), the expected time and the blockers in plain words (a document not accepted with its reason, the certificate still missing, the forms not signed).
- **Where**: the owner portal's Home ("Your next step", which replaces the six-step path once a car is on its way) and each car's page (with the uploads its route asks for), the Admin owner page, the staff owner page (now open to Sales for the owners they brought in), and the **Owner pipeline** board (Sales, customer service, Fleet: `/staff/owner-pipeline`; Admin: Marketing > Owner pipeline): every car by stage, days in stage, next action and whose it is, **Nudge** (branded email and text, once in 10 minutes a car) and a call script per stage.
- **Notifications**: the owner is emailed and texted when a stage becomes theirs (`owner_stage_your_turn`, once per car and stage); Admin and Fleet get a To do (`owner_stage_turn`) when it is theirs, closed by itself when the stage moves; a stage waiting `owner_stage_stall_days` (3) alerts the Admins (`staff_owner_stage_stalled`) and gives the salesperson (else an Admin) a To do (`owner_stage_stalled`). `app.owner_stage_sweep` runs every 15 minutes (pg_cron); every action here and every document upload refreshes the car at once. The backfill put every onboarding car in the pipeline quietly.
- **Admin override**: Advance this step, or set any stage done, not needed, open or back to the records, with a reason; each in `audit_log` (`owner_stage_override`) and on the table's audit trigger.
- **Guide me**: `owner-transfer` (owners: Transfer my car to CarmaCars) and `sales-move-owner` (Sales, customer service, Fleet, Admin: Move an owner forward). `flows.ts` now lists every step file: the 36 of 4100 were not registered, so their workflows never showed in the menu.
- **Doors**: ops_read/va_read/admin_read `owner_stages`; va_read/admin_read `owner_stage_board`; ops_do/va_do/admin_do `owner_stage_do` (appointment, visited, needs, inspected, nudge, set, advance); admin_read `incorporation_referral`; admin_do `incorporation_referral_save`, `incorporation_referral_mark`. Server: `public.server_incorporation_click` (`/go/incorporate`).

## B. Incorporation referral

Research (Nov 24, program pages opened):

| Provider | Program | Payout | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ownr** (RBC Ventures) | Affiliate program on PartnerStack: [ownr.co/affiliates](https://www.ownr.co/affiliates); listing [affililist.com/affiliate/ownr](https://www.affililist.com/affiliate/ownr) | **$50 per incorporation** (or managed corporation plan), $20 per sole proprietorship; 90-day cookie | Online Ontario and federal incorporation in about 20 minutes, Canadian, RBC-owned (CarmaCars banks with RBC). **Chosen** |
| Ownr refer-a-friend | [help.ownr.co: refer a friend](https://help.ownr.co/en/articles/3708450-refer-a-friend-to-ownr) | $50 credit to the referrer's own corporation, $50 off for the friend | Customer program, no cash: not usable for CarmaCars |
| LegalZoom | [legalzoom.com/partner-programs](https://www.legalzoom.com/partner-programs) (Impact) | from $125 CPA on business formation | US states only; Canadian incorporation not covered |
| Ontario Business Central | [become a partner](https://ontariobusinesscentral.ca/partners/become-a-partner.php) | "referral programs, revenue-sharing" named, no amounts, contact only | No published program |
| Incorporate.ca / Corporate Ontario, Wise | No affiliate or referral program found for incorporation | none | Not usable |

Built provider-agnostic: `incorporation_affiliate` (provider name, affiliate link template with `{ref}` for the referral's code, commission note, sign-up page; **off until the link is pasted**, which turns it on) and `incorporation_referrals` (who was referred, for which car, first and last opened, the code, then incorporated and commission received, entered by hand). Admin > Settings > **Incorporation referral**, summed up on Admin > Referral partners. It follows the RentalCoverage affiliate of 0164 (one settings row, clicks recorded, payments by hand), the model of the referrals CarmaCars makes; `referral_partners` is for businesses that refer renters to us and does not fit. While it is off, the owner's Incorporate step asks for the corporate documents instead and names no provider.
