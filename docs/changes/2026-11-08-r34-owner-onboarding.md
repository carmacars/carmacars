## Fixes from the database test run

- **The forms were made again on every change.** `app.registry_forms_refresh` (0822) remade each draft or ready form whenever any watched row changed (an onboarding status, an invoice choice), even when nothing on the form changed; one car got its two forms made five times before anyone opened them. Now a new fill identical to the current one (same template, mode, values, data sheet and missing list) is dropped and the current fill stays. A change that does alter a form (a RIN, an address, the method) still makes it again. Test 11 E: "the fills replace the drafts" counted every replaced fill of the car, which since 0822 also counts the pair made by themselves when the terms were set; it now counts the drafts that the second fill replaced (still 2), and a new assertion checks no form is made again unchanged.
- **The server could not run part P's functions.** 0821 to 0825 revoked execute from public, anon and authenticated but never granted service_role on 24 functions; the end of 0826 grants it (tests 06, 08, 09).
- **Sending notifications was slow (0827).** `app.process_events` builds a payload per recipient, and every layer of it read `app.settings()` again (a 640-column row) and rendered the booking again. A staff alert to about 200 staff read the settings 15,684 times and rendered the same booking 1,238 times (2 alerts: 39 s). Now the settings are read once per transaction (cleared by any change to `program_settings` or `usage_services`), and while one event is rendered its booking, vehicle, contact block and setting words are made once for all its recipients. Same 2 alerts: 2 settings reads, 7 booking renders, 11 s, identical notifications. This is why the reopen email in `99_r28_operations` fell outside its 1-minute window (the flush before it took over a minute).
- **99_staff_alerts depended on how long the run took.** Fleet counts as on duty when seen in the last 15 minutes (`chat_shift_minutes`). The test marked fleet as seen once at its start, so on a slow full run the 0466 section began more than 15 minutes later and fleet got none of its alerts (7 failures). Run on its own, or after 99_r34p_onboarding, it passed. The section now marks fleet as seen right before it starts.
- **Test VIN.** `99_r34p_onboarding.sql` used VIN 1HGCM82633A004352, also used by tests 87 and 99_r28_platform, so the car was "already with CarmaCars"; it now uses KMHL14JA0MA340821 (valid check digit, used by no other test).
