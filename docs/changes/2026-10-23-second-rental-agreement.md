# The second rental agreement: Blind Spot Car Rental's agreement on every booking, October 23, 2026

The owner's request: "In addition to the current rental agreement, this one needs to be filled out and in there as well,
in accordance to each rental. The address needs to be changed on this document from the current address visible there
on the top left and twice in box 15 to the mailing address we have. And the signature needs to be changed from SAM to
AHMAD. Again this rental agreement along with the other rental agreement need to be pre filled by the rental details and
renter details." Then, relayed the same day: Blind Spot asked for the representative's name written in a handwriting
style ("Ahmad"; an uploaded image of the real signature replaces it), and for CarmaCars' own HST number in box 1 in place
of Blind Spot's.

CarmaCars rents under Blind Spot Car Rental's insurance. Every booking now has two agreements: CarmaCars' rental
agreement (unchanged) and Blind Spot's own two-page form, filled in from the booking, the renter and the car. The renter
reads both and signs both with one signature.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0127_second_rental_agreement.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0127`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0127_gen_guides.mjs`. Code: `web/src/features/blindspot/` (`layout.ts` what goes in each box, `fill.ts`
the drawing, `RentalForms.tsx` the Admin card, `sample.ts` the made-up booking of the sample, `script-glyphs.generated.ts`
the script hand) and `web/src/server/portal/blindspot.ts`. Tests: `web/src/features/blindspot/fill.test.ts`,
`supabase/tests/80_second_rental_agreement.sql`.

## 1. The template: private, uploaded and checked in Admin > Settings > Forms

Blind Spot's PDF is never in the repository (it is public): the Admin uploads it on the new first card of
**Settings > Forms**, "Blind Spot Car Rental agreement". It is stored in the private documents bucket
(`forms/templates/BLINDSPOT-RA/<uuid>.pdf`) with its SHA-256, as a `pdf_templates` row of the new kind `booking_overlay`
(form code `BLINDSPOT-RA`). The upload must open and be A4. Like the other templates it does nothing until an Admin
presses **Checked: use for bookings**; **Download the filled sample** (and **Sample billed to an insurer**) fills it for a
made-up booking with the real settings. Every render checks the stored file against the recorded SHA-256. The registry
forms' list and mapping screen leave it out (it is not mapped field by field; the database refuses it there).

The form has no fillable fields (a LiveCycle form saved again by macOS): each value is drawn at the place measured on
Blind Spot's form, in Helvetica, shrunk to fit its box (never past it, never below 5 pt).

address, P.O. number, insured vehicle information and policy number (not recorded for insurer bookings), destination,
the replacement vehicle column, time and date in, K/M in, driven and charged, per hour late, VLF, LDW, T&LDW, PAP and

balance due, charges 59 and 60, refund. The renter's initials (the clean-up line under the kilometre rates and box 40's
X) are not captured by the signing flow and stay blank; additional drivers sign CarmaCars' agreement through their own
link, and box 10's signatures stay blank.

## 4. Signing: one signature, two documents

When the rental agreement opens (offer approved, details saved, a rental staff added), the second agreement is rendered,
stored with the booking (`agreements/bookings/<id>/blindspot-<sha>.pdf`, a `rental_agreement` document titled "Blind
Spot rental agreement CC-…") and recorded in `rental_agreement_companions` with its SHA-256. The same booking gives the
same bytes, so it is stored once; a change (the renter's details, a new template or signature) replaces it while it is
unsigned. A booking whose agreement was opened before the template was in use gets it when the signing page opens.
Making it never stops the rental agreement: an error is logged and it is made on the next opening.

The signing page shows "Blind Spot Car Rental agreement" under the rental agreement, with **Download PDF**, and says the
one signature signs both. The signature sends both SHA-256: the database refuses a second agreement that changed since the
page was opened (nothing is signed; "Reload the page and sign again"), signs the rental agreement as before, and records
the same signature on the second agreement. A booking with no second agreement signs exactly as before.

Once the rental agreement is countersigned, the signed copy job also renders the signed second agreement: the renter's
signature in boxes 16, 17 and 20 and a signature record page (the unsigned SHA-256, the renter's time, IP address and
device, the representative), stored once. It is in the renter's Documents, the booking's Drivers and documents tab, and
attached to the "Your signed rental agreement" email (which waits up to five minutes for it).

## 5. Admin > Today

Until done, Admin > Today (and Settings) list: "Enter CarmaCars' HST number" (Program settings), "Upload Blind Spot's
rental agreement PDF" then "Check the Blind Spot rental agreement" (Settings > Forms), each with its link.

## 6. What the owner does

1. Settings > Forms: upload Blind Spot's blank PDF, download the filled sample (and the insurer sample), check it, press
   **Checked: use for bookings**.
2. Program settings: enter CarmaCars' HST number (box 1 stays blank until then).
3. Optional: upload a PNG of Ahmad's signature to use instead of the written name, and his full name for the record.

## Guides

- `admin/settings`: the Settings page's Forms tile, and the new task "Blind Spot rental agreement".
- `renter/sign-and-pay`: the Blind Spot agreement below the rental agreement, one signature for both.
