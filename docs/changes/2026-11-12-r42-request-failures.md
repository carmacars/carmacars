# A request to book that fails says exactly why, keeps everything, and reaches Sales (Release 42: 2670, 2671)

Branch `r42-reqfail`. Migrations `2670_r42_request_failures.sql` and `2671_r42_request_failures_guides.sql` (the guide
chapters, generated by `supabase/seed/2671_gen_guides.mjs`). SCHEMA_MARKER 2671. Database types regenerated (one table, two
public functions). DB test `supabase/tests/99_r42_request_failures.sql` (and the hourly limit in `08_booking_app.sql`); unit
tests `web/src/features/booking/refusal.test.ts`; e2e `web/tests/e2e/request.spec.ts`.

The report: customers sending the Request to book form saw "We could not send the request. Please try again or call us."

## What was happening

- That sentence was shown for any failure that was not a database refusal (no answer from the database, a busy database, an
  error in a step after the request was already saved) and for a refusal without a message. A database refusal showed the
  database's own words at the top of the form, with no field and no step.
- Was the data lost? No: the form saves to the device on every change and to `form_drafts` (`booking_request`) after 0.8 s,
  and coming back fills it in again. But the person was not told, and nobody on staff knew the request had failed.
- A step after the request was saved (the contact capture, the phone box count, Book now) could throw and turn a saved
  request into "could not send".

## What changed

| Part | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Every refusal | `app.request_refusal` maps each raised error and each `ok: false` reply of `public.submit_booking_request` (and the triggers and functions it calls) to a code, the form field and a plain message that says what to change. `submit_booking_request` itself is unchanged |
| The form | The message is shown beside its field, the form jumps to that step and focuses the field, and everything typed stays. Fields that had no error line got one (pickup and return place, booking type, add-ons) |
| Unexpected | An error nobody planned for: "Something went wrong on our side, not yours. We kept your request and our team will contact you". Staff see the database's own words; the customer never does. If even the follow-up cannot be kept, the form says the answers are saved on the device and to send again, and promises nothing else |
| One more try | No answer or a busy database is tried once more with the same request key (the key makes a second send safe) |
| After the save | The steps after a saved request can no longer turn it into a failure |
| Draft | Saved to the device at once and to `form_drafts` after a pause, as before; now also saved at once when a send fails |
| Follow-up | `public.server_booking_request_failed` keeps the person, the car, the dates and the reason in `booking_request_failures`, one row per person, car and day, and raises one Sales To-do (`request_not_sent`, high; urgent when unexpected). A request that goes through closes the row and the To-do. Rows are deleted after 90 days. At most 30 an hour are kept from one network address |
| Admin | People > Renters > All leads shows **Requests that did not go through** with the count, the reasons and the people (`public.server_request_failures`, Admin only) |

## Refusal reasons found

Dates (return before pickup, notice, longer than one booking, car taken, car leaving the fleet, non-refundable too close);
the car (not listed, not open for bookings, example car, no rates, no VIN or plate, service overdue); places (delivery or
return address missing, airport not on the list, owner's location not offered); the driver (email, phone, name or date of
birth, under the minimum age, under the car's age, licence class, too many additional drivers, a driver's details missing, the
same driver twice); coverage (none chosen, no card, card does not cover the car, a box not ticked); consent; the note too
long; too many baby seats; an add-on past its notice or not offered; an account that is not active; 3 requests already open;
the hourly limit of one network address; the security check; the request key.

## Loosened

| Rule | Before | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Requests an hour from one network address | 5 | 30 (an Admin's own value stays) | People on one mobile network or office Wi-Fi share an address; real customers were refused. A person is still limited to 3 open requests |
| An add-on past its notice or not offered | The whole request was refused | The request is sent without the add-ons and its note names them for staff | The form already says add-ons are confirmed in the offer; an add-on should not cost the booking |
| The same driver listed twice, or the renter listed as an additional driver | Refused | Sent once | A copy is never what the person meant |
| An airport our list no longer names | Refused as "a delivery needs its address" | The airport code is sent and the database says "choose the airport again" only if it does not know it | The message named the wrong field |
| A card that does not cover the car | Refused after Send, at the top of the form | Said at the coverage step, beside the card | Same rule, said where it can be fixed |

Not loosened: the notice before pickup, the ages, the licence classes, the coverage rules, 3 open requests per person.
