# Advertising and marketing automation, October 25, 2026

The owner's spec "CarmaCars Advertising Automation" (October 6, 2026), tasks 4 to 9 of its section 7, and owner
recruitment from classified ads under the anti-spam law (CASL). No paid service and no AI: every text is a template filled
from the car's own record, in the manner of the writing kit (`web/src/features/kit/`).

Migration `supabase/migrations/0167_advertising_automation.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0167`; 0166 is kept for the guides
refresh), its catalogue block generated by `supabase/seed/0167_gen_catalogue.mjs` (after `node emails/build.mjs`) and its
guides block by `supabase/seed/0167_gen_guides.mjs` (`GUIDES_BASE=origin/main`). Tests:
`supabase/tests/99_ads_automation.sql`, `web/src/features/ads/ads.test.ts` and `web/tests/e2e/ads-automation.spec.ts`.

## 1. Listing text per car and channel (task 4)

- **Admin > Marketing > Listings** (`/admin/marketing/listings`): choose a car and a channel (Facebook Marketplace,
  Kijiji, Craigslist, Instagram, Facebook Page, Google Business Profile). The page writes the title, the price line, the
  description or caption, the hashtags, the tracked link and the photo order, each with a Copy button and its length
  against the channel's limit (Kijiji title 64, Craigslist 70, Marketplace 100; Instagram caption 2,200 and 10 hashtags;
  Google post 1,500 with one photo and a Book button, no phone number; Facebook Page 3 hashtags).
- The words follow the spec's section 2 templates and come from `app.ads_facts()` only: rates, kilometres, extra km,
  deposit, delivery fee, the 18 to 20 setting, "no credit card" cars, liability coverage only on a car on the fleet policy,
  the site's ages and maximum rental days, the car code. A missing fact drops its sentence. No review, rating or claim.
  Brand CarmaCars; no long dashes; the numbered company name is refused (`app.ads_text_problem`).
- Tracked link: `/cars/<slug>?utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<marketplace|classified|social|post>&utm_campaign=<car code>`.
- Warnings: the car does not take requests, has no daily rate, has no photos, or shows sample photos (do not post stock
  photos as the car).
- Facebook Marketplace is never posted by the server: the text and the photo order are for posting from the owner's own
  profile in their browser (Meta takes no vehicle listings from business Pages).
- **Mark as posted** records the ad (`ad_postings`: car, channel, address, date, the text and price line, and a
  `snapshot` of the figures it says). One live ad per car and channel.

## 2. Rate change tasks (task 6)

- A change of `program_settings.ads_rate_change_bps` (300, 3%) or more in the daily, weekend, weekly or 30-day rate, any
  change of the deposit, kilometres, extra-km price or delivery fee, or the car stopping (or starting again) to take
  requests, marks every live ad of the car `needs_update` and opens one task per ad (`ad_update`, queue sales, due in a
  business day) with what changed, the new price line, the ad's address and the link to the new text. A second change
  updates the same task. Back to what the ad says: the task is cancelled.
- Checked at commit by deferred constraint triggers on `rate_cards`, `vehicle_listings` and `vehicles` (a rate card is
  replaced in two steps), and every 6 hours by the ads job (a card dated ahead).
- **Mark the ad updated** (or closing the task in Staff tasks) records the new figures; **Taken down** ends the ad.
- **Admin > Marketing > Posted ads** (`/admin/marketing/ads`): live, to update, taken down.

## 3. Bookings per channel (task 5)

- `booking_attribution`: the first and last visit of the renter's browser before the request (0060/0086 sessions: UTM
  source, medium, campaign, referring site, landing page, and the channel worked out by `app.ad_channel_of`), captured
  when the "Request sent" page view ties the visit to the booking, and for older or staff-added bookings when the report
  first reads them. Kept with the booking, so the source outlives the 90 days of visit history.
- `bookings.marketing_source` (with note, who and when): the source staff set by hand, on the booking page line
  **Came from** (`booking_marketing_source`). It wins over the visit.
- **Admin > Marketing > Bookings by channel** (`/admin/marketing/report`): requests, bookings (confirmed, on trip,
  returned, closed), revenue (rental charge after discounts) and conversion by channel and by month, first or last touch,
  with a bar chart. Every request counts once: staff source, else the visit unless direct, else a referral code, else how
  it reached us (phone, email, WhatsApp), else direct, else not known.

## 4. Facebook Page and Instagram drafts (tasks 7 and 8)

- `social_drafts`: a post for a day, its channels, caption, base link (the tracked link is added per channel when copied
  or published), photos, status (draft, approved, posted, skipped) and what was posted where (`posted`: by hand or API).
- The ads job writes this week's and next week's drafts (from today on) every 6 hours, from `web/src/features/ads/social.ts`
  with the writing kit's engine: Monday a new car (published in the last 14 days) or a featured one in turn; Tuesday the
  owner program; Thursday the cars free this weekend; Friday the requirements (Instagram); Sunday a price highlight or a
  seasonal trip from the kit's day trips (Facebook Page). A day already drafted is left alone. Admin can press
  **Write drafts for this week**. Off with `program_settings.ads_social_drafts_enabled`.
- **Admin > Marketing > Social drafts** (`/admin/marketing/social`): the week by day, Edit (back to draft), Approve, Skip,
  copy the caption per channel and the link for the Instagram bio, **Posted on Instagram / Facebook Page** with the post's
  address, and a post of one's own.
- **Meta publishing** (`meta_publishing`, **Admin > Settings > Meta publishing**): the code path is built
  (`web/src/integrations/meta.ts`: a Page photo or link post; an Instagram media container then media_publish, then the
  permalink) and **off**. It needs the Page id, the Instagram professional account id and a Page access token (kept with
  0147's vault helpers; the row keeps the last four characters) and **Switch on**. Then the ads job publishes approved
  drafts on their day (at most 3 tries; a refusal is shown on the draft and the settings page) and records the post links.
  Nothing is faked: while off, `server_ads_job` returns no token and nothing is due.
- The ads job: `/api/jobs/ads`, run by the Worker's cron tick when `server_jobs_due` says `ads` (every 6 hours, or an
  approved post due while publishing is on).

## 5. Owner recruitment and the consent log (task 9)

- Leads: `leads.kind = 'owner_recruit'` with `listing_url`, `listing_site`, `contact_kind` (private seller, dealer,
  rental business, other business), `business_name`, `vehicle_text`, `do_not_contact_at`. Typed by staff from the ad;
  nothing is collected from the classified sites.
- `recruit_consents`: the consent log, never edited (an update is refused; a change is a new row): express (needs its
  proof), implied by an inquiry (ends after 6 months), implied by a business relationship (2 years), implied by a
  published business address with no "no solicitation" note (never a private seller; the page is required), referral
  (one message, naming the referrer), withdrawn, do not contact. Each with channel (email, text, both), how, where and
  when, and who recorded it.
- `recruit_calls`: the call log with the spec's script. A yes needs the person's words and becomes express oral consent;
  a no is do not contact.
- `app.recruit_consent_state(lead, channel)`: allowed or the reason (no address, do not contact, unsubscribed, replied
  STOP, withdrawn, expired, no consent yet, a private seller's ad, the referral's one message used).
- **Send** (`recruit_send`) only when allowed: an email as the new marketing email `owner_recruit_outreach` (the
  greeting, the message, a button to `/list-your-car`, the footer with CarmaCars, its mailing address, the reason for the
  email and the one-click unsubscribe; never turned into a text), or a text through the Quo outbox that names CarmaCars
  and ends "Reply STOP to opt out." (quiet hours apply). Templates in `web/src/features/ads/recruit.ts`: after a call,
  reply to an inquiry, a dealer or rental business, after a referral; two texts. `program_settings.recruit_outreach_enabled`
  stops all of them.
- The unsubscribe link (0018) and a STOP reply (0096) add a withdrawal to the log by trigger.
- **Admin > Marketing > Owner recruitment** (`/admin/marketing/recruit`) and each lead's page.

## 7. For the owner to decide or do

- **Meta publishing**: a Facebook Business app, the Instagram account as a professional account linked to the Page, the
  permissions pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish
  (Meta may require App Review and Live mode), a long-lived Page access token, the Page id and the Instagram account id.
  The steps are on the settings page. Until then, copy and post.
- **The lawyer** confirms the recruitment approach before outreach starts (the spec's section 5), including how an oral
  yes is proven (a recording or a note made during the call) and the Do Not Call List duties for calls.
- **The mailing address** (Site settings, mailing address) must be set: the anti-spam law requires it on every
  commercial email, and the footer shows it.
- Google Business Profile and TikTok posting stay by hand (their approvals, task 10).
