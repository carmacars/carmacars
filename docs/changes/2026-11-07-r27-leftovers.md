## 2. One model per car family (0520)

- `app.car_model_family(make, model, trim)` and `web/src/features/vin/model-family.ts`, one rule with the same cases
  (`web/src/features/vin/model-family.cases.json`, read by the unit test; `supabase/tests/99_model_family.sql` holds the
  same list): the model is the family as the maker names it (Mercedes-Benz A-Class, C-Class, CLA-Class, GLA-Class, GLB,
  GLC, GLE, GLS, S-Class, G-Class, AMG GT; BMW 3 Series, X5 ...), the code and its words are the trim (C300 4MATIC,
  CLA250 4MATIC, 330i xDrive). A value it cannot read with certainty stays exactly as typed; other makes are untouched.
- Every save of a car runs it (trigger `vehicles_a1_model_family`, after 0087's name case): VIN decode, permit scan,
  partner sheets and manual entry all save through `vehicles`. VIN decode (`mapVpic`) and the 01 Motors sheet apply it
  in the browser and the server too.
- Every car on file was renamed by the rule at migration time, each change in the audit log (`model_family`, with the
  names before and after), and its plain copy written again. The listing's public address (its slug) never changes.
- `/cars`: the Model filter lists each family once with its trims nested under it, grouped by make when no make is
  chosen. `?model=C-Class&trim=C300 4MATIC` filters one trim; an old `?model=C300` link finds its family.
- Data health: "Model family not recognized" lists the Mercedes-Benz and BMW cars whose model is not a family we know,
  each with its link.

## 3. The listing page's steps and its photos (0521)

- The "Pipeline" card (step counters nobody could read) is replaced on the Admin, staff, field and owner listing pages
  by four numbered steps: **1 Photos (n of 14)**, **2 Cover plates**, **3 Review**, **4 Live on the site**, each with
  what it needs and one button to where it is done (`ops_read listing_steps`). A step that stopped still shows with
  its Retry. The Listings tables' column is "Work done".
- A photo filed under the wrong shot: **Remove** on the Photos card (`ops_do listing_photo_remove`). The photo is marked
  replaced (kept privately, audited `listing_photo_remove`), the shot is free for the right one. Not on a live listing
  (the review's Reject is for that).
- The plate box is suggested by itself on every outside photo, the extra "More outside photos" included: as soon as
  the review opens, each outside photo that has no box and no suggestion is sent to the vision model one after another,
  and the answer is kept on the photo with the image size (the vision route), so **Cover the plate** opens with the box
  drawn. Nothing is covered without a person pressing Cover.
- Interior windows (`web/src/features/listing/window-check.ts`): a dashboard or seats photo is cut out like the outside
  photos; the cut-out's transparency is what shows through the windows. The quality check, all four must hold:
  (1) 3% to 40% of the frame see-through (under 3%: no window, the photo is edited as before), (2) at least 75% of it in
  the top 65% of the frame, (3) at most 6 regions of 1% or more and at most 12 specks under 0.3%, (4) at most 6% of the
  pixels half transparent. Pass: the studio is placed behind the interior (`windows_replace_v1`). Fail, or a cut-out
  that cannot be read: the photo itself is blurred behind the interior (`windows_blur_v1`, flag `windows_blurred`), so
  a wrong cut only ever blurs part of the same photo.
- Two-seaters: a car with 2 seats or fewer (vehicle_listings.seats) needs no Rear seats photo (`app.shot_required`: the
  missing shots, the capture screen, the photo problems).

## 4. Alternative searches (0522)

Turo alternative, RideAlike alternative and Luup alternative (for rideshare drivers) are rental terms of the place
page multiplier (0095), each with its own words, so the pages read "Turo Alternative in Mississauga", "Luup Alternative
for Rideshare Drivers in Port Credit, Mississauga". Each note and first question say CarmaCars is not affiliated with,
approved or endorsed by the company named; nothing is said about the other company. They are in the sitemap files and
follow the quality gate and the canonical rules like every rental term.

## 5. Alerts at the right moment, and the app's links (0523)

- An owner is asked to turn alerts on in the owner portal once the application is approved (again after a later
  approval), staff (Admin, assistants, field) at their first sign-in, whatever the device remembers;
  `users.alerts_asked_at` keeps the answer for every device. A person with a device already on is never asked.
- Program settings `app_store_url` and `play_store_url` did not exist: built (Settings > Program, card The CarmaCars
  app). When set, a phone browser sees **Download the CarmaCars app** (the site footer and the alerts invitation) with
  its own store's link; never inside the app, never on a computer. `public.app_links()` reads them.
