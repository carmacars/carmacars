# Referral partners and the RentalCoverage affiliate link, October 25, 2026

The owner's request (October 22 to 23): referral programs for body shops and tow trucks (and, cheaply, dealerships and
insurance brokers) that send renters, and an affiliate program with RentalCoverage for the places the site suggests
coverage from another company.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0164_referral_partners.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0164`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0164_gen_guides.mjs`. Tests: `supabase/tests/99_referral_partners.sql` (88 assertions),
`web/src/features/referral/model.test.ts` and `flyer.test.ts`, and `web/tests/e2e/referral-partners.spec.ts`.

## 1. Referral partners

- **Table `referral_partners`**: kind (`body_shop`, `tow_truck`, `dealership`, `insurance_broker`), status (`pending`,
  `active`, `paused`, `ended`, `declined`), business, contact, phone, email, address, payout method, the kickback (flat
  cents or basis points of the rental charge), a unique `referral_code` (4 to 12 capitals and digits, never one an owner
  or field staff member holds), the private page version and where it came from (`admin` or `signup`).
- **Rate**: `program_settings.referral_partner_flat_cents` (5000) and `referral_partner_percent_bps` (1000) are the
  defaults the Add form starts with; each partner keeps its own rate. A rate change applies to rentals tied to the
  partner from then on; what is already earned keeps its rate.
- **Admin > People > Referral partners** (`/admin/referral-partners`): Partners, Kickbacks (the ledger) and Payments made
  tabs; Add a partner (address autofill), Approve or Decline a sign-up, Pause, Resume, End, Edit, Tie a booking (with a
  reason), New private page link. Every change is audited.
- **Sign-up**: `/partners/body-shops` and `/partners/tow-trucks` (in the sitemap) with a form (Turnstile, rate limited:
  `public.server_referral_partner_signup`). It creates a `pending` partner and a task for Admin. A pending partner earns
  nothing and its code ties no rental. There is no approval email: the Admin presses Write them their link.
- **Link, QR code, flyer**: `carmacars.ca/r/<CODE>` sets the `cc-ref` cookie for 60 days (owner and staff codes keep their
  30 days). `/admin/referral-partners/<id>/flyer` makes a one-page Letter PDF (pdf-lib, `qrcode-generator`) with the
  business, the code and a QR code of the link.
- **Attribution**: `app.resolve_referrer` (the booking trigger) now also looks at partner codes, after owner and field
  staff (one referrer per booking). Sources of a code: the cookie, the code typed in "Did someone refer you?" on the
  request to book (a code written with capitals or digits wins over the cookie; a plain word such as a first name does
  not), and "Referred by (partner code)" on Add a rental (`referral_code` in `app.manual_rental_create`; an unknown or
  inactive code is refused). A browser session cannot write `bookings.referral_partner_id` (guard trigger); Admin ties a
  booking with `referral_partner_credit`.
- **Earned when**: the booking is completed and paid in full (`app.booking_paid_in_full`, the rule the staff commission
  uses) and no payment is in dispute. Base: the rental charge after discounts only (`app.commission_base_cents`).
  Ledger `referral_partner_earnings`: `accruing`, `pending_approval`, `approved`, `paid`, `reversed`, `void`, with
  clawback rows for a refund or a lost dispute, one earning per booking and partner. Approve and Record a payment
  (`referral_partner_payouts`) follow the staff commission patterns.
- **Owner statements**: the kickback is a booking expense before the owner's share, added in
  `app.booking_staff_commission_cents` (used by `app.compute_booking_split`), so it sits on the same line as staff
  commissions.
- **Partner page** `/partners/portal/<id>/<key>`: the key is an HMAC of the partner id and the page version (a new link
  ends the old one). It shows the link, the code, referrals by the customer's first name and the month only, and the
  earnings by state. Never indexed (`/partners/portal/` is in the crawler rules).

## 2. RentalCoverage affiliate

- **Table `coverage_affiliate`** (one row): link (https only, a `{id}` in it is replaced by the affiliate ID), ID, the
  name shown, and a switch. It is off until an Admin pastes a link and keeps it on; with `{id}` and no ID it stays off.
- **Admin > Settings > RentalCoverage affiliate**: paste or change the link, clicks by place and month, the latest
  clicks, and `coverage_affiliate_payments` (what RentalCoverage paid, entered by hand: the site cannot see sales).
- **Where it shows** (only while on): Requirements and coverage, the credit card coverage guide and the coverage step of
  the request to book. The text says it is a separate company's optional product, not issued or handled by CarmaCars,
  that it does not replace the coverage option the renter chooses, that the renter should check its terms for a rental in
  Ontario, and that CarmaCars may earn a commission at no extra cost. It says nothing about what it covers. The link is
  `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"`.
- **Tracking**: every link goes to `/go/coverage?p=<place>[&b=<booking number>]`, which records a click
  (`coverage_clicks`: place, the `cc_vid` visitor cookie, the booking when known; rate limited per address) and redirects
  to the affiliate link with `utm_source=carmacars`, `utm_medium=referral`, `utm_campaign=coverage_<place>`. While the
  link is off, nothing is counted and the visitor lands on Requirements and coverage.

## 3. For the owner to decide or do

1. Join RentalCoverage's affiliate program, then paste the link (and ID) in Admin > Settings > RentalCoverage affiliate.
2. Approve the first sign-ups, press Write them their link and send the flyer.
3. Kickbacks appear on owner statements on the staff commission line. Check the owner agreement's wording on kickbacks.
4. Partners are not emailed when a payment is recorded; they see it on their private page.
5. A paid kickback reversed by a later refund stays on the ledger as a negative line for an Admin to settle.
