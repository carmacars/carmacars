# A higher deposit on a debit or prepaid card

Migration 0262. On a booking that takes a debit or prepaid card (the Admin's override, the site-wide setting, a car that
needs no credit card), the deposit hold on such a card is higher: Program settings, Deposit with a debit or prepaid card
(2: a $300 deposit becomes $600). An Admin can set another amount for one booking on its Money tab (Change the debit
deposit, with the reason; audited). It applies to the automatic hold of the pickup day, the deposit link and Collect a
card > The deposit hold: the pay page says "the deposit is $X with a debit or prepaid card" before the card is entered,
and a hold placed with the other kind of card than its amount was for is voided and asked again for the right amount on
the same link (nothing is held twice). The Money tab shows "Debit deposit: 2x ($600.00)"; the renter's offer and pay
pages show the debit amount once the booking takes such a card.

## Guides

No guide change: the amount is shown where the hold is taken; nothing to do differently.
