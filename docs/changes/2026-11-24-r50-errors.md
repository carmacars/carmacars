# Staff bug reports and server errors (Release 50: 4700)

## The open bug reports

- **BR-4** (Sales, /staff/sales-leads, "I cannot call any leads or clients"): every Call and Text from a Sales rep came
  back 400. Sales is shown masked numbers ("(647) •••-••11", 0970), and the server looked the real number up only by the
  masked text in phone_masks. That text keeps the area code and two digits, so among hundreds of leads several numbers
  share it, and the tie was broken only by an exact name (a lead with no name, or one person's two leads under two
  spellings, was refused). Numbers seen more than 30 days ago were refused too. Now the button also sends the lead or
  renter it belongs to. The server uses that record's number when it masks to what was shown, then phone_masks (90 days),
  then the leads, renters and conversations with that masked number. The real number still never reaches the browser
  (app.phone_unmask_for).
- **BR-3** (Admin, CC-10599): Renter approved by phone was refused once the booking moved to the renter's own
  insurance. Removing the Protection Plan line meant the charges no longer matched the open offer. Staff approving on
  the renter's word now accept the booking as it stands: the offer is sent again with the current charges (same
  expiry) and approved at once, with a note in the history. A renter accepting by themself is still asked to look at
  the new offer. The coverage itself has been approvable by hand since 3410.
- **BR-2** and **BR-1** (field, /staff/field and photo-shoot jobs): fixed in hotfix 3410 (field pages stay light inside the
  staff frame in dark mode; a photo shoot of a listed car starts new listing photos). Checked again in this release.
