## 2. Pickup and return

The pickup condition report's odometer and fuel (completed, or in progress) are in the facts of both documents.

- **Signed before pickup (online)**: completing the check-out makes version N, "as picked up": both documents with the
  Pickup record (CarmaCars) and boxes 26 and 28, the fuel out circle and the deposit received (Blind Spot), countersigned
  by the staff member who did the check-out, referring to the check-out report. The signed originals stay as signed. The
  renter gets both by email (`renter_agreement_version`, both PDFs attached).
- **Signed at pickup (in person)**: Sign now on this device renders the open agreement again once the readings are in and
  before anyone signs (the same facts give the same document: nothing is reissued otherwise), so the signed copy already
  holds them; the database keeps what the document printed (`rental_agreements.rendered_pickup`) and makes no separate
  copy at check-out when the readings match. The staff view says which applies.
- **Check-in** makes the closing copy: boxes 25 to 31, hours late, extra days, damages, fuel, the other return charges,
  the balance and what was paid; CarmaCars' Return record, the return charges and the final totals.

## 3. Dynamic agreements: versions

`agreement_versions` (with `agreement_version_signers`): version 1 is the agreement as the renter signed it (what it
printed at that moment, `signed_snapshot`); a trigger on every table the agreements read (the booking, its drivers and
lines, the renter and drivers' records, the car, the coverage proof, the deposit, the payments, swaps, condition
reports) queues the booking; ten seconds later (several changes make one version) the site compares (`app.agreement_plan`)
and renders both documents for what changed:

- **Material** (the dates, the price, the car, a driver added or removed, the coverage option): the version waits for the
  renter's signature and the affected drivers' (a driver added; every driver when the car, the dates or the coverage
  change). Each gets their own link (`/sign/agreement/<token>`) by email and text; the booking shows "Agreement v3
  waiting for signature" with Resend and "signs on this device"; customer service gets a task. Until then the earlier
  version applies. A change the renter already signed as an amendment (an accepted change or extension) is recorded as
  signed, with the amendment's number.
- **Correction** (an address typo, a plate written differently, a licence number fixed, the insurer's policy details): a
  corrected copy, filed and emailed, nothing to sign, in the history with what changed.
- Every version is in the booking's documents and the renter's, with the list of what changed (before, now), both PDFs,
  who signed or countersigned. A version still waiting is replaced by a newer one.

Before signing, the open agreement follows the booking the same way (it is rendered again; the same facts give the same
document). The long-rentals branch (`longrent`: booking edits after the start, fee changes, renter corrections, monthly
periods) writes the same tables, so its edits make versions with no code of its own; its monthly periods' agreements
are its next checkpoint and can use `server_agreement_plan` and `server/agreements/versions.ts` the same way.

## 4. The completeness check

`app.agreement_missing` lists what the agreements still print blank, each "required" when a claim needs it: the renter's
name, date of birth, address and mobile; each driver's licence number, province and expiry; the car's plate and VIN; the
coverage option and, for OPCF 27, the insurer and policy number, for card coverage, the issuer and last four digits; for an
insurance replacement the claim number, the adjuster, the insured's policy number and the insured vehicle. Recommended:
the licence class, each additional driver's address, the car's colour and fleet number, the policy expiry and province,
the adjuster's phone, CarmaCars' HST number.

The booking's Rental agreements card lists them with an input each (Save the missing fields). Check-out
(`app.complete_handover`) refuses a booking missing a required field: "The rental agreement is missing what a claim
needs: ...". An Admin can override with the reason (`agreement_overrides`, in the booking's history). Switch:
`agreement_claim_check` (on).

## 5. Signatures that hold up

Every rental agreement signature (the renter remotely, an additional driver's link, in person on a staff device), every
amendment and every new version goes through the same steps (`EsignFlow`), and the database refuses a signature
without them (`app.record_signature`, `app.sign_booking_amendment`, `server_agreement_version_sign`; switch
`esign_strict`, on):

1. **Identity**: a 6-digit code by text (the CarmaCars number, Quo) or by email to the signer's contact on file; only its
   hash is kept, 5 tries, 10 minutes, 5 codes a session. The licence on file is shown (front, back, selfie, verified).
2. **Read**: the signer says they read the documents.
3. **Initials** on the key terms, worded from the booking: damage and the deductible, the deposit and later charges, fuel,
   kilometres and late returns, the coverage choice, cancellation (drivers: the first, third and fourth; an amendment:
   its payment).
4. **Consents**: to sign electronically (Ontario Electronic Commerce Act, 2000), to receive records electronically, to the
   card on file being charged as the agreement says (the renter).
5. **A drawn signature** (finger, pen or mouse). Typed names only with the accessibility fallback an Admin turns on
   (`esign_typed_fallback`, off).

A session belongs to one document: when the agreement is made again while the page is open (the renter saves their
address or licence, so the PDF and its signer change), the steps start again for the new document.

Each step is an `esign_events` row (append-only): its time, IP address, device, and the place when the device shares it.
Every signed PDF (CarmaCars', Blind Spot's, a version's) ends with a **certificate of completion**: each signer, how they
signed (remotely, or in person and who collected it), the code and where it went, the licence on file, each initial and
consent with its time, the place, IP and device, the audit trail, the SHA-256 of each document before signing. The signed
file's own SHA-256 is recorded when it is stored: `/verify` hashes a file in the browser and says whether CarmaCars
recorded it (tamper evidence). A PAdES signature (an X.509 signature inside the PDF) is not added: it needs a signing
certificate from a trust service, which CarmaCars does not have; the recorded hashes and the certificate page give the
same evidence for a dispute.

## 6. The dispute evidence pack

When a card payment is disputed (a `disputes` row: the long-rentals branch records them from Stripe's
`charge.dispute.created`), the pack is built by itself (`dispute_packs`, the agreements' job queue) and the booking's
documents are put on legal hold. One PDF: a cover with what a card network asks (the customer, the service and its dates,
the card's CVC, address and postal code checks and 3D Secure from Stripe, the signing trail), the signed agreements with
their certificates, amendments, receipts, the check-out and check-in reports with readings and photos (while under
Stripe's 4.5 MB), the licence images, payments and charges, every email and text to the renter and the booking's history.
With Stripe connected it is uploaded and staged as the dispute's evidence (not submitted); an Admin checks it and presses
Submit to Stripe. Build the dispute evidence pack makes one at any time (an Admin or Finance).

## 7. Background work

`agreement_jobs` (version, version_signed, dispute_pack): run by the PDF jobs' requests (`/api/jobs/pdf`, the Worker's
five-minute cron through `server_jobs_due`), kicked right after a check-out or check-in, retried after 1, 2, 5, 15 and 60
minutes, then a task for an Admin. A booking touched again while its job runs runs again.

## 8. What the owner does

`agreement_claim_check` and `esign_strict` (on). For insurance replacements, fill the insured's policy and vehicle on the
booking's Rental agreements card (the completeness check asks for them).
