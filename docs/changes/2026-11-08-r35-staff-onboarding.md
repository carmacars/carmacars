## B. Inactivity lock (coordinator item 1)

`staff_inactive_lock_days` (10) and `staff_inactive_warn_days` (7). `app.job_staff_inactivity` (in the hiring job, every 15 minutes) warns by email (`staff_inactivity_warning`) and locks; Admins are alerted (`staff_inactive_locked`). `app.door_begin` refuses every call of a locked account; `server_signed_in` records each sign-in (the staff list's last sign-in) and the sign-in page says "Account locked for inactivity". Unlock on Settings > Staff. Last use is kept to the hour (`staff_profiles.last_seen_at`). Staff on file at 1060 count from the deploy.
