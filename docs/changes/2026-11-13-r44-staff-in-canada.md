# Staff who live in Canada are never contracted or shown as being in the Philippines (Release 44: 2820)

Migration `2820_r44_staff_in_canada.sql`.

- **The agreement's terms**: a staff member who lives in Canada (their location says Canada; or, with no location on file, they work in person, do field work or are paid by Interac e-Transfer or to a Canadian bank) gets Canada, CAD and payment by Interac e-Transfer or direct deposit on their agreement, a remote Sales person included. The assistant defaults of Program settings (the Philippines, USD, OnlineJobs.ph) stay for everyone else.
- **The records**: every active staff member with no location, or one that said the Philippines, who works in person, does field work or is paid in Canada, was set to Canada once (Toronto when no city was on file).
