# Offers and rentals made easy, October 24, 2026

The owner: "The booking flow has fundamental flaws. U need to make it easier for us to adjust offers and to send out
offers without requests and to input rentals." Then: "I don't see anywhere to accept a deposit in cash and rental payment
on card or e-transfer and vice versa", "Need a way to bill through Stripe through the website so we don't have to use
Stripe directly. For example for deposits in person for credit cards" and "What's the difference between protection plan
and coverage price? That's very confusing. Why are there 2 options?"

Migration `supabase/migrations/0129_offers_and_rentals.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0129`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0129_gen_guides.mjs`. Tests: `supabase/tests/82_offers_and_rentals.sql`, `web/src/features/offers/model.test.ts`,
`web/src/server/pay-token.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/offers-and-rentals.spec.ts` (both widths); the existing rental specs read the
new "paid by" selector.

## What was slow (the audit)

- **An offer needed a request first.** Staff typed a quote (New quote: name, email, mobile, date of birth, licence class,
  car, dates), pressed Create the request, landed on the review screen, typed the price again and only then sent the
  offer: two screens, no price before the request existed, and the renter got a "request received" email for a request
  they never made. The customer was found by email only; a caller already on file by phone was made a second time.
- **An offer could not be changed.** The review screen went read-only as soon as the offer was out: Withdraw to edit
  (the renter got a "withdrawn" email), edit, then send again (a second email). An approved, unpaid offer the same.
- **A rental needed a forced check.** Add a rental would not submit before Check the calendar and the price; the renter
  search needed its Find button; one way of payment for the whole booking (no deposit in cash with the rental by card,
  nothing for a Turo payout or an insurer billed later); no "signed on paper"; no "does not block the calendar".
- **Money was one lump.** "Record a payment received another way" for the whole booking, Finance only; a deposit could
  only be a card hold or charge taken by a process the web code never ran; no way to start a card payment from the
  booking (staff used Stripe's dashboard).
- **Two Protection Plan fields.** The review screen had "Coverage price" (it set the coverage_sold fee) and, among the fees,
  "CarmaCars Protection Plan" (the same fee line, because the screen's list of coverage lines missed coverage_sold).
  Two inputs for one charge, under two names.

## What changed

### 1. Send an offer without a request

Admin > Bookings > **Send an offer** (`/admin/offers/new`; also on Bookings and Requests), and **Send an offer** for
customer service and sales (`/va/offers/new`, and beside a car in Find a car). One screen: the customer found by name,
phone or email as you type, or added (name, mobile, email, date of birth; one already on file with that email, or that
phone when no email is typed, is used, never made twice, and the screen shows the match); the car by car code, plate or
name with Free or Not free for the dates; the dates and places (delivery with the distance, airport); the price from the
rate sheet, a total or a rate per day, plus tax or tax included, with its reason; the kilometres, promo code, deposit,
discount and each fee (an Admin); how damage is covered and the Protection Plan price; add-ons; how the rental and the
deposit are paid; who found the client; the message, how long it is open, and Email, Text, both or neither. The right
side is the offer as the renter will see it, priced by the database as you type (`app.staff_offer_preview`: everything
made and priced, then rolled back).

`app.staff_offer_create` makes the request as the quote form does (`app.staff_quote_create`, so the sales choice, the
custom price and its flags, the commission work unchanged), applies the review screen's edit, and sends the offer
(`app.send_offer`). The renter approves, signs and pays as for any offer; the calendar holds the car only then. No
"request received" to the renter and no new-request alert to staff for a request staff made (`app.offer_direct`).
Email only: `_email_only` in the offer email's payload keeps it from turning into a text; Text: the Program settings
wording of 0085 (`phone_sms_offer_sent_text`) with the offer link, queued to the renter's mobile as a staff text
(`app.offer_text`). Save without sending keeps it as a request.

### 2. An offer edited in place

**Edit the offer** on a booking that is Offered, or approved and not paid: the review screen stays editable, highlights
each changed field, lists the before and after, and **Save and send the update** sends the next version
(`app.offer_revise`: the offer goes back to requested inside the call, the edit is applied, the offer is sent again; no
"withdrawn" email). **Save without telling the renter** saves it quietly. An approved offer edited goes back to Offered;
an agreement opened for the old version is voided (the renter signs the new one). A confirmed booking: **Change the
booking** opens Propose a change, as before. **Withdraw the offer** remains, for taking it back for good. The review
screen previews an offer that is out as if it were open (0030's preview applied the edit to a request only).

### 3. Payments by part and method

Every booking has how its rental and its deposit are paid (`bookings.rental_pay_method`: card, e-Transfer, cash, cheque,
bank transfer, platform payout, insurer; `bookings.deposit_pay_method`: card, cash, e-Transfer, none), set on the offer
screen, the review screen and Add a rental. On the booking's Money tab, **Money by part** shows what is open for the
rental, the fees and later charges, the deposit and the card on file:

- **Record a payment**: what it covers (rental, fees, deposit, later charge) and how (e-Transfer with its reference, cash,
  cheque, bank transfer, platform payout, insurer). `app.record_booking_payment` writes the payment with its method and
  `manual_payments.covers`, ties the lines it pays once that part is paid in full (statements and owner revenue read the
  lines' payment), and sends the renter a receipt. **Print** prints a receipt to hand over. Admin, Finance, and customer
  service and sales when Program settings let assistants take "paid another way", within the single limit.
- **A deposit in cash or by e-Transfer** is a deposit row of method `offline` with `paid_method` (`app.record_offline_deposit`).
  The settlement after the return works as for a charged deposit; what it releases is never refunded to a card: the
  refund payment is in cash or by e-Transfer, Finance gets the task "Give the deposit back the way it came", and
  **Returned** records it with the reference (`app.settle_deposit` wraps 0020's, `app.deposit_return_offline`).
- **An offer paid another way** is confirmed by staff once the renter approved the current version, signed (on screen or
  on paper) and the money is in (`app.confirm_offline_booking`; recording the rental's payment confirms it at once).
  Customer service gets the task "Confirm a booking paid another way" when the renter approves. The confirmation rule
  of 0011 and 0025 now lets a rental billed to the insurer or paid through a platform confirm without a payment at
  booking, and a booking whose deposit is cash, e-Transfer or none confirm without a card on file. The renter's pay page
  shows how to pay (where to send the e-Transfer, from Program settings, E-Transfer email, or the site's public email)
  in place of the card form.

### 4. Card actions through Stripe, from the booking

**Collect a card** (Admin, every assistant function, field staff): a payment of a part, the deposit hold, or saving the card
(no charge). The renter types the card in Stripe's Payment Element on the pay page `/pay/<token>`: on the staff device
handed to them (**Open the card form**, the link works for 2 hours), or by a link texted (Quo) or emailed (the booking's
message) to them (3 days). The token is 32 random bytes; the database keeps only its SHA-256 (`payment_requests`).
The result is read again from Stripe on the server, by the pay page and by the webhook (`purpose: staff_request`), and
recorded by `public.server_payreq_settle`: the card is saved on file, a charge is a payment of its part (with a receipt),
a hold is a deposit of method card_hold, held, with its expiry (about 7 days; the Money tab shows the days left). A rental
longer than the hold limit takes no hold: save the card, then charge the deposit.
**Charge the saved card** (Finance, an Admin): a later charge or the deposit, with the reason the renter's receipt shows
(`app.card_charge_begin` / `card_charge_finish` around Stripe's off-session charge). **Capture the hold** (all or part,
with the reason; the rest is released) and **Release the hold** (`app.hold_action_begin` / `finish`, partial capture
through `amount_to_capture`). Refunds stay the approved refund of 0032. Staff never type a card number: Program settings,
**Staff may type a card the renter reads out**, is off by default and stays off until Stripe enables MOTO on the account.

### 5. Add a rental, fast

Quick start buttons (Phone, Walk-in, Turo or another platform, Insurance replacement; for an Admin, Owner using own car
and Placeholder) set the source, the way of payment and the rest in one tap. The renter search runs as you type, with
"Add them as a new renter" when nobody matches. The calendar and the price are checked by themselves once the car and
the dates are in (Check again stays). One selector for how the rental is paid (e-Transfer, cash, card on file, payment
link, platform payout, insurer billed later, cheque, bank transfer, unpaid), one for the deposit (card hold, cash,
e-Transfer, none for an Admin), **Already signed on paper** (no signing email; a task asks for the scan) or send it to
sign, and **Blocks the car's calendar** (untick it, with the reason, for an insurance replacement with dates not known
yet). The wrapper of `app.manual_rental_create` records the payout or the insurer and the deposit in the same call; an
assistant may add a rental paid through a platform or billed to the insurer.

### 6. One name for the Protection Plan

The renter's option 3 is the Protection Plan, and its price (the `coverage_sold` fee, from Settings > Protection Plan
prices or set on the booking) is one field: **Protection Plan price** on the review screen and the offer screen. The
duplicate "Coverage price" field is gone (the screen counts coverage_sold as the plan's line, not as a fee). No internal
cost of the plan exists in the data, so there is nothing to show to Admin only. Staff wording follows: the car's tab
**Fees and Protection Plan**, its card **Protection Plan prices (this car or its class)**, "How damage to the car is
covered" with "Option 3: the Protection Plan" on Add a rental and New quote, and the database's refusals ("changes the
Protection Plan price").

## Privileges

Every new function is the service role's only, reached through the staff door (`app.door`: ops reads `offer_form`,
`offer_preview`, `find_customers`, `booking_money`; ops actions `offer_create`, `offer_revise`, `record_booking_payment`,
`confirm_offline_booking`, `deposit_return_offline`, `payreq_create`, `payreq_send`, `payreq_cancel`, `card_charge_begin`,
`card_charge_finish`, `hold_action_begin`, `hold_action_finish`; `offer_revise` also as admin_do and va_do). Who may: send and
edit offers, an Admin, customer service and sales (sales not once approved), within the review screen's limits for
assistants (the rental charge with its reason, the Protection Plan price for customer service, 48 hours at most);
record payments, Admin, Finance, and customer service and sales when allowed, within the single limit; collect a card,
every staff member who deals with renters; charge the saved card, capture and release, Finance and Admins. Commission
approval by an Admin is unchanged. `payment_requests` has the admin policy and the audit trigger.
