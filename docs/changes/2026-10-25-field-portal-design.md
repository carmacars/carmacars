# The field portal, designed for the phone in the cold, October 25, 2026

The owner's words: "need to make each staff portal extremely easy to use and extremely good UI. Attractive, appealing and
interactive." This is the field portal (`/field/**`): drivers and handover staff standing outside, one hand, gloves, bright
sun or night, patchy data. Nothing in the business logic changed: the same server actions, database functions, routes and
`data-testid`s. Migration `supabase/migrations/0159_field_portal_design.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0159`) only republishes the
guide chapters, generated by `supabase/seed/0159_gen_guides.mjs`.

## 1. What changed on the screens

| Where | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My jobs (`web/src/app/field/page.tsx`) | One bordered card a job, in a list | The next job is one black card at the top: the time set large with "in 25 min" (red when late), the kind and its status, the car, the plate as it looks on the car, the place with **Open in maps** and **Call** with the renter's first name (from the job's own read, `field_read('job')`), what to put in the car and the office's note, then the one button that starts it. The rest of the day follows as a timeline (time on the left, a dot per job, green once done). Today and Tomorrow are a segmented switch with counts. |
| Vehicle moves | **End the move** ended the move on the first tap, with the fields above it | **Start a move** and **End the move** open a panel with the fields and their own confirm buttons (**Start the move now**, **End the move now**) and Cancel; "Saved." confirms. A gloved tap no longer ends a move. Damage found later works the same way (**Save the damage**). |
| One job (`web/src/app/field/jobs/[id]/page.tsx`) | Plain header | The same black card: time, date, plate, **Open in maps**, **Call**, the office's note; the booking's facts fold away under the checklist once the job runs. Acknowledge and Start each say what they are for. |
| Check-out and check-in (`web/src/features/field/Checklist.tsx`) | "Step n of m" and the screen | A sticky head: step count, the plate, a progress bar (one segment a step), and where the answers are (Saved, Saving, or "No signal. Answers kept on this phone." with **Try again**). The question is set large; Back and Next are 68 px tall in the bottom bar. |
| Ready check | Green and red lines | A summary first ("Ready: the car may be released", or "2 of 6 to fix before the car is released"), then each line with a tick or a cross in a disc (never colour alone) and its fix under it. |
| Yes or No | Two buttons | Two 80 px buttons with a tick and a cross; Yes turns green, No red. |
| Odometer | Number field | A large number field with "km" and the last reading (or the reading at pickup) under it. |
| Fuel or charge | A slider | Eight segments to tap, E to F as the car's gauge reads (the slider under them is the same value for the keyboard and screen readers). |
| Keys | Small plus and minus | Round 68 px plus and minus with the count set large. |
| Photo set | A list of 19 rows | Camera first: the next photo to take is a big card (its name, what to frame, **Take this photo**), with a meter of photos taken; all 19 shots stay below as tiles, ticked when done; a failed upload shows **Send again now**. |
| Done and Stop | A heading | A large tick (or cross) with the result. |
| Other job kinds (`JobChecklist.tsx`) | Numbered cards | A meter of steps answered; each card ticks once answered. |
| The frame (`layout.tsx`, `web/src/styles/field.css`) | "CarmaCars field" in bold | The wordmark with a "field" tag; 48 px menu buttons; 56 px buttons throughout. |

Offline: a step whose save fails for want of signal moves on (the answers are kept on the phone as they are given, and the
completion sends them all again); the save is retried when the phone is back online or on **Try again**. A refused answer
still stops the step. Acknowledge, Start and the moves say "No connection" instead of failing silently.

Design: black and white with CarmaCars red only for the thing to press, green and red only for yes and no; contrast at
least 7:1 for text on the job card. Dark mode follows the phone (the field frame only; the staff check-out under Admin
and VA stays light). Motion only answers a tap (the step sliding in, the progress bar, the done tick) and only under
`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

Not done: a photo of the car on the job card needs the car's photo in `field_read('jobs')` (a database change, out of scope
here); the plate stands in for it.

## 2. The intermittent address test

`web/tests/e2e/address-autofill.spec.ts`, row "field portal: vehicle move, now at" (`test.fixme` since release 8). Cause:
the row opens the sheet by pressing `move-end`, but that button was the submit itself: it ended the move with no place, and
the page's refresh removed `#fb-to_place` while the test typed into it, so no suggestion list appeared. Whether it failed
depended on whether the refresh landed before the suggestions (and on whether the move could end at all for that user), hence
intermittent. Now `move-end` opens the panel and only **End the move now** ends the move; the row waits for the field and the
`fixme` is gone.

## Guides

`docs/guides/src/guides/field.py`, `docs/guides/content/field.json` and `docs/guides/src/field.html` changed by hand
(export.py did not run here; the PDF and its photos are rebuilt by the guides run after the deploy):

- `field/my-jobs`: Your job list: the next job is the big card at the top (time, how long until it, plate, **Open in maps**,
  a button to call the renter, what to put in the car), the rest in time order. Open a job: the call button. Move a car:
  **Start a move**, Car and Why, From and the odometer, **Start the move now**; **End the move**, Now at and the odometer,
  **End the move now**.
- `field/a-handover`: Fuel or charge: "tap the level on the gauge, in eighths from E to F" (was "slide"). Photo set: **Take
  this photo** for the next one. The tip on photos without a connection: "No signal on a step? It says "Answers kept on this
  phone" and you can go on: press Try again, or they are sent when the signal returns."
- `field/quick-reference`: Move a car: **Start a move**, then **End the move** and **End the move now**.

0159 publishes the three chapters where the live text is still ours and keeps them as suggestions where an Admin edited one.
