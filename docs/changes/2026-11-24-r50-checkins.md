# Monthly check-in visits for long-term renters (Release 50: 4000 to 4002)

Migrations `4000_r50_checkins.sql`, `4001_r50_checkins_notices.sql` and `4002_r50_checkins_guides.sql`, generators `supabase/seed/4001_gen_catalogue.mjs` and `supabase/seed/4002_gen_guides.mjs`.

## What it does

- **Who**: a rental of at least `checkin_min_days` (28), a long-term weekly rental (`rolling_rentals`, 2981), a rideshare or delivery driver (`rideshare_doc_requests`, 1303), or any booking staff switch on (`checkin_booking_switch`). On by default (`checkin_enabled`); a booking can be switched off. Installment and rent-to-own plans have no record of their own yet: staff switch those bookings on.
- **When**: every `checkin_interval_days` (30) from the pickup or the last visit. The visit is made `checkin_lead_days` (5) before it is due with the renter's reminder (`renter_checkin_reminder`, email and a text as the message channel policy (2971) and the renter's text opt-out (0085) allow), and one gentle follow-up on the due date if no time is picked (`renter_checkin_followup`). Never a requirement, never a fee.
- **Renter**: the trips page and the booking page show "Next check-in visit: around <date>" with why it helps and **Pick a time**: office slots (`checkin_slot_minutes`, `checkin_day_start_hour` to `checkin_day_end_hour`, Monday to Saturday, `checkin_slot_capacity` a slot) or **By request** with when and where suits them. The time can be changed or taken back.
- **Staff** (Admin, customer service, Fleet, field work): **Check-in visits** (Admin: Cars; Fleet: Checks; customer service: Renters; field: Today) with Booked, Due, Overdue (soft) and Done; each visit's checklist: photos of the four sides and the odometer (required), the odometer reading, new damage (where and what), tire tread, fluids and warning lights, notes. **Book a time for the renter**, **Skip this month** (with the reason), **Start a visit now**. Fleet and the Admins are told when a time is picked (`staff_checkin_booked`) and the morning of a visit (`staff_checkin_today`); never a text.
- **Records**: the reading goes into the maintenance tracker (`vehicle_odometer_readings`, source `checkin`) and the tread onto the tires on the car; the next service due is kept with the visit. Anything to look after raises the Fleet To do `checkin_maintenance`; damage logs a damage intake (the Damage tab) with the Admin To do `checkin_damage`. Every step is on the booking's timeline; the owner's car page lists the visits (date, km, tread, condition, next service; never the notes or photos).
- **Guide me**: the step file `checkin-visit` ("Do a check-in visit") in the Admin, Fleet, customer service and field portals.
- **Security**: new tables with row-level security, `admin_all` and the audit trigger; every function security definer with `search_path = public, app, pg_temp`, the server role only. Staff go through the door (`checkin_queue`, `checkin_visit`, `checkin_do`); the renter through `server_checkin_renter` and `server_checkin_book`, which check the booking is theirs. Photos are stored by the server under the booking (`bookings/<id>/checkin/<uuid>`) and the checklist accepts only those keys.
- **The job**: `app.checkin_sweep()` every 15 minutes (pg_cron where available).

## Guides

- `va/fleet`: Check-in visits, the checklist and what it raises.
- `va/customer-service`: the queue, booking a time for a renter, the switch.
- `admin/maintenance-and-service`: the settings, the switch and the records.
- `field/my-jobs`: doing a visit at the car.
- `renter/during-your-trip`: the monthly check-in visit and Pick a time.
