# Search: car copy written by itself, landing pages, guides, IndexNow, Google reviews (October 19, 2026)

Migration `0061_seo.sql`; tests `supabase/tests/40_seo.sql` (and suites 03, 07, 11, 19 for the new rules); unit tests
`web/src/features/seo/copy.test.ts`; end to end `web/tests/e2e/seo.spec.ts` (phone 375 and desktop 1440); the SEO audit
`web/tests/lint/seo-audit.mjs`; Lighthouse `web/tests/lint/lighthouse.mjs`. No applied migration was edited.

The owner's goal: carmacars.ca ranks for car rental searches in Toronto and the GTA, and no person types listing copy.
This changes two rules of `docs/blueprint/14-seo.md` and `09-listing-pipeline.md` that said machine-written text is never
published without a person's edit: the owner decided that copy written by the model from the database's facts is
published by itself when it passes the checks below (section 1). A person may still edit; nobody has to.

## 1. Car copy, written by itself

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Which cars | Every real car (not a sample) with a listing that is not unpublished, not retired, exited or Turo only (`app.car_copy_eligible`) |
| The facts | `app.car_copy_facts`: year, make, model, trim, colour, body, seats, doors, fuel, transmission, drivetrain, luggage, engine and equipment from the specs or the VIN decode, the classes; the rates in force as text (daily, weekend day, weekly, monthly with its days, km a day, extra km, deposit, delivery and return pickup fees, HST); the area, city, delivery and airports; the rules (minimum age, the young driver bands this car takes, licence, card, identity check, longest booking); what every rental includes; the public photos; the staff note. Never the VIN, plate, owner, odometer or address |
| The structure | `car_copy.copy`: search title (25 to 60 characters), search description (90 to 155), heading (20 to 70), introduction (50 to 130 words, two paragraphs), highlights (3 to 6), good for (2 to 4), what's included (3 to 5, from the facts only), pickup and delivery, requirements (3 to 5), questions (4 to 6), a description per public photo (alt text, under 125 characters, naming the model). The specs table and the rates are rendered from the data, not written |
| When it is written | On add (the first scan), when the facts change (a rate, a spec, the area, a photo approved: the hash of the facts moves), on Write again, and once for every real car already on file (the first scans after the deploy). The scan runs with every claim of the search job (every five minutes) |
| At once | The plain copy from the facts (`app.car_copy_template`), so a new car's page is never empty and publishing never waits; the written copy replaces it within minutes |
| The writer | Workers AI (`@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast`), through `/api/jobs/seo`, called by the Worker's cron every five minutes (`custom-worker.ts`). Prompt and style guide: `web/src/features/seo/copy.ts` (`carSystemPrompt`, `carUserPrompt`) |
| The checks (server) | `carCopyProblems`: every length and count; no long dash, no exclamation mark; the brand written CarmaCars; never the numbered company; no "insurance" or "coverage" sold by us (the Protection Plan rule) and no "full coverage"; no AI-sounding or sales words (60 built in, plus Program settings `listing_banned_phrases`); never the VIN, plate, odometer or owner; **every number must be in the facts** (amounts, seats, years, ages, kilometres, spelled numbers too); no claim of equipment, fuel economy, range, power, safety rating, condition or a price promise that the facts do not support; unique text: under `seo_copy_similarity_max_bps` (50%) of shared five-word sequences with another car of the same model, under 80% with any other car |
| The checks (database) | `app.car_copy_problems` again before publishing (lengths, dashes, the numbered company, banned phrases), then the listing lint of 0022 |
| Retry | A failing answer is asked again with the problems named, the last time strictly (temperature 0), `seo_copy_max_attempts` (3) in a run. If every attempt fails, the page keeps its current text and the car is tried again after `seo_copy_retry_hours` (6). Out of neurons: stops at once, tried later |
| Published | `app.car_copy_apply`: the listing's headline, description, features, questions and search text follow it (old screens and the listing lint keep working), each public photo gets its alt text, the car's address goes to IndexNow, and an approved listing that waited for its copy is published |
| A person's edit | Admin, Fleet or customer service: the listing review and the vehicle's Listing tab show the copy card (`CarCopyPanel`). Change, Save and lock: the field is kept by every later version until Unlock. Audit log `car_copy_edit`, `car_copy_unlock` |
| No more typing | The listing review no longer has copy text boxes; the listing pipeline's draft step stores a plain draft without calling the model (`plainCopy`), so neurons are spent once |
| The budget | Program settings `seo_ai_daily_neurons` (4,000) for search copy, and the shared `chat_ai_total_daily_neurons` (10,000, the free allowance) for all Workers AI use. Each call is logged in `provider_calls` (`seo_car`, `seo_page`, `seo_article`) |
| Listing lint | `listing_headline_chars` 20 to 70, `listing_description_words` 40 to 160, `listing_features_count` 3 to 12 (the copy's structure); the banned phrases gained the AI words |

On the car page: the heading, the introduction, About this car (Highlights, Good for, What's included), the requirements
and pickup lines, the questions, the car's own reviews, the links to the landing pages it belongs on, and the breadcrumb
Home > Fleet > Make Model > Year Make Model Trim.

typed amounts (`articleProblems`): young drivers, 407 ETR tolls, what you need, Pearson airport, monthly vs weekly, winter

## 5. Google review request

`renter_google_review_request` (catalogue 155 emails): `google_review_delay_days` (3) after a booking closes, only when
`site_settings.google_place_id` is set, with the same rules as the on-site request of 0025: not unsubscribed, nothing
open or unpaid on the renter, and a booking that had a problem (an incident, a dispute, an unpaid balance, a damage
charge) waits for the staff decision; only a yes sends either request. One per booking (`marketing_sends`); nothing is
offered for a review; the email asks for an honest review, good or bad, and links to the profile's review form.

Google's policy forbids selectively soliciting positive reviews. The hold on problem bookings is the owner's decision
of 0025 (a person decides, the system never excludes by rating); keep the yes as the usual answer to stay within it.

On-site reviews already exist (0016, 0034): published only with the renter's consent, shown in full on the car's page,
and marked up on that car only as above.

## 8. Build and deploy

- Rebased on 0062 (calendar sync, recovery emails, site check). Production applied 0062 before this migration; the
  database suite was also run in that order (all migrations but 0061, then 0061): all passed. The guides block
  releases only this change's chapters against 0062's text (base commit 5391afe).
- Deploy run 39289461880 (0062) stopped while building the Worker: prerendering `/rent/mercedes-benz-glc300` hit the
  anon role's 3 s statement timeout (57014) three times, though the call takes about 75 ms. Now the build does not
  prerender model pages, landing pages and guides (they render on the first visit and are cached for the hour, as
  before), and during `next build` only a database read cancelled by a timeout or a lock (57014, 55P03) is tried twice
  more after a pause (`web/src/lib/db/supabase.ts`). A visitor's request is never retried.
- Write again on a car (and Unlock) now goes ahead of the cars already waiting (`car_copy.asked_at`), so the copy
  asked for is written in the run the button starts.
- Deploy run 39304715018 stopped before deploying: the crawl found a hydration error on `/va/live` (0060), where
  "3 min ago" can turn to "4 min ago" between the server and the browser. Those relative times are now marked as
  allowed to differ (`suppressHydrationWarning`).
- Deploy run 39310793167 (0061 and 0063) applied both migrations and then stopped while building: the sitemaps were
  pre-rendered and their reads were cancelled at the 3 s statement timeout, each request hanging past 60 s. The likely
  cause (production could not be queried from this session to prove it): every DDL statement of a migration asks the
  database API to reload its schema cache, and the free database stays busy for minutes after two large migrations.
  Run 39298010067, which applied no migration, built normally, and the same reads take about 75 ms afterwards. Now:
  the sitemaps are read on each request (`dynamic = 'force-dynamic'`, cached by the CDN for the hour), never during
  the build; during the build each worker reads each public row once (`repo.ts`: 145 reads became 33); and the deploy
  waits after migrations until the database API answers five reads in a row within a second (at most 10 minutes) before
  building. The remaining 33 build reads are the cached public pages (home, fleet, hubs, legal pages): rendering them on
  every request instead would cost Worker CPU on each visit (Error 1102 risk).
- Deploy run 39319314247 stopped on the crawl: `/admin/settings/staff` was 1 px wider than a 375 px phone, from a long
  test account address in the Admin access list. A long address in an admin list now wraps.
- Migration `0068_seo_speed.sql` (suite 47). The root cause of the sitemap timeouts was in 0061: the sitemap dated each
  landing page by reading the whole public fleet once per page (about 5 s with 20 cars locally, past the anon role's
  3 s timeout), and a landing page read the fleet on each render (about 0.5 s locally; the live check of run 39334242566
  had one answer 500). Each landing page now keeps a snapshot of its cars and prices, written from one fleet read every
  10 minutes (pg_cron `carmacars-seo-snapshots`) and by the search job, used while a stamp of the cars, listings, rates,
  photos and classes is unchanged (otherwise the page reads the fleet live, as before); the sitemap dates a landing page
  from the row. Locally: sitemap 4.9 s to 42 ms, a landing page 0.5 s to 0.1 s, the hub 0.6 s to 41 ms.
  Also: the car copy scan (every search job, Write again, an edit) evaluated `site_facts()` several times per car and
  took 17 to 40 s with 41 cars; it now reads it once per scan: 157 ms, the same facts and hashes.
