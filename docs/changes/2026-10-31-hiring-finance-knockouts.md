# Every role: basic spoken English or under a year of experience is not invited (0219)

Two minimum requirements now apply to every role on carmacars.ca/careers:

- Spoken English: "Basic: I am more comfortable in writing" is a knock-out. Field staff and Sales in person, which did not
  ask, now ask it (just before the start date), with the same answers and points as the remote roles.
- Experience: "Under 1 year" on the role's years question is a knock-out (every remote role and Sales in person). Field
  staff keeps its own minimum: two years of driving.

A knocked-out candidate gets no link to book a call: the role's referral rule offers the next role with the reason, or the
polite decline. Applications already received keep their status.

## Guides

No guide change: the hiring guide explains knock-outs and the role offered instead; which answers knock out is in the questions themselves.
