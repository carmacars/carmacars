# Release 48, part MAP: staff and app users on the live map

Migrations `3350_r48map_people_locations.sql` (catalogue block: `supabase/seed/3350_gen_catalogue.mjs`) and
`3351_r48map_guides_academy.sql` (guides block: `supabase/seed/3351_gen_guides.mjs`). Schema marker 3351. Built on r48-trk.

The owner (Nov 20): staff locations and app users' locations show on the live map in Admin and in the staff portals that have
the map; staff only on shift or on a job, after a one-time electronic monitoring policy (Ontario ESA Part XI.1); renters and
owners only with the phone's permission and only around a booking's pickup or return; everyone can turn it off except staff
during an active job; only the latest position plus job points, no history.

## What was built

### Database (3350)

- `location_prefs` (sharing on by default, the policy version accepted; audited), `person_locations` (one row per person, the
  latest position only, never audited) and `location_job_points` (a job's, pickup's or return's points, one every 2 minutes at
  most, deleted after 30 days). RLS on, no grants to anon or authenticated; everything goes through the door.
- `app.location_state` decides on every report: staff (Admin, Fleet, customer service, Sales, Finance, field) after the policy
  and only on shift (an open clock entry or field shift) or on an active field job (in progress, or due within the hour);
  renters and owners two hours either side of a confirmed or on-rent booking's pickup or return. A report that does not
  qualify deletes the latest position at once.
- Door: `ops_do location_report | location_prefs_save | location_policy_accept`, `ops_read my_location | live_people`.
- Reads: Admin and Fleet see everyone; customer service and Sales see renters at a pickup or return (what their Visitors map
  already shows); Finance and field staff see no one (their Car map shows cars only).
- Purge: `app.job_tracker` (the 5-minute job) runs `app.location_purge`: positions that stopped qualifying or are older than
  2 hours, job points older than 30 days. A field job set done or cancelled takes its position off the map at once.
- Notification `staff_location_policy`: the staff member's copy of the policy, branded email, in-app and push (topic
  documents), transactional and locked in preferences (`user_can_mute = false`). Its link goes to the profile card (field staff:
  `/staff/field/profile#location`).

### Website

- `/api/me/location` (GET state, POST report, prefs, policy) and `/api/staff/live-people` (the layer).
- The **People** layer with role filters (office staff, field staff, renters, owners; only the filters the reader's scope
  allows) on the Live map (Admin Visitors, TV mode, staff Visitors) and on Fleet tracking (Admin, Fleet's car map). Markers are
  initials in a ring of the role's colour (dashed for renters and owners, a dot for a field job), MapLibre and Canvas.
- `LocationSharer` in the staff, renter and owner portals: asks the database whether the person qualifies (every 5 minutes and
  when the page comes back), watches only then, sends the latest good fix once a minute. In a browser it never asks for
  permission by itself; the settings card's **Allow on this device** does.
- **Location on the live map** card (`#location`) on the staff profile, the field profile, Account > Profile (renters) and
  the owner's Account > Profile: the state in plain words, the policy and **I have read it and accept** (staff), the switch
  (locked during an active job), **Allow on this device**.

### Store app

- `@capacitor-community/background-geolocation` (plugin `BackgroundGeolocation`, called through `lib/native/bridge.ts`'s new
  `watch`): keeps reporting with the app in the background while the person qualifies, with the phone's own notice.
- `native-setup.mjs`: iOS `NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription`, a clearer `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`,
  background mode `location`; Android `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`,
  `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`. `check-app.mjs` knows the plugin.

## Decisions

- The spec says the Expo app; the store app is the Capacitor shell (apps/carmacars), so the location API is wired there.
- Positions are rounded to 5 decimals (about 1 m) and never audited; the policy acceptance and the switch are audited.
- A report less than 20 seconds after the last, or older than 2 minutes, is not stored.
