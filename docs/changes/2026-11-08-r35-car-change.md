# Change the car on an offer or a confirmed booking before pickup (Release 35, part W: 0910, 0911)

Branch `r35-w`. Migration `0910_r35w_booking_ops.sql` (part 1); guides in `0911_r35w_booking_ops_guides.sql`. SCHEMA_MARKER 0911. No
applied migration is edited. Test: `supabase/tests/99_r35w_booking_ops.sql` (sections 1 to 3).

The owner, November 8: change the car of an offer or a confirmed booking before pickup (`app.swap_vehicle` of 0020 only works on
rent and said "change the booking instead", which did not exist for the car).

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Admin and customer service on a request, an offer or a confirmed booking; Sales on a request or an offer; Fleet on a confirmed booking (`app.car_change_allowed`). `bookingCan.changeCar` shows **Change the car** on the booking page |
| Which cars | `car_change_options` (door `ops_read`): every car free for the whole booking (`app.vehicle_is_available`), bookable, not leaving the fleet before the return, allowed for an 18 to 20 driver when there is one, an Owner Booking on the same Owner's cars, with a rate sheet; each with its new total and the difference |
| The price | `car_change` (door `ops_do`): the new car's rate sheet (daily, weekend, weekly, monthly, 3+ day rates, km included, rate per km) for the booking's dates, then `app.sync_booking_price`; staff lines, add-ons, drivers and the coverage choice stay. `app.booking_before_write` lets this one update change a confirmed booking's rates (`app.car_change` setting) |
| An offer | Withdrawn and sent again at the new price (the renter's one email is the new offer; the "offer withdrawn" event is set aside) |
| A confirmed booking | More: left open on the booking (the renter's **Pay the balance**, the pay link or the saved card charge). Less: a refund `pending_approval` on the booking payment with a Finance To do. Recorded as a change request applied at once (`source staff_car_change`): the renter's one email is "Booking updated" with the car before and after and the money in this change's words (`app.notification_payload` wrapped); owners are told as for any change |
| Agreement | The agreement job (0154) makes the new version to sign (the car is a material change). Its email is folded into the change email when the signer gets the link by text (`app.agreement_version_notify` wrapped); a signer with no mobile gets it by email |
| Jobs, history | Open handover and return jobs move to the new car; `booking_vehicle_swaps` keeps the swap; booking history `car_changed`; audit `booking_car_changed` |
| Refused | On rent or picked up (Swap vehicle), a placeholder, an insurer booking or provisional dates (the calendar), an open extension or change request, the same car, a car not free |

## Guides

- `admin/requests-offers-and-bookings`: Change the car before pickup.
- `va/fleet`: Change the car of a confirmed booking.
