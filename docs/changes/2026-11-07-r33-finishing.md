# Release 33, part H: finishing fixes (0740 to 0745)

Branch `r33-h` from `r33`. Migrations 0740 (check-in and check-out wording), 0741 (expired offers become leads), 0742 (change your own email),
0745 (the guide chapters). SCHEMA_MARKER 0745.

## 1. Check-in and check-out change meaning

The owner's terms: **check-in is the pickup** (the start of the booking), **check-out is the return** (the drop-off at the end). The site used the
opposite. Every word people see is flipped; no code, enum, column, URL or stored record changes (`pickup`/`return`, `return_check_in`, the handover
routes, test ids and the `checkin_*` variables stay).

- Screens: Admin, staff, field, renter and owner (the booking page card "Check-in, check-out and signing", the photo reports, the next-step
  buttons, Today's list, the field job buttons "Check in (pickup)" and "Check out (return)", the job kind "Return check-out", the statement
  "Check-out statement", the settings labels). The renter's own "Check in yourself" (at pickup) and "I've returned the car" were already right.
- The mid-trip message is no longer called a check-in (it would read as the pickup): "Mid-trip check".
- Emails (`emails/src`, rebuilt with `node emails/build.mjs`): the return complete, final statement, deposit released, damage found, review ask,
  agreement version, return findings and digest wording; the catalogue rows are updated by 0740 (generated by `supabase/seed/0740_gen_catalogue.mjs`).
- 0740 also rewrites the messages and history lines of the database functions (read as they are now, so a later change to a function is not undone),
  the field job label in the job notices ("Return Check In" becomes "Check-out (return)"), the status transition notes, four task rule descriptions,
  the mid-trip text template label and the academy lesson line.
- Not changed: the rental agreement's own text (its defined term "Check-in" means the return, section 4.3, and is part of signed versions). Changing it
  needs a new agreement version and is for the owner to decide.

## 2. The default filter chip

Every list with the filter bar (`ListFilters`) remembers the last choice on the device, and the default chip ("Active", "All") linked with no
parameter, so the remembered choice came straight back and the default could not be chosen. The default chip now carries `reset=<filter>`: it shows the
default and `RememberFilters` forgets that filter's remembered choice (the others stay). Unit-tested (`chipHref`, `afterReset`). The chip row may now
hold 6 chips (it was 5).

## 3. Requests and offers

- The five numbers (Waiting for an offer, Past the reply target, Offers out, Accepted not paid, Offers expired) are filter links; "Past the reply
  target" is its own filter (`state=late`). The chosen one is marked, as is the chosen chip.
- New chip "Accepted, not paid". Same page for staff (the shared component).

## 4. Old expired offers become leads (0741)

An offer expired for 7 days or more leaves Requests and offers (Expired shows the last 7 days) and becomes one renter lead in `leads` (kind contact,
source `expired_offer`): the contact, the car and the dates wanted, assigned by `app.lead_real_assignee`, with a To do `expired_offer_lead` for Sales
assigned to the same person. Once per booking (unique index). Not for a blocked renter, a test address, an owner's booking, or a renter who booked
anyway after the offer expired. It runs in the existing 15-minute job `app.job_capture` (`app.expired_offers_to_leads`), and 0741 runs it once for
today's old expired offers without the new-lead alert. Admin sees them under People > Renters > Leads, "Expired offers".

## 5. Change your own email (0742)

Everyone signed in (renter, owner, investor, staff, field staff) can press **Change my email** on their profile. The sign-in service's email change
flow does the confirming: a link to the new address, and the old address is told (secure email change is switched on in
`scripts/deploy/auth-templates.mjs`; the template names both addresses). The sign-in keeps the old address until the links are opened. When the
callback sees the new address on the sign-in, `app.account_email_changed` updates the account, renter, owner, investor and account-holder driver
records. The local stand-in does the same with two mailbox entries. The text "To change it or your email, call or message us" is gone (the
legal-name lock text stays). An Admin can still change an email from the record.

To apply on the sign-in service (not done by the release): `node scripts/deploy/auth-templates.mjs` and send the result to the auth config, as for the
other templates.
