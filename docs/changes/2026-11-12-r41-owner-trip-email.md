# Owners hear about a trip when it is complete, and every Monday (Release 41: 2620, 2621)

Branch `r41-owner-trip-email`. Migrations `2620_r41_owner_trip_email.sql` and `2621_r41_owner_trip_email_guides.sql` (the guide
chapters, generated by `supabase/seed/2621_gen_guides.mjs`; the catalogue rows of 2620 by `supabase/seed/2620_gen_catalogue.mjs`).
SCHEMA_MARKER 2621. DB test `supabase/tests/99_r41_owner_trip_email.sql`; `07_notifications` and `25_operations_gaps` follow the new rules.

The owner, November 12: "Don't email owners when a booking comes in. A lot of bookings get cancelled. Inform them when a
booking is complete and the car comes back." And: a weekly summary for owners.

| Part | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Off | `owner_new_booking` (a booking is confirmed), `owner_booking_confirmed` (an Owner Booking is confirmed) and `owner_booking_changed` (a confirmed, upcoming booking is changed or moved) are off in `notification_catalogue.is_enabled`. Their events are still recorded and processed at once with the note "code disabled": no email, text, push or in-app notice. The owner portal's booking list and calendar are unchanged. |
| Trip complete | New `owner_trip_completed` (email and in-app notice): once, when a booking on the owner's car is returned (status `returned`, or `closed` when it skips `returned`; check-out is the end of a booking). Only when `app.booking_counts_for_owner` (2480) is true and the owner may see the booking (`app.booking_owner_view` is not unavailable, 0156); never for Owner Bookings or Turo trips. It shows the car, the trip dates, the days, the distance driven when both odometers were recorded and the owner's share of the trip (`booking_splits.owner_payout_cents`). While the booking's revenue is hidden from the owner until the statement (0156), or the car is on a flat monthly pay, the amount is left out and the email says it shows on the next statement. Link: `/owner/bookings/<number>`. Trigger `bookings_owner_trip_completed`. |
| Monday summary | New `owner_weekly_summary` (email and in-app notice): `app.job_owner_weekly` runs every 15 minutes (pg_cron `carmacars-owner-weekly`) and, on Mondays from 9:00 Toronto time, considers each owner once for the week (`owner_weekly_summaries`), sending only when the owner had activity. Per car: the trips returned last week (Monday to Sunday) with dates and the owner's share (the same hidden-until-statement rule: "On your statement"), the confirmed bookings of the next 14 days (dates only, no renter details), and the owner's share of the month so far from `app.owner_month_to_date` (provisional). Only bookings that count toward the owner's revenue are used. |
| Setting | Program settings `owner_weekly_summary_enabled`, on by default. |
| Guides | The notification lists no longer promise an email per booking; the Monday email and the trip email are explained. |

## Guides

- `owner/notices-profile-and-leaving`: no email when a booking comes in; the trip-complete email and the Monday summary
- `admin/text-alerts-email-text-or-both`: owners' defaults are completed trips, not new bookings
- `admin/settings`: the owner emails and the switch for the Monday summary
