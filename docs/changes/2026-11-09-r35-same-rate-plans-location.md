## A. The same rate (coordinator item 10)

| Path | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Edit on a booking (`booking_staff_edit`) | A new length with no rental typed: the rental is the booked rental times new days over old days (a week at $525.45 made two weeks is $1,050.90, not the rate card again), and each per-trip fee (`app.booking_per_trip_fees`: a flat booking fee per rental, never delivery or pickup) scales by the same ratio as a line override. The edit's reason says "Same rate as the booked period (7 to 14 days)". The form previews it |
| Extensions (`preview_extension`, used by the renter's request, the offers and staff approval) | Whole days: the booked rental a day (`app.booking_rental_per_day`) times the added days, the per-trip fees for the added days at the booked rate, the per-day fees as before, delivery never again. An extension offer (0480) still takes its discount off that, with the booked rate as the regular price. `same_rate: true` shows "Same rate as the booked period" to the renter. Hours stay at the hourly price |
| Plan rolling | The next period of a long rental moves the return through the same extension path, so it keeps the rate |
| Pending payment (`admin_apply_booking_edit`) | The same scaling when the length changes |
| Late returns | Unchanged: the permanent rule of 1063 (whole days at the full effective daily cost, then the hourly fee) |

## B. A fixed amount per payment (coordinator item 13)

`bookings.pay_fixed_cents`. **Change the plan** has **Fixed amount per payment** for an Admin, on a weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly plan
(`booking_pay_every_set` refuses it otherwise, and for anyone else). Each payment is that amount (`least(fixed, what remains)`), the
last takes the remainder; the total and the payments made never change. Recorded on the booking's history. Empty removes it.

## C. The applicant's location (coordinator item 14)

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Settings | `in_person_area_km` (100) and `in_person_area_cities` (the GTA list, for a city the places table does not have) |
| The form | An in-person role asks city, province or state, and country. Only these, the phone's country (`app.phone_country_code`) and the connection's country (`cf-ipcountry`) are kept: `job_applications.location_*`, `phone_country`, `ip_country`, `location_area` |
| Outside the area | Not taken: the applicant is told and sent to Remote Sales with their name and email (`outside_area`). An application without the location fields (an old link, the API) is taken with the area unknown |
| Admin | The list and the candidate page show Lives in, the phone and connection countries, and Location mismatch (`app.location_mismatch`); **Move to Remote Sales** (`hiring_move_remote`) refers an in-person Sales applicant outside Canada with the 0173 referral email |
| Hire | `hire_preview` chooses Sales remote outside the area (`outside_area`); `hire_onboard` refuses Sales in person there |
| Welcome | The profile step asks every hire for city and country (`staff_location_save`): in the area Sales keep their mode (in person by default), elsewhere Sales is remote. The staff list shows the location |
