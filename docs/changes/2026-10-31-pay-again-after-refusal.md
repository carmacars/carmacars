# A renter can pay again after a refused or cancelled card

The booking's card payment was one per offer version. Once a payment was refused and cancelled (for example a debit card
before debit was allowed for the booking), the pay page kept handing back that cancelled payment, so the card box stayed
empty and the page sat on "Card approved. We are finalizing your booking." Now the next try starts a fresh payment, still
one per press (two presses never make two payments).

## Guides

No guide change: the renter simply pays again; staff do nothing different.
