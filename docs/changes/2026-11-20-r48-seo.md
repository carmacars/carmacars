# Search: why traffic fell and what was fixed, the tracker pages, every service on every page, redesigned search pages, where a lead came from, duplicate leads (Release 48, SEO: 3230 to 3233)

Branch `r48-seo` (on `r48-int`). Migrations `3230_r48seo_lead_source.sql` (new `contact_sources`; wraps `app.ad_channel_label`,
`app.sl_row`, `app.sales_lead`, `app.sl_listed` and `app.door` as `_pre3230`), `3231_r48seo_trackers.sql` (replaces
`public.tracker_product` and `public.sitemap_families`; wraps `app.sl_source` and `app.notification_recipients` as
`_pre3231`), `3232_r48seo_services_pages.sql` (new `public.service_facts`, five landing pages, the template epoch `r48-1`) and
`3233_r48seo_guides_academy.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/3233_gen_guides.mjs` and `3233_gen_academy.mjs`). No applied
migration is edited.

The owner, November 20: "SEO has gone to shit and traffic has dropped significantly. Fix all Google Search Console issues."
Then: design the search pages (longer but easier to read), promote every service, add the tracker as a product with its
own pages, show on the lead page where a lead came from, and deal with duplicate leads.

## 1. Why traffic fell

The live site cannot be reached from the build machine, so this is an audit of the code and the live Program settings (read
through the Admin connector), with the pages run locally. Each cause is marked **decided** (the owner's earlier decision,
left as it is), **fixed** (this release) or **owner** (needs a click only the owner has).

| # | Cause | Evidence | What was done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Most indexed pages were turned off between November 11 and 14.** Releases 37 and 40 (2350, 2402) made the place pages Ontario only (every US, Mexican and other-province place page answers 404), dropped vehicle pages of model years before 2005, and made synonym terms answer 301. Search Console had 172K pages indexed on November 8, most of them long-tail place and vehicle pages. | `scraper-guard.ts` (`offMarketPlace`, `oldModelYear`), `place_market`, `vpage_min_year = 2005` in the live settings | **Decided**: those pages brought visits from outside our market and cost CPU. Their clicks are gone on purpose. What matters now is that the pages we keep are strong: sections 3 to 6 |
| 2 | **A first visit to a place or vehicle page could get "One moment, loading the page"** (status 200, `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, a script that sets a cookie and reloads): every agent with a browser's user agent and none of our cookies, when the page had no kept copy. That is exactly a person arriving from a search result, and the fetchers of answer engines that do not name themselves and keep no cookies. A slower first paint for search visitors, and an empty page for those fetchers | `scraper-guard.ts scraperGuard` (r27b, November 6) | **Fixed**: a visit whose Referer is a search engine or an AI answer engine (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Brave, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude and others, `FROM_ENGINE`) is never checked. Hosting networks are still held to kept copies. The tracker pages are never checked (they are product pages, not a tree that multiplies) |
| 3 | **Three hostnames serve the whole site on the company's server**: `carmacars.ca`, `www.carmacars.ca` and the deploy's `new.carmacars.ca`, each with status 200. Every page names the canonical address, but crawlers spend visits on the copies and Search Console lists them as duplicates | `infra/vps/web/site.caddy`, `scripts/vps-server.mjs` (no host rule), `scripts/deploy/cf-site.sh` | **Fixed** (`src/lib/edge/canonical-host.ts`): `www` answers one 301 to the canonical host for pages (never the API); any other hostname of the domain keeps answering for the deploy's checks with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` |
| 4 | **Cached-only serving since November 11** (`usage_cached_since`): the vehicle and search pages come from their kept copy, up to a week old, while usage is above 70% of the allowance. Search engines get a page, but its date and content move slowly | Live settings; `scraper-guard.ts tailServing` | **Decided** while the site is on the Worker. It ends with the move to the company's server in this release (no allowance there). The template epoch `r48-1` (3232) moves every programmatic page's lastmod once, so the sitemaps ask for a fresh read after the deploy |
| 5 | **The search pages were walls of text with one template.** 129K pages were "Crawled, currently not indexed" on November 8. The depth sections (release 35) made the pages longer, not easier: every section open, no hierarchy, the same order on every page | `Depth.tsx`, the landing and place templates before this release | **Fixed**: section 3 |
| 6 | **The tracker page was thin**: `/gps-tracker` had four features, "Quoted for your car" for both prices (the settings were empty) and two questions. Nothing answered "kill switch for car" or "GPS tracker for rental car" | Live settings: `tracker_product_monthly_cents` and `tracker_product_install_cents` null | **Fixed**: section 4 |
| 7 | **Services were only in the footer and the menu.** A place or vehicle page linked to other place and vehicle pages, almost never to the chauffeur, delivery, coverage or owner pages, so those pages had few internal links | The templates | **Fixed**: section 5 |

Checked and found sound, so not a cause: `robots.txt` (search and AI crawlers named, full scope: the live `search_scope` is
`full`; a failed settings read falls back to open, never to Disallow), the sitemaps (each file under 50,000 addresses,
`place_sitemap_file_urls` 25,000; a failed read of the index or a main file answers 503 with Retry-After), canonicals and
hreflang (`pageMeta`), no `loading.tsx` on any public page (so a missing page answers 404, not 200: release 48 PF kept it so),
structured data built from the database, IndexNow on with its back-off, the bot shield (only SEO tools are refused; a
crawler on a portal page gets 403), the crawler guard (search and answer crawlers are never limited by it).

**Owner, after the deploy** (no API exists for these): in Search Console press **Validate fix** on "Crawled, currently not
indexed", "Duplicate without user-selected canonical" and "Not found (404)"; in Settings > Crawl stats check the host
status; and submit `/sitemap.xml` again if the deploy's `gsc` run did not (`docs/runbooks/release.md` step 6).

## 2. Search Console issue classes

| Class | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Not found (404), linked from inside the site | `/gps-tracker` answers 308 to `/trackers` and is in no sitemap; the services module links a landing page only when it exists (`service_facts.landing`) and the footer links `/trackers` only while the product is on; the install pages link only places the place read returns. `tests/e2e/r48-seo.spec.ts` follows every link of the module |
| Duplicate without a canonical, or Google chose another | The host rule (cause 3). One address for the tracker. Each tracker intent page has its own title, heading, answer, section, comparison table and questions (`pages.test.ts` fails if two repeat) |
| Crawled, currently not indexed (thin) | The templates of section 3; the tracker cluster replaces one thin page with a product page and seven pages each answering one search (their own copy overlaps by 13% at most on the site's similarity measure, `seo_copy_similarity_max_bps` allows 50%; the five new landing pages by 23% at most); install pages are indexed only for a place the place pages index and we reach, otherwise `noindex, follow` |
| Structured data errors | The tracker's `Product` has an `AggregateOffer` with prices and `PreOrder` (never `InStock` while `trackers_available` is off); `FAQPage` matches the questions on the page; `Service` on install pages names its area; every node is built from the same values the page prints |
| Mobile usability | Every new block is checked at 320, 375, 768, 1280 and 1440 px (`web/tests/shots/r48-seo`), with no sideways scroll; tap targets are 44 px or more; inputs are 16 px; tables scroll inside their own box |
| Redirect chains | `/gps-tracker` is one hop (a `next.config.ts` redirect, before any render); `www` is one hop to the final address with its path and query |

## 3. The search page templates (the kit)

`web/src/features/seo/kit/` and `web/src/styles/seo-kit.css`: the blocks every product, service and search page is built
from, server-rendered with no client code, in the site's own tokens and the owner's design rules (alpha borders, OKLCH
gradients, nested corners, snap carousels with faded edges, hover only where there is a pointer, motion under 300 ms and
none under reduced motion, 44 px targets, the primary action alone and red).

- `KitHero` (one action), `KitToc` (jump chips), `KitSection` (kicker, heading, one line), `KitTiles` (icon tiles),
  `KitPrices` (price cards), `KitCompare` (comparison table), `KitSteps`, `KitTrust` (trust badges), `KitFacts` (label and
  value pairs an answer engine can quote), `KitFold` (the long detail one tap away; the text stays in the page),
  `KitSticky` (the phone's sticky action; hidden on wide screens and in the store app), and 27 line icons (`icons.tsx`).
- **Landing pages** (`/car-rental/<slug>`): trust badges, price cards from the page's live lowest rates, the add-on cards
  of the page, the copy as folds (the first open), the depth sections as folds, questions, the services module, the sticky
  action "From $X a day: See the cars".
- **Place pages** (`HubPage`): trust badges, the depth sections as folds, "Everything for <place>" as a fold, a link to the
  place's tracker install page, the services module, the sticky action (See the cars, or Send a request on a service page).
- **Events, airports, renting vs owning** (`SeoShell`): trust badges, folds, the services module, the sticky action.
- **Vehicle pages** (`Questions` in `vehicles/parts.tsx`): the services module (four cards).
- `/chauffeur`, `/delivery`, `/list-your-car`, `/credit-card-rental-coverage`, `/car-rental`, `/detailing`, `/body-shop`:
  the services module and, where there is one action, the sticky action.
- The language versions keep their own strings: the new English blocks are left off them until they are translated.

## 4. The tracker cluster

- **`/trackers`**, the product page: the answer in one sentence, price cards for the three tiers, what it does as icon
  tiles, how it works, a comparison with a plug-in tracker, a Bluetooth tag and a hidden toggle, the waitlist form with
  quotable facts and trust badges, install links, questions, and the other tracker pages.
- **Seven intent pages** (`web/src/features/trackers/pages.ts`): `kill-switch-for-car`, `starter-interrupt-device`,
  `gps-tracker-for-rental-car`, `anti-theft-tracker-toronto`, `fleet-tracking`, `turo-host-gps-tracker`,
  `toll-and-ticket-billing`. Turo is named only as what was searched, with the not-affiliated sentence.
- **`/trackers/install`** and **`/trackers/install/<place>`**: gated like the place pages. The place comes from
  `public.place_hub`; a region that is off answers 404; a place the place pages do not index or we do not reach is
  `noindex, follow`. The sitemap lists the 200 nearest Ontario cities in reach (`public.tracker_install_places`).
- **Prices from Program settings** (3231): `tracker_product_tiers` ($25 a month per car for 1 or 2 cars, $20 for 3 to 9,
  $15 for 10 or more) and `tracker_product_install_cents` = $150 once per car. Nothing is typed into the pages.
- **Not in stock**: while `trackers_available` is off every page says "Shipping soon: join the waitlist or pre-order"
  (`tracker_product_launch_note`), the button is **Join the waitlist**, the structured data says `PreOrder`, and the unit
  test refuses "in stock" anywhere. Turning `trackers_available` on makes the same pages take orders.
- **The waitlist form** (`WaitlistForm.tsx`, `public.submit_tracker_waitlist`): two fields to join (email, number of cars),
  the price for that many cars as it is typed, the rest optional. It makes a lead for Sales with the source **GPS tracker
  waitlist** or **GPS tracker pre-order**, its To do and the staff alert of 0460 (email, in-app, push, per their
  notification preferences), and queues the person's email `tracker_waitlist_joined` (CarmaCars branding, from the owners
  address) with their price. The person is not a user yet, so there is no preference to set; the email is transactional.
- `/gps-tracker` answers 308 to `/trackers`; `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` carry the product, each price, that it is not
  sold from stock today, and its questions.

**For the owner to confirm**: the pages describe the engine shut-off as a starter interrupt that keeps a parked car from
being started (it never says the tracker stops a moving car). If the hardware does something else, change
`tracker_product_features` in Program settings and the two sentences in `pages.ts`.

## 5. Every service on every search page

- **Five landing pages** (3232, editable in Admin > Settings > Search): `rideshare`, `g2-licence`, `with-a-charger`,

## 6. Answer engines

Answer-first copy (each tracker page opens with a one or two sentence answer and a facts block), questions with
`FAQPage`, the tracker and the services in `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` ("When to recommend CarmaCars" names the car owner,
the host and the small fleet), and the lead source below tells Sales when ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot or Claude
sent someone.

Crawl: every new page is in a sitemap (the tracker pages and the install pages of the 200 nearest Ontario cities in
`/sitemap-families.xml`, `/services` in `/sitemap-pages.xml`, the landing pages in `/sitemap-landing.xml`), linked from the
footer or the services module of every page, and queued for IndexNow once by 3232.

## 7. Where a lead or a request came from (3230)

- `web/src/lib/track/source.ts`: the first page of a visit keeps the referring page, the landing page, the campaign tags
  and an ad click id (gclid and the others) in one first-party cookie, `cc_src`, for 90 days. Nothing is sent anywhere by
  itself; the server reads it only when the person sends a form or a request to book.
- `contact_sources`: one row per lead or booking with the channel (`app.traffic_channel`: Google search, Google Ads, Bing,
  ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, a campaign's own source, another site, direct), the
  referring page without its query, the first landing page, the campaign, the click id and the search terms (from
  `utm_term`, or the `q=` of a referring search page). Written by the contact, owner, vehicle, place, tracker and
  chauffeur forms and the request to book (`web/src/server/source.ts`).
- **Google does not send the words searched.** For a search visit without terms, the lead page lists the landing page's
  top Search Console queries (`app.gsc_page_queries`) and says so.
- **Shown to Admin and staff alike** (the shared lead pages): a badge on each lead card (black for an AI engine, red tint
  for search and ads) with the terms and the first page, and the card **Where they came from** on the lead's page.

**Privacy**: `cc_src` is a first-party cookie holding a page address, a referring address without its query, campaign
tags and a click id. It is not listed by name in the privacy policy's cookie section yet; add one line there (owner or
lawyer), as for `cc_vid`.

## 8. Duplicate leads (3230)

- **The same person**: leads of one kind of customer (renter, owner, partner) with the same email, the same phone (last ten
  digits) or the same browser.
- **One click**: the lead page's **Possible duplicates** card lists each one with the reason; **Merge** keeps the oldest
  lead, fills its blanks, keeps the other's message and form under **Also wrote**, moves calls, texts, chats, notes, claims
  history and open To do's, and closes the other as merged (`leads.merged_into`; listed nowhere; its page links to the
  kept one). **Merge all into the oldest**, and **Different person** (never suggested again). Admin, Sales and customer
  service, through the door (`lead_merge`, `lead_merge_all`, `lead_not_duplicate`); every merge is in the audit log with
  the merged lead's row.
- **The auto-merge rule**: a new website form from someone with an open lead of the same kind, by email or phone, inside
  `lead_auto_merge_days` (14), joins it at the end of the transaction that made it. Never by browser alone, never a lead
  staff or the system made, never into a handled lead (that one is offered for a click). `lead_auto_merge` turns it off.
- No event is added: a merge is staff's own action on their own screen, and the new-lead alert of the joined form still
  goes out and opens the kept lead through the merged one's page.

## 9. Conversion

## Not done

- **The live site was not crawled** (it is blocked from the build machine): the causes above come from the code and the live
  settings. The Search Console counts will say which mattered most a few days after the deploy.
- **The language versions** (`/fr` and the others) do not carry the new English blocks.
- **No undo button for a merge**: the audit log keeps the merged lead's full row; undoing is by hand.
- **A request to book stores its source** (`contact_sources.booking_id`) but the booking page does not show it yet; the
  Marketing report still reads `booking_attribution`.
- **The tracker intent pages' copy is in code** (`pages.ts`), not in Admin > Settings > Search like the landing pages; the
  prices, the name and the features are settings.
- **No local production build.** `next build` was killed for memory five times on the shared build machine (and once for
  disk), so the page checks ran against the development server: the e2e spec at both widths with axe, and the layout check
  at five widths. `tests/lint/seo-audit.mjs` reached the fixed pages and the landing pages (its two findings, the title and
  description lengths, are fixed) before the server was killed; `linkcheck`, `crawl-roles` and Lighthouse did not run. CI
  runs all of them on the real build: read their results before the deploy.
- **Core Web Vitals** were not measured (no build, no field data from here). By construction the new blocks add no client
  script except the waitlist form, and the services module adds one database read, shared for five minutes per server.
