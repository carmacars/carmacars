# Guide me: step by step in the live screens (Release 50: 3950)

Migrations `3950_r50_guide_me.sql` and `3951_r50_guide_me_guides.sql`, generator `supabase/seed/3951_gen_guides.mjs`.

## What it does

- **Every staff portal** (Admin, and the staff portal for Customer service, Sales, Fleet, Finance and Field work): a **Guide me** button in the header. Its menu has **Keep Guide me on** (off by default, kept per person on the server) and the workflows of the person's functions. A page that starts a workflow has a small **Guide me through this** link; the Stuck? prompt offers **Guide me through it** too.
- **The coach**: a ring around the element to use and a small card beside it (one instruction, Step X of Y, Back, Skip, Next, Exit, and the guide chapter it follows). A step to do moves on by itself when the page's own state says it is done (the form's fields, the checklist's parts, the route); Next stays off until then. A step already done when it opens shows Done and waits for Next. The ring and the card never take the page's clicks, the card is never over Report a bug, and it fits a 320 px screen.
- **It follows the work**: a run survives every page of its workflow and a reload (on the device), and resumes on another device (on the server, for 7 days).
- **Workflows**, one declarative step file each in `web/src/features/guide-me/flows/` (the element's `data-guide` name, the instruction, the condition): send an offer, answer a booking request, record an e-Transfer or another payment, pickup handover, return handover and deposit review, approve an owner's car, log a field expense. A new workflow is a new file listed in `flows.ts`; `engine.test.ts` fails when a step names an element no code carries, or a guide chapter that does not exist.
- **Admin, Academy**: **Guide me: walkthroughs in the live screens**, the last 30 days: per staff member started, completed and stopped (and the workflows completed), per workflow the step people stop at most.
- **Security**: both tables (`guide_me_prefs`, `guide_me_runs`) have row-level security with `admin_all` and the audit trigger; the functions are security definer with `search_path = public, app, pg_temp`, executed by the server role only (`/api/guide-me` for the signed-in person); staff and Admins only; at most 400 writes a person a day; an Admin viewing as someone writes nothing.
