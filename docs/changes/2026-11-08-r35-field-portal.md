# The field staff portal: one Today list, shifts, pay details and renter help (Release 35, part X: 0930 to 0933)

Branch `r35-x` (on `r35-t`). Migrations `0930_r35x_field_work.sql`, `0931_r35x_field_shifts_pay.sql`, `0932_r35x_field_academy.sql` (academy
sections, generated by `supabase/seed/0932_gen_academy.mjs` from `supabase/seed/academy/r35x.mjs`) and `0933_r35x_field_guides.sql` (guides,
`supabase/seed/0933_gen_guides.mjs`). No applied migration is edited. Test: `supabase/tests/99_r35x_field_portal.sql`; unit tests in
`web/src/features/field-portal/model.test.ts`.

The owner asked for a phone-first field portal built around the shift, with the field duties as one ordered list, one Add button, shifts
that can be given up, claimed and swapped, a pay details step, on-the-spot renter help, onboarding prep, and Sales for field staff who want it.

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Four tabs | Today, My shifts, My pay and Training at the bottom of the phone screen; Sales appears for a person who asked to do sales. The staff menu has the same four links |
| Today | The shift and Clock in at the top (the location is read at the tap only, 0881), how much of the day is done, the person's To do (receipts, pay details), then one ordered list built from the data: every pickup and return of the work day (deliveries and collections too), onboarding prep, then cars not checked clean in `field_clean_every_days` days (7), cars needing service or not roadworthy (status update to Fleet), and cars without a full listing photo set (a photo shoot opens in one tap). Tomorrow shows what is planned so far |
| One card per car and time | A pickup card holds its clean check, its add-ons and the handover, with one big button for the next step; the rest (maps, call, renter help, what stops the pickup) folds under More |
| Add-ons | Each add-on that goes in the car is ticked on the pickup (`field_addon_ticks`). The field handover is refused until each is ticked; an Admin can override (new override check `addons`) |
| Clean checks | Tick and photo before each pickup and after each return, and as a routine cleaning task (`vehicle_clean_checks`) |
| Add | Money spent (receipt photo, car, booking; Finance approves it as a company expense under the new field categories and marks it paid back; a To do while the receipt is missing, `field_spends`), a service record (what, where, odometer, cost, invoice photo: recorded on the car through the maintenance tracker, its expense line made at once, Fleet and Finance each alerted with a To do), photos of a car, and a problem with a car (the existing report) |
| Clock out | Must-dos still open need a reason each (`field_clock_open_items`); Fleet gets an alert and a To do |
| Fleet and Admin | The field jobs board shows each person's list of the day with its completion and what was left open; onboarding prep is given from the board and from the owner's onboarding page |
| Onboarding prep | Fleet or an Admin gives a field person the prep of a car joining the fleet on a day they hold a shift (safety certificate, maintenance, cleaning, listing photos, plates and permit, tracker). Each item is ticked with its uploads; done items mark the owner onboarding checklist (registry visit, tracker, photos); costs go through Add and are linked to the prep (`field_prep_tasks`) |
| Renter help | From a pickup card or any current booking found by number, name or phone (searches logged): a payment or the deposit hold by pay link, a QR for the renter's own phone, the renter typing on the staff phone, or the card reader when paired; the licence on the pickup; the agreement signed on the staff phone. The booking page rules have a field viewer (`bookingCan`: money, handover, sign, paper signatures, verify in person); refunds and voids stay with Finance and Admin |
| My shifts | Claim free shifts (given up by someone: first to claim takes it) and open ones; the upcoming shifts with the work planned so far; give one up with a reason (offered by alert and text to everyone eligible and free; under `field_late_cancel_hours` it is flagged late on the record and counted on the board); swaps between two people; Fleet and Admin alerted with a To do when nobody claims by `field_offer_alert_hours` before the start (a 15 minute job); past shifts with jobs, photos, money spent and hours |
| My pay | The pay details step first: full legal name, email, phone, SIN (encrypted by the website before it is stored; only the last 3 digits in clear, shown as ***-***-123) and a void cheque (a private file). Only Finance and Admin open the SIN or the cheque, each time with a reason, logged in the audit log and the data access log. Every field person without them has a To do; Finance sees who is missing them (Field spending). Hours, statements and how they are paid below |
| Sales for field staff | "I want to do sales too" on Profile adds the Sales academy path (it never holds their field work); the Sales function is added the moment the path is passed, and the Sales tab opens with the Sales pages, adding a rental and the referral link |
| Old pages | My hours opens My pay; Report a problem opens Add on Today; Cars now and the map are links on Today; Add a rental is on the Sales tab |
| Academy | Five field sections: the Today list, the Add button, shifts and pay, renter help, onboarding prep. Field staff who had passed everything get a week before the new sections hold them |

Catalogue and count tests touched: `web/src/features/staff/nav.test.ts` (the field links, the Finance money tabs), `web/src/features/academy/gate.test.ts`
(the pages open while held), `web/src/features/shared-pages/viewer.test.ts` (the field viewer). End-to-end specs updated for the moved pages (the field pay, gaps and staff sales specs in `web/tests/e2e`).
