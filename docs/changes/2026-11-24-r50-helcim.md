# Card payments on Helcim (Release 50: 3710)

Migration `3710_r50_helcim.sql`. The owner (Nov 24): Stripe closed the account; card payments move to Helcim, a Canadian processor, with Stripe still selectable if it is restored. Setup: `docs/ops/helcim-setup.md`.

## What it does

- **One switch**: `PAYMENTS_PROVIDER` = `helcim` | `stripe` | `off` | `fake` (`web/src/integrations/payments/provider.ts`, `cardProvider()`, the seam shared with the e-Transfer fallback). A processor without its key is **off** (helcim without `HELCIM_API_TOKEN`); production never runs the fake.
- **The adapter** (`web/src/integrations/payments/helcim.ts`, beside `stripe.ts`, same `PaymentsAdapter`): Helcim has no PaymentIntent, so each payment is kept in `helcim_intents` (ids `hc_...`) in Stripe's words; every caller (settle, pay links, deposit guard, refunds, the webhook) is unchanged. HelcimPay.js checkout sessions (`/v2/helcim-pay/initialize`: preauth, purchase, verify) give the card forms their "client secret" (the checkoutToken, renewed after 55 minutes); the secret token stays on the server. Capture, refund (a reverse when Helcim refuses a full refund in an open batch), reverse (a released hold), saved-card purchase and preauth (`cardData.cardToken`), all with idempotency keys (`helcim_calls` keeps each result for good; Helcim keeps keys 5 minutes).
- **Card forms** (`web/src/lib/card-js.ts`): Sign and pay, pay links, the offer pay box, add-ons, gift cards and owner invoices load HelcimPay.js (`secure.helcim.app`) instead of Stripe.js. The modal's answer goes to `POST /api/payments/helcim/confirm`, which checks its hash (SHA-256 of the data and the secret token), reads the transaction again from Helcim (approved, type, amount) and only then marks it held or paid; the form then runs its usual finish step. A declined card gets a new session.
- **Deposits**: a Helcim preauth must be captured within 7 days. The hold's expiry is placed + 7 days (the Re-authorize To do runs on time). A rental longer than that: the deposit is charged and refunded at release (`server_helcim_hold_plan`), `deposits.held_as_charge`, shown on the pay page and the booking's Money tab ("Charged to the card, refunded at release"). Extended holds are Stripe only.
- **Webhook**: `POST /api/webhooks/helcim` (HMAC-SHA256 of `webhook-id.webhook-timestamp.body` with the base64 verifier token, 5-minute tolerance; the event id claimed in `webhook_events`, provider `helcim`). A `cardTransaction` is read from Helcim and tied to its payment (a paid modal the browser never confirmed: the one open session of that customer and amount in 2 hours), then settled through the same steps as Stripe's webhook (`web/src/server/intent-events.ts`, moved out of the Stripe route).
- **Payments provider**: `payments.provider` (stripe | helcim), set by a trigger from the processor details (an `hc_` id is Helcim's), backfilled to stripe for the history.
- **Connector**: `helcim_account`, `helcim_find_payments` (admin:read) and `helcim_record_paid` (admin:write, preview then confirm), mirroring the stripe_* tools; an untied payment (Helcim dashboard or terminal) is linked through the same plan as Stripe's (`app.stripe_untied_plan` takes `hc_` ids).
- **CSP**: `https://secure.helcim.app` in script-src, frame-src and connect-src, and the payment permission. The API host is never allowed to the browser.
- **Security**: tokens only from the environment; the checkout secret never leaves the server; the confirm route refuses other origins; every function is security definer with `search_path = public, app, pg_temp`, executed by the server role only; the tables have row-level security on and no grants to anon or authenticated.

## Not on Helcim

Owner pre-authorized bank debits (card or e-Transfer instead), Stripe card readers, and refunds of Stripe payments (in the Stripe dashboard). Helcim reports no card funding: Visa Debit passes as credit.

## Guides

No guide change: the card forms, the Money tab and the staff steps are the same; only the processor's card window differs. Setup for the Admin is in `docs/ops/helcim-setup.md`.
