# The Live map (0242, 0243)

The owner, Release 24: "make a cool dashboard for the admin portal that has a map of the greater toronto area with little
pins with information below it on what kind of lead they are ... this dashboard will be put on a tv or monitor so make
sure its aesthetic and cool and impressive." Then: widen the first view beyond the GTA, and design for a fleet layer of
the cars' real positions once CarmaCars' trackers report. Then the owner rejected the first look; it was redesigned as
a wallboard (readable across a room in 3 seconds: the map as the hero, few big numbers, high-contrast dark, no dense
chips, no novelty visuals).

- **Page:** Admin > Today > **Live map** (`/admin/live-map`, Admins only, two-step sign-in when required). Always dark
  (near-black navy). Four numbers (on site now, requests today, on rent now, collected this month as $48.2K: payments
  succeeded less refunds since the 1st, the same sum as Overview's cash), the map edge to edge, the four stages with
  their counts (press one to hide it), then a compact sortable table of the leads (stage, name, city, score, top reason,
  car, last seen; a row opens its signal chips, contact, the car, where the place comes from, a link to the booking or
  renter, and **Show on the map**). **Show details** and **Open TV mode** (`?tv=1`). Hover or press a dot for its card.
- **TV mode** (`?tv=1`): over the admin frame (menu and guide banner hidden), no scrollbars at 1920x1080 or 3840x2160,
  the pointer hidden after 3 seconds still. The map fills three quarters of the screen edge to edge, with the brand,
  the clock and the four numbers over its top and the stages with their counts along its bottom. The right quarter: the
  hottest lead (the car's listing photo, or its name in large type when it has no real photo; the masked name, stage,
  city, score ring and one reason line), crossfading to the next every 10 seconds, and the live feed: the last 6 events,
  one line each ("Maya C. · started a booking · Markham · 2m"), new lines sliding in at the top. No phone digits, no
  email. Three type sizes, set by one size that follows the screen height (18 px at 1080 lines, 36 px at 2160).
- **Dots:** one per person (the renter when known, directly or through the browser's visits; else the browser), as
  their most advanced step: on rent now, booking (confirmed, not started), request (requested, offered, held, awaiting
  payment; 30 days), form sent (an enquiry: contact, chat, phone, sample car, owner program; not spam or closed; 30
  days), unfinished form (contact details typed into a form not sent, `contact_captures`; 30 days), game player (30
  days), previous renter (returned in the last 30 days or back on the site), on the site now. Four stage colours:
  green on rent or booked, amber requests and forms, cyan on site and games, grey past renters. Small glowing dots
  (10 px across at 1080 lines, 20 px at 2160); a hot lead (score 60 or more) is larger with a soft halo; a visitor on
  the site breathes slowly (cyan, the one motion colour); a new event sends one ripple out from its dot. Crowded dots
  merge into a count bubble at city level (press it to zoom in), in the colour of its most advanced stage. Dots on one
  spot are spread about 250 m, the same way on every refresh.
- **Masking:** the name as first name and last initial ("Visitor in Mississauga" when anonymous), the phone as its last
  4 digits (table only), no email; in full only with Show details on the admin page.
- **Score:** the sum of the signals' points, at most 100 (hot 60, warm 30, as Live visitors): the most advanced step
  (on rent or booking 40, request 35, form sent 25, form started 15), a game prize claimed 12 or a game played 8, past
  rentals 10 each (at most 20), a returning visit 8, pages (4 or more 5, 10 or more 10), minutes on the site (3 or more 5,
  10 or more 10), cars viewed (1 or 2: 4, 3 or more: 8), on the site now 6, from an ad 3, and the Live visitors reasons of
  the latest visit with their own points (Signal settings). Worked out in `web/src/features/live-map/model.ts`.
- **Where a pin goes** (most precise first; never invented, nothing is geocoded):
  1. the visit's position: Cloudflare's `request.cf` latitude and longitude of the visitor's network, rounded to 0.01
     degree (about 1 km) by the Worker and again by the database (`visitor_sessions.geo_lat`, `geo_lng`, recorded from
     this release on by `app.track_ingest`, kept as long as the visit). Game plays, unfinished forms and enquiries carry
     the browser's id, so they are placed through the browser's latest visit with a position;
  2. the centre of the renter's home city (`renters.city`), from the GeoNames places of 0095 (`places`, kind city,
     Ontario first; new index `places_city_name_idx`);
  3. the centre of the visit's city (Cloudflare's city name) the same way;
  4. else not on the map, counted under the legend ("3 with no location"); a pin outside southern Ontario is counted as
     outside the area. No booking or renter stores delivery coordinates today (the address field's coordinates are kept
     only for the business address and the delivery origin), so there is no street-level pin. No postal code table is
     used (none free and offline is in the repository).
- **The map:** MapLibre GL JS 4.7.1 (BSD-3-Clause; its build for a strict content security policy and its worker) served
  from `web/public/vendor/maplibre-gl-4.7.1/`, loaded on this page only (not in the Worker's bundle), drawing the
  site's own basemap: `web/public/live-map/basemap.geojson` (131 KB) from Natural Earth 10m (public domain): every lake
  of the area (Lake Ontario, Lake Erie, Lake Huron and Georgian Bay, Lake Simcoe and the smaller ones), the major
  highways (Natural Earth has the 400, 401, 403, 407, QEW and the US interstates; it has no 404, 410, 427, Don Valley
  Parkway or Gardiner), and 12 city labels with collision handling, in Noto Sans (OFL, `web/public/live-map/fonts`).
  Water a little lighter than the land, highways as faint lines. No tile service, key or third-party request: it draws
  the same offline, and the content security policy is unchanged. `web/scripts/live-map/basemap.mjs` rebuilds the file
  from the Natural Earth downloads. The first view frames Kitchener-Waterloo and Guelph, Niagara (St. Catharines),
  Peterborough and Cobourg, and Barrie and Lake Simcoe, Toronto in the middle; it widens to take in any dot outside it,
  never past southern Ontario (also the most it zooms out). A browser without WebGL gets a note and the list.
- **Fleet layer (the seam for the trackers):** layer toggles Leads and Fleet. `app.vehicle_positions` lists each car's
  last tracker position (installed device, reported in the last 7 days): vehicle_id, car code, name, lat, lon, heading,
  speed, status (on rent, available, in service), updated_at; `tracker_status.heading_deg` is new for the tracker service
  to fill. The read returns them with the car's photo as `fleet`; the page draws each car as its photo in a ring of its
  status colour with an arrow for its heading, gliding to each new position over 2 seconds. It is empty until the
  trackers report (the toggle says "waiting for trackers"); nothing here ingests positions.
- **One read:** `app.live_map(p_by, p)` (security definer, Admins only, `admin_read live_map` through the door, a wrapper
  `door_pre0242`): the tiles, the totals per kind, the pins (the last 30 days plus what is current, current first, at
  most 500; a time ahead, such as a booking made for later, counts as now), their cars and photos (once per car) and the
  fleet, in one call with no per-pin round trips. `p.who` (renter or browser ids) keeps only those people (the tests). The website
  keeps one answer 20 seconds per Worker instance (`web/src/server/admin/live-map.ts`), masks it per request, and the page
  asks again every 30 seconds while visible (`/api/staff/live-map`, `details=1` for Show details), updating the pins in
  place.
- **Privacy:** Admin only; TV mode always masked, the normal page masked until Show details; masking happens on the
  server, so a masked screen never receives the full details. The visit's position is approximate and never a typed
  address.
- Code: `web/src/features/live-map/` (`LiveMap.tsx`, `engine.ts`, `model.ts`, `config.ts`,
  `live-map.css`), `web/public/live-map/`, `web/public/vendor/maplibre-gl-4.7.1/`, `web/scripts/live-map/basemap.mjs`, `web/src/app/admin/live-map/page.tsx`, `web/src/app/api/staff/live-map/route.ts`,
  `web/custom-worker.ts` (the beacon's position).
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_live_map.sql`, `web/src/features/live-map/model.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/live-map.spec.ts`.

Not done: street-level pins for deliveries (no coordinates are stored for a booking's delivery address); the tracker
service that fills `tracker_status`; the 404, 410, 427, Don Valley Parkway and Gardiner on the basemap (not in Natural
Earth; an OpenStreetMap extract would add them).
