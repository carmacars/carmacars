# Release 49: checks (notification and data map, guides accuracy, Documents tab, e2e, Android test build)

- **Notification and data map check** (`scripts/check-notification-map.mjs`, map in `scripts/notification-map.json`, a step of the fast CI job): every event of the platform (booking states, offers, payments, owner onboarding, hiring, field jobs, tolls, claims, trackers, rewards) lists the portals it touches and the email or notice each gets. CI fails on a code that is in no catalogue, a code for the wrong audience, a missing template, and an emitted event (`app.emit_event`) with no catalogue row. Portals touched with no message yet are printed as GAP lines on every run (see below).
- **Guides and academy accuracy gate** (`scripts/check-guides-accuracy.mjs`, a step of the fast CI job): every page address in the guides must be a route of the app (or a redirect), every **bold button or setting label** must be text the app has, every `setting_name` must exist, and every academy chapter link must be a real chapter. Non-label bold phrases are listed with a reason in `scripts/guides-accuracy-allow.json`. Fixed (3451): the Quo call settings are named "Calls in the browser: nobody answered, ..." in Admin; the renter guide and the academy lesson "When the card does not cover it" no longer name two buttons that do not exist ("Use OPCF 27 on my policy instead", "Ask for the Protection Plan"): the renter picks another card or another coverage choice.
- **Booking Documents tab** (3450): a document removed or replaced (`removed_at`, 0821) is no longer listed on the booking's Documents card (it opened a 404). `supabase/tests/99_r49_booking_documents.sql`.
- **End to end**: the drawn-initials step of the signing helper waits for Save initials to enable and for the save response itself, no longer a timeout (ops.spec, desktop flake).
- **Android test build**: `apps-native` platform `android-test` builds an installable APK as a run artifact, no store and no secret (`docs/apps/native-publish.md`, "Android test build").

## Known gaps of the map (printed by the check; each needs a decision, not wiring)

- A late rental: the owner of the car hears nothing until the return (`owner_trip_completed`).
- An unanswered request: Sales have no code of their own (`staff_request_waiting` goes to customer service and the Admins).
- An overdue renter balance and a refund: finance see them in the digest and To do only.
