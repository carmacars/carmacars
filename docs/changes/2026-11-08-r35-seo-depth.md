## D. The coverage count (Admin > Settings > Search showed 1%, 19 of 1,649)

The share was taken over every address inspected, and two thirds of each sample come from the long tail of the place and
vehicle files (millions of addresses), most "URL is unknown to Google". Search Console's 172K indexed and 129K not indexed
are of the pages Google knows, so the two figures never measured the same thing. The run now stores buckets (indexed by the
verdict PASS or any indexed state; crawled; discovered; unknown; other) and the priority and long-tail parts
(`coverage.ts`, `gsc-coverage.mjs`); the trend and the card show indexed of the addresses Google knows, the priority pages
indexed, and the unknown ones apart. Older rows are read from their states by pattern (`app.gsc_coverage_buckets`).

## E. IndexNow

The sweep sends `indexnow_sweep_batch` addresses a request (1,000, was 10,000) and the changed pages `indexnow_queue_batch`
(100, was up to 500). A 5xx or no answer waits 10 minutes, doubling with each in a row up to 6 hours (`streak_5xx`; the
changed pages' `q_backoff_s` doubles too); 429 keeps its rules; a 2xx resets. 1101 lifts a back-off left by a 5xx so the sweep
resumes from its cursor. The `gsc` workflow claims 1,000 a round and sends 30 rounds.

("CarmaCars vs 01 Motors in <place>", place pages only). They name 01 Motors only as what the searcher compared, say the page is

## G. Sourced page data (the seo-data workflow)

`.github/workflows/seo-data.yml` (weekly, or by hand) runs `web/scripts/seo-data.mjs` on the runner: the GTFS stops of GO
Transit, TTC, MiWay, YRT, Brampton Transit and DRT, OpenStreetMap hotels (Overpass), fueleconomy.gov and NHTSA (recalls,
5-star ratings) for the fleet's models, and an optional curated CSV of OEM or open dataset specs (tires, rims, colours,
performance) or maintenance costs. Rows are loaded through `scripts/deploy/sb.sh` into `app.server_seo_data_load`, which
refuses a row without an https source or a fetch time; a feed that fails is skipped. Nothing is committed. Until a run
loads data, the pages render without those sections.
