# Card picked from the list, two OPCF 27 pages, the fuel shortfall line, delivery by distance (Release 35, part B: 1400 to 1404)

Branch `r35-b`. Migrations `1400_r35b_card_pick.sql`, `1401_r35b_opcf27_pages.sql`, `1402_r35b_fuel_shortfall.sql`,
`1403_r35b_delivery_distance.sql`, `1404_r35b_guides.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/1404_gen_guides.mjs`). No applied migration is
edited. Test: `supabase/tests/99_r35b_card_fuel_delivery.sql`; unit tests `web/src/features/coverage/cards.test.ts` (the card search),
`web/src/features/delivery/words.test.ts`.

The owner, November 11, 2026: the renter picks their card from a searchable list and is told clearly when it does not cover the car;
OPCF 27 needs the pink slip and the endorsement page before the booking is confirmed; the fuel shortfall is prepared at the return from
the tank, the pump price and a multiplier plus a fee (never charged to a card by itself); delivery is $75 with 15 km included, then $2 a
km, each way.
