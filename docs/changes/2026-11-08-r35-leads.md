# Release 35, part Y: one Sales lead list with claims, outcomes and the Sales dashboard (migrations 0950, 0969)

Branch `r35-y`. SCHEMA_MARKER 0969 (raise to the highest merged migration when merging).

The owner, November 8 (item 12): every "New lead" alert, push and email links straight to THAT lead; a live-chat lead says
"Live chat" and the lead shows the phone the alert showed; one comprehensive Sales lead list (Admin and Sales, the same on the
staff portal) with every lead, filters, search and a priority sort; claim rules editable in Settings > Sales; a Sales
dashboard for each salesperson and a team view with a leaderboard for the Admin; Renter leads with the new leads first;
Owners > Leads.

## What changed

1. **Every lead in `leads`.** Two sources that were not leads become one, by the 15-minute job (`app.job_capture`, as 0741
   did for expired offers), once each: an inbound call missed and not handled from a number with no live booking
   (`missed_call`), and a visitor who turned Hot on Live visitors and left an email, with analytics consent and no lead or
   booking in 30 days (`visitor_signal`). Neither raises the new-lead alert (each has its own).
2. **Plain words and the phone.** `app.sl_source` names the source (Live chat, Contact form, Phone number box, Discount game
   claim, Expired offer, Booking request not sent, Owner application not finished, Owner program form, Earnings calculator,
   Missed call, Live visitors signal, and so on); the alert uses the same words, so a chat lead never says "Website form".
   `app.sl_phone` falls back to the lead's live chat or its typed contact; leads saved without the phone they had get it now
   (one-time, data-driven).
3. **Types.** Renter, owner (owner program, owner recruitment from a business) and partner (insurers, dealers, rental
   businesses, fleet suppliers). Sales see owner leads that are theirs or nobody's (0342 kept).
4. **Claims** (`lead_claims`, `lead_claim_log`; the request-claim pattern of 0177): Claim holds a lead for the minutes of its
   type (`sales_lead_settings.claim_minutes`, 15 for renter, owner and partner); each logged call, text, email or note
   (`lead_contacts`) extends it by the same window up to `extend_max_minutes` (60) from the claim; Unclaim at any time; a
   claim that runs out returns the lead to the pool (logged `expire`); at most `max_active` (25) live claims a person; an
   Admin reassigns (claim and assignment) or releases; Next lead claims the oldest hot (under `hot_hours`, 24) unclaimed lead.
5. **Outcomes** on the lead: contacted, call booked, applied, won (with the booking), lost with a reason; Mark handled. Won and
   lost close the lead and end the claim. A confirmed booking of the lead's contact within 60 days closes the open renter
   lead as won, credited to the booking's Sales person (the existing attribution of 0057 and 0177, which also decides sales
   pay), else to the person who last claimed it.
6. **Reads and actions** through the door (`admin_*`, `va_*`, `ops_*`; each function checks the person: an Admin, Sales or
   customer service): `sales_leads` (filters type, source, status, age, assigned, mine, search by name, email, words or phone
   digits, sort priority, newest or oldest, 50 a page), `sales_lead` (the form text, the chat transcript, calls, contacts,
   claim history), `sales_dashboard`, `sales_lead_settings`; `lead_claim`, `lead_unclaim`, `lead_contact`, `lead_outcome`,
   `lead_handled`, `lead_reassign`, `lead_next`, `sales_lead_settings_save` (Admins).
7. **Dashboard** (`app.sl_figures`): leads claimed (claims and reassignments) today, this week (from Monday) and this month;
   won; close rate = won / claimed; contacted % (claimed leads then called, texted or emailed); average minutes from the
   claim to that first contact; claims that ran out. The Admin's team view ranks by won, then close rate.
8. **Links.** The new-lead, unfinished-request and owner-lead alerts and the owner application email open
   `/admin/sales-leads/<id>` for Admins and `/staff/sales-leads/<id>` for staff (`app.notification_payload` wrapped); a task
   about a lead (New owner program enquiry, website enquiry, game lead, expired offer) opens that lead.
9. **Pages.** People > Renters gets All leads and Sales team; the lead page; Owners > Leads (`/admin/owners?view=leads`,
   `/staff/owner-leads`); staff Leads (All leads, Mine) for Sales and customer service; Renters > Leads (Admin and staff)
   shows the new leads first as cards, with the expired offers, typed contacts and game leads below, folded; the Sales
   person's To do shows My leads; Settings > Sales. Cards are one column at 375 px.
