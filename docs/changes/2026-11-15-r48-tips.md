# Tips (Release 48 part 2: 3053)

Migration `3053_r48_tips.sql`. On by default (`program_settings.tips_on`).

- **The renter adds a tip** on the pay page (checkout), on the booking after the return (the receipt) and from the link in the "Returned" email (`/account/bookings/<number>/tip`): 10, 15 or 20% of the booking total (`tip_percents_bps`) or an amount they type, paid by card on Stripe's own form (a payment request covering `tip`, captured at once; `public.renter_tip_payreq`, `server_payreq_settle`).
- **Staff record a cash or e-Transfer tip**: the Money tab's **Tips** > **Record a tip**, `record_payment covers=tip` and the connector (`app.record_tip`). The same e-Transfer reference twice is one tip. Voiding the payment voids the tip.
- **The split** (`tip_shares`, `app.tip_split`): half to the booking's salesperson and half to the staff member who did the pickup handover (the pickup check-in report, else who recorded the pickup). No staff salesperson (a direct booking) or no staff handover: CarmaCars keeps that half as company revenue. One person who did both gets it all. An odd cent goes to the salesperson's half. The salesperson's share is a Program setting (`tip_sales_share_bps`, 50/50). An Admin may give one tip another split, with the reason (`tip_split_set`: all to one person or to CarmaCars, or back to the rule).
- **Where it shows**: each person's share on **My sales** and the field **My pay** (`my_tips`); every tip, each person's total and CarmaCars' share on **Admin > Money > Tips** (`tips_report`); the booking's Money tab (with the split) and the renter's receipt email. A tip is never a line of the booking: no owner revenue share, no statement, not in Rental due or Total paid (`payments.purpose = 'tip'`).
