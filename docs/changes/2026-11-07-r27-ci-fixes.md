# Release 27: the full suite green again

Fixes found by the first combined run of `r27` (run 41539198549).

## Product

- Admin and staff frame on a phone: the bell, Profile, Assistant, Guide and Sign out no longer run past the edge at 375
  (tighter buttons on one line, wrapping on a narrower screen).
- Admin pages: the main column never grows wider than the window for a wide table or row (Settings > Add-ons at 1440, the
  section rows on top at 375); the table scrolls in its own box as before.
- The date field (one calendar everywhere): clicking into a filled field and typing, pasting or autofilling replaces the
  whole date instead of landing in front of the old one.
- `/admin/staff` (the link of the field job and availability staff alerts) opens Team > Staff and roles instead of a 404.
- Plate cover: the box suggested when the listing review opened (0521) is moved onto the plate's edges once the photo is
  loaded, as a box asked for in the editor is, and the editor says how many boxes were suggested.
- Sales > Marketing: the kit of the car picked has its own Claim button (the car need not be on today's list); the kit's
  fields, photos and replies are styled in the staff portal too (the styles moved from admin-ui.css to the marketplace
  components), with the label above the value on a phone.
- Stuck? help: the guide chapter beside the page can be reached by keyboard to scroll it.
- Alert settings: the quiet hours switch moves at once when pressed (it waited for the save; a refusal puts it back).
- Investor portal: the sheet tabs (My investment, Calculator...) show again in the revamped portal, which hid them.
- Marketing > Channels: the chart's table for screen readers no longer widens the page on a phone.
- Admin menu: the current one-link area (Cars, Money...) keeps its black under the pointer (white on grey failed contrast).
- Alerts: the feed's column never grows past the window on a phone (a long alert pushed it 2px past the edge).
- Field work: a phone alert's link to a job that is no longer theirs (passed on or removed) opens My jobs instead of a 404.

## Tests

- `99_ai_interviews`, `99_hiring_waiting_for_day` and `99_portal_emails_deposits` process only their own
  events (the backlog other suites left took minutes: each staff alert goes to
  every Admin the suites made), which kept the fast job past its 20 minute limit.
- The fast job's limit is 30 minutes (was 20): the database suite is 12 to 18 minutes since r27; the full suite runs
  beside it for longer, so a deploy is never later for it.
- `99_staff_alerts` counts its burst only (refund alerts of earlier suites in the last minutes made it one short).
- End to end specs follow the r27 product: the tab row may carry a count, Calendar sync is reached from More fleet, the
  turnaround on the car page is the one buffer between trips (90 minutes), the date fields are found by their exact label,
  the visitor history quick ranges sit under the live map, Next up names the lane of the most urgent task, and one pass of
  the event queue may take longer than 15 seconds; the Marketing spec's photos have a checked plate, its seller is in
  person (the Academy path) and it waits for the Posted log itself; an Admin's phone alerts have seven topics, all on; the Visitors spec opens Chat
  before Texts and calls; menu links may carry a count; the Marketing fixture photos are images that exist (a published car
  with missing photos broke other pages' checks); the Marketplace spec waits for its own lead; the sideways tab row is not
  an element past the screen; g then c is the shortcut checked (Applications has no letter since it is a tab of People); Ctrl+K
  arrows past its first line (the record search) to the page; the Sales menu says Marketing; the portal fixtures name a car
  as the pages do (with its model family); the crawl signs in only as someone who still holds the role.

## Guides

No guide change: the fixes restore what the r27 guides already describe.
