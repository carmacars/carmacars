# Release 27: the full suite green again

Fixes found by the first combined run of `r27` (run 41539198549).

## Product

- Admin and staff frame on a phone: the bell, Profile, Assistant, Guide and Sign out no longer run past the edge at 375
  (tighter buttons on one line, wrapping on a narrower screen).
- Admin pages: the main column never grows wider than the window for a wide table or row (Settings > Add-ons at 1440, the
  section rows on top at 375); the table scrolls in its own box as before.
- The date field (one calendar everywhere): clicking into a filled field and typing, pasting or autofilling replaces the
  whole date instead of landing in front of the old one.
- `/admin/staff` (the link of the field job and availability staff alerts) opens Team > Staff and roles instead of a 404.

## Tests

- `99_ai_interviews`, `99_hiring_waiting_for_day` and `99_portal_emails_deposits` process only their own events (the backlog other suites left took minutes: each staff alert goes to
  every Admin the suites made), which kept the fast job past its 20 minute limit.
- End to end specs follow the r27 product: the tab row may carry a count, Calendar sync is reached from More fleet, the
  turnaround on the car page is the one buffer between trips (90 minutes), the date fields are found by their exact label,
  the visitor history quick ranges sit under the live map, Next up names the lane of the most urgent task, and one pass of
  the event queue may take longer than 15 seconds.

## Guides

No guide change: the fixes restore what the r27 guides already describe.
