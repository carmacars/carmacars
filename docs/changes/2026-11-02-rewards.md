# CarmaCars Rewards (0212)

Abbas's request (Release 22, item 1, with the ways to earn added the same day): a renter loyalty program, on by default.
Migration `supabase/migrations/0212_rewards.sql`; email `emails/src/emails/renter_rewards_expiring.mjs` (catalogue row by
`supabase/seed/0212_gen_catalogue.mjs`). Code: `web/src/app/account/rewards/`, `web/src/features/rewards/`,
`web/src/server/actions/rewards.ts`, `web/src/app/admin/settings/rewards/page.tsx`, `web/src/app/cars/[slug]/page.tsx`.
Tests: `supabase/tests/99_rewards.sql`, `web/src/features/rewards/types.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/release-22.spec.ts`.

- Points: 1 per dollar of the rental charge after every discount (fees, add-ons, coverage, tax and the deposit never
  count), when the booking closes (deposit settled). Owner bookings earn nothing.
- Ways to earn (`reward_actions`, each on or off with its points): referral 500 to the referrer and 250 to the friend at
  the friend's first closed rental (the renter's own code on `/r/<code>`; owner and staff kickbacks are untouched), a
  follow on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook (50, once per platform, granted on the click from the Rewards page; a follow
  cannot be checked, and the page says so), a review on carmacars.ca (100 per rental, any rating; the car page shows
  "Received reward points"), a Google review (100 per rental, any rating, on the renter's word with the name on the
  review; Admin sees every claim and can reverse it; hidden until the Google review page is set), a damage-free rental
  (200: no incident, no damage, cleaning, smoking, late or key fee, returned within 30 minutes of the time), the
  birthday (100) and the first rental (100). Every award has a unique source key, so it is given once.
- Tiers by rental days in 12 months: Member, Silver (10 days, 5% off), Gold (30 days, 10% off), with a perk line. The
  discount is a renter-only promo code, so it comes off the rental charge only and is shared with the owner like any
  promo code.
- Redeeming: from 500 points, 100 points = $1, a one-time code of the renter's own valid 90 days (a promo code, so the
  same rental-charge-only rule and owner sharing). An unused code that ends, or is given back, returns the points.
- Expiry: 12 months after the last earn or redemption; `renter_rewards_expiring` goes 30 days before, priority 4 (after
  customer, owner and staff email under the 100-a-day cap). `notification_low_priority` keeps that class through
  `app.notification_catalogue_refresh`.
- Admin > Settings > Rewards: the numbers, ways to earn, tiers, Google reviews claimed, a renter's points with Add or
  take off and Reverse (with the reason the renter sees).
- The renter's writes are `public.rewards_mine_*` (not `my_*`), so an Admin viewing as the renter cannot use them.
- Google: points for a Google review are Abbas's decision (Google's review policy does not allow incentivized reviews).
