# R50 hotfix 2: the academy fix once, leads after the academy, the evening townhall (5070 to 5073)

Migrations `5070_r50h2_academy_fix_once.sql`, `5071_r50h2_leads_after_academy.sql`, `5072_r50h2_townhall_evening.sql`, `5073_r50h2_guides.sql`. Asked by the owner on Nov 25.

## What the owner asked

- "Academy wait time for wrong answers is too long. Reduce it."
- "Give people the chance to correct the wrong answers once before marking their quiz as failed."
- "Sales people shouldn't be getting leads while they're still stuck in academy."
- The townhall at 18:30, the interview call slots at 17:30, and Join opens the call in the site when no call link is set.

## Changes

- **The waits (5070).** `academy_fail_cooldown_minutes` 30 to 2 and `academy_attempts_per_day` 3 to 10, in production and as the column defaults. 10 a day keeps the shorter wait from running into the daily cap.
- **Fix once (5070).** A learner's first check below `academy_pass_bps` records no attempt. It returns the questions that are wrong (never the right answers or the explanations). The first answers and the wrong questions are kept on the started quiz (`academy_quiz_starts.fix_wrong`, `fix_answers`, `fix_at`). The second check takes the first answers and changes only the questions marked wrong; it is graded and recorded, and a fail starts the 2-minute wait. Starting the quiz again while a fix is pending gives back the same fix, so nobody gets a second free first check. An Admin's reset of the section clears it. Admins previewing a quiz keep the one-step check.
- **The quiz page.** After a first check below the mark: a warning "Fix the ones marked wrong" with the count, a red edge and "Marked wrong: choose again." on those questions, the others locked with "Right. Kept as you answered.", and the button **Check my fixes** (`web/src/features/academy/Study.tsx`, `academy.css`).
- **Leads after the academy (5071).** Before: the lead routing (1151) asked `app.staff_fully_onboarded`, which is true for everyone while `staff_onboarding_gate` or `academy_gate` is off, and its academy step leaves out the Sales path for a person who opted into Sales from another function (0931). The alerts to whoever is on shift (`app.staff_alert_users`: on_duty, unassigned_on_duty) never asked at all. Now `app.staff_leads_ok` (fully onboarded and `app.staff_academy_cleared`: an Admin, a test account, no staff record, exempt, waived by an Admin, or every path passed, the Sales path too when they work Sales; it ignores the switches) guards every path:
  - lead alerts: `app.staff_alert_users` leaves such a person out of every shift route and the lead and visitor rules; a notice about a lead or a visitor is not written to them in `notifications`, `notification_outbox`, `push_outbox` or `phone_outbox` (bell, push, email, text, WhatsApp);
  - the claim pool and holds (2320): a claim by such a person is refused (trigger on `lead_claims`);
  - an assignment is moved to the previous salesperson or the pool (trigger on `leads`), and an Admin's reassign is refused (`app.lead_reassign`); the assignee list (`app.lead_assignee_ok`) and the return target (`app.lead_previous_owner`) leave them out;
  - the lead list, a lead's page and the dashboard (`app.sl_may`, `app.sl_sees`) give them nothing;
  - visitors and hot visitors: a visitor assigned to them is cleared (trigger on `visitor_sessions`), and a lead or visitor To do is put back in its queue (trigger on `tasks`);
  - remote sales use the same claim, assignment and alert paths;
  - AI auto hire of Sales (`hiring_auto_hire_sales`) makes a staff member at the first onboarding step: they get no lead until the academy is passed or waived.
  Every open lead held now by such a person goes back (`app.leads_return_unonboarded`). The answer is kept per statement (`app.leads_ok_memo`), so a list asks it once.
- **Townhall (5072).** `townhall_time` 18:30 (and its default); the invites follow by themselves (5000). Every interview day still to come moves to 17:30 with its length kept, and its open slots are made again (`app.hiring_day_slots`); a booked slot keeps its time, past days are not touched. With `townhall_call_url` empty, `app.calls_in_site()` is true whatever `calls_mode` says, so Join on the Townhall page, the portal card and the staff bar opens the call room of 5010 (`/staff/townhall/live`, `/admin/townhall/live` for an Admin), and `/api/townhall-join` sends there too. The calendar invites and the feed carry that page (`app.townhall_call_page`).
