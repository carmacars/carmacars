# VA screening, schedule and calls (0179)

The owner: VA interviews are 10 minutes, back to back, on Saturday. The screening asks about the internet connection,
English, social media skills, their skills and experience, their understanding of the role and of CarmaCars, their
agreement to the hours and the pay, other jobs, school, education, whether they want a career or a job, and whether they
would like to move to Canada. Every VA who does not pass goes to Sales (remote). Customer service starts at 9:00 and
fleet at 11:00 Toronto time; fleet is a full operations role.

## What changes
- **The three VA roles** (customer service, fleet operations, finance and billing) ask the new questions. Refusing the
  schedule or the pay is a knock-out; moving to Canada is asked but never scored.
- **Every VA who is knocked out or below the bar** is referred to Sales (remote) when it is open (declined with the
  reason when it is closed).
- **Pay is monthly**: US$400 (customer service), US$450 (fleet), US$200 part time (finance). The role pages show the
  new hours: customer service from 9:00 am, fleet from 11:00 am Toronto time, 6 days a week, 10 hours a day.
- **Fleet operations** lists its full duties: paperwork and complete rental agreements, tows, parts, covering customer
  service, and the next day's work list before the shift ends.
- **The call guide** is a 10-minute call. The Saturday interview day itself is set in Admin > Hiring > Interview days.

## Guides
No guide change: the hiring screens are unchanged; only the roles' content and questions change.
