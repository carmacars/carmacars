# The sample cars back, sample photos, and why the real cars are not on the site (migration 0065)

The owner, October 19, 2026: "Add the sample cars back for a moment, the website looks rough without them. And assign
sample photos to cars uploaded without photos."

Before: the owner had deleted 18 of the 20 sample cars with Delete all sample cars. Two stayed (2023 Toyota Corolla and
2023 Hyundai Elantra, kept by old requests), retired, plus two "Corolla Test" sample drafts in onboarding. The 8 real cars
were all in onboarding or ready to list, none on the site, so the fleet page was empty.

| What | How |

## 2. A request on a sample car is an enquiry

`public.submit_booking_request` sends a request on a sample car to `app.sample_car_enquiry`: a lead (kind `sample_car`,
reference EQ-1001 and up, the car, dates, places, note and contact), and a customer service task "Suggest a similar real
car" due at the first reply target. The same request sent twice is one enquiry. The renter lands on `/book/sent/EQ-...`,
which says the car is an example and that a person is finding a similar real car; nothing is booked or charged.

No booking can be created on a sample car from anywhere (a request, Add a rental, an import): a trigger on `bookings`
refuses it with "This is an example car (a sample car), not a real one: it cannot be booked. Choose a real car". The
local stack's sample cars keep taking real bookings (`vehicles.sample_bookable`, set only by the local seed) because the
end-to-end tests book them.

The live chat's facts mark each sample car, and the assistant is told never to quote or promise one and to take a request
for a similar real car instead.

## 3. Sample photos for cars without photos (0054, checked)

A car listed without real photos shows the sample photo of its make and model, make and body type, or body type from the
library (Admin > Settings > Sample photos), or the built-in picture of its body type, captioned "Sample photo". Checked on
every surface:

| Surface | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Fleet grid, landing pages (`/car-rental/<slug>`), model pages, similar cars, featured cars | `public_listing_row` cover: the sample, caption "Sample photo" (CarCard) |
| Car page | `public_listing_photos`: the sample, "Sample photo. Photos of this car are coming soon." |
| Open Graph and JSON-LD image | The car page's first photo, so the sample's absolute address (tested in e2e) |
| Live chat | Shows no photos (text only) |
| Emails | `vehicle_image_url` takes real approved photos only; without one the template shows the car's name panel. A renter's confirmation never shows a picture that is not the car |
| Staff screens | "Using sample photos" on the car, in Listings and on To do; Settings > Sample photos lists the cars and the photos they show; Admin > Cars > Not on the site yet marks them |

The 8 real cars have no photos, so each shows a sample photo as soon as its listing is published; nothing else to do.

## 4. Why the real cars are not on the site

`app.vehicle_public_gates(vehicle)` lists, in order, what is left before a car is on the site, each with its one action and
who it waits on: retired or closed, the open onboarding steps (owner details, ownership permit, registry forms,
agreements signed, Forms C and D, upfront payment, registry visit, tracker, insurer confirmation), the enrollment
confirmation, rates, the plate check (never on sample photos), the listing review (with the details to fill in), a paused
listing, bookings turned off, arrears. Admin > Cars shows "Not on the site yet" with each car's next step; the verify
workflow prints it per car (names and steps only, no plate or owner) and puts the full list in the run summary.
