# R50 hotfix 2, UX: the stepped tracker, the pickup countdown, the game in Rewards, demo portals, the weekly Admin call (5150 to 5152)

Migrations `5150_r50h2_admin_call.sql`, `5151_r50h2_rewards_game.sql`, `5152_r50h2_guides.sql`. The owner's requests of Nov 27 (with his reel of a stepped tracker).

## Changes

- **One stepped tracker** (`web/src/components/portal-ui/Milestones.tsx`, styles in `ui-kit.css`, motion in `motion.css`): a round node per step on a line, done (black, a check), now (the red ring, "Your turn" or whose turn), later (grey), and a finish flag. A step done since this device last looked draws its stretch of line and its check pops; the last step done pops the flag with the confetti moment, once per device. Each step is a button that opens what it means, when it was done and where to do it. Transform and opacity only, 300 ms or less, off under reduced motion. Used by:
  - the renter's booking page (`StepTracker`: request to final statement; the to-do list stays under it);
  - the owner's application path (`OwnerPathCard`) and each car's onboarding steps (`StageTracker`, owner, Admin and staff);
  - **From sale to pickup** on every booking's overview for Admin and staff (`readiness.ts` `saleSteps`): request answered, offer accepted, agreement signed, paid, licence and coverage checked, deposit and drivers, car prepared, picked up; steps that wait on the renter say so;
  - the Rewards tiers on the renter's Rewards page.
- **Pickup countdown** (`PickupCountdown.tsx`): days, hours, minutes and seconds in tabular figures, ticking each second while the page is in view, once the booking is confirmed and until the car is picked up, with the pickup place (never on an offer).
- **Add to calendar** (`AddToCalendar.tsx`): one menu: Apple Calendar and other calendars (.ics with the pickup and the return, each with a reminder), Google Calendar and Outlook (their new-event pages with the pickup filled in). On the booking page from the offer until the pickup.
- **The game in Rewards (5151)**: renters (`/account/rewards`), owners (`/owner/rewards`) and staff (`/staff/rewards`, `/staff/field/rewards`) play the discount games for CarmaCars Rewards points. The database draws the prize from `program_settings.rewards_game_prizes` (10 to 250 points by default; each prize 1 to 1,000 points), never a discount code, so no money leaves; the day's plays follow `game_max_per_day` (1 when it is 0), the switch `game_enabled` and `rewards_game_on`, and the game weights. Points go to the person's renter rewards account once per play (`reward_ledger` source `game:<play>`); a person with no renter account keeps them on the play until one exists. A test account plays but earns nothing. Points redeem only by the existing rules (500 points minimum, the rental charge only).
- **Demo portals** (`/staff/demo`, `/admin/demo`, Me > Academy > Demo portals and Team > Demo portals): a renter portal (Request sent to Closed) and an owner portal (Application to Earning) with made-up data, stage by stage: the next step, the countdown, the tracker, the money, what the customer is sent. Nothing reads or writes the database; every button only says what it would do. Every staff member (office and field) and the Admins.
- **The weekly Admin call (5150)** (`/admin/admin-call`, Team > Admin call): Sunday 7:00 pm Toronto, 60 minutes by default (`program_settings.admin_call_weekday`, `admin_call_time`, `admin_call_minutes`; Change the day or time on the page, or Program settings). The deck builds itself the day before and again three hours before (the townhall job), with 14 sections: shifts and claims, hours and pay owed, academy, hiring and no-shows, social media, the numbers, owners, deposits, insurance, claims, prepared money actions, tolls, fleet, To dos. Approve and Deny in place, once per item, with who decided: a shift claim denied comes off the shift; a To do denied is closed with the reason; a money action approved stays open, urgent, and no money moves; a deposit or a payout decision becomes a Finance To do done on its own screen. The call is in the site (room `admin_call`, Admins only) with the deck beside it. Every Admin gets the weekly invite by email (`admin_call_invite`, .ics, METHOD:REQUEST, weekly RRULE), an update when the schedule changes, and a cancellation (`admin_call_cancelled`) when they are no longer an Admin; an alert an hour before (`admin_call_soon`).
