# Reminders for the townhall and the interviews; AI interviews that decide (Release 41: 2590, 2591)

Branch `r41-reminders`. Migrations `2590_r41_reminders.sql` and `2591_r41_reminders_guides.sql` (the guide chapters,
generated by `supabase/seed/2591_gen_guides.mjs`). SCHEMA_MARKER 2591. DB test `supabase/tests/99_r41_reminders.sql`;
`99_ai_interviews`, `99_staff_alerts` and `99_r35k_staff_onboarding` follow the new rules.

The owner, November 12: 29 of 29 staff missed the townhall of Wednesday November 11. "Remind everybody about the town hall
and about the interviews, including myself, through notifications and the actual staff portal." And: "notify me when a new
applicant comes through, when an AI interview gets done and when a live call gets booked, but make sure there's no action
required other than when an actual interview gets booked. Make the interviews difficult, a pre-screening to the academy."

| Part | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Townhall reminders | Staff alerts (bell, phone push) to every active staff member and every Admin: the day before at 10:00 am Toronto, the morning of at 9:00 am, an hour before (1068's alert, as before) and 15 minutes before ("check in now", the join link; not to anyone checked in). Each once per person and call. A job every 5 minutes (`carmacars-staff-reminders`). These alerts are never folded into "N or more alerts" without a push. Normal tier: the staff quiet hours (9 pm to 8 am) still hold them |
| Townhall card | Every portal home (To do of every office function, Sales included; Today at a glance; field Today; Admin Today) from the day before until the call ends: date and time, countdown, **Join / check in** (from 15 minutes before; through the site, which counts the join), **Add to calendar** (`/api/townhall-ics`), and "You missed the last townhall: one more miss locks your account" for staff who missed the last one |
| Interview reminders | Admins: the morning of (9:00 am, the day's calls), an hour before the first call of the day, and 10 minutes before each call with the candidate, the role and the link (**Call** for a phone call). Slots have no interviewer of their own, so every Admin gets them. Admin Today: **Interviews today**. Applicants' reminders unchanged |
| Live call booked | The one hiring event that needs a person: a staff alert and an Admin To-do (`interview_call`) due at the call; done by itself when the call is no longer booked; rebooking replaces it |
| New applicant, AI interview done | Alerts for information only ("Nothing to do"): the new applicant with the role; the interview's score, the bar and the outcome |
| AI interview decides | `app.hiring_ai_auto_decide` (each hiring job run). Pass = score at or over the bar (Program settings `hiring_auto_hire_mark`, now 80) with no knock-out (the screening's or one the AI lists) and not "decline". Remote roles: hired by 1060's one-step auto-hire, now for every remote role (the account, the agreement, onboarding starting with the academy). Field: invited to book the live call (1068's flow, same bar). Fail: the decline email, or the Sales referral when the AI saw a Sales fit and no knock-out; sent once (`hiring_decisions`). `hiring_ai_outcomes` keeps each outcome. The interviews waiting today were decided once by the same rule at the end of 2590. The "waiting for your decision" line is gone (it returns only if **Decide by hand** is chosen) |
| A harder interview | New questions: reliability, a typed customer message (written English) and, for Sales, an objection to answer; topics `reliability` (10) and `written` (10), weights still 100. Up to 4 probing follow-ups (`hiring_ai_followups`, now 0 to 6). The follow-up and scoring prompts are stricter; the AI lists knock-out answers and any one fails. The candidate page and the consent call it the pre-screen to the academy |
| Settings | Hiring > Roles and links > Interview settings: the bar (50 to 100), follow-ups (0 to 6), **Decide by hand / Decide automatically**; the card shows the week's outcomes. `hiring_ai_harder` turns the new questions off |
| Privacy | The consent and the privacy policy say the interview score decides by itself and a person reviews any decision on request (an Admin's own edit of the policy is left as it is) |
