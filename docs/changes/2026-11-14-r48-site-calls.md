# Site calls open the caller pop-up, and every call keeps its recording, transcript and summary (Release 48: 2995)

Migration `2995_r48_site_calls.sql`.

Calls through the site (the browser phone, Twilio) never opened the caller pop-up or its Call helper, and the renter's page showed no recording, transcript or summary for any call. What was wrong:

- `app.caller_live` listed only incoming calls: a call placed from the site (`outbound_calls`) never opened the pop-up. It now lists the caller's own site calls too (`direction: 'out'`, masked for Sales).
- The pop-up was for Admins, customer service and Fleet only (`app.caller_popup_ok`, and the staff portal mounted it only for those), while incoming calls ring customer service and Sales (`phone_inbound_ring`). Every office role gets it now; whoever the browsers ring and whoever answered see the incoming call.
- The pop-up asked every 10 seconds (once a minute when idle), so a call was often answered before it showed. `phone_activity` and `outbound_calls` now bump the live topic `staff:calls`; the pop-up reads again within about a second (Realtime, or 2 s polling), with a 60 s safety read.
- A call to the business number that rang the browsers was never recorded. The ring now records both sides (`record-from-answer-dual`), each browser reports when it answered (`ring-answer` leg: answered time and who), and the recording (`in-recording` leg) is copied to private storage (`app.inbound_call_media`), transcribed and summarised like site calls.
- `app.renter_activity` carries a call's recordings, transcript, Quo's full summary and the voicemail; the Activity log (renter page and booking history, Admin and staff) shows Play, Show the transcript and the summary (`features/activity/CallExtras.tsx`).
- Backfill: the five-minute phone job looks up calls of the last 60 days missing their recording (site calls, at Twilio) or their recording, transcript or summary (Quo calls, at Quo's API), each once (`app.call_backfill`, `runCallBackfill`). Without a Quo summary, the AI one-liner is written.
- Quo: the webhook already subscribes to every call and text event and updates an existing webhook whose events differ; parsing of each event is now unit tested.
- A site call's transcript has no speaker label in the summary prompt (it was labelled "Caller").
- Tests: `web/src/features/phone/site-calls.test.ts`, `supabase/tests/99_r48_site_calls.sql`; older suites now expect Sales to get the pop-up and the Call helper.

## Guides

- `admin/texts-and-calls-the-quo-phone-line`: Calls through the site open the pop-up, who sees it, recording, transcript and summary on the renter's Calls, the backfill.
- `va/texts-and-calls`: Calls through the site, Sales get the pop-up, Play and the transcript on the renter.
