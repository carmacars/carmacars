# Resign in the staff portal (Release 50: 5020 to 5022)

Migrations `5020_r50_resign.sql`, `5021_r50_resign_notices.sql` (generator `supabase/seed/5021_gen_catalogue.mjs`),
`5022_r50_resign_guides.sql` (generator `supabase/seed/5022_gen_guides.mjs`); emails
`emails/src/emails/staff_resignation_confirmed.mjs`, `staff_resignation_received.mjs`, `staff_resignation_withdrawn.mjs`;
`web/src/features/staff/resign.ts`, `Resign.tsx`, `ResignForm.tsx`; tests `supabase/tests/99_r50_resign.sql`,
`web/src/features/staff/resign.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/r50-resign.spec.ts`.

## Why

A field hire resigned by email. Staff now resign themselves in their portal, with a record, the emails and the
offboarding work in one place.

## What it does

- **Staff portal, every role** (VA: customer service, Fleet, Finance, Sales; field staff; staff still in onboarding):
  **Resign** is the last section of **Profile** (`#resign`), and on **Welcome** while onboarding keeps the portal closed.
  The form: **My last day** (a date; today + `program_settings.staff_notice_days`, 7 by default, the notice of both
  staff agreements; tomorrow at the earliest, today for someone in onboarding or the VA trial period, within 120 days),
  an optional reason, **Continue**, then **Before you confirm** (when the access ends, final pay, what to return, how to
  withdraw) and **Confirm my resignation**. Pending: the last day and **Withdraw my resignation** until the last day.
- **What resigning does** (`app.staff_resign`, door `ops_do`/`field_do`/`admin_do staff_resign`): records it in
  `staff_resignations` (the day given, the last day, the reason, who and when; RLS admin only, audited); their field jobs,
  shift plans and open shift offers after the last day go back to the queue; nobody books them a shift or assigns them a
  job after it (`field_shift_book`, `assign_field_job`); **one** offboarding To do (`staff_offboarding`, Admin queue,
  dedupe per resignation) listing final pay up to the last day, the keys, cards and equipment (not tracked: to collect),
  their open jobs and shifts, and the leads, chats, To dos, claims and owners to hand back.
- **Emails** (CarmaCars branding, never a text, never in the staff digest): `staff_resignation_confirmed` to the person
  (final pay timing from the agreement and the pay settings: field, the pay date of the two-week period holding the last
  day with vacation pay; VA, the regular pay run; what to return; how to withdraw), again as an update when an Admin moves
  the last day; `staff_resignation_received` to the Admins and Finance (push topic alerts); `staff_resignation_withdrawn`.
- **The last day**: the job `app.job_staff_resignations` (pg_cron every 15 minutes) ends the access after the last day
  through `app.staff_access_end`, the same function `admin_do staff_end` (End access, the connector's `end_staff_access`)
  now runs; their open To dos and chats go back to the queues, their lead and request claims are released, their standing
  shifts end. Someone in onboarding or the trial period who leaves today loses access at once, after the confirmation
  screen (Sign out).
- **Admin**: Staff and roles shows **Resigning: last day** (or **Resigned** after it) with the day given, the reason and
  the **Offboarding To do** link; **Change last day** (earlier or later, from today), **Withdraw resignation**, **End access
  now** (closes the resignation as ended), and **Record a resignation** for someone who resigned by email.
  `admin_read staff_resignations`; the connector's `list_staff` returns `resignation`.
- **Security**: every function security definer, `search_path = public, app, pg_temp`, the server role only; only the
  person or an Admin acts; the new foreign keys are indexed.
