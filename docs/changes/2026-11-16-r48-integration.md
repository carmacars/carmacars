## Follow-ups

- **Field checklist**: a booking with one driver reads **No additional drivers** in the Ready check, the same as Admin (`Checklist.tsx`; two or more drivers still read "Additional drivers accepted").
- **The tip link in the text** (migration `3072_r48_tip_text.sql`): the Returned text ends with `Add a tip: https://carmacars.ca/account/bookings/<number>/tip`, as the email does. The channel policy (2971) still decides which of the two the renter gets. An Admin's own wording is kept (the line is added once, only where the text has room). Test: `supabase/tests/99_r48_tip_text.sql`.
- **Greyed-out button hints**: the generic ones now say the specific thing to do first (`Act`'s default, "Finish the steps above first" in 8 places, "Change something first" in 4, "Choose one first" on the four card forms, which really meant "wait for the card form to load", "Choose at least one first" in 6, "Type it first", "Connect it first", the field Complete button, which now lists what is still to do). The three Act buttons of the late-return panel pass their own reason.
- **The Worker smoke `/cars` 503**: `scripts/worker-smoke.mjs` warms `/cars` first with one request that is not aborted at 60 seconds (240 seconds at most); the checks then hit a warm route. `web/src/lib/db/local.ts` finds workerd by any of its markers (not `navigator.userAgent` alone), and a connection closed under a call (`isClosedConnection`, `closed.ts`) drops the shared pool, so the next call, or the smoke's retry, opens fresh connections; a read (the function lookup) is run again once, a write never. Unit: `closed.test.ts`. Not run against the Worker build here (it is not built in this change).
- **The background remover** (`infra/vps/web/rembg`): our own image, not a third-party tag: `python:3.12-slim`, `rembg[cpu]==2.0.87` (checked on PyPI), fastapi, uvicorn, python-multipart, and a small `server.py` with the one call the site makes (`POST /api/remove`, multipart `file` and `model`, a PNG back) and `/healthz`; the `isnet-general-use` weights are downloaded while the image is built. The deploy (`scripts/deploy/web-vps.sh` sends the folder, `web-vps-remote.sh` builds `carmacars-rembg:<content hash>` on the server once per content, starts it with the web compose, writes `REMBG_URL=http://carmacars-rembg:7000` into `web.env`) never fails because of it: a build or start that fails is a warning, the site health check does not look at it, and without it the sections keep their photos. The runtime image was not built here (no Docker on this machine): the first deploy builds it.
- **DB tests** that depended on the day or hour they ran: `99_person_roles` and `99_r41_protection` both made the user `pr_admin` (now `prl_admin` in the first; `99_person_roles` failed on main); `99_r35c_role_kpis` now uses last week (a whole week in the past; it failed in the first hours of a Monday); `99_owner_debit_amounts` asks for the debit preview of the day the run is billed (the invoices are billed from today once past the 15th); `99_r35x_field_portal` keeps its pickup before Toronto midnight (after 23:40 it fell on tomorrow's list). `20_gap_close` (scorecard) passed in every hour tried here (Toronto 01:00 to 03:00 on a Monday), so its failure at the other hour was not reproduced; the clock cannot be moved on this machine.
- **Expo app: Location on the live map** (branch `r48-loc`, on migration 3350). Two mobile API routes on the same door as the site's `/api/me/location` (bearer token): `GET /api/m/v1/me/location` (`ops_read my_location`) and `POST /api/m/v1/me/location` (`report` with source `app`, `prefs`, `policy`). The rules are `apps/carmacars-native/src/logic/location.ts` (unit: `location.test.ts`): staff only after the electronic monitoring policy and only on shift or on an active job, never off duty, and the switch cannot be turned off during an active job (`locked`); renters and owners only in the server's pickup or return window and only while the app is open; nothing without the phone's permission. `src/location` runs them: a watch while the app is open, or for staff who chose Always / Allow all the time the `expo-task-manager` background task (Android foreground-service notice, iPhone blue pill); a fix is sent at most every 30 seconds while moving (25 m, or the phone's speed), none while still; asked again every 5 minutes, when the app comes back and whenever a report is no longer kept; everything stops on sign-out or another account. Profile > Privacy > **Location on the live map** is the web card (state line, policy and accept, the switch, Allow on this phone). Config: `NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription`, `UIBackgroundModes` location, Android `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` (no longer blocked) and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`; `scripts/check-app.mjs` checks them. Google Play asks for the background-location declaration (staff on shift or on a job; a short video of the settings screen) at the first upload with it.
