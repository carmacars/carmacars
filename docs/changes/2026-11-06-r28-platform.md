## B3. API access and the Claude connector

- 0562: `api_keys` (the SHA-256 of each secret only, scopes, revocable), `api_oauth_clients`, `api_oauth_codes`,
  `api_calls` (append-only: who, tool, record, before, after, outcome). `app.api_call` checks the key, the scope, that its
  person is still an active Admin and the rate limit (Program settings `api_calls_per_minute`, 60), runs the tool as that
  person (`door_begin`: the audit log and every guard trigger see them) and logs it. Tools: whoami, list_bookings,
  get_booking, update_booking, list_requests, update_request (a request before its offer), list_owners, get_owner,
  update_owner; each update changes only its listed fields (`features/api-access/tools.ts`), never prices, payments or status.
- `/api/mcp`: the MCP server (JSON-RPC over POST). OAuth 2.1 for Claude: `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` and
  `/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server` (rewrites), Dynamic Client Registration (`/api/oauth/register`: Claude's
  callback and loopback addresses only), the consent page `/oauth/authorize` (Admins only, scopes to tick), the token
  endpoint (PKCE S256, one-hour access tokens, 30-day rotating refresh tokens).
- Admin > Settings > API access: the setup steps, Make a key (shown once), the keys and connections with Revoke, the call log.

## B8. Listing photos in any order

- 0566: no named shot is required; a listing is complete with `listing_min_photos` (10) photos that can go on the site
  and an outside photo for the cover (`app.listing_missing_shots`, which the steps, the submit and the publish ask);
  the 14 named shots stay as the shot list's guide. Step 1 of the listing steps reads "Photos (n of 10)". The extra
  outside and detail photos take up to 30 each.
- **Add photos** on every listing (`features/listing/ListingCapture.tsx`: Admin, staff, field photo shoots, the owner's
  car page): up to 30 at once, uploaded one after another with the shot `auto`; `/api/listing/upload` asks the vision
  model which shot it is (`features/listing/photo-sort.ts`, purpose `photo_sort`, the live AI share) and files it there,
  or with the extra outside or detail photos when that shot is taken (never a replacement) or the model cannot say.
  Outside photos get the studio background as before; inside and detail photos are kept as taken.
- A photo the model sees a watermark, logo or caption text on is flagged (`quality_flags` "watermark"): it never goes
  public (a trigger) and does not count toward the 10 until the mark is gone or the photo is replaced.
- The photos already on cars not yet published (the four Mercedes listings) are queued to be processed again.

### B8 fixes from production (Nov 6), 0567

- **Cover the plate crashed** (SecurityError: the canvas tainted by the photo from the storage host). The editor and the
  automatic plate suggestion now load the photo from the site itself (`GET /api/listing/plate?photo=`, the same rights as
  saving, `private, no-store`) with `crossOrigin` set, so the canvas can always be read back; any read that still fails
  shows a message instead of breaking the page (`plate-cover.ts` `readSafely`, unit tested). Shared by Admin and every
  staff role that reviews listings (one component).
- **Studio framing** (worker preset `studio_v3`, `features/listing/subject-frame.ts`): the segmenter's cut-out is read in
  the Worker, the car is its largest piece (pieces away from it, such as a corner watermark's letters or a caption, are
  made clear), cropped to its own edges with a soft contact shadow drawn under the tyres, and placed at about 80% of the
  frame's width (front and rear views held by the height) with the wheels on the floor line, the same rule for every
  angle. No separate shadow image; an unreadable cut-out is placed as before.
- **Marks on inside and detail photos**: a flagged photo is shown to the vision model once more (purpose photo_sort,
  the live AI share, `server_photo_mark_gate`) for where the marks are; the edge band holding them is cut off (Cloudflare
  `trim`) and the photo framed again. A mark in the middle, or no model, keeps it flagged and off the site. The job's
  context carries `photo.watermark`; the result clears the flag only when the worker says the mark is gone (the result's
  flags list used to replace the photo's flags and dropped it).
- **Processed again after the deploy** (`app.studio_v3_requeue`, run by the migration, idempotent): every photo that can
  go on the site of the four Mercedes listings (CLA250, C300, A220, AMG GT C; inside and detail photos checked for marks),
  and outside photos framed the old way on cars not yet published whose plate nobody covered. Each leaves the site until
  approved again and its plate is checked again on the new framing (the old boxes do not fit it), so no plate shows.
- The email logo at a stable address for signatures: `https://carmacars.ca/brand/carmacars-logo-email.png` (480 x 77,
  black letters, transparent).
