# Texts held for the quiet hours go at the hour on the clock, also when the clocks change (0282)

A text that is not urgent waits for the end of the quiet hours (21:00 to 8:00 by default). The end was worked out as
midnight plus 8 hours: on the night the clocks go back the text went at 7:00, and on the night they go forward at 9:00.
Now (migration 0282) it goes at 8:00 on the Toronto clock every day.

## Guides

No guide change: the quiet hours are set and shown the same way.
