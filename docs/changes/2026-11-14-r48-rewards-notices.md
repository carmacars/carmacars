# Rewards emails and notices, and birthday and holiday rewards by tier (Release 48: 3000, 3001)

Migrations `3000_r48_rewards_notices.sql` and `3001_r48_rewards_occasions.sql`.

CarmaCars Rewards sent renters only the 30-day expiry reminder. It now tells them what happens, through the notification pipeline (an email, the notice in the renter portal, a text only when there is no email, per the channel policy of 2971). Each has its switch and its 30-day counts under Admin > Settings > Rewards, **Emails and notices to renters**, with **Preview** (the email with sample values, from `web/src/generated/rewards-emails.json`). Every event has its own dedupe key.

- Account emails: `renter_rewards_welcome` (the account is made or the first booking confirmed, once), `renter_rewards_points` (one email for everything earned at once: a deferred trigger on `reward_ledger`; never a follow or an Admin adjustment, never for a booking marked Do not email), `renter_rewards_tier_up` (with the tier's perks, once per tier in 12 months), `renter_rewards_free_day` (the Black free day, with `/book?promo=<code>`), `renter_rewards_expiring_7d`, `renter_rewards_free_day_ending` (30 and 7 days).
- Marketing, with the unsubscribe link and implied consent: `renter_rewards_near_tier` (48 hours after a rental, within 20% of the next tier), `renter_rewards_waiting` (30 days after the last rental, at most every 60 days, nothing coming up), `renter_rewards_statement` (the 1st, for a balance or the month's activity), `renter_rewards_birthday` (14 days before), `renter_rewards_holiday` (10 days before each holiday weekend; Christmas and Boxing Day are one email). Hourly job `app.job_rewards_notices`, marketing from 10:00 to 19:59 Toronto time.
- `rewards_settings.notices_enabled` switches all of them; the numbers are Admin settings (`admin_do rewards_notices_save`, `rewards_notice_set`).
- Birthday and holiday rewards: `reward_tiers.birthday_bps`, `holiday_bps`, `occasion_enabled` (Gold 10%, Platinum 15%, Black 20%), `reward_holidays` (the ten Ontario holidays by date rule, editable), `renters.birthday_month`, `birthday_day`, `birthday_year` (the licence date of birth wins). A pickup within 7 days of the birthday or on the holiday weekend gets one line `occasion_discount` ("Birthday reward -10%" with a minus sign) off the rental charge, the bigger of the two, capped at the car's lowest daily price, never on an Owner Booking, an insurer's booking, a Turo trip or a price staff typed. Quotes for the signed-in renter show it; a request or offer locks it (`bookings.occasion_kind`, `occasion_bps`) and it counts with the rental charge in owner revenue, commissions, the split and refunds, like a promo code.
- The renter: **Birthday and holiday rewards** on the Rewards page and **Birthday** on Account > Profile (`public.rewards_mine_birthday`).
- Notifications > Email preferences showed the raw subject of an optional email ("{{discount_percent}}% off your next CarmaCars rental"): each optional email now has a plain name, any other is shown with its merge tags taken out, and a notice never shows a tag (`features/portal/pref-label.ts`, with its test). The DB test checks that every Rewards email has every value its subject, preheader and template read, and that no Rewards notice holds a tag.
- The earlier test suites run with the notices and the rewards off (`supabase/tests/helpers.sql`).
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r48_rewards_notices.sql`; unit: `features/rewards/occasions.test.ts`. Generators: `supabase/seed/3000_gen_catalogue.mjs`, `web/scripts/rewards-email-previews.mjs` (both with `--check`).
