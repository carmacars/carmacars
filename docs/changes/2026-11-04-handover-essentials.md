# The handover essentials first

The owner: the driver's licence, the signed rental agreement and the check-out photos are a hard priority. Migration 0263.

- Licence uploads take JPG, PNG, HEIC and HEIF (iPhone), WEBP, GIF and PDF scans (the first page), from the camera or
  from the files ("Take photo" and "Choose a file"). Each is turned into a standard JPEG in the browser (location data
  dropped, large photos made smaller), with plain errors when a file cannot be read. On the renter's verify page, the
  licence link page, Sign now and the field ready check.
- The booking's Next step (Admin and staff) puts the licence first, then the signature, then the deposit ("No deposit
  yet: take it now" on the pickup day), with the checklist: licence approved, agreement signed, deposit taken, check-out
  photos (and check-in photos, km and fuel once the car is out). The field handover's ready check already lists them
  and keeps the check-out closed until they are done.
- The gates stand in the database: no car is released without every driver's licence verified, the agreement signed, the
  deposit held or waived by an Admin with a reason, and the complete photo set with km and fuel; no check-in without its
  photos, km and fuel.
- The day before pickup (from 10:00), the renter is reminded of what is still missing (the licence, the signature): by
  text by default, by email when there is no mobile (Program settings, Essentials reminders: text, email or off). The
  evening before (18:00, a setting), staff get a task "Pickup tomorrow: ... missing ..." naming what is missing.

## Guides

No guide change: the booking page and the handover screen list what is missing; the steps to do each are unchanged.
