# The car cards: swipe, hover through the photos, and a drive-off that shows (Release 50: 5030)

Migration `5030_r50_card_matte.sql`. Web `components/CarCard.tsx`, `lib/card-photos.ts`, `lib/card-photos-model.ts`,
`styles/car-card.css`, `features/listing/studio-matte.ts`, `server/site-cutouts.ts`, `features/listing/worker.ts` (the gate
keeps its crop window), `components/Effects.tsx` (reveals and the page transition).

The owner (Nov 25): the car animations still were not right: "the scrolling through pictures by swiping when on mobile and
the scrolling through pictures when hovering or the car driving away thing."

## Why each one was wrong

- **The drive-off never showed.** A card drove off only when its cover had a side cutout (3150), and cutouts were made only by
  the background remover (rembg) with sharp (`server/site-cutouts.ts`), which exist only on the company server. The listing
  worker runs in the Cloudflare Worker, so every run answered "sharp is not installed" and no cutout was ever made. Since
  3701 the cutout must also be cut from the live side render, and no render had finished in production (`photo_standard`:
  0 renders done on 40 cars), so no card could have one.
- **The swipe** was a hand-made gesture (pointer events, a 36 px threshold) that changed the photo only when the finger was
  lifted: no momentum, no snap, nothing moving under the finger, and it was off on the home rail.
- **The hover** cycled the photos on a 0.9 s timer, so the pointer did not choose the photo, and the hover also started the
  drive-off: two motions fought for the same photo.

## What it does now

- **Phone**: the card's photo is a native scroll-snap track: one photo a snap, momentum, the rubber band at the ends, on every
  card (the Cars grid, the home rail, similar cars, landing, area and language pages). The browser tells a sideways swipe from
  a page scroll, so a vertical scroll is never taken. A tap (no swipe) opens the car. Dots show where you are. The first photo
  is the side profile (the gallery's order, 3701); the next photo loads as the card comes near the screen, the rest lazily.
- **Mouse**: the photo is cut into as many segments as it has photos; where the pointer is picks the photo, with a 180 ms
  crossfade, and the dots follow. Leaving the card goes back to the side profile. The photos load after 60 ms of rest on the
  card. **Keyboard**: the arrow keys on the focused card do the same.
- **The drive-off plays on a click or tap**, never on hover, so hover only scrubs photos and the click is the one moment the
  car leaves. The cover snaps to the side profile, the car winds up 3% back, then pulls away nose first along a slight arc,
  the rear squatting and the nose lifting, with two trailing copies for motion blur at speed; its studio floor shadow stays
  and fades. The car page starts loading 140 ms after the click (the owner's limit is about 250 ms) and the car keeps
  driving while it loads (the page transition skips its frozen snapshot for this click). A page that did not open within
  about 2 s gets the car back (it fades in where it stood); back from the browser's cache, the cards are reset. Wheels do
  not turn: the matte is one piece. Reduced motion or the pause control: no drive-off, no crossfade; the photos still change.
- **The cut**: the worker now cuts the car from the studio render itself, with no background remover: the render against the
  scene's plate, cropped by the same window the studio gate used (kept now as `r45.gate.measures.win`), inside the car's box
  and above the floor line, each row and column filled, the edge feathered (`studio-matte.ts`). It saves a 480 x 320 PNG
  whose alpha is the car (about 4 KB) and the plate cropped like the render (what is behind the car once it has gone). The
  card masks the render it already shows, so nothing loads with the page: the matte and plate load on first intent (hover,
  focus, touch, or a phone card coming into view, not with Save-Data). It runs every listing worker run, two cars at a time,
  on the Worker and the company server alike; every side render made at r48.3 from now on gets one, and a car with only an
  older rembg cutout is due again. A render made before this release has no crop window and gets no matte until it is made
  or checked again (3701 checks every render again).
- **Cards rise in** only in the public lists (the fleet grids and the home rail), 260 to 300 ms, staggered by 70 ms, opacity
  and transform only; the old 1.2 s photo zoom and the 1.1 s shine are gone.

## Database (5030)

- `site_cutouts.kind` (`cutout` from rembg, `matte` from the plate), `plate_path`, `box`.
- `app.site_cutout_source`: a studio render's plate (`showroom.background`), the gate's window and the car's box.
- `app.site_cutouts_due`: also a render that has a plate and only an older cutout.
- `app.site_cutout_save` keeps kind, plate and box (a matte without a plate is refused) and refreshes the listing row;
  `app.photo_side_cutout` hands them to `cover.cutout`.
