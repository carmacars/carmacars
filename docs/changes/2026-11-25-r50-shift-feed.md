# Field shifts in Google Calendar, by subscription (Release 50: 5050)

The owner (Nov 25): "employee shifts should get added to the carmacars admin google calendar automatically, best way would
be to maybe have automated emails or something? but also admin is getting absolutely spammed with emails". No email: each
person subscribes once to a private calendar link, and Google keeps it up to date.

Migration `5050_r50_shift_feed.sql`, route `web/src/app/api/shift-feed/[token]/route.ts`, card
`web/src/features/shift-feed/` (`ShiftFeedCard`, `ShiftFeedControls`, `links.ts` with its unit test), action
`web/src/server/actions/shift-feed.ts`, tests `supabase/tests/99_r50_shift_feed.sql` and `web/tests/e2e/r50-shift-feed.spec.ts`.

## What it does

- **shift_feeds**: one live link per person and scope; the token is 64 random hex characters (two random UUIDs). RLS with
  `admin_all`, the audit trigger with `redact` (the token never reaches the audit log). **Regenerate link** revokes the
  old row and makes a new token; **Turn off** revokes it.
- **Scopes** (`app.shift_feed_scopes`): `all` (every field shift) for an Admin and Fleet (`app.user_runs_fleet`); `mine`
  (their own claimed shifts) for field staff (`app.is_field_person`).
- **The calendar** (`app.shift_feed_ics`, from the 5000 ICS helpers): `METHOD:PUBLISH`, `X-WR-CALNAME`, the Toronto
  `VTIMEZONE`, `REFRESH-INTERVAL` one hour (Google ignores it and refreshes every few hours). From 14 days back to 60 days
  ahead, cancelled shifts left out. Each shift: `DTSTART`/`DTEND` with `TZID=America/Toronto`, "Field shift: <who> (1
  open)" or "Field shift: Open", the tasks (field jobs on the shift that are not cancelled), the note, and the link
  (`/admin/field-jobs?slot=` for an Admin, `/staff/fleet?slot=` for Fleet). The `mine` feed shows the tasks given to the
  person and links to My shifts. A revoked token, a suspended person or one who lost the scope gets 404.
- **Route** `/api/shift-feed/<token>.ics`: `public.server_shift_feed` with the server role only; `text/calendar`,
  `noindex`, `no-referrer`, cached 5 minutes privately.
- **Door**: `admin_read` / `ops_read` / `field_read` `shift_feed`, `admin_do` / `ops_do` / `field_do` `shift_feed_set`
  `{scope, action: create | regenerate | revoke}`.
- **Card** "Add shifts to Google Calendar" (or "Add my shifts ..."): the link, **Copy link**, **Add to Google Calendar**
  (`https://calendar.google.com/calendar/r?cid=<webcal URL, url-encoded>`), **Regenerate link** and **Turn off** (each
  asks once more), and the line that Google refreshes every few hours. On Admin > Field jobs, Fleet's Field jobs and
  Profile, and the field portal's My shifts and Profile. Nothing shows for a person with no scope.
