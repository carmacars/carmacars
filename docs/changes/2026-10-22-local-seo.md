## 4. Places

- Tables: `place_regions` (the 13 provinces and territories of Canada, the 50 states, DC and 5 territories of the United
  States, the 32 states of Mexico; all on), `places` (country > province or state > region > city or town >
  neighbourhood, airports and landmarks), `places_staging`, `place_imports`.
- Compact storage: integer ids, smallint kind, region and flags, 4-byte real lat and lng, hash indexes on slug and source
  id. Measured on a million synthetic places (28-character slugs, 15-character names): 120 MB of table, 220 MB with its
  indexes, about 230 bytes a place.
- Sources (web/src/features/places/gazetteer.ts, unit tested on scripts/places/fixtures): GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) for the
  tree, airports with IATA codes and landmarks; OpenStreetMap place nodes (ODbL) for neighbourhoods and towns, merged with
  a GeoNames place of the same name within 10 km (towns) or 3 km (neighbourhoods); the US Census Gazetteer (public
  domain) confirms US cities within 15 km and adds active incorporated places. A small place inside a large city's
  radius (0.5 x the square root of its population in thousands, 3 to 30 km) becomes that city's neighbourhood; an
  incorporated place or a seat never does.
- The places workflow (.github/workflows/places.yml, Run workflow only): GeoNames and the Census in one job, one job per
  Geofabrik extract for OpenStreetMap (osmium tags-filter and export), then build and load region by region, Ontario
  first, under Program settings place_db_cap_mb (6 GB; Supabase Pro includes 8 GB). A region that would pass the cap is
  skipped and named with the projected size. Each run is recorded in place_imports with the counts per country and kind.
  The workflow has not been run: this workspace cannot reach the sources.
- Turning a region on: it is on in Admin by default; run the workflow with that region (or all).
- Attribution on every place page and on /place-data, which also offers the derived places database on request (ODbL).
- The local stack loads the test places (Toronto and the GTA, Kingston, Buffalo, Guadalajara) the same way.

## 5. Terms and the place pages

- `place_terms`: 31 rental, 14 buying help, 27 detailing and 20 body shop terms, each with what is true about it, a
  synonym pointer (empty: its own pages), on or off; `shop_services`: 4 detailing and 4 body shop services, "price on
  request" until an Admin sets a starting price.
- Addresses: /car-rental/<place> (the base rental page; a place with a landing page is that landing page),
  /car-rental/<term>/<place>, /buy/<term>/<place>, /detailing/<term>/<place>, /body-shop/<term>/<place>.
  /car-rental/car-rental/<place> redirects to /car-rental/<place>; a rental term's Toronto page redirects to its landing
  page. The vehicle pages (branch vehiclepages) have a /buy catch-all: when both are merged it should try a buying help
  term first (two segments, the first a sale term from public.place_terms_public), then the vehicle pages.
- A page from data only (public.place_hub, web/src/features/places/copy.ts): the straight-line distance from our location
  (central Toronto until Program settings has our map position), delivery from our lowest real delivery fee within
  place_delivery_reach_km (80 km), the nearest airport, the landmarks under the place, the live cars, the family of
  places, questions answered from the same data. A place beyond the reach says we serve the Toronto area and have no
  location there, and takes a request. Outside Canada the prices are said to be in Canadian dollars. Mexico in English.
- Buying help pages: the notice (Program settings sale_disclaimer: never a dealer, lender or leasing company), rent before
  you buy, and "Find me this car" or "Ask about financing or lease options".
- Detailing and body shop pages lead with the rental offer ("Need a car while yours is detailed? Rent one, delivered";
  "Your car is in the shop? Get an insurance replacement rental, delivered").
- Indexing: one switch per family (rental, buying help, detailing, body shop; on), the quality gate (off; on: only places
  within the reach, and rental terms with a real matching car), a synonym's pages are noindex.
- Sitemaps: /sitemap-places/<family>.<term>.<n>.xml, place_sitemap_file_urls (25,000) places a file by primary key range,
  listed in the sitemap index (at most 49,000 files). No page is rendered for a sitemap.
- Cost: the place pages and the landing pages are dynamic (no page cache entry, so no R2 or KV write per page); the
  Worker entry keeps a 6-hour edge copy in the free Cache API (src/lib/edge/page-cache.ts) and browsers get
  `public, max-age=300`. Measured locally, warm: about 30 ms of server CPU per render (the Next.js server, 20 renders of
  three pages); the hub read itself takes 10 to 40 ms in the database. The pages read the settings without the fleet
  (getTokensLite).
- IndexNow: the home, hub, contact and service pages now, a landing page when it may be indexed (and when its cars
  arrive), the province and country pages after an import. Individual place pages are not queued.
