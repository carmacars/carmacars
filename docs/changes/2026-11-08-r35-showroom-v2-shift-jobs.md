## B. Photos without the image service (production: 134 photos failed on its quota, 17 cars left on sample photos)

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Local steps | `web/src/integrations/photos/local.ts`: resize (the site's sizes, JPEG), the black studio composite, interiors (cover and trim), the window layer (with a heavy blur), the preview and the touch-up are made in the Worker: one decode, one area-averaged pass down (sides capped), one encode, no transformation. `hybridPhotos` asks the image service only for the cut-out and a HEIC photo |
| Call sites moved off transformations | `worker.ts` `resize` (the sizes), `processPhoto` (composite, normalize), `cleanMarks` (normalize with the trim), `windows` (layer), `touchUp` (preview, enhance), `fallback` (the original now downscaled here); `drive-import.ts` `toJpeg` (a JPEG or PNG locally; HEIC still the service). | Optional service | Program settings `listing_cf_transformations` (off): on, the service makes every step; a quota, outage or "not enabled" answer still falls back to local. `public.server_listing_image_mode` tells the worker |
| A quota never fails a photo | A quota on the cut-out puts the original on the site (downscaled), flagged, with a `usage_paused` reason so 0080's usage retry makes it again; an outage is tried again and on the last attempt the original is used too. With the allowance used, inside photos, edits and sizes go ahead (only the cut-out waits) |
| Retry | `listing_jobs.retry_queued_at`; `app.job_photo_retry` every 10 minutes, oldest first, 6 a run; the intake leaves Failed when none of its photo jobs is failed. `ops_do photo_retry_failed {intake | all}` (Admin or Fleet): **Retry failed photos** on Admin > Listings, per listing and for all. 1050 marks every photo job failed now |
| Site delivery | Checked: images are served as stored (`images: { unoptimized: true }`, no `/cdn-cgi/image` link), so viewing the site uses no transformation |

## C. Field jobs on shifts (owner: "field auto assign to shifts, not to people... when a person claims a shift, they can see the tasks, and fleet can assign tasks to field")

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| A job's shift | `field_jobs.shift_slot_id`, set by the trigger `field_jobs_shift`: the published shift covering the job's time, else that day's nearest (`fx_slot_for`). 0880's day rules stay, a day "with shifts" now being a day with a published shift (taken or not). With automatic assignment off (`field_jobs_auto_assign`), jobs are left as before |
| Its people | `fx_slot_pick`: the least busy person on the shift; several share them (`fx_slot_rebalance`, run whenever someone joins or leaves a shift); nobody on it, the job waits on the shift. Giving a shift up, giving it away or swapping moves its jobs to whoever holds it next; a cancelled shift sends its jobs to another shift; a new shift takes the waiting work of its day |
| Field staff | My shifts: open shifts and given-up ones show "On it: 3 pickups, 1 return, 2 cleans" (`fx_slot_jobs`; cleans are detailing jobs); your shifts show their jobs. Today: **Also on your shift** lists the jobs of the others on your shift, **Take it** (`field_do job_take`) |
| Fleet and Admin | The board shows each shift's jobs (marked when nobody is on it) and each job's shift; **To a shift** (`ops_do field_job_shift`) moves a job between shifts; **Assign** takes a field person on a shift that day (refused on a day with published shifts when they hold none) or an Admin (no shift); **Unassign** leaves a job on no shift and with nobody |
| Alert | `app.job_field_shift_jobs` every 15 minutes: a shift starting within 24 hours with jobs and nobody on it raises a To do (`field_shift_no_one`, Fleet) and an urgent alert to Admin and Fleet on duty, once (`field_shift_slots.jobs_alerted_at`) |
| Existing work | 1051 puts future automatic jobs on their person's shift, sends the not acknowledged ones whose person holds no shift that day to the job's shift, and puts every unassigned future job on a shift |
| Tests changed | `99_r35t_field_assign_gate.sql`: the days with only an open shift are now days with shifts, so work due then stays on its day (on that open shift) |
