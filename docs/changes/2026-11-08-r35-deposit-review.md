# Deposit review, Finance countdowns, Finance does sales too (Release 35, part RR: 1010, 1011, 1012)

Branch `r35-rr`. Migrations `1010_r35rr_deposit_review.sql`, `1011_r35rr_academy.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/1011_gen_academy.mjs`
from `supabase/seed/academy/r35rr.mjs`), `1012_r35rr_guides.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/1012_gen_guides.mjs`). Tests:
`supabase/tests/99_r35r_deposit_review.sql`, `web/src/features/deposit-review/model.test.ts`, `web/src/features/accident-card/model.test.ts`.

The owner, November 8: Fleet checks the before and after photos of every return for damage and reports to Finance, who double-checks
before billing or releasing the deposit; the list is fuel, km, tickets, damage, late return and cleaning, checked by Fleet and then by
Finance. Finance checks the highway toll operator and every jurisdiction for parking tickets before a release, with the links, on a set
schedule 2 to 3 times a week. Finance sees countdowns on every booking. Finance staff can opt in to Sales. The accident card shows the
renter's own plan policy and the provider's claim page.

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Review | `deposit_reviews` with seven `deposit_review_items` (fuel, km, damage, late, cleaning, tickets, tolls), made by a trigger when a booking with a deposit is returned (never an Owner Booking or a placeholder); returned bookings of the last 30 days still waiting for their deposit got one at the migration. Fleet signs each item first (OK, or an issue with a note, the amount and the return photo numbers), then Finance double-checks it; nobody but an Admin double-checks their own Fleet check; Fleet cannot change an item Finance checked. Each item shows the facts the site has (the readings of 0910, the return report, the late time, the notices on the car) and a suggestion. History lines when each side finishes; To dos `deposit_review_fleet` then `deposit_review_finance` |
| Tolls and tickets | Finance's tolls item is the toll operator check (`deposit_review_toll_url`). Each ticket place (`deposit_review_ticket_places`, name and official link, copied onto the review when it is made) is recorded as none found or a ticket (`deposit_review_ticket_checks`); a ticket is logged through 0021's `app.log_toll_ticket` with the place and the plate, so it is matched and charged in Tolls and tickets as any other. The tickets item waits until every place is checked |
| Release | `app.deposit_blockers` (0155) adds the unfinished review: no part of a deposit is released until both sides signed every item; an Admin releases past it with the reason (0155's `deposit_overrides`, append-only and audited). Billing stays 0750's charge after the return and 0155's damage charge, from the booking's Money |
| Sweep | `app.drr_sweep` from the jobs tick: on each day of `deposit_review_sweep_days` (mon,wed,fri) from 6:00 Toronto, one Finance To do `deposit_review_sweep` with every plate returned since the last sweep and every booking still inside the toll and ticket window, and a morning digest line. Pages `/admin/deposit-reviews` and `/staff/deposit-reviews` (Fleet and Finance; views Open, Fleet, Finance, This sweep, Done) |
| Countdowns | `app.drr_countdowns`: the deposit hold's authorization expiry with the automatic re-authorization of 0750 (24 hours before); the deposit return promised to the renter (`deposits.release_due_on`: 7 business days after the return, `deposit_release_business_days` of the rental terms; with no check-in recorded, `deposit_release_reminder_days` after the return); the next payment of a weekly, biweekly or monthly plan (0330), overdue once passed. Red within 24 hours or past, amber within 48; soonest first or by column. On Money > Countdowns (Admin and Finance), the top of Finance's To handle and each booking |
| Alerts | Staff alert rules `finance_hold_expiring`, `finance_deposit_return_due`, `finance_payment_due` (normal tier: the quiet hours, the dedupe and the burst merge of 0462 apply) at 48 and 24 hours, and once when a payment is overdue; Finance and Admin morning digest lines with the counts |
| Sales | Finance staff opt in to Sales as field staff do (0931): `/staff/sales-start` (My numbers > Do sales too), the Sales path for remote work on their academy without holding their Finance work, the Sales function once it is passed |
| Accident card | A booking with the plan sold with the rental shows the renter's own policy number (`bookings.plan_policy_number`, entered by staff on the booking when not captured at purchase, else the activation reference) and the provider's claim page (`accident_plan_claim_url`), never a CarmaCars policy |
| Portals | Admin: Money > Payments tabs Countdowns and Deposit reviews, booking pages. Fleet: Checks > Deposit reviews. Finance: Money tabs Countdowns and Deposit reviews, To handle. The review page and the countdowns are the same in each portal |
| Academy | One section on the Fleet path and one on the Finance path; staff who had passed their paths keep a week before it holds them |

The deposit return promise used for the countdown is the one already in the rental terms: 7 business days after the return
(`deposit_release_business_days`); no new setting was added.

## Guides

- `va/fleet`: Deposit review: check each return first.
- `va/finance`: Deposit review: double-check, tolls and tickets, then bill or release; the sweep; countdowns; do sales too.
- `admin/tolls-and-tickets`: Deposit reviews before a release, the settings, the plan policy number on the accident card.
