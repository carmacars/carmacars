# Listing photos: one standard, every photo made by Gemini from the original (Release 45: 2840 to 2843)

Migrations `2840_r45_photo_standard.sql`, `2841_r45_photo_order.sql`, `2842_r45_photo_admin.sql`, `2843_r45_photo_guides.sql`; web `features/listing/r45.ts`, `worker.ts` (makeR45), `PhotoStandard.tsx`.

## Why Release 44 changed nothing visible

- **Made from the Cloudflare cut-out.** "Redo with Gemini" queued each photo from its kept cut-out (`photo_scene_queue` sends the mask as an edit): the car stayed the segmenter's cut-out pasted in the drawn room, and Gemini only relit that composite. Only a poor mask reached the background swap.
- **Narrow job.** `job_photo_spec` took 4 photos every 20 minutes, outside photos only, only those with a kept mask, each once a standard. Interiors and photos without a mask were never made again.
- **Daily cap.** 100 Gemini calls a day (500 at most by the column's check), 10 kept back: about 90 photos a day for about 100 cars with many photos each.
- **No missing angles.** Generated views (2200) were made only on a press, waited for approval, came after the real photos and were never first.
- **Inside photo first.** Nothing stopped a car with no outside photo on the site from showing an inside one first.

## The standard r45.1

- **From the original, by Gemini.** Every listing photo (new uploads in `process_photo`, the fleet in the new job `photo_r45`) is one Gemini edit of the original upload; the worker is given the local steps only (no Cloudflare cut-out, composite, resize or shrink). Outside photos: the whole background replaced by the showroom (the empty room is the second image). Inside photos: cleaned up, sharp. Other details: cleaned, sharp.
- **The check.** One more Gemini call (`GEMINI_CHECK_MODEL`, default gemini-2.5-flash) compares the result with the original: original background, warped, wrong colour, wrong model, parts changed, text elsewhere, readable plate, blurred, clutter, the view. With the site's edge check (`backgroundReplaced`) the verdict is `judgeCheck`. Refused: made again twice, then off the site and flagged (`r45.state = flagged`, quality flag `r45_flagged`). No answer is a refusal.
- **Plates (owner decision).** Blurred, never drawn: Gemini is told to blur each plate in place, and the site blurs every plate box the check found (`blurPlates`: a mosaic then three box blurs). A plate-like box off the car (high in the frame, or wider than a plate) refuses the photo. `plate_auto`, the plate redo and the plate editor blur too; the CARMA plate and the front plate edit are gone.
- **Angles.** `STANDARD_ANGLES`: front_left, front, left, rear_left, rear, dashboard, front_seats, rear_seats, trunk. A missing one (a flagged photo does not count) is a new `vehicle_photos` row with `ai_generated`, made by Gemini from up to 3 of the car's originals (same side first) in the same room, approved and public when it passes the check; a real photo of the angle replaces it.
- **Order and first photo.** `app.photo_rank`: the standard set in order, then front_right, right, rear_right, extra outside, then dashboard, front seats, rear seats, trunk, wheel, other. `app.cover_photo` returns no inside photo and `app.public_listing_photos` returns nothing while the first would be an inside one; `listing_primary_guard` refuses publishing such a car.
- **Throughput.** `listing_showroom_ai_daily` 2000 (check up to 20000), `listing_photo_r45_reserve` 50 for new uploads, `listing_photo_r45_batch` 12 waiting at once. `app.job_photo_r45` every 5 minutes (cron `carmacars-photo-r45`; `carmacars-photo-spec` removed), newest listed cars first; the worker makes 2 fleet photos side by side. A quota answer pauses Gemini 10 minutes (was an hour). Each photo is made once at r45.1 unless a person asks.
- **Admin.** Listings (Admin and Fleet) > **Photo standard**: per car photos at the standard / total, AI angles, flagged, last run, **Redo this car now**, **Redo every car**. "Redo photos with Gemini" and "Redo every car with Gemini" call the same redo. The listing review marks **AI generated** and **Flagged: off the site**.

## After deploy

About 100 cars with about 9 photos each (real and generated) is about 900 photos: 900 image calls and 900 checks. At 2 photos at a time each 5 minute run (about 30 seconds each), the fleet is done within about a day; the 2000 a day limit leaves room for retries and new uploads.
