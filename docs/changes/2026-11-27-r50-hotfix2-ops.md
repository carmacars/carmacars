# Hotfix 2 R50: 407 statements, the earnings calculator, expenses from receipts, ownership documents, the winter tire fee (5140 to 5146)

Migrations `5140_r50h2_tolls_407.sql`, `5141_r50h2_owner_calc.sql`, `5142_r50h2_winter_tire_fee.sql`, `5143_r50h2_expense_receipts.sql`, `5144_r50h2_ownership_documents.sql`, `5145_r50h2_notices.sql`, `5146_r50h2_guides.sql`. Asked by the owner on Nov 27.

## What was wrong

- **407 ETR statements ("super broken").** The reader (`web/src/features/tolls/parse.ts`) took each statement line as text and guessed its values from their place on the line: a distance printed without "km" was read as money; the amount was the line's last number and the fees the last less the first, wrong on a line with a toll, a camera charge, a trip toll charge and a total; a plate printed once above its trips was never given to them; the CSV download's account details above its header were taken as the header; a plate written with its province ("ABCD123 ON") matched no car; and a CSV amount left the fees out while a PDF amount had them in. The two real files of the finance To do's (the 2026-11-18 statement, PDF and CSV) were not reachable from here: no browser was connected and the connector has no toll-read tool. The tests use the published 407 layouts with made-up plates and numbers.
- **The earnings calculator.** The class came from the make alone (Acura: Premium) whatever the car's age, the rate from the premium rate card, and the month was that rate times the days: a 2015 Acura RDX made more than a 2025 Honda CR-V.

## Changes

- **407 statements (5140).** A statement is read as a table: the header names each column, each value is read from its own column (the PDF's text runs keep their position, `pdf.ts`; a CSV's header row is found under the account details, `headerRow`, and mapped, `autoMap`). The line's amount is what the renter owes (the total column, else the toll plus every fee column), Of which fees is the fee columns. A plate heading gives its trips their plate; a header on two lines, a place name on two lines and a day without its year (the year before when it falls after the statement date) are read; a credit is left out and said; a line whose charges do not add up is flagged. The 407 profile gets the plate headings and the statement charges that are not trips. Matching and charging are unchanged and tested with a statement: each line to whoever had the car at that minute, the boundary margin sends a near-edge line to a person, one administration fee per booking and statement; a ticket the same way.
- **Earnings calculator (5141).** At the occupancy (80%) a typical car makes the typical month for its owner ($1,200), never under the floor ($800): typical x the age factor (5% a year either side of a 5-year-old car) x the class's demand. A premium or luxury make older than 8 years is priced as the car under the make (its body, else standard). The days slider starts at the occupancy; the share and the season move the month. All Program settings.
- **Winter tire fee (5142).** The booking's **Winter tire fee** card (Admins, Finance; customer service and Sales while they may change fees) sets the rental's rate a day (0 removes the tires) and a cap, with the reason; insurer rentals too (the line is on the insurer's invoice items). Recorded in the booking's fee changes and history; the renter of a rental they pay is emailed.
- **Expenses from receipts (5143).** Expenses (Admin) and **More finance / More fleet > Expenses** (staff): take or upload the receipt photos, the vision model reads the amount, the HST, the date, the shop, the category and the car, the person checks and saves. The car's program owner pays by default (an open deduction on the next statement, the receipts in the owner's documents, an email); or CarmaCars (a company expense on the car), paid with the company's money or the person's own.
- **Ownership documents (5144).** **Vehicles > Ownership documents** (Admin) and **More fleet / More customer service > Ownership documents**: choose many permit photos or PDFs; each is read for its VIN and plate and filed on its car (VIN first, else plate) as its ownership permit, in the owner's portal, with an email. One that matched no car waits with a Fleet To do: pick the car or set it aside with the reason.
