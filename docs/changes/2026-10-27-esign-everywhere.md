## 1. What changed for the signer

Migration 0154 gave the rental agreements a signature that holds up in a dispute. Every other signature now goes through
the same steps (`EsignFlow`), with the same evidence:

1. **Identity**: a 6-digit code by text (the CarmaCars number) or by email to the signer's contact on file; only its hash
   is kept, 5 tries, 10 minutes, 5 codes a session.
2. **Read**: the signer says they read the documents.
3. **Initials** on the key terms of their documents (below).
4. **Consents**: to sign electronically (Ontario Electronic Commerce Act, 2000) and to receive the records electronically.
   No card consent: none of these documents charges a card.
5. **A drawn signature**. Typed names only with the accessibility fallback an Admin turns on (`esign_typed_fallback`).

| Signature | Where | Key terms initialled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The owner's agreements and Vehicle Schedules, Forms A and B, the partner agreement (one package) | `/owner/sign` | the Enrollment Agreement's three confirmations (own insurance, fees every month, the Tracker) when it is in the package |
| Form A or B alone | `/owner/documents/screen/a`, `/b` | none (the forms have no term to initial) |
| The partner agreement alone | `/owner/partner-agreement` | money (section 4), insurance and condition (5), ending (10) |
| Another Owner named in the Title Schedule | `/sign/owner/<token>` (the code by email: only their email is on file) | the three confirmations |
| Form C on screen | `/owner/documents/form-c/<car>` | its three acknowledgments; the witness beside the owner still types their name and the statement |
| An updated Vehicle Schedule | `/owner/sign/schedule/<id>` | the updated terms (it was a typed name before) |
| The investment agreement | `/investor/sign` | Schedule C (the legal advice choice stays) |
| The staff agreements, VA and field | `/va/sign`, `/field/sign` | confidentiality, the rules on money, keys and cars, ending the agreement (a generated agreement; an uploaded PDF has no known terms, so none) |

The statements the owner ticked before ("All of these", the initials typed in a box) are now the initials drawn in the
signing steps. The signed copies still print the signer's initials beside each confirmation; the drawn initials, with
their times, are in the certificate. "Draw instead" and "use your name" are gone: the signature is drawn.

## 2. The database

- `esign_sessions.context` takes two more values: `documents` (signer_ref: the signer's user; the documents the session
  covers in `documents`, each `{kind: packet | request | schedule_change, ref, title, sha256}`) and `owner_cosigner`
  (signer_ref: `owner_cosigners.id`). `public.server_esign_start` sends them to `app.esign_start_documents`, which checks
  each document against the records (open, the signer's, the SHA-256 shown) and takes the name and contact from them.
  One session covers a signing package; `signed_refs` records each document as it is signed and the session is closed
  with the last.
- The signing functions refuse a signature without a complete session covering the document, and a drawn signature
  other than the session's (`esign_strict`, on): `app.sign_request_sign` (Forms A, B and C, the partner, investment and
  staff agreements), `app.owner_sign_agreements`, `app.owner_cosigner_sign`, `app.schedule_change_sign` (the name
  recorded is the session's signer). Each signature record names its session (`signature_records.esign_session_id`,
  `vehicle_term_changes.esign_session_id`).
- `app.esign_doc_initials` lists each document's key terms; `web/src/features/signing/terms.ts` words them (a unit test
  reads the migration so the two cannot drift).
- The code by email for these signers is `document_signing_code` (the renter's `renter_signing_code` names a booking):
  to the signer's own email, never as a text, always sent.

## 3. Certificates and /verify

- Every signed copy ends with the certificate of completion of 0154 (`web/src/features/signing/certificate.ts`): each
  owner agreement and schedule (both Owners when there are two), each form, the partner, investment and staff agreements
  (`public.server_esign_certificate_for_request`).
- An updated Vehicle Schedule now has a signed PDF (the text signed, the owner's drawn signature, CarmaCars'
  countersignature, the certificate), filed with the new schedule in Documents (`public.server_schedule_change_attach`).
- `/verify` finds every signed copy CarmaCars recorded (`public.server_verify_sha`): the rental documents as before, and
  any document that is the signed copy of a signature request, a document sent to sign or an updated schedule.

## 4. What stays as it was

- The condition reports at check-out and check-in, signed by the renter and the staff member on the staff device: both
  are present, the staff member is signed in, and the report's photos and readings are the evidence. Not converted.
- CarmaCars' countersignatures (automatic, by the signing officer's standing authorization) and an Admin's
  countersignature of an investment agreement: CarmaCars signing its own documents.
- Documents signed on paper and recorded by staff.
- The unused typed-signature paths were removed (`signOwnerAgreements`, `signRequest`, `SignatureInput`).

## 5. What the owner does

Nothing to set up: it is on (`esign_strict`). A signer who cannot draw: an Admin turns on `esign_typed_fallback`.
