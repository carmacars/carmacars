# The offer editor: one form, edits that stay, no withdraw first (Release 48: 2940)

Migration `2940_r48_offer_fees_in_place.sql`.

The owner: "When editing an offer, I shouldn't need to withdraw it first", "When you put through an edit, it just reverts back", "Send the offer button on the top doesn't even work" and "way too many repetitive buttons".

- **No withdraw first**: a fee changed or a charge added from the booking's Money tab while the offer is out (offered, or approved and not paid) was refused with "An offer has been sent: withdraw it (back to requested) before changing its terms" (the booking's terms guard of 0025), while the card said the offer follows. `app.booking_fees_change` and `app.booking_custom_fee` now open the offer for the edit, change it, and send the next version (the renter told unless `notify` is false), as the offer screen and the coverage switch already did. Inside an offer edit nothing changes (it sends once).
- **Edits that stay**: after Save, the offer screen kept the boxes as typed and compared them with the booking before the save (its change list did not follow the saved booking), so fields stayed marked as changed, "Re-priced, not saved" stayed up, and the price typed by hand came back as an empty box with the rate sheet hint after a reload. The screen now starts again from the booking as saved after every save, and a price typed by hand shows in its box (with its reason) after a save and a reload; it is sent again only when changed.
- **Send the offer at the top**: the Next step button linked to the tab you were already on, and a link to the same page does not move (Release 27), so it did nothing. It now goes to the one main button (`#rv-send`) on the same page.
- **One form, one main action**: Car and dates, Price, Extras and fees, How it is paid, Message, then **More options** (how long it is open, Email or Text, who found the client, Book it now, rate type, the rate card, discount, lead-time pricing, extra kilometres, the price limits, insurer billing, the calendar and the internal note). One main button says what happens: **Send the offer** (a request) or **Send the update** (an offer that is out), and **Save without sending** beside it; Enter in a box saves without sending. The step bar, the second Review and send card, the second message box and Save and send / Save without telling the renter are gone. The same screen for an Admin, customer service and Sales. The Send an offer screen lost its step links, which repeated its headings.
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r48_offer_edit.sql` (edits of an offer that is out persist on the booking and the version sent, told or not, a later edit keeps the earlier one, a stale screen is refused, Money tab fees and charges in place), `web/tests/e2e/offer-edit-in-place.spec.ts` (the top Send the offer, an out offer edited, saved, reloaded and sent at 375 and 1440), `offers-and-rentals.spec.ts` and `admin.spec.ts` on the new buttons.
