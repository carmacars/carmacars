# The training academy (0207, 0208, 0209)

Release 21b, item 9. Migrations `0207_academy.sql` (tables, rules, the door), `0208_academy_content.sql` (the first
content, generated by `supabase/seed/0208_gen_academy.mjs` from `supabase/seed/academy/*.mjs`), `0209_r21b_guides.sql`.
Code: `web/src/features/academy/` (model, ScenePlayer, Study, Paths, pages, LessonPreview, academy.css),
`web/src/server/academy.ts`, `web/src/app/staff/academy/`, `web/src/app/admin/academy/`. Tests:
`supabase/tests/99_academy.sql`, `web/src/features/academy/model.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/release-21b.spec.ts`.

- Paths: customer service, fleet, finance, Sales in person, Sales remote, field work, Admin. A person's paths follow
  `staff_profiles.functions` (Sales by `sales_work_mode`) and the Admin role (`app.academy_paths_of`). A section can be on
  several paths (the policies): passed once, it counts on each.
- Sections of lessons, and a quiz per section. The quiz is graded in the database (`app.academy_quiz_submit`; the answers
  never reach the browser before), passes at `program_settings.academy_pass_bps` (80%), and every attempt is kept. A passed
  section opens the next one of the path; retries are allowed.
- Lessons are videos made in the browser: scenes (caption, narration, a picture: title, chat, list, steps, money, rule) in
  HTML and CSS with motion, captions on screen, a progress bar, play, pause, back, next, replay and speed (0.75x to 1.5x),
  narrated by the Web Speech API (`speechSynthesis`) in an English voice; with no English voice, captions only, each scene
  timed by its reading length. No media file is stored. A lesson played to the end (or stepped through) is recorded.
- Content: 19 sections, 43 lessons, 101 questions, written from the guides and the policy in the database: prices, offers,
  deposits and release, coverage (OPCF 27, credit card coverage, the Protection Plan), ages 18 to 25, licences, additional
  drivers, cancellation, late returns, kilometres, fuel, cleaning, tolls and tickets, damage and the $2,500 deductible,
  requests and claiming, documents and e-signing on the spot, insurer-billed rentals and adjusters, quotes and offers,
  discount codes, cash and the card reader, remote Sales duties, owner recruitment and objections, commission tiers,
  handovers and returns, Finance, Fleet and Admin work. Each objection is a chat scene: the renter's words and ours.
- Admin > Academy: progress per staff member (also the Academy column of Settings > Staff), a person's sections with
  lessons watched, best score and attempts, **Open early** with a reason (`academy_unlocks`); the editor of each section
  (title, lessons and their scenes with a preview, questions); **Take the Admin path** (`/admin/academy/learn`).
- Program settings: `academy_gate` (off by default: progress only). On, `app.my_roles` returns `academy_locked` for a staff
  member who has not passed their paths, and `requireStaff` sends every staff page but the academy, the guide and the
  profile to `/staff/academy`. Admins are never held.
- Door: `ops_read` academy_home, academy_section; `ops_do` academy_viewed, academy_quiz_submit; `admin_read`
  academy_progress, academy_content; `admin_do` academy_unlock, academy_section_save, academy_lesson_save,
  academy_lesson_delete, academy_question_save, academy_question_delete.
