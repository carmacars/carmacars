## 1. The offer price (owner: "lowering the price for increasing the time")

The offer (Send an offer, the review screen, a revised offer) and the checkout quote run the same pricing (`app.price_lines`
from the same rates, add-ons, coverage, drivers, lead time and discounts); 99_r35h_longer_never_cheaper prices five car and fee
setups both ways for every length from 1 to 60 days and finds them equal to the cent. What made a longer stay cheaper was the
rate tiers applied to the whole stay:

| Cause | Example |
| --- | --- |
| The 3+ day rate (0093) | 2 days at a $100 daily rate are $200; 3 days at a $60 3+ day rate were $180 |
| A monthly rate set below the weekly rate or 6 days | 6 days $600, 7 days the $500 monthly cap |

The monotonic guard: `public.calc_rental_charge` never charges N days less than a shorter stay from the same pickup. Inside each
tier the charge grows with the days, so the guard keeps the highest of the N-day tier price and the 1 to 6 day prices: N+1 days
cost at least what N days cost (3 days at $200 in the example). Every caller follows (the checkout quote, offers, Add a rental,
booking changes, the rate form's examples, the partner sheet; `rentalCharge` in `fleet-01-motors.ts` the same). The rental line
says "raised to the price of a shorter stay (a longer stay never costs less)". Not guarded: a promo code with a minimum length
(`promo_codes.min_rental_days`) is a deliberate step down. The day count, the per-trip fees and the HST of 1105 were checked
and are not a cause (1105 is the owner Insurance Fee only).

## 2. Extensions at the all-in price a day (owner: "based on the rates they're already paying ... averaged out per day")

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| The price | The booked period's lines charged to the renter with their HST (rental after discounts, every fee, one-time fees such as delivery, HST) over its days (`app.booking_all_in`), times the added whole days |
| Hours | The hours left under a day at the hourly late fee with HST, never more than an all-in day (`app.extension_all_in`) |
| Discounts | None: 0480's day discount and the 10% off hours are no longer applied (the settings stay, 0, marked unused) |
| The line | One line, "Extension: 3 days at $104.19/day (all-in)", tax included; its HST is the booking's own share (`hst_at_booking / total`), so amount plus HST is what is paid and the books never add HST on top |
| Paths | The renter's Extend page (the six offers and a date picked), approval, payment, the calendar's extension (`app.preview_extension`), and staff **Edit** making a confirmed or on-trip booking longer (`app.booking_staff_edit`: the per-trip fees stay as booked, the rental charge is solved in dry runs so the new total is the all-in price) |
| Not changed | An insurer-billed booking and an Owner Booking; a payment plan's next period and provisional dates (`admin_apply_booking_edit`, 1066's same rate); late returns (1063) |
| The screen | The Extend page shows each added day's price ("Each added day: $104.19 ...") and, for an option, "3 days at $104.19 a day: $312.57"; a date picked shows the price a day and the total, HST included |

## 3. RentalCover quotes

Partner API (Cover Genius, `https://partner-docs.covergenius.com/rentalcover/quick-start`): header `X_API_KEY`;
`POST /insurances/quote` with `FromDate` and `ToDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`), `CustomerAge`, `Country` and `DestinationCountry`
(CA), `FirstName`, `LastName`, `Email`; the answer's `Reference`, `TotalAmount` and `Policy.Code` are kept.
`web/src/integrations/rentalcover` is the client (no imports in `client.ts`, unit tested with a fetch stub); `web/src/server/rentalcover.ts`
asks the database for the facts (`public.server_rentalcover_facts`: the car's check, the renter's name, email and age at pickup, a
fresh quote for the same renter and dates), calls the API when there is none, and records the answer or the failure
(`public.server_rentalcover_record`, table `rentalcover_quotes`: the raw quote, the markup, our price, the request without the
email; service role only).

| Where | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The request form | Once option 3 is chosen and the renter's name, email and date of birth are in, `checkoutCoverQuote` gets a quote id; the price check (`app.price_quote`) prices the plan at our price; the request saved is priced from a quote too (`public.server_rentalcover_apply`) |
| Offers and the review screen | `offer_preview`, `offer_create`, `offer_revise`, `send_offer`, `booking_save` and `booking_preview` carry `rentalcover_quote_id`; `app.rentalcover_apply` sets the Protection Plan line (a booking line override) while the booking is a request; a price staff type wins |
| Our price | The quote, plus the markup when `rentalcover_markup_enabled` (off) with `rentalcover_markup_pct` and `rentalcover_markup_flat_cents` (both 0). Only our price is shown. The booking keeps `rentalcover_quote_id`, `rentalcover_quote_cents` and `rentalcover_price_cents` |
| Cache | `rentalcover_quote_cache_minutes` (15) |
| Failure | The failure is recorded (status failed, the error) and logged without the person's name; the plan keeps its current price (the Protection Plan table, or staff's) |
| Settings | Worker secret `RENTALCOVER_API_KEY` (GitHub secret, synced by the deploy job like the other Worker secrets; `.env.example`); `RENTALCOVER_API_BASE` (repository variable), default the staging API `https://api-staging.rentalcover.com` |

Not done (TODO(rentalcover-purchase) in `client.ts`): `POST /insurances/purchase` needs the renter's card number, expiry and
security code plus their address. Card data never reaches this server (Stripe holds the card), so no purchase call is made: the
owner agrees with RentalCover how a sold policy is paid (an invoice to CarmaCars, or RentalCover's own payment page) first.

## 4. The RentalCover value cap

`vehicles.replacement_value_cents` (Admin, **Value for the Protection Plan** on the car page, `admin_do vehicle_cover_value`, audited).
The car's value for the cap (`app.vehicle_cover_value`): the replacement value, else the last Black Book value of its buyout x 1.2,
else unknown, and unknown counts as over (`rentalcover_unknown_value_allowed`, off). `rentalcover_max_vehicle_value_cents`
($50,000). At or over the cap only OPCF 27 or credit card coverage: the checkout quote marks the plan not offered and a choice
of it as a problem (`coverage_not_offered`); the request form (`booking_car.protection_plan_offered`) and the offer screen
(`staff_offer_form` cars' `rentalcover`) leave option 3 out with the reason; trigger `bookings_z1_rentalcover_cap` refuses option 3
on such a car whatever the path (a request, an offer, a change, a coverage switch). Data Health (Fleet and Admins): **Car value
missing** lists the active cars with no value on file.

## 5. What we still need from you

`app.renter_still_needed(booking)`: from `app.booking_readiness`, `app.sign_pay_state`, the uploads and the drivers, every item the
booking requires of the renter with its status (missing, review, rejected with the reason, done) and one action: our open
question, the offer to approve, the date of birth, the home address, the signature, the payment, each driver's licence (front
and back) and selfie, each additional driver's acceptance, the proof of coverage (options 1 and 2), the deposit.
`public.my_booking` and `public.my_bookings` carry it as `still_needed` (`app.my_still_needed` checks the booking is the
renter's). The checklist (`web/src/features/portal/StillNeeded.tsx`, `still-needed.ts`; test ids `still-needed`,
`still-needed-<booking number>`, `still-needed-item-<key>`, `still-needed-action`, `still-needed-bar`, `still-needed-count`) is the
first thing on /account for each current and upcoming trip not complete, and at the top of each request and booking. The
reminders' "Still missing: ..." (`needed_items` of `app.ntf_booking`) lists the same items.

## Decisions for the owner

- RentalCover markup: off until RentalCover confirms a markup is allowed; then set the percentage or the fixed amount.
- The Protection Plan line carries HST as it does today; RentalCover's quote may already include the tax on insurance. Confirm
  with your accountant whether HST belongs on the plan we resell.
- Every car needs its value entered (Data Health lists them) before the Protection Plan is offered on it again.
- The extension line is a rental line, so the owner's share counts the whole extension (fees averaged in) as rental.
- A promo code with a minimum length can still make a longer stay cheaper.
- RentalCover's production URL and how a policy is paid (the purchase call).
