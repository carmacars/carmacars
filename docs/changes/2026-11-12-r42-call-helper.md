Branch `r42-callhelp`. Migrations `2670_r42_call_helper.sql` (the table, the reads and actions, the first scripts) and
`2671_r42_call_helper_guides.sql` (the guide chapters, generated by `supabase/seed/2671_gen_guides.mjs`). SCHEMA_MARKER

`supabase/tests/99_r42_call_helper.sql`; unit tests `web/src/features/caller/call-helper.test.ts`; e2e

The owner asked for an extremely easy helper for new hires inside the pop-up staff already get on a call, and for the
pop-up itself to look clean and to minimize instead of only closing.

## 1. The Call helper

Inside every call of the caller pop-up (Admin portal, and the staff portal of customer service and Fleet):

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Say | The greeting to read out. It is picked from who is calling (a caller we do not know, a renter with a booking, a renter, a lead, an owner, a staff member) and carries the staff member's first name, and the caller's first name and booking when known. A caller known only by a number, or a greeting that is off, gets the greeting for a caller we do not know |
| What is the call about? | One big button per situation. The ten seeded: New booking or price question, Change or extend a booking, Running late or late return, Pick-up and delivery, Deposit and payments, Insurance and protection, Accident or damage, Complaint, Owner wants to list a car, Job applicant. Each opens 3 to 6 short steps; words in quotes are shown as what to say; **All situations** goes back |
| Links | A step links to the right page as `[Send an offer](/offers/new)`. The pop-up puts the reader's own portal in front (`/admin` or `/staff`), sends a page that has another address in the other portal to the page of the same job (Card coverage), and shows plain words where that portal has no such page (Hiring, for staff). Only a portal page or an `https://` address is accepted |
| Search the quick answers | A search box over the FAQ (16 seeded: age, what you need, deposit, deposit back, kilometres, late return, extending, cancelling, delivery, debit cards, another driver, insurance, fuel, smoking and pets, tolls, rideshare). Every word typed must match the question, the answer or the row's search words; a match in the question comes first |
| Hide | One tap puts the helper away for people who no longer need it, remembered in the browser |

### The words are in the database

`app.call_helper_items` (kind `greeting`, `situation` or `faq`; a situation's lines are its steps). An Admin edits them in
**Settings** > **Call helper** (`/admin/settings/call-helper`): change a greeting, add, change, reorder, switch off or
remove a situation or a question. A change shows on the next call, with no release, and is in the audit log with the
words before and after. Greetings are fixed in number (the pop-up looks them up by caller), and the one for a caller we
do not know cannot be switched off.

### No policy number is typed

The scripts were written from the staff guides (customer service, texts and calls) and hold no amount. A word in braces
is filled in by the database each time the helper is read (`app.call_helper_facts`):

| Word | From |
| --- | --- |
| `{min_age}`, `{offer_hours}`, `{km_per_day}`, `{deposit_release_days}`, `{debit_deposit_times}`, `{cancel_day_one}`, `{cancel_48h}`, `{cancel_24_48h}`, `{cancel_under_24h}` | Program settings |
| `{late_grace}`, `{late_fee_hour}`, `{late_fee_cap}`, `{extra_km}`, `{fuel_markup}`, `{smoking_fee}`, `{pet_fee}` | Fee defaults (the default in force for all classes) |
| `{deposit_tiers}` | Deposit tiers that are offered |

So the answer to "what if I am late?" changes the day the fee default does. The Admin page lists each word with today's
value and where it comes from; a word in braces that is not on the list, a link that leaves the portal without https, or a
situation of more than 8 steps is refused on save (`app.call_helper_problem`, the same check the seed passes).
`{staff}`, `{caller}`, `{booking}` and `{car}` are filled in by the pop-up from the call.

### Reads and actions (the door)

| Name | Who |
| --- | --- |
| `call_helper` (read) | Whoever has the caller pop-up: Admins, customer service, Fleet (`app.caller_popup_ok`). Through `/api/staff/phone` |
| `call_helper_admin` (read), `call_helper_save`, `call_helper_remove` | An Admin |

The table has row-level security and no grant to a browser role; every function is the server's only.

## 2. The pop-up

- Restyled with the design tokens only (`styles/tokens.css`): the card, a quiet tile for the booking, balance and
  deposit, the kind of caller as a pill, the call's timer beside its state. It sets the semantic tokens for itself, so the
  shared buttons and links inside follow, and it turns dark with the device (`prefers-color-scheme`).
- **Minimize**: the line button at the top right of a call turns it into a chip at the bottom of the page with the
  caller's name and the call's timer; the chip opens it again. A click anywhere outside the panel and Esc minimize too
  (never close). A dialog of the page that is open takes Esc first. The cross still closes the call in this browser. A
  new call always opens by itself; what is minimized is remembered across pages for two hours, like closed calls.
- Keyboard: every control is a button with a name, focus moves to the chip when a panel is minimized from the keyboard
  and back to the panel's Minimize button when the chip is pressed. One quiet status line announces who is calling
  instead of the whole panel being a live region.
- A phone: still a sheet across the bottom of at most half the screen, scrolling inside.
- Same component in the Admin and staff layouts (`features/caller/CallerPopup.tsx`), so both portals get it.

## Not changed

The caller's details, the notes, Create lead, Open the renter's page and who gets the pop-up (0800, 0970). The handover,
check-in, check-out and late return screens are another branch's (`r42-handover`): the late return script here only
points to the booking's late return panel.
