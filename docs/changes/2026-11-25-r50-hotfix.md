# Hotfix R50: the cars are back, a calmer top bar, calendars (5064 to 5066)

Migrations `5064_r50h_photos_fallback.sql`, `5065_r50h_calendar_feed.sql`, `5066_r50h_guides.sql`. Reported by the owner on Nov 25 (11:44 Toronto), about 40 minutes after R50 went live.

## What was wrong

- **No car on the cards, the car pages or the home hero.** 3701 made every public photo a studio render that passed the studio gate (`app.public_listing_photos`, `app.photo_row_cover`). A car with none got one "Photos coming soon" placeholder: the scene's empty plate, which is the dealership background with no car, no logo and no name. Production had no passed render yet, so every car, every example car and every sample photo turned into the empty plate. The hero and the home sections (`web/src/components/SlotCars.tsx`) did the same: no live render meant `/studio/coming-soon.jpg`.
- **The staff top bar was too full.** The Admin and staff bars had up to 10 buttons (Profile, Academy, the assistant, What's new, Guide, Guide me, Report a bug, Sign out, plus the bell). R50 added Guide me. On a phone the labels wrapped into narrow columns.
- **Calendars.** The interview call's calendar invite (5000, `app.interview_invite`) goes only to the applicant. No Admin or interviewer ever gets one. The Admin's notice of a booked call (5051 `app.admin_now_email`) is a plain alert email with no .ics, capped at 3 an hour (5063). The townhall invite is not digested or capped for an Admin (`app.staff_email_own` keeps it on its own), but it is an email the person must accept.

## Changes

- **Photos (5064).** A car with at least one live outside render shows its renders, as 3701 does. A car with none shows its photos exactly as before release 50: `app.public_listing_photos_pre3701` and the listing row of `app.public_listing_row_pre3701` (its real photos, the 3150 renders, or the built-in sample photos with their caption). The placeholder stays only for a car with no photo at all. The listing cache and the landing pages' snapshots are refreshed. The card shows the sample photo's caption and the studio covers in turn as before; the drive-off still needs a matte (`data-matte`), and runs only once the matte, the plate and the photo have loaded.
- **Hero.** With no live render, the hero and the home sections show their own section photo (the car, the logo and the name) as before release 50. The empty plate shows only when a section has no photo.
- **Top bar.** Every portal bar (Admin, every staff function, field, owner, investor) keeps the portal name, the bell and a new **Account** menu (`web/src/components/AccountMenu.tsx`). Profile, Academy, the assistant, What's new, Guide, Guide me, Report a bug and Sign out are in the menu. Nothing is removed. The menu closes on Escape or a click outside; on the Admin and staff rail it opens in the rail's flow (the rail scrolls). A dialog that an item opens still shows after the menu closes.
- **Calendar feed (5065).** The private calendar feed of 5050 also carries the weekly townhall (the next 8 meetings, one event each, UID `townhall-YYYYMMDD@carmacars.ca`, a cancelled meeting left out) on every feed, and every booked interview call on an Admin's feed (time, the applicant's first name, the role, the call link and the applicant's page; UID `interview-<application id>@carmacars.ca`, so a moved call moves). The card is now **Add shifts, townhalls and interviews to Google Calendar** (field staff: **Add my shifts and townhalls to Google Calendar**).

## Not fixed here

- **Phone alerts turning off.** No R50 change touches the push devices: `push_subscriptions` and `server_push_result` (3420) are unchanged, `usePushDevice.ts`, `push-sw.js` and the CSP `worker-src` are unchanged, and production has `push_enabled` on. The cause is not found in the code. The next step is to read `push_lost` for today's rows (reason and endpoint) in the database.
