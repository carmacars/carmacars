# The Finance dashboard and the bank reconciliation (Release 49: 3480, 3481)

The owner (Nov 20): "RBC new account may have API access for payouts and balances: check, then build a finance dashboard."
What was checked and the path chosen: `docs/finance/bank-data.md` (RBC offers a Balance and Transactions API to business
clients through their advisor; open banking is not live yet; aggregators are unconfirmed for business accounts; the RBC
CSV/OFX download is always available and is built now).

Migrations `3480_r49fin_finance.sql` (tables, functions, door, notice) and `3481_r49fin_guides_academy.sql` (guides, academy).

- **Bank data** (`bank_accounts`, `bank_imports`, `bank_balances`, `bank_lines`): Admin and Finance read them (row-level
  security, read only); written only through the functions; every table audited. Only the last four digits of an account
  and a SHA-256 of the full number are kept.
- **Import** (`app.bank_import`, door `bank_import`): the browser reads RBC's CSV (classic: Account Type ... CAD$, USD$;
  RBC Edge and others: Date, Description, Debit/Credit or Amount, Balance) or OFX/QFX (`web/src/features/finance/bank-file.ts`)
  and sends the lines. Each line is kept once: the key is the day, the amount and its place among the same day's lines of that
  amount in the file, so the same file twice, overlapping downloads, or the other format of the same days add nothing; two real
  identical lines on one day are two lines. The balance comes from OFX, a CSV balance column, or is typed (`bank_balance_set`).
- **Matching** (`app.bank_candidates`, `bank_auto_match`): exactly one record that fits matches by itself (money in: a Stripe
  payout, an e-Transfer, bank transfer or cheque recorded on a booking or an owner invoice, an insurer invoice; money out: an
  owner or staff payout, a commission payout, a referral partner payout, an expense with its HST, a refund sent by transfer),
  within `finance_match_window_days` (4). A record is matched to one line only (unique index). By hand: `bank_line_match`
  (pick a record that fits, set aside with what it is, undo with a reason). Each step in the audit log.
- **To dos and notice**: a line still unmatched `finance_bank_unmatched_days` (2) after it posted, within
  `finance_bank_todo_window_days` (60), is a Finance To do (`bank_unmatched`) and the notice `staff_bank_line_unmatched`
  (Finance morning digest and staff bell; Admins as well). Matching or setting it aside closes the To do; undoing raises it
  again. Checked at every import and daily (`app.job_bank_unmatched`, 10:35 UTC).
- **Dashboard** (`app.finance_dashboard`, door `finance_dashboard`): cash position (bank, Stripe balance and payouts on the way
  read live on the server, free cash), money in and out of the month, deposits held, HST for the quarter (collected on renter
  lines of rentals that ended in it, owner and insurer invoices; paid on expenses and payouts; Insurance Fee lines never carry
  HST, and one that does is listed), renter balances overdue, insurer invoices unpaid by age, twelve months of money in and
  out beside the monthly statement's net, and the reconciliation counts.
- **Screens**: Admin > Company > Finance (`/admin/finance`, first tab of Company) and the same page for the Finance role,
  Money > Finance (`/staff/finance`): Overview and Bank lines (import, match, set aside, undo).
- **Live feed** (`web/src/integrations/bank/`): the `BankFeed` interface and a fake; Program setting `bank_data_provider`
  (`file` until RBC's API or an aggregator is connected); **Pull the bank feed now** shows when a feed is set. A feed goes
  through the same import.
