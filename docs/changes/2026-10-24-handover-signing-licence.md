## 1. Check-out and check-in: what existed, what was missing

The field portal already had the full checklist (0020, 0031): the ready check, the renter, the original licence and the
card, the odometer and the fuel or charge (both required by the database), the keys, the 19 guided photos in a fixed
order (every side and corner, the roof, the windshield, the four wheels, the instrument cluster, the seats, the trunk,
the keys), damage marks on a car diagram with a close-up each, the tracker, both signatures, and an upload queue that
keeps photos on the phone (IndexedDB) and sends them when the connection returns. The return compares with the pickup,
works out the lateness after the grace period and opens an incident for new damage.

## 2. The return settlement at check-in

When a return completes, `app.return_auto_charges` runs the Check-in assessment of 0020 (`app.assess_check_in`) at once
(switch `booking_return_auto_charges`, on): the mileage overage at the booking's rate, the late return extra days and fee
(the fee defaults' grace period and daily cap), the fuel or recharging and the fuel service fee. A gas car returned short
with no pump receipt is priced at the refill price per eighth of a tank (`statement_fuel_cents_per_eighth`, $10 by
default) plus the fuel markup; entering the receipt replaces it. A missing fee default never stops the return: the lines
are left to Finance, as before.

Staff waive a line (an Admin, Finance or Fleet) with the reason (`check_in_waivers`): the line is voided ("waived: ...")
and stays out when the assessment runs again; a line already on a statement is refused (credit it on a new statement).
An Admin corrects the actual check-out or check-in time with the reason: the condition report's time, the minutes late,
the extra days, the end reason and the late lines follow, and the history keeps both times.

"Late by 2 h 10 min" (or "Overdue by 45 min" while still out) shows on the booking, in Admin > Bookings and on the report.
The renter's "Returned" email now says "Returned 3 h late. 50 km over the included distance. Fuel 25% lower than at pickup",
with the new damage, in its findings line.

## 3. Signing on the spot

**Sign now on this device** on the booking (Admin, Fleet, Sales, Customer service) and on a field job: the staff view lists
who still signs (the renter, each additional driver) with their licence on file; the staff member ticks that the name
matches the licence and that they saw the original licence (and can photograph the licence: front, back, selfie), then
presses **Hand the device to** the signer. The signer reads CarmaCars' agreement (and is told the one signature also
signs Blind Spot's), ticks the statements (four for the renter, three for a driver) and signs with the name on file or
by drawing. **Back to the staff view** for the next signer.

`app.sign_in_person` records the same signature as remote signing (`app.record_signature`, the second agreement's
SHA-256 checked for the renter as 0127 does) and, at the insert (the records are append-only), who collected it, the
device and the identity check. The signed copy's record page reads "signed in person on a CarmaCars device, collected by
<staff>; device ...; identity checked". A rental added by hand is charged and its signed copy filed on signing, as when
the renter signs in their portal (`server/portal/added-charge.ts`, shared now).

**Email the signing link** sends the "Sign your rental agreement" email again; **Text the signing link** queues a text from
the CarmaCars number with the link to the signing page (an opted-out number is skipped and said). Additional drivers keep
their own links (Drivers and documents).

## 4. The licence with the request and before the offer is accepted

The documents step opens at the request for the licence images (the coverage proof keeps 0072's rule). The Request sent
page, the renter's booking, the offer page and the verify page ask for the front, the back and a selfie (switch
`booking_licence_selfie_required`, on) and the licence number and expiry when missing. A licence on file from an earlier
booking is reused while its files are kept and the licence is valid after the return (`app.licence_reuse`: the same
files, their keep-until moved to this rental's, the same staff decision).

Any acceptance of an offer needs it: `app.approve_offer` (the renter's, staff on the renter's word, and any offer made by
staff on the offers branch, which accepts through it) refuses "Upload the driver's licence before accepting the offer:
... still needed", and the payment facts carry `licence_missing` so no payment starts without it. A rental added by hand
is not an offer accepted and is left alone. The switch is `booking_licence_required` (on), under Program settings,
Booking requests and offers. Additional drivers upload theirs (and their licence details) on their own signing link.

## 5. Addresses fill in everywhere

The address lookup (Google Places with the daily cap of 0113, 3 characters and 350 ms before asking) now also gives the
chosen place in its parts: `/api/address/place` calls Place Details in the same session (`addressComponents`, the same
Essentials field set) and `features/forms/address-parts.ts` fills street, unit, city, province, postal code and country.
Over the cap the field says "Address suggestions are paused for today: type the full address" and every line stays typed
by hand.
