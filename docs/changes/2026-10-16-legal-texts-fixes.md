## 1. One Late Fee rule

The texts said three different things: the PAD agreement and the debit notice said a returned debit adds the Late Fee,
the "no debit on file" email said an invoice "still unpaid after its due date" takes it, and section 4.4 of the
Enrollment Agreement says "If a charge fails, CarmaCars adds the Late Fee for each Vehicle affected and retries on the
Retry Date".

What the code does (unchanged, 0007 and 0037): the Late Fee is added once to an invoice (one per vehicle) when the bank
returns its automatic debit, and the debit is tried again from the retry day; when the retry fails too, the invoice is in
arrears and the amount is deducted from the owner's revenue. An invoice merely unpaid gets no Late Fee, and neither does
a debit the owner started from an invoice page that the bank returns (the invoice stays due). This matches 4.4 read with
4.3 ("CarmaCars charges each Vehicle's Fees on each Billing Date"), so the code stays and the texts follow it.

The rule is one sentence from `app.owner_debit_terms()` (`late_fee_rule`), built from the settings:

> If your bank returns the automatic debit of an invoice, we add the $50.00 Late Fee to that invoice once and try again
> from the 18th, as section 4.4 of your Enrollment Agreement sets out.

It is printed by the PAD agreement (both forms), the Banking page, the invoice list and invoice page, and the emails
`owner_debit_notice`, `owner_debit_setup_needed` (the wrong sentence is gone), `owner_fee_invoice`, `owner_payment_failed`
and the PAD confirmation. The PAD agreement for paying an invoice now adds `own_debit_rule`: a debit the owner starts from
an invoice page that the bank returns adds no Late Fee.

## 2. No typed days or amounts

Every email gets the settings in words (`app.ntf_setting_words()`, an emitter's own value wins): `debit_day`,
`retry_day`, `payout_day`, `approve_by_day`, `arrears_removal_days`, `notice_period_days`, `deductible_amount`,
`safety_certificate_days`, `retransfer_business_days`, `refund_days`, `deposit_release_days`, `holdback_days`,
`min_renter_age`, `id_retention_days`, `non_refundable_percent`, `incident_report_hours`, `interest_after_days`. The 26
emails that typed one of them read it now (their catalogue rows are regenerated by `supabase/seed/0051_gen_catalogue.mjs`).
The fee invoice email's "due on the 15th" is the invoice's own date (`due_date`). Screens: owner Invoices, Payouts,
Statements and a statement, the application wizard (`owner_program_facts` gives the days), Admin > Invoices, Admin >
Owner debits (the retry day was the number with "th" added, which read wrong for 1, 2, 3, 21, 22 and 23), the statement PDF and Stripe's fallback
interval description.

Left as they are: the user guides (their text describes the defaults and is edited in Admin > Settings > Guides) and
copy that states a fact with no setting behind it (a bank debit takes up to 5 business days to clear).

## 3. PAD notice days at least 10

`program_settings.owner_debit_notice_days` is now checked between 10 and 30 (it was 1 to 30); the default stays 10. The
owner PAD agreement does not waive the Rule H1 pre-notification, so no waiver setting was added. Admin > Settings shows
the range and refuses a lower number before saving.

## 4. CarmaCars' own PAD confirmation

New email `owner_pad_confirmed`, once per debit mandate, when it becomes active (set up under Banking, confirmed by the
micro-deposits, or saved with a debit paid from an invoice page). A deferred constraint trigger on `owner_payment_methods`
reads the row at the end of the transaction, so a setup saved and marked pending in the same call sends nothing yet. It
carries the payee (the business name, `company_trade_name`; "<legal name>, operating as CarmaCars" only when the new
setting `owner_debit_show_legal_name` is on), the address and phone of the settings (the email footer's), the billing
email, the bank and last 4, personal or business, the date accepted, the debit day, the notice days, the first automatic
debit date, the Late Fee rule, how to cancel, the recourse rights, and a link to the agreement on the Banking page.
The on-screen agreement (both forms and the agreement in force on the Banking page) shows the same payee block.

## 5. The booking amendment is countersigned

When the renter signs, CarmaCars countersigns automatically (method `automatic`, `booking_amendments.countersignature_record_id`)
in the name of the staff member who proposed the change or approved the renter's request, at the same time, with the
document's SHA-256 and the wording "Countersigned for CarmaCars automatically when the renter signed, in the name of the
staff member who proposed or approved the change." The amendment says so in section 4 and its signature table names that
person; the signed copy shows "Signed electronically for CarmaCars" and the record line. Text version `amend-v2-2026-10`.

## 6. Paper signatures: CarmaCars signs when staff record it

While staff record a paper signature, CarmaCars' automatic countersignature takes the time of the recording (a trigger
on `signature_records` during `app.record_paper_signature`); the signer's and witnesses' records keep the date signed on
paper.

## 7. Rentals added by hand: awaiting signature

A rental staff add by hand is confirmed at once but is "awaiting signature" until its agreement is completed (signed in
the portal, or the paper copy recorded by an Admin): `app.booking_awaiting_signature()`, in the readiness
(`awaiting_signature`, and `ready_for_handover` false), the Admin booking list and page, the renter's portal (badge,
blurb, progress), and the field ready check, which lists "Rental agreement signed" and stops the handover. The handover
itself is refused by the trigger `bookings_awaiting_signature_guard`. A past rental recorded for history and an Owner
Booking are not concerned.
