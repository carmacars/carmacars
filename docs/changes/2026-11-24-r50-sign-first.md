# Sign first, then cars, and owner fixes (Release 50, owners: 3800, 3801, 3810, 3820)

Migrations `3800_r50ow_sign_first.sql`, `3801_r50ow_guides.sql`, `3810_r50ow_owner_fixes.sql` and `3820_r50ow_document_check.sql`. The owner (Nov 24): "The agreements should be signed before cars get transferred over ... make this process for owners much more straightforward." A fleet supplier who signed up that day had nothing to sign: the Fleet Supplier Lease Agreement and the ServiceOntario forms were only made when an Admin approved a car. Program and flat-rate owners had the same gap (their Enrollment Agreement was made with a car's terms). The fixes of 3810 come from an owner's reports of the same day.

## What it does

- **Agreement at signup** (`app.owner_master_ensure`, a deferred trigger on `owners`). Once the profile has what the agreement needs (`app.owner_master_missing`: the legal or corporation name, the address, the email and, for a corporation, the signing officer and title), the master agreement is made: the Fleet Supplier Lease Agreement with the Title-Holding and Lease Agreement for a fleet supplier (the default terms of Program settings, `fleet_lease_default_*`), the Vehicle Enrollment Agreement of the pay model otherwise. No car is needed: `packetKeys` lets the owner sign it with an empty Schedule A ("No Vehicle yet ..."). E-signature as before (0193), countersigned at once by the signing officer of Settings. A supplier's pay model is the fleet lease from signup (`app.owner_pay_model`), so his signed lease is never swapped for a standard one when his first car is added.
- **Schedule A per car.** A fleet supplier's car gets its numbered Schedule A entry when it is added (`app.fleet_schedule_cars`, from the application, staff's Add a car and the VIN list) and its ServiceOntario forms then. Cars added after the master is signed come in by the Additional Vehicle Schedule: one e-signature per batch, by both parties (`owner_schedule_ready` asks for it). The VIN list works for suppliers, with a tier and plate per row (`app.owner_add_vins`). Our lease text (`docs/source/v2_fleet_lease.md`) has no section 16.6; the Additional Vehicle Schedule (Vehicle defined in section 1) is the confirmation both parties sign.
- **Gates** (Program setting **Sign first**, `owner_sign_first`, on). `app.owner_sign_gate`: a car of the owner program is not approved (a supplier's application), delivered (`app.fleet_lease_car_set`, a supplier's car) or rented (a trigger: `onboarding` or `ready_to_list` to `available` or `on_rent`) until the master agreement and its Schedule A entry are signed. An Admin's approval before the signature is kept (`owner_applications.sign_wait_*`) and goes through by itself at the end of the signing transaction (`app.sign_first_release`); if it cannot, an Admin To do `owner_sign_release_failed`. A program owner's car gets its Schedule A entry when its terms are set (its fees are set then), so its gate is delivery and renting.
- **What is missing**: `app.owner_sign_status` (door `owner_sign_status`: the owner, an Admin, customer service, Fleet, Sales for their own owners) and **Agreements before cars** (`SignGate`) on the Admin owner page (Overview and Terms), the staff owner page and the application, with **Send reminder** (`app.owner_sign_reminder`, door `owner_sign_reminder`: Admin, customer service, Fleet; once in 10 minutes). The Admin agreements checklist names a missing profile.
- **The owner portal's guided path** (`features/owner-path`): Your profile, Sign the agreement, Add your cars, Photos and inspection, Approved, Delivered; the current step with one big button, the rest in plain words; 375 px wide without overflow.
- **Backfill**: every owner of the program without a master agreement whose profile is complete gets one, waiting for the signature, and is told; a supplier's cars in intake get their Schedule A entries and forms (the supplier who signed up on Nov 24 among them).
- **Notices** (email, in-app, push topic documents or requests, texts for the three owner signing notices): `owner_master_agreement_ready`, `owner_schedule_ready`, `owner_sign_reminder`, `owner_car_delivered` (owners); `staff_owner_signed` (Fleet and the Admins, with what comes next).
- **Connector**: `owner_agreements` (admin:read: status, cars, what is missing, the links), `send_owner_agreement_reminder` and `set_onboarding_method` (admin:write, previewed then confirmed).
- **Owner fixes (3810)**:
  - A long rental's money: the owner's booking card shows the rental charge agreed for the whole rental and, from stored values only, what the approved statements carried so far and for how many of its days (`app.owner_booking_money`). Booking CC-10008's stored rental charge ($573.00) is the total typed by hand less its fees (a young driver fee of $1,680.00 for 48 days, a $480.00 Protection Plan and $75.00 delivery): each extension raised the per-day fee and lowered the rental. That record needs an Admin's correction; the card now says what is on the statements.
  - Pickup not recorded, car presumed out: a confirmed booking whose pickup passed with no handover keeps its car busy after its return time (`app.vehicle_is_available`, `app.booking_date_conflicts`) until the handover, the return or a no-show is recorded; the owner portal lists it on a trip ("Pickup not recorded, car presumed out"); Fleet (then the Admins once its return passed) get a To do every 15 minutes until done (`app.job_presumed_out`, pg_cron).
  - Form D's Part 2 printed the owner's drawn signature: the PDF renderer counted signature rows per table (`render.ts` now counts across the document). Form D copies already filed with a drawn signature are filed again (their pdf job queued again, the old copy marked replaced) and the owner told (`owner_document_reissued`).
  - HST per fee: the Insurance Fee and the first and last month never say "plus HST"; the Tracker and Install Fees say it when there is an amount (the Vehicle Schedule, the agreement-ready email, the application).
  - Onboarding Method: an Admin changes it before the Vehicle Schedule is signed (**Change the method** on the Terms tab, `app.set_onboarding_method`); Schedule VS-1012 is set to Transfer. Unsigned agreements and Forms A and B are made again from the records when the owner opens them (content keys); signed copies download from Documents as before.
- **Every owner, every car (3820)**, Abbas: fix these for everyone, not only the owner who reported them.
  - The signing officer's title is a role: a name is refused (`owners_title_role`); titles on record that are a name are emptied where the agreement is not signed yet, so the owner is asked for it.
  - The Onboarding Method agrees with how the car is held: an unsigned schedule's method is one `app.onboarding_options` opens for its holder, title and mode. With none chosen the screens fell back to the first option of the list (a personal car showed a new Owner Corporation or Co-Registrant); they now take the recommended one, else Transfer (`app.default_onboarding_method`, `ApproveStep`, `AdminForms`).
  - First and last month is two months of the Insurance Fee unless agreed otherwise (`app.owner_terms_resolve`); a schedule kept the amount of an earlier fee when the fee changed (the Sonata: $700, two of $350, with $284 now). An unsigned schedule's amount that was two of the old fee now follows the new one; the unsigned ones off the rule with no first and last invoice issued are set back to the rule. Draft invoice lines of insurance with HST lose it.
  - **Document check** (Admin > Owners, door `owner_documents_check`, connector `owner_documents_check`): what is still inconsistent, by owner and by issue, with the fix. The counts before the fixes are in the audit log (`owner_documents_audit`).
- **Security**: every new function is security definer with `search_path = public, app, pg_temp`, executed by the server role only.

## What's new

- For owners: you sign your agreement first, before any car comes over, and your portal shows six steps with one button for the next thing to do.
- For owners: a fleet supplier can add cars from a list of VINs, with each car's tier and plate.
- For admins and staff: **Agreements before cars** on the owner page says what is missing; **Send reminder** emails the owner.
- For owners: a long rental shows what your statements carried so far; a car whose pickup was not recorded shows as out.
