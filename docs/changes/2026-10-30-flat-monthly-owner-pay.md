# Flat monthly payment per car (0196)

The owner, October 30, 2026: "Some cohosts don't care for monthly splits or paying us for insurance; they just want a
monthly payment per car. We need a way to reflect that."

Migration `supabase/migrations/0196_flat_monthly_owner_pay.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0196`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0196_gen_guides.mjs`. Tests: `supabase/tests/99_flat_monthly_owner_pay.sql`.

## 1. The arrangement, per car, with history

`vehicle_owner_arrangements`: a flat monthly payment for a car (the amount a month, from a date, to an optional last day,
both counted). No period on a day means the car's revenue share terms apply, as before. A change ends the period in force
the day before the new date and replaces anything set from that date on (cancelled, never deleted). One period at a time
per car (a trigger refuses overlaps).

Admin > Owners > an owner (or the car's page), Cars and terms: **Pay** on the car's row. Flat monthly payment (amount,
From, Last day optional) or Revenue share (from a date), a reason, **Review** then **Confirm** (`car_pay_set` through the
staff door, an Admin only). The row shows "Flat monthly payment $900.00 a month since ...". Program owners' cars only (not
a shareholder's, a partner's or a company car; investor and partner cars are out of scope).

A date inside a month whose owner statement is approved or paid is refused: an approved statement never changes.

## 2. What the owner is paid

- Each month of a period is an owner credit (`owner_credits` kind `flat_monthly`) on that month's statement, a
  **Flat monthly payment** line (`line_type flat_payment`). A part month is paid by the day: the days of the period inside
  the month, within the car's Enrollment Date and Exit Date, over the days of the month. The credits are worked out again
  on every build of a draft and settled with the statement like any credit.
- Rentals: a booking part that counts on a day of a flat period (the day it ends, as for the statement month) stays with
  CarmaCars: its split's company share is 100% (`company_share_bps` 10000, owner payout 0, `booking_splits.flat_arrangement_id`),
  so the company statement and reports keep the whole rental, and a later refund, chargeback or extra charge never reaches
  the owner. On the owner statement the rental is listed with no Owner Revenue and no Revenue Share ("flat monthly payment:
  CarmaCars keeps the rental"). A Turo trip ending on such a day likewise (its terms' share kept in
  `turo_trips.terms_share_bps` and put back if the period goes).
- Fees: no Insurance Fee or Tracker Fee for the days of a flat period. A month all flat has no monthly invoice; a part
  month is billed for the other days only.
- HST on the payout is added as for any payout when the owner's HST number is on file.
- `owner_statement_vehicles.flat_payment_cents` and `owner_statements.flat_payment_cents` carry the totals; the owner
  portal statement, the PDF and the Admin statement show them.

## 3. Expenses and owner costs

- A booking expense (0156) on a flat booking is not taken from the owner. An Admin may press **Charge to the owner** beside
  it on the booking (`booking_owner_expense_charge`): it becomes an owner cost (`owner_deductions` kind `booking_expense`)
  on the statement of that booking, with its reason. Undone with **Do not charge the owner** until the statement is
  approved.
- Kept as before: owner costs recorded on the owner's account (tolls and tickets of the owner's own use, claim shortfalls,
  plate renewal, maintenance, agreed costs). They are the owner's own costs, whatever the pay arrangement. Refund and
  chargeback shares never arise on a flat booking (the owner was paid nothing from it).
- The owner's booking rows list only the expenses charged to them on a flat car.

## 4. The owner portal

Vehicles: the car shows "Flat ... a month", no insurance or tracker fee, and "How you are paid" on the car page. The month
to date and the statements show the Flat monthly payment line. Booking amounts follow the existing visibility settings.

## Judgement calls

- The arrangement of a booking is the one on the day it counts (the day it ends), for the whole booking.
- The Tracker Fee stops as well as the Insurance Fee during a flat period.
- Monthly invoices already issued for days later made flat stay as they are; Review lists them so an Admin voids or
  credits them.
- No email to the owner when the arrangement is set; it shows in their portal.
