# r50 hotfix2: API certificate, pay offline, offers that never say Sent unsaved, instant booking, pay later

Migrations `5110_r50h2_pay_offline.sql`, `5111_r50h2_ended_never_blocks.sql`, `5112_r50h2_connector_set_setting.sql`,
`5113_r50h2_instant_complete_profile.sql`, `5114_r50h2_pay_later_start.sql`, `5115_r50h2_connector_rentals.sql`, `5119_r50h2_guides.sql` (generator
`supabase/seed/5119_gen_guides.mjs`); test `supabase/tests/99_r50h2_hotfix.sql`.

## Why

- api.carmacars.ca failed TLS in browsers (staff Google sign-in, Admin file links, vps-health 000). The Origin CA
  certificate lost `*.carmacars.ca` (f4df37d3), but Caddy 2.10.2 kept serving the old wildcard one for api. and meet.:
  `caddy reload` without `--force` skips a config whose text did not change (deploy-vps 45956912204, tls-report).
- Stripe closed the account; renters still tried to pay by card.
- An offer showed Sending, then Sent, then Send the offer again, and was never sent: the shared button feedback guessed
  success when no new error appeared, and an ended booking (pickup never recorded) kept blocking the car (3810).
- CC-10673: the car went out before payment; with no processor the booking stayed pending payment.

## What it does

- **Deploy**: every Caddy reload forces; when Caddy hands out a certificate other than the file's, or an Origin CA one
  for a DNS-only host, it is restarted; the deploy fails unless api. has a publicly trusted certificate. deploy-vps
  `action=test` runs a read-only `tls-report` (records, proxy state, certificates per host). The background remover and
  speech images are built on the GitHub runner and sent; nothing is built on the server.
- **Pay offline**: card payments are off in Program settings (set by an Admin). `app.card_payments_state()` returns the
  direct deposit details an Admin types under Invoices: how to pay CarmaCars (`cc_pay_*`, 1108); none is in the
  repository. Pay pages, the extension, tip and add-on pages, gift cards, owner
  invoices and the owner's debit setup show e-Transfer and direct deposit and no card form while cards are off.
- **Buttons**: a success word (Sent, Saved) shows only after the action worked and stays until the person changes
  something; an error in the button's own form or card counts as a failure; a held button refuses a second press. The
  offer review screen says Sent only on a saved offer and scrolls to the confirmation. On a phone the Report a bug
  button sits inside a pop-up's flow, never over it.
- **Calendars**: an ended booking never blocks its car (5111).
- **Connector**: `set_setting` (Admins, preview then confirm, before and after logged; secrets and bank details refused).
  `create_offer` documents `coverage_option` (1 OPCF 27, 2 card, 3 Protection Plan). 5115: `add_rental` (Add a rental: a
  pickup already passed is recorded on trip in the same step), `change_coverage` (also on a rental in progress),
  `confirm_pay_later` and `start_rental_pay_later`, each the screen's door action, previewed then confirmed.
- **Instant booking**: a complete profile and a verified licence, whatever the tier; never a sample car or an owner with
  `instant_book_off` (01 Motors). Cars page filter **Instant booking**.
- **Pay later**: Admin actions **Confirm, pay later** and **Car already out: record the pickup** (door `admin_do
  booking_pay_later`, `booking_start_pay_later`); Finance To do `collect_pay_later`. The incident card works while the
  handover is recorded and payment is owed.
- **Wording**: the renter's Do this next button names the item ("Add your date of birth", never "Next: Add it"); a car
  photo that fails to load shows the placeholder.

## Inbound email after the switch

deploy-vps 45974166959 (`email-report`): Email Routing on the carmacars.ca zone is `unconfigured` with no rules, so the
switch's "0 rule(s)" changed nothing. Mail to carmacars.ca addresses goes to Google Workspace (MX aspmx.l.google.com),
untouched by the switch. Turo emails reach the site through the Gmail Apps Script (`scripts/turo-gmail.gs`), which posts
to `https://carmacars.ca/api/inbound/turo`, now served by the server. Both Workers have no cron trigger and no custom
domain: the server's cron is the only one.
