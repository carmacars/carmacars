# The earnings calculator counts the car's year (Release 50: 5013)

Migration `5013_r50_calc_year.sql`, maths `web/src/features/owner/earnings.ts` (`rateFor`, `yearFactor`), tests `web/src/features/owner/earnings.test.ts`.

## What changed

- **Found by Sales**: "What you could earn" gave a 2012 and a 2024 of the same make and model one rate. The model average ignored the year.
- **Each model carries its cars by year**: `owner_calc_data().models[]` adds `years` (each year's average daily rate and count, from the cars' real years) and `avg_year`.
- **The rate**: the same model within two years of the car when there are any (their average); otherwise the model's average moved by the years between: 5% less a year older (down to 55%), 3% more a year newer (up to 120%). With no year, the model average as before. The class and rate card fallbacks are unchanged (the class already ages by its rules).

## Guides

No guide change: the calculator's page already says the estimate is from cars like yours and is rough; the numbers are now closer.
