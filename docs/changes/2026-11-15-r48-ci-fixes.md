# What the release 48 checks found, fixed, and Propose a change priced as it lands (Release 48: 3050)

Migration `3050_r48_ci_fixes.sql`.

The full checks of the merged release (deploy run 43743996484) failed in the end-to-end tests and the role crawls. The causes:

- Dates and times typed by staff were read in the browser's own time zone. The offer editor, Change car or dates, a blocked time on the fleet calendar and every date and time box of the Admin forms turned "10:00" into the browser's 10:00: right in Toronto, five hours off in a browser set to UTC, and wrong for a staff member abroad. They are Toronto time now, shown and saved (`toLocalInput`, `fromLocalInput` in `web/src/features/admin/Act.tsx`).
- The Rewards welcome (3000) went to an owner who applied to list a car: the application makes the account and its renter record before the owner record. The account's welcome now goes when the email address is confirmed, to a renter-only account (as the account email); the first confirmed booking still sends it to anyone who has not had it.
- Release 48's squished-text fix (break-word instead of anywhere) lets a long word, such as an email address, set a table column's or a grid's width. The request's Check it and send step and the owner's paid invoice were wider than a phone: the email column and the paid card now break a long address inside the box.
- The Scene box of Studio photos was as wide as its longest choice (wider than a phone): it fits its column now.
- Tables that now scroll sideways (a guide's table, an owner's updated Vehicle Schedule, a fleet lease's cars) can be reached with the keyboard (`tabindex`, a named region); the published guide chapters get the same wrapper (no text or version change).
- **Propose a change** priced one more day of a 1-day booking ($401.15 in all) at $931.12: its preview added the booking's own fee lines to fees it priced again, so a fee in both counted twice, and accepting landed on another total than the one agreed. The preview now prices the change as accepting it would (made for a moment, priced, undone: `app.change_trial`); a later return alone costs the booking's all-in price a day (rental, fees and coverage, HST included), as Edit booking and the connector's change_booking do; staff may type the new total instead (**Or the new total**), and the rental charge is worked out to land on it. The preview shows the price a day. `app.preview_booking_change` and the previews that call it are volatile now.
- **Send to the renter** says why it is greyed out: press Preview the price first, fix what the preview says, or write the reason.
- Tests that followed screens release 48 changed on purpose: the academy row counts the sections of every path a person has (The renter's side was added for all staff, 2990); an Admin's "Who found this client?" sits under More options (2940); a guest who makes an account gets the Rewards welcome with the account email (3000).
- Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r48_rewards_notices.sql` (the welcome on confirmation, never for an owner applicant, a linked record); `supabase/tests/99_r48_ci_fixes.sql` (one more day costs the all-in day, a typed total to the cent, the preview changes nothing, accepting lands on the agreed total).
