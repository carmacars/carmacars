# Careers and hiring, October 26, 2026

The owner's request (October 26): automate hiring from Indeed (and OnlineJobs.ph for the remote roles): answer every applicant
with screening questions for their role, score them against a rubric, give the qualified ones a link to book a 15 to 20
minute call (all calls on one day), and give the interviewer a rubric to fill in during each call, with rankings, so the
highest ranked are hired. Built into carmacars.ca instead of Google Forms; the content of the owner's Google Apps Script
draft (questions, points, knock-outs, pass mark 60, emails, rubric, call guide) and section 4 of
`docs/source/va_hiring_package.md` were the source. Changes the owner made while it was built: anyone who does not qualify for
Field staff is offered Sales instead of a decline; Sales has no right-to-work question; Sales is two roles by where the work is
done (in person in Toronto, or remote), both commission only (remote 5 points lower on every rate) and both hired into the
existing Sales function; the remote assistant roles are built to the same standard, closed until opened.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0173_careers_hiring.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0173`), its email block generated by
`supabase/seed/0173_gen_catalogue.mjs` and its guides block by `supabase/seed/0173_gen_guides.mjs`. Tests:
`supabase/tests/99_careers_hiring.sql` (113 assertions), `web/src/features/careers/model.test.ts` and
`web/tests/e2e/careers-hiring.spec.ts` (375 and 1440, with axe).

## 2. Applying and booking

- `/careers` (the open roles; noindex and out of the sitemap while none is open), `/careers/<slug>` (the role, with
  JobPosting structured data: one posting a page, the pay only where it is a figure, TELECOMMUTE with the countries for a remote
  role, `validThrough` 60 days after posting), `/careers/<slug>/apply` (noindex), `/careers/book/<token>` (noindex, out of
  robots). The footer shows Careers and the sitemap lists the pages only while a role is open.
- The form: contact details, then the role's questions, one column, about five minutes; saved on the device as it is typed
  (`localStorage`, restored on return); Turnstile, a honeypot and a rate limit per connection
  (`program_settings.hiring_apply_hourly_limit`, 10 an hour). `?src=indeed|onlinejobs|referral` and the `utm_` parameters are
  recorded. The same address applying twice to a role is answered as the first time.
- `public.server_hiring_apply` scores on the server (`app.hiring_score`) and `app.hiring_screen` decides: invited (the booking
  link), referred (the other role, a one-click link with `?ref=<token>` that fills in the name, email and phone and links the two
  applications; nothing is sent when they already applied for that role, a note says so), declined (a knock-out with no role to
  offer), or below the bar (the owner reviews; only a confirmation email).
- Interview days (`interview_days`, `interview_slots`): from, to, 15-minute slots with a 5-minute break, one candidate a slot,
  phone (staff call the number on the application; Call links are `tel:`, which the Quo app answers) or video (a Jitsi Meet link
  per booking), for some roles or all. Booking locks the slot row: two candidates never share a one-place slot. Move and cancel
  from the same link. Toronto time, with Manila time beside it for a remote role.
- Seeded: Thursday, October 29, 2026, 9:00 to 19:00, by phone, for Field staff and Sales in person (only when the database is
  built before that day).
- Emails (audience `applicant`, from hello@, replies to contact@): `applicant_received`, `applicant_invited`,
  `applicant_declined`, `applicant_referred`, `applicant_booked`, `applicant_reminder` (6 pm the evening before, and an hour
  before), `applicant_cancelled`. A text an hour before when phone and texts are on (`program_settings.hiring_sms_reminders`).
  `app.job_hiring` (pg_cron `carmacars-hiring`, every 15 minutes) sends the reminders.

## 3. Admin > People > Hiring

- Applications: filters by role, status, source and score; Invite anyway, Decline (the email can be left off).
- Calls: the calls of a day with Call or the video link and Start. The candidate page: every answer with its points, the
  knock-outs, linked applications (a referral both ways), a note, the rubric beside the call guide.
- Rubric (`interview_scores`): Communication 20, Reliability and availability 20, Role skills 20, Customer or sales instinct
  15, Judgement on the scenarios 15, Attitude 10, each 1 to 5; interview score of 100; final = 40% screening + 60% interview;
  rank per role (no-shows and withdrawals not ranked). Decision: Hire, Maybe, No, No show.
- Shortlist: each role ranked. Hire: the existing staff invitation (field staff, or the assistant portal; both sales roles are
  the Sales function, the work mode passed as `work_mode` with the invitation and kept on the application for 0174), then the
  existing generated staff agreement is sent to sign from the same page.
- Interview days: add, change, turn off, delete (not with bookings). Roles and links: open or close each role, pass mark and pay,
  the apply link with `?src=indeed` (or `?src=onlinejobs`) and the message to paste into Indeed.

## 4. Privacy

Applications are for Admins only (row-level security and the doors; no audit trigger on applications, so the audit log keeps no
copy). The name, contact details, answers and notes of a candidate who is not hired are removed 12 months after the decision
(`program_settings.hiring_retention_months`). The privacy policy's tables say what is collected and for how long (the live copy is
changed only where it still carries our text; `docs/source/website_terms_policies.md` too).
