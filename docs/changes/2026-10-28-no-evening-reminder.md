# Interview reminders: the hour before only (migration 0181)

- Booked interview candidates no longer get the reminder email at 6 pm the evening before; they get the one about an hour
  before the call (and the text, when phone and texts are on). Resend's free plan sends 100 emails a day, and a full
  interview day spent about 30 of them the evening before.
- Program setting `hiring_evening_reminder` (off) turns the evening email back on.

## Guides
No guide change: the hiring screens are unchanged; only when the reminder email goes.
