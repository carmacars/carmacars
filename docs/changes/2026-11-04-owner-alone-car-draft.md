# An owner on their own, and a car before its VIN or plate

Migration 0264.

- Owners > Add an owner: the full legal name, email, phone, individual or corporation, and the terms agreed (Insurance
  Fee, Tracker Fee, Revenue Share: the owner's default for their cars), without a car. Nothing is emailed unless "Invite
  the owner now" is ticked (then the usual invitation to choose a password). The owner is listed on Owners at once.
- Add a car for an owner takes a car with only its year, make and model (trim, colour, odometer and notes if known) when
  neither the VIN nor the plate is known yet: saved as an onboarding draft with its fleet number. Until a VIN or plate is
  added on the car page it is never listed, never bookable and not put on the fleet policy ("VIN or plate needed" on the
  car, and a task in To do that closes by itself once one is added).
- Notes on a car: on the add-car form and the car page (Edit the notes).

## Guides

No guide change: the forms say what each field is for; the guide chapters on owners and cars stay accurate.
