# Hiring calls fit the screen, Previous and Next call; make, model and colour filters on /cars

- Admin > Hiring > Calls: the remote call's confirmation (Confirmed, Waiting, Cancelled) now shows under the status
  instead of in its own column, so the table fits the screen without sideways scrolling.
- A candidate's page has "Previous call" and "Next call: <name>", in time order for that day, straight to the rubric.
- The cars page (/cars) has Make and Model choices and Colour boxes in its filters; they show as chips, clear with
  "Clear filters", and are kept in the address like the other filters.

## Guides

No guide change: the filters and the two buttons are labelled where they appear.
