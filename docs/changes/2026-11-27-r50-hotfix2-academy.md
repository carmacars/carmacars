# R50 hotfix 2: the academy basics, payouts after the whole academy, stuck pages, field routing with shifts paused, no VA label for in-person staff (5170 to 5175)

Migrations `5170_r50h2_academy_basics.sql`, `5171_r50h2_academy_when_stuck.sql`, `5172_r50h2_academy_shorter.sql`, `5173_r50h2_field_routing_paused.sql`, `5174_r50h2_basics_guides.sql`, `5175_r50h2_no_va_in_person.sql`. Asked by the owner on Nov 27.

## What the owner asked

- The academy causes a huge backlog. The first sections that cover the basics open portal access and work; the full academy is needed only for commission and pay payouts (gate them, show the reason). Shorten it: cut repetition, audit every quiz question against its lesson. A staff member stuck on a page or hitting repeated errors gets that page's section, required before continuing that area. Admins open any lesson and quiz directly. Keep fix once, the 2-minute wait (5070) and the leads gate (5071).
- Field routing with shifts paused: work not done carries over to the next shift; the person on shift sees every pickup and return of the day and the work up to the next shift; days with pickups or returns and no shift go to the shift before or after by timing. Pause shifts by migration, keep the claimed shifts and the routing.

- REQ-065: "still says VA as a role for my in-person staff". VA only for remote assistants.
- Test fixtures with hard-coded past dates (`44_sample_cars_back.sql` asked for a pickup on Nov 20) become relative to now().

## Found in production (Nov 27, Admin connector)

- Every open field job had nobody (`assigned_to` null): Program settings `field_jobs_auto_assign` was `none`, which switches off the shift routing of 1051 (`app.field_jobs_shift`). So the person on shift saw none of the day's pickups and returns. 5173 sets it to `least_busy`.
- Production still has `academy_fail_cooldown_minutes` 30 and `academy_attempts_per_day` 3: 5070 (the 2 minutes and 10 tries) is not live yet. It goes live with this branch.

## Review fixes (fresh-eyes review, Nov 27)

1. **No loop for an unsigned agreement.** `requiredFor` never holds the onboarding pages (`AGREEMENT_OPEN`: the documents to sign, Welcome, the profile, the guide, the academy), `requireStaff` skips it while `agreement_locked`, `academy_assign_stuck` assigns nothing then (`why: agreement`) and `academy_required` lists nothing then.
2. **Routing only to worked shifts.** `fx_route_slot` takes a shift with a `fx_slot_people` member (on the plan, field, not academy-locked) first, else an open shift (1051: claiming it brings its jobs). `field_route_run` also moves open jobs with nobody on a live shift nobody works. With no live shift near, the job is left unassigned (off any shift); that and a job left on a shift nobody works give Fleet one To do (`field_jobs_no_shift`, one open at a time).
3. **The statement path only.** The pay guard skips a `pay_statement` payout only when `field_pay_line_paid` sets `app.pay_line_commission` around its own insert. An Admin's `commission_payout_record` meets the hold with any method, unless the academy is waived.
4. **Commission held, only when it is.** `app.academy_commission_held(staff, period_end)`: the hold and an approved commission of that period waiting. The payroll line's `academy_hold` and `commission_held` of Record all payments use it.
5. **One alert a pay period.** Record all payments sends `academy_pay_held` once a person a pay period (any line of the same period already alerted: none again).
6. **Migrations.** 5172 reports an unexpected count of removed questions as a notice (never stops a deploy). 5170 and 5172 are re-run safe (renames once, `create or replace`, patches skip when applied, trigger dropped first; checked by running both twice). 5173 inserts the settings row when there is none, so `field_slots_auto` is false on a fresh database too.
7. **An Admin clears a stuck section.** `admin_do academy_assignment_clear` (Admins only, a reason required): the row keeps `cleared_by` and `clear_reason`, the audit log the change. Admin > Academy > the person: **Clear** beside each required section.
