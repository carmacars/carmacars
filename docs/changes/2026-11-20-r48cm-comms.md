## What the diagnosis found (Nov 20)

- **Email (Amazon SES).** The production keys are deployed (the Nov 15 deploy passed AWS_SES_* to send-outbox) and `email_ses_enabled` is on, yet every email goes through Resend's free plan: the usage report of Nov 20 00:54 UTC read "Resend 84/100, waiting 91, held: daily email limit 91" with Cloudflare off. SES sends nothing because it refuses every address it has not verified (MessageRejected "Email address is not verified": the account is in its **sandbox**, `email_ses_daily_max` still at the sandbox's 200), so each email falls to Resend, whose 100 a day run out by the afternoon and the rest wait for the next day. The sender now records this (`email_provider_state.ses_sandbox_at`) and the Admins get one alert a day with the step. Owner: AWS console, region Canada (Central) > Amazon SES > Account dashboard > **Request production access** (transactional); then raise `email_ses_daily_max` and turn on `email_production_ready` in Program settings.
- **Texts (Quo).** The key works (quo-check, Nov 20) and the webhook is enabled at carmacars.ca/api/webhooks/quo; sends fail with Quo 402 (prepaid credits used up). Owner: Quo > Settings > Billing > **Add credits** (and auto-recharge).
- **Calls (Twilio).** The repository has the Twilio secrets; TWILIO_TWIML_APP_SID is empty (the site finds or makes its TwiML App by name). The live logs and Twilio's own answers could not be read from this session (the live site is not reachable through its proxy; dispatching a read-only comms check with production secrets was refused), so the failing step is not confirmed. **Settings > Calls > Run test call** now names it (keys, account active or trial or suspended, balance, the TwiML App and where it sends calls, the caller ID verified, the setting, the webhook, the token, then a real test call). The most likely, in order: the account balance or trial (Twilio Console > Admin > Account billing > Add funds / Upgrade), the Quo number not verified as a caller ID (Phone Numbers > Manage > Verified Caller IDs > Add +12893125991), an API key deleted (Account > API keys & tokens).
- A read-only check of all three (`quo-check` workflow, job `comms`: the send logs of email, texts, calls and phone alerts, and Twilio, SES, Quo and the Worker's secret names as each provider sees them, GET requests only, no key printed) is in the repository for the next run by hand.

## Changes

- **A text that cannot go falls back to email** (`app.text_fall_back`): the email the text replaced goes again (0096), any other text is emailed as `text_fell_back` (branded, the same words), and the text says "not sent: fell back to email". A staff member's own text is not emailed without them (they see the refusal at once).
- **Quo out of credits pauses texts** (`phone_provider_state`): nothing is tried while paused, every due text falls back at once, one text is tried again every 30 minutes (`text_retry_paused_minutes`) and the first that goes ends the pause. The Admins get **one** alert a day (`texts_not_sending`), never one per text.
- **Never a late text**: a text waiting longer than `text_stale_minutes` (2 hours), one whose email already went, and a reminder no longer relevant (booking cancelled, picked up, returned, its moment passed) are dropped before texts are claimed; a stale or irrelevant drop sends no late email either. Refilling the credits sends nothing stale (tested).
- **Calls**: the TwiML App's Voice URL is checked even when TWILIO_TWIML_APP_SID is set and pointed at this site when it points at another host; the token's refusal says why (Twilio's answer) instead of "did not accept the API key"; the browser's errors carry Twilio's code. The caller ID stays the Quo number (TWILIO_CALLER_ID, else Site settings' phone, +12893125991). CSP was checked: the staff and Admin pages allow wss://*.twilio.com, eventgw and sdk.twilio.com on the Worker and the server alike.
- **Settings > Calls: Test call** (Admin): every step with what it found and what to press; then a real call from the page that only plays a message (`test=1` with a 10-minute token for this Admin; nobody is dialled, nothing recorded).
- **Phone alerts stay on**: what turned them off was the server deleting a device the push service refused or dropped (404/410, three refusals, keys changed), another person signing in on the same browser, and the 11th device; the browser then kept its subscription but the portal showed alerts off and never sent it again. Now every loss is recorded (`push_lost`), the browser subscribes again by itself on every staff and Admin page (and replaces a subscription made with old keys) unless the person turned the device off themselves, and the portal says **Alerts stopped on this device** with Turn on when the permission must be given again.
- **Staff inactivity**: 5 days in a row without using the portal suspends a staff account (was 10; warned on day 3). The days count from this release (`staff_inactive_grace_from`), not from this week. A suspended person gets only **You no longer have access** with **Click here to restore it** (`/staff-restore`), and **Your access is back** when an Admin approves; every other staff email, text and phone alert is suppressed. A suspended sign-in goes straight to the restore page. The request reaches the Admins once (`staff_restore_requested`); **Unlock** on Staff approves it.
- `users.staff_notices_off`: **Dwight Laird** (Sales, dwightlair@gmail.com) asked to stop getting staff notifications: turned off, logged in the audit log (`staff_notices_off`), his waiting ones cancelled.
- **Townhall**: Wednesday Nov 18's townhall is recorded as not held; nobody is marked missed and a lockout it caused is lifted.
- **Interviews**: an interview call of this week the owner missed is moved to the next open slot and the candidate gets the branded "Moved:" email (`applicant_booked`); with no open slot the Admins get an alert to add times.
- **Academy and guides**: the section **Work chats** on every staff path (one lesson, two questions; staff who had passed everything get 7 days): all work communication happens in the group work chats, never in private messages. The rule is in the staff guide and the field guide (Start here) and the Admin guide (People and access).
