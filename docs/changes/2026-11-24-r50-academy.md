# Academy: a funny wrong answer in every quiz question, and pictures in every section (Release 50: 4200, 4201)

Migrations `4200_r50_academy_jokes_visuals.sql` (generator `supabase/seed/4200_gen_academy.mjs`) and `4201_r50_academy_guides.sql` (generator `supabase/seed/4201_gen_guides.mjs`), tests `web/src/features/academy/r50.test.ts` and `supabase/tests/99_academy_jokes.sql`.

## Joke answer options

- **Every multiple-choice question carries one extra, obviously wrong, funny option** (light workplace humour about cars, renters, Toronto traffic and coffee; never about people). Every existing question and option stays, and the right answer is the same option as before.
- **Where they come from**: the bank is `web/src/features/academy/jokes.json` (option text and a playful line each, 72 of them). The generator puts the bank into the migration as `app.academy_joke_bank()`. A `before insert or update` trigger on `academy_questions` adds one joke at a pseudo-random place when a question has none and moves the index of the right answer with it, so it also survives regeneration: a later migration, a generator or an Admin saving the plain four options gets the joke back by itself. A section does not repeat a joke. The options limit is now 2 to 7 (six and the joke).
- **Scoring is unchanged**: the joke is never the right answer, picking it is simply wrong. After the quiz is submitted the question's line is playful ("Not quite. Nice try. The coffee can wait. The renter cannot."). In the Admin editor a joke is marked "(a joke, always wrong)".
- **Checks**: `r48.test.ts` still counts the four real options of each source question; `r50.test.ts` checks the bank and the generated migration; `99_academy_jokes.sql` checks that every question in the database has exactly one joke, never the right answer, the right answer follows the same option wherever the joke lands, a question saved again gets its joke back, and grading a quiz of jokes scores zero.

## Pictures in every section

- **Every section ends with a lesson, "See it"**, of picture scenes (76 sections). Four new scene kinds join the existing ones, all with alt text:
  - **Screens** (`pic`): the guides' own screenshots with numbered pins and a legend (the pins come from the guide), as webp under 150 KB in `web/public/academy`, lazy-loaded, with their size set so the page does not jump.
  - **Good and bad photos** (`compare`): a good and a bad example of a car photo, a dashboard and odometer, and damage, drawn from the sample cars so no real person or plate is shown.
  - **Flow diagrams** (`flow`): inline SVG with an icon from the portal icon set on each step, across on a wide screen and down on a phone.
  - **Do and Do not** (`dodont`) pairs and **icon lists** (`icons`).
- The scene player lets a picture scene grow with its content (no clipped 16:9 box at 320 or 375 px), and the lesson video (`video.ts`) draws the new scene kinds too.
- **Build**: `node web/scripts/academy-images.mjs` converts the screenshots and draws the example photos (`supabase/seed/academy/r50images.mjs`, sizes in `images.json`); the pictures of each section are in `supabase/seed/academy/r50visuals.mjs`.

## Guides

- `admin/academy`: the picture scenes, the See it lesson and the funny wrong option in the editor.
- `va/academy`, `field/academy`: the See it lesson and the funny wrong answer in every quiz.
