# The application form says so when the pay asked does not match the role's pay

On carmacars.ca/careers, a candidate who answers "No" to a role's fixed pay, or types an hourly figure above the most the
role pays, sees a note before going on: the role's pay, that it is set and cannot change, and three choices (accept the
pay, keep their answer, or see the other open roles). The note shows once per application; their choice is what is sent.
A role with its pay "discussed at interview" shows no note.

## Guides

No guide change: the hiring guide describes what happens after an application is sent; the form's note is self-explanatory.
