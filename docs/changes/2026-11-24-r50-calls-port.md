# Porting the calls in the site to another build of the kit (Release 50: 5010)

The calls (docs/changes/2026-11-24-r50-in-site-calls.md) are built so another build that shares this kit's platform
(the same door, Supabase stack, deploy-vps flow and email system) gets them with configuration, not code changes.

## What is generic and copies as is

- `web/src/features/calls/` (the page, the adapters, the token and webhook code) and `web/src/server/calls/`: no
  CarmaCars name, no townhall logic. `CallRoom` takes the join request, the title, optional slides, an optional side
  panel and the live list flag.
- `web/src/app/api/calls/*`: token, presence, webhook, guest, live, fake. They call only `door call_join`, `call_live`
  and the four `server_call_*` functions.
- `infra/vps/livekit/` and the `livekit_up` step of `web-vps-remote.sh`, `calls_env` of `web-vps.sh`, `cf-site.sh
  media`: the media host is `meet.<DOMAIN>` (or `CALLS_HOST`), the keys are derived from that build's own
  `LINK_SIGNING_KEY`. Each build runs its own LiveKit (separate keys, separate rooms); two builds on one server need
  different ports (`rtc.tcp_port`, the UDP range, TURN) in their `livekit.yaml.tmpl` and compose file.
- The CSP option `calls` with `callPage` and `mediaOrigins` (`src/lib/security/csp.ts`).

## What a build supplies

1. Migration: the tables `call_rooms`, `call_participants`, `call_guest_links`, the setting `calls_mode`, and
   `app.call_event` / `app.call_live` / the guest functions as in 5010. Replace `app.call_join` with that build's rule
   of who may join which room (its own roles), keeping the returned shape (`room`, `identity` `u-<user>` or
   `g-<id>`, `name`, `role` presenter | host | member | guest).
2. Attendance: in `app.call_event`, the block `if r.kind = 'townhall'` writes CarmaCars' `townhall_attendance`; point it
   at that build's meeting table, or drop it (`call_participants` alone keeps join and leave times).
3. Guest links: `app.job_application_call_link` is tied to `job_applications`; another source of guests (a customer
   video consult, a supplier call) inserts into `call_guest_links` with its own `application_id`-style key and sends
   the link in its own email, in that build's branding.
4. Calendar invites (5000): `app.ics_build` and `calendar_invites` are generic; the townhall sweep and the interview
   hook are CarmaCars' callers. The organizer address in `app.ics_build` and the email templates are per build.
5. Settings: `CALLS_PROVIDER=livekit` comes from deploy-vps; `fake` stays the local and CI default.
