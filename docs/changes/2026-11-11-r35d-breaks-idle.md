# Breaks and inactivity for the office staff (Release 35, part D: 1800 to 1829)

Branch `r35-breaks`. Migrations `1800_r35d_breaks_idle.sql`, `1828_r35d_breaks_academy.sql` (generated by
`supabase/seed/1828_gen_academy.mjs`) and `1829_r35d_guides.sql` (generated by `supabase/seed/1829_gen_guides.mjs`). No applied
migration is edited: `app.va_clock_state`, `app.va_clock_out`, `app.va_timesheet`, `app.payroll_line_json`, `app.kpi_board`,
`app.server_phone_event` and `app.door` are wrapped; `app.pay_clock_minutes` is replaced. Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r35d_breaks_idle.sql`,
`web/src/features/staff-clock/model.test.ts`, `web/src/features/kpis/model.test.ts`.

## A. Breaks

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Start and End break | Next to the clock on the portal home (`ClockCard.tsx`), with a running timer and an optional reason (`staff_breaks`, `app.va_break_start`, `app.va_break_end`). One open break at a time, only while clocked in; clocking out ends it. No fixed length |
| Reminder | A break open past `va_break_reminder_minutes` (30) alerts the person and the Admins once (`staff_break_long`) and shows a banner on the clock card |
| Admin edit | Timesheet, Edit or Remove with the reason (`app.va_break_edit`, Admin only, inside its clock entry, no overlap; `audit_log` with the before) |
| Timesheet and payroll | Per person and day: clocked, worked, breaks (count, total, longest). Payroll lines carry `break_count`, `break_minutes`, `break_longest`. `va_breaks_paid` (on): off takes break minutes off the payroll hours |

## B. Inactivity

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | `ActivityBeat.tsx` on every staff page of the office staff: pointer, key, scroll, focus, at most one POST a minute to `/api/staff/activity`; no content, URL or screenshot. Every action through the door counts (`app.va_activity_touch`, one write a minute) |
| Alerts | `app.job_va_breaks_idle` (pg_cron every minute): over `va_idle_alert_minutes` (10) without activity, while clocked in, not on a break and within scheduled hours, one alert "<preferred name> (role) inactive since HH:MM, N min" to the Admins (`staff_va_idle`) and one follow-up at `va_idle_followup_minutes` (30) |
| Pauses | `app.va_pause`: a call linked to the person (Quo user on `staff_profiles.quo_user_id`, set by an Admin on the timesheet; the Call button; a note in the caller pop-up) while active and `va_idle_call_grace_minutes` (1) after; the townhall for those who joined or were present, to its end plus `va_idle_townhall_grace_minutes` (10) |
| Reports | `staff_idle_periods`: the Admins' timesheet per person and day, and "Idle minutes" on the KPIs against `kpi_idle_minutes_week` (120). RLS: Admins only; Finance's timesheet has no idle time |
| The person | One line on the clock card that activity is noted (the fact only) and "Still there?" after `va_idle_nudge_minutes` (8) |

`va/your-workday` (Start and End break, the inactivity reminder), `admin/people-and-access` (breaks on the timesheet, the inactivity record), through `1829_r35d_guides.sql`.
