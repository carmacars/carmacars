# R51 staff: easy onboarding with no dead end (5304, 5305)

Branch `r51-staff`. The owner's requests of Nov 27, 17:29 and 17:45 Toronto: REQ-130 to REQ-135 in `docs/owner/requirements.md`.

## Easy onboarding (REQ-135, 5304)

The owner: "jamal got the academy waived for him and now it says your agreement first and theres no buttons to click anywhere other than resign".

Cause (code, not guessed): `/staff/welcome` showed a Sign button only when a sign request was already sent (`ag.state === 'sent'`). The agreement is made by the agreements job (1060, `web/src/server/staff-agreement-auto.ts`), and `app.staff_agreements_due_rows` skips a person for a day after each try and stops after 5 tries. A person whose agreement was never made then read "It is being prepared from your record" with nothing to press. Production (connector, Nov 27): the staff member is Sales and Field, academy waived, agreement unsigned with no request sent, when they can work not set, WhatsApp not confirmed, no pay details. Which of the job's states (waiting for a value, failed, stopped after 5 tries) holds his agreement is not readable through the connector: unknown. Sign now covers all three.

- **Sign my agreement now** (`web/src/features/staff-onboarding/SignNow.tsx`, `web/src/server/actions/onboarding-now.ts`, door `ops_do staff_agreement_now`): clears the job's wait and its 5-try stop for the person, makes and sends the agreement at once (`sendStaffAgreementNow`) and opens it to sign. When it still needs something only CarmaCars sets (pay, start date, vehicle types) or making it fails, the page says what, and the Admins get the urgent alert `staff_agreement_stuck` (once a day a person) with what is missing.
- **Welcome is one checklist** for every staff role (`web/src/app/staff/welcome/page.tsx`, `stepAction` in `web/src/features/academy/gate.ts`): each step left has one big button (Finish my profile, Sign my agreement now or Sign my agreement, Open the academy, Set when I can work, Join the group and confirm, Add my pay details, Ask again), done steps are ticked, and the box at the top names the next step (`app.staff_welcome` `next`) with its button. The pay details step is new on the list; it does not hold the portal (payouts need it).
- No dead end: every blocking redirect in `web/src/server/door.ts` (agreement, onboarding) lands on Welcome, and Welcome always has a button for the next step.

Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r51_easy_onboarding.sql` (a person in the reported state: the next step, the pay step, Sign now clears the job's stop, the Admin alert, sent and signed); `web/src/features/academy/gate.test.ts` (every step left has a button).
