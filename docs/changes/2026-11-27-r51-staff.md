## The academy is answered by the person (REQ-130, 5199)

The owner: "put it on the academy on each question as a disclaimer somewhere that computer use is not allowed and carmacars will sue u and make sure that it just makes sure that they cant use ai and claude chrome to complete the tests".

- Every question of a started quiz, and the quiz card, show: "Answer yourself. Using AI tools, browser agents or computer-use software on this test is not allowed and breaks your agreement; CarmaCars may take legal action." (`web/src/features/academy/integrity.ts` `HONEST_NOTE`, `Study.tsx`).
- The staff agreements carry the same sentence, nothing more: the Field Staff Agreement section 2.3 and the contractor agreement section 2.4 (`docs/source/field_staff_agreement.md`, `docs/source/va_agreement.md`, regenerated `source.generated.ts`, version `person-83ace0d595`). New agreements carry it; signed ones are not reissued. **For the owner's lawyer: review this sentence before relying on it.**
- What the quiz page watches while a quiz is open (`IntegrityWatch`): `navigator.webdriver`; globals and document keys that browser drivers leave (chromedriver, Selenium, Playwright, Puppeteer, PhantomJS, Nightmare); a devtools protocol probe (a logged error whose stack is read, as a protocol client does); events not made by a person (`isTrusted` false: scripts and form-filling tools); a mouse press with fewer than 3 moves before it (a pointer that jumps, as agents click); paste, copy and cut on the quiz (blocked, counted; the right-click menu is off and the questions cannot be selected); the page losing focus or being hidden (counted once until it is back); the time between the first picks of each question.
- The database decides (`app.academy_integrity_reasons`): automation, synthetic_events, pointer_jumps (2 or more), paste, copy, focus_lost (more than `academy_focus_loss_max`, 2), too_fast (half the questions, at least 3, under `academy_answer_floor_seconds`, 2). On any reason with `academy_integrity_void` on (the default): the attempt is voided (never graded, kept with its reasons and the counts, a failed try for the wait and the tries a day) and the Admins get the alert `academy_integrity`. Off: graded, the reasons kept and alerted. Admin > Academy > the person: **Voided or flagged attempts**.
- A submit with no report (the API, the tests) is graded as before: a script that can call the API can also send a clean report, so the absence proves nothing.
- **Limits, honestly**: no web page can fully stop an AI agent. An agent that controls a real browser with real mouse moves, or a person reading the screen to an AI on another device, is not detectable from the page. These checks make the usual tools (browser extensions that drive the page, scripted clicks, copy and paste into a chat, switching tabs) detectable and void the try. Which markers Claude in Chrome itself leaves is not documented: none is matched by name.

## Staff lines behind Quo extensions (REQ-132, 5200)

The owner: "i need all staff to have a dedicated twilio line that corresponds with an extension number that is on quo ... (nobody will ever know what those numbers are)".

- `staff_phone_lines` (extension, last 4 digits, state, monthly cost, the Quo step) and `staff_phone_line_secrets` (the Twilio number and its id; row-level security on, no grant to any browser role). Staff see their extension on Profile, never the number; an Admin sees the last 4 digits; renters never see it.
- Admin > Settings > Staff, **Phone line**: Set extension (the next free from 1 when empty), **Get a number** (Twilio's price of a Canadian local number in `phone_line_area_code`, 647, and the last 4 digits of the number it would sell; nothing bought), **Buy for USD x a month** (bought only when the price still matches, pointed at `/api/webhooks/twilio/incoming`, recorded; if recording fails the number is given back at once), **Release**, **Added in Quo** (`web/src/server/staff-lines.ts`, `web/src/features/phone/StaffLine.tsx`, `POST /api/staff/phone` actions `line_quote`, `line_buy`, `line_release`, Admin only, never while viewing as someone). A test server (not a public https address) can never buy (`lineBuyBlocked`); no test calls Twilio.
- A call to the line rings only that person's browser phone (the portal and the app, which is the site) while they are online, shown as "(your line, ext N)"; their phone takes incoming calls whatever their role. Not online or not answered: the line's message (`phone_line_message`), the call on the caller's history as every call, and the urgent alert `staff_line_missed` to that person. The person hears `staff_line_ready` when the line is bought.
- **Cost**: a Canadian local number at Twilio is billed monthly (the amount is not known here: Get a number shows Twilio's live price before any purchase) plus Twilio's per-minute price for incoming calls. Each forwarded call also uses two legs at Quo (Quo: forwarded calls count double toward usage).
- **Quo cannot be set by API** (checked Nov 27 against Quo's API reference, version 2026-03-30: phone numbers can be listed and renamed, business hours and recording settings changed; no call flow, phone menu, forwarding or extension endpoint). Quo's phone menu takes one digit (0 to 9) a menu, up to 10 options, on the Business and Scale plans; multi-digit extensions such as 101 are not available. Forwarded calls keep the caller's number as caller ID, are not recorded or transcribed by Quo and do not reach Quo voicemail.
- **Owner steps in Quo, for each staff line** (after Buy):
  1. Find the number: Twilio Console > Phone Numbers > Manage > Active numbers, the one named "CarmaCars staff line ext N". The site never shows it.
  2. Quo (web or desktop) > Settings > Phone numbers > the CarmaCars number > Edit call flow.
  3. Drag a **Phone menu** step after **Incoming call** (or open the one there).
  4. **Add keypad option**, pick the digit of the extension, rename it to the person's first name.
  5. Set its destination to **Forward call** and enter the number from step 1. Save, and check the flow is Live.
  6. Back on Admin > Settings > Staff, press **Added in Quo**.
  For more than 9 people: one digit opens a second Phone menu, and its digits are the people.

Sources: https://www.quo.com/docs/llms.txt, https://www.quo.com/docs/2026-03-30/phone-numbers/update-a-phone-number.md, https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/administration/call-flows/phone-menu.md, https://support.quo.com/core-concepts/administration/call-flows/call-forwarding.md

## Insurer emails that bounce (REQ-136, 5204)

The owner (Nov 27 18:22): "emails coming from blindspotcarrental @ carmacars are not getting passed insurance's firewall. can u fix that and make sure it doesnt happen again".

Cause (the bounce of Nov 25 in rent@'s Gmail): the insurer's Exchange answered `550 5.7.129 RestrictedToRecipientsPermission` ("the sender isn't on the recipient's list of senders to accept mail from") while SPF, DKIM and DMARC passed for carmacars.ca. That is the recipient mailbox's accept list. Nothing on our side makes them accept it; the fix makes every refusal visible, retried and acted on.

- **Bounces read**: `scripts/turo-gmail.gs` now also posts mailer-daemon and postmaster emails from rent@'s Gmail to `/api/inbound/bounce` (the same token). `web/src/features/email-bounces/ndr.ts` reads the failed address, the status, the server's answer and our reference (`X-Outbox-Ref`, a neutral header `supabase/functions/send-outbox` now adds to every email). Only those fields reach the database (`app.server_email_bounce`), which acts only on an email we sent to that address in the last 14 days, once per email. A bounce never suppresses the address.
- **An insurer email that bounced** (invoice, estimate, payment receipt): the version is marked bounced with the reason (`insurer_invoice_versions.bounced_at`, `bounce_reason`; the frozen-version rule allows only these and `fallback_to`), the claim history says so, and Admin gets one urgent To do "Insurer email bounced" with the reason, the adjuster, the insurer's general claims contact and an allowlist request ready to send (signed Blind Spot Car Rental).
- **Fallback**: `insurers.claims_email` and `claims_phone` (Settings, Insurers). A bounced invoice or estimate goes again, once a version, to the claims email when it is another address: the same Blind Spot email.
- **Delivery per version** on the insurance replacement page: queued, sent, delivered (sent, no bounce in 24 hours: the providers here give no delivery receipt), bounced with the reason, or failed.
- **One fact the owner should know**: the allowlist request has to name the sender address, `blindspotcarrental@carmacars.ca`, so its domain shows carmacars.ca. The text never names CarmaCars otherwise. A sender on a domain of its own (for example a Blind Spot domain) would avoid that; not changed here.
- **Owner step**: paste the updated `scripts/turo-gmail.gs` into its Apps Script project (rent@'s Gmail) and run `setup` once. Until then no bounce is read. The send-outbox function deploys with the next deploy.
