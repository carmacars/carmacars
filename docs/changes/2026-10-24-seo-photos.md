# Licensed stock photos for the SEO pages, October 24, 2026

The owner: the programmatic pages of every year, make, model and trim (and the place and service pages) mostly have no
photo. Approved: properly licensed images for those pages; never scraped copyrighted photos. Then: no paid services;
get close to full coverage for free (generation years from Wikipedia, Commons categories by generation and model year,
Openverse as a second source, fallbacks to a real photo before the drawing).

Migration `supabase/migrations/0149_seo_photos.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0149`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0149_gen_guides.mjs`. Pipeline `web/scripts/seo-photos/run.mjs` (with `offline-check.mjs` and
`generic.json`), workflow `.github/workflows/seo-photos.yml`. Tests: `web/src/features/vehicles/stock-photos.test.ts`,
`supabase/tests/89_stock_photos.sql`, `web/tests/e2e/stock-photos.spec.ts` (both widths), the offline check.

## Sources

| Source | Verdict |

| **Wikimedia Commons** (with English Wikipedia and Wikidata for the generations) | Used, first. Only public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA files (any version or national port). ## How a photo is chosen (`web/src/features/vehicles/stock-photos.ts`, `web/scripts/seo-photos/run.mjs`)

1. **The model on Wikidata**: a search for "<make> <model>", a hit named like it (label or alias) that has a maker or
   brand and is not a company, a disambiguation page or a work. It gives the English Wikipedia article and the Commons
   category.
2. **Its generations from Wikipedia**: the article's generation sections ("Tenth generation (FC/FK; 2015)",
   "Fifth generation (XA50; 2018)"; not sales, safety, motorsport...), each with its infobox's model years (the North
   American line when the value lists markets), else production years (and month), else the heading's year; its image
   (an editor chose it); and its own article ({{Main|Toyota Camry (XV70)}}), read for the years and image when the main
   article lacks them (at most 8 a model). Wikidata's generation items (part of the series, subclass, part of,
   instance of, has parts, follows and followed by) only when the article gives none.
3. **Model years of each generation**: the infobox's model years when given ("present": up to the catalog's newest
   year); else production that starts in the second half of a year (or in a year whose month is not known) is the
   next model year, in the first half the same year. A generation runs to the year before the next one's first model
   year (or its own end). With production dates only and no end, the newest generation runs 8 model years at most, 5
   when a generation is listed without a date.
4. **Candidates for each generation**, best first, stopping at a score of 80: the Wikipedia infobox image (or the
   Wikidata image); the Quality images of the generation's Commons category (found among the model category's
   subcategories by code, "Toyota RAV4 (XA50)", or ordinal, "Honda Civic (tenth generation)"); the categories by model
   year ("2019 Toyota RAV4", the first years of the generation); the rest of the generation's category; a Commons search
   by title and year; files whose structured data depicts the generation (P180); then Openverse.
5. **Scored and filtered**: the file names the model ("GMC Yukon" is never a Tahoe), front three-quarter view in the
   name or description (English, German, French, Spanish, Polish), Quality, Featured or Valued image, a file year inside
   the generation (never more than 2 years before or 3 after), at least 960 px wide, landscape (1.15 to 2.4), JPEG, PNG
   or WebP. Never rear, interior, engine, badge, wheel, sensor, mirror, screen, crash, police, taxi, race, toy, drawing,
   render, concept or wrapped cars. Duplicates are counted once.
6. A model with no dated generation gets one recent photo of the model (taken within the 12 model years before the
   catalog's newest).

## What a page shows (the fallbacks, `public.vehicle_stock_photo`)

1. The photo of the page's model year's own generation (exact).
2. Else the next generation's when the year is one model year off its range (near).
3. Else the nearest photo of the model, with "Representative photo of the <make> <model>, not the exact year" in the
   credit line (model). The owner prefers a real photo to the drawing.
4. Else the studio drawing (no photo of the model at all, or the setting off).

A make page shows its most popular model's photo, a model page its newest generation's; colour pages keep the drawing
in their colour.

## The pipeline (`web/scripts/seo-photos/run.mjs`, `.github/workflows/seo-photos.yml`)

- **Order**: the Admin's Replace and Pick again requests first; then our fleet's models (not sample cars), the popular
  list in its order (`vehicle_catalog_popular`, 154 models from `web/scripts/catalog/popular.txt`), then the newest and
  longest-running models. Each model is recorded in `vehicle_stock_photo_checks` when done: the next run carries on
  with the models not yet looked at (looked at again after 180 days).
- **Polite to Wikimedia**: one request at a time, at least a second apart; User-Agent
  `CarmaCarsStockPhotos/1.0 (https://carmacars.ca; hello@carmacars.ca)` (also as Api-User-Agent); `maxlag=5` on the API;
  429, 502, 503 and 504 retried after Retry-After (or a growing wait); only the standard 1280 px thumbnail is
  downloaded, never the original; 50 minutes and 400 models a run at most.
- **Files**: the thumbnail stays in memory, is turned to the camera's orientation and converted to WebP (quality 78) at
  640 and 1280 px (never enlarged) by sharp, which writes no metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP and ICC stripped; the offline
  check proves it), and is stored in the public bucket `vehicle-public` at
  `seo-photos/<make>/<model>/<from>-<to>-<sha>-<width>.webp` (the hash of the download: a new photo is a new address, so
  the files are cached for a year). The files a new photo replaces are deleted. Nothing goes to git; the runner's disk
  is not used.
- **Schedule**: every day at 06:47 UTC, on by default; it waits (one notice) until migration 0149 is in the database,
  and stops at once when Program settings "Show stock photos" is off. The generic photos are fetched on the first run
  (and on a run that asks). Run workflow: write (default), limit, minutes, models (`toyota/camry,...`), generic.
- **Dry run on a push** (any branch, when the pipeline changes): no secrets, no writes. The offline check (a stand-in
  for Wikidata, Commons and storage), then the popular list chosen live, with the picks, the models missing and the
  generic photos as annotations (the picks also gzip+base64 in parts).

## The database (0149)

- `vehicle_stock_photos`: one row per make, model and generation (or the model in general), and the generic photos by
  slot (rental, detailing, bodyshop, sale). File, source page (https) and source name (Wikimedia Commons, or "<site> via
  Openverse"), author and link, licence (CHECK: public domain, CC0,
  CC BY, CC BY-SA) and licence page, stored path, widths, size, status (active, hidden, pending, failed), the files an
  Admin rejected (never picked again), who picked it. Row-level security, the admin policy, granted to no visitor,
  audited. A row is active only once stored. Separate from `vehicle_photos`: never a fleet car's photo.
- `vehicle_stock_photo_checks` (each model looked at) and `vehicle_stock_photo_runs` (each run, its counts and errors).
- `program_settings.vpage_stock_photos` (on by default).
- The pages' reads (anonymous): `public.vehicle_stock_photo(make, model, year)` (the fallbacks above, with `match`:
  exact, near or model) and `public.stock_photos_generic(slot)`. `app.stock_photo_coverage` counts model years exact,
  near and model only, the popular models with generation photos, and the photos by source.
- The pipeline (service role): `app.stock_photo_targets`, `app.stock_photo_save` (an Admin's choice, a hidden row and a
  pending request are left alone; returns the files to delete), `app.stock_photo_save_generic`, `app.stock_photo_pending`,
  `app.stock_photo_resolve`, `app.stock_photo_run_begin`/`_finish`, `app.stock_photo_coverage`.
- Admin through the door: `admin_read 'stock_photos'`, `admin_do 'stock_photo'` (hide, show, replace with a Commons
  file, pick again; Admins only). `app.door` wrapped (`door_pre0149`).

## What visitors see

- **Vehicle pages** (`/cars/<make>`, model, year, trim, the trim in a place; the same pages under `/buy` and every
  `/t/<term>` tree): the head shows the generation's photo instead of the studio drawing, with its credit always
  visible under it: "Photo: <author>, <licence>, Wikimedia Commons. Representative photo, not one of our cars" (or
  the photo's own source, and "Representative photo of the <model>, not the exact year; not one of our cars" for a
  photo of another year; the author links to the file's page, the licence to its page). Alt text: "<Make> <Model> of the <from> to <to>
  generation". A responsive srcset (640w, 1280w), the photo's own width and height (no layout shift); the head image
  is the page's main image and loads first (as the drawing it replaces did), every other stock photo is lazy. The
  Product's `image` is an ImageObject (contentUrl, width, height, license, creditText, creator, copyrightNotice,
  acquireLicensePage); og:image is the 1280 px photo at its real size. Colour pages keep the studio drawing in their
  colour (a photo would show another colour). Our cars below always show their own photos.
- **Place pages** (`/car-rental/...`, `/detailing/...`, `/body-shop/...`, the buying help pages) and `/detailing`,
  `/body-shop`: one of the slot's generic photos (a Toronto highway, a car being washed or detailed, a body shop, cars
  for sale), the same one for the same address, credited, lazy, with an ImageObject. The fleet's own cards stay below.
- Before the migration or the first run, and for a model with no photo at all, the pages are exactly as before.

## What staff see

- **Admin > Settings > Vehicle pages**: the switch "Show stock photos (licensed, credited) on the vehicle, place and
  service pages" (on), and the card **Stock photos**: models and model years with a photo, the popular models still
  without one, the generic photos, the last run; every photo by make with its thumbnail and credit, and **Hide** (off
  the page at once, never picked again), **Show**, **Pick again** and **Replace** (a Commons file address or name; the
  next run checks its licence and refuses it with the reason when it is not allowed).
