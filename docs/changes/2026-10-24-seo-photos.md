## Sources

| Source | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| **Wikimedia Commons** (with Wikidata for the generations) | Used. Only public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA files (any version or national port). Refused: NC, ND, GFDL alone, fair use, "free" with no named licence, files Commons marks non-free, and files tagged with personality or insignia restrictions. Each photo keeps its author, licence, licence page and Commons file page, and the page credits it. A CHECK on the table refuses any other licence. |
| Manufacturer press and media sites | Not used. Their terms are editorial only: Porsche's newsroom, for example, says its content is "intended exclusively for use by journalists as a source for their own media reporting" and "not intended for commercial use, in particular for advertising purposes" (https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/images-videos/porsche-newsroom-terms-and-conditions.html). Toyota's terms page (https://pressroom.toyota.com/terms-agreements/) could not be read from here; treated as unclear and skipped, as the owner asked. |
| Google Images, dealer sites, any file without a clear licence | Never. |
| IMAGIN.studio (paid, studio renders by make, model, year and paint) | Evaluated, not integrated. Access is by a customer key from service@imagin.studio; each image requested through getImage is charged as one "Micro Use License"; images must be shown straight from IMAGIN's CDN (no download, server cache or modification without a separate agreement); no public price list (a quote) (https://docs.imagin.studio/advanced/manuals/getting-started). Would cover every model and year in matching studio angles, but costs per view and ties the pages to their CDN. Needs the owner's approval and a quote first. |

## How a photo is chosen (`web/src/features/vehicles/stock-photos.ts`)

1. **The model on Wikidata**: a search for "<make> <model>", a hit named like it (label or alias) that has a maker or
   brand and is not a company, a disambiguation page or a work.
2. **Its generations**: items that are part of the series of the model, a subclass of it or a part of it
   (`haswbstatement:P179|P279|P361`), named like the model and followed by a generation word ("Toyota Camry (XV70)"),
   never another car ("Toyota Camry Solara", "Daihatsu Altis") or a race, police or concept version. Production start
   (inception or start time, with its month when known) and end.
3. **Model years of each generation** (North American): production that starts in the second half of a year, or in a
   year whose month is not known, is the next model year; in the first half, the same year. A generation runs to the
   year before the next one's first model year (or a year past its production end), clipped to the catalog's years. A
   year no generation covers has no photo: its page keeps the studio drawing (never another generation's photo).
4. **The photo of each generation**: the generation's Wikidata image (an editor already chose it), then the Quality
   images in its Commons category, then the rest of the category. Scored: front three-quarter view in the file's name or
   description (in English, German, French, Spanish, Polish), Quality, Featured or Valued image, the model's name in the
   file, a file year inside the generation, at least 960 px wide, landscape (1.15 to 2.4), JPEG, PNG or WebP. Never
   rear, interior, engine, badge, wheel, crash, police, taxi, race, toy, drawing, render, concept or wrapped cars.
5. A model whose generations Wikidata does not date gets one photo of the model in general: shown on the make and model
   pages only, never on a year or trim page.

## The pipeline (`web/scripts/seo-photos/run.mjs`, `.github/workflows/seo-photos.yml`)

- **Order**: the Admin's Replace and Pick again requests first; then our fleet's models (not sample cars), the popular
  list in its order (`vehicle_catalog_popular`, 154 models from `web/scripts/catalog/popular.txt`), then the newest and
  longest-running models. Each model is recorded in `vehicle_stock_photo_checks` when done: the next run carries on
  with the models not yet looked at (looked at again after 180 days).
- **Polite to Wikimedia**: one request at a time, at least a second apart; User-Agent
  `CarmaCarsStockPhotos/1.0 (https://carmacars.ca; hello@carmacars.ca)` (also as Api-User-Agent); `maxlag=5` on the API;
  429, 502, 503 and 504 retried after Retry-After (or a growing wait); only the standard 1280 px thumbnail is
  downloaded, never the original; 50 minutes and 400 models a run at most.
- **Files**: the thumbnail stays in memory, is turned to the camera's orientation and converted to WebP (quality 78) at
  640 and 1280 px (never enlarged) by sharp, which writes no metadata (EXIF, GPS, XMP and ICC stripped; the offline
  check proves it), and is stored in the public bucket `vehicle-public` at
  `seo-photos/<make>/<model>/<from>-<to>-<sha>-<width>.webp` (the hash of the download: a new photo is a new address, so
  the files are cached for a year). The files a new photo replaces are deleted. Nothing goes to git; the runner's disk
  is not used.
- **Schedule**: every day at 06:47 UTC, on by default; it waits (one notice) until migration 0137 is in the database,
  and stops at once when Program settings "Show stock photos" is off. The generic photos are fetched on the first run
  (and on a run that asks). Run workflow: write (default), limit, minutes, models (`toyota/camry,...`), generic.
- **Dry run on a push** (any branch, when the pipeline changes): no secrets, no writes. The offline check (a stand-in
  for Wikidata, Commons and storage), then the popular list chosen live, with the picks, the models missing and the
  generic photos as annotations (the picks also gzip+base64 in parts).

## The database (0137)

- `vehicle_stock_photos`: one row per make, model and generation (or the model in general), and the generic photos by
  slot (rental, detailing, bodyshop, sale). File, Commons page, author and link, licence (CHECK: public domain, CC0,
  CC BY, CC BY-SA) and licence page, stored path, widths, size, status (active, hidden, pending, failed), the files an
  Admin rejected (never picked again), who picked it. Row-level security, the admin policy, granted to no visitor,
  audited. A row is active only once stored. Separate from `vehicle_photos`: never a fleet car's photo.
- `vehicle_stock_photo_checks` (each model looked at) and `vehicle_stock_photo_runs` (each run, its counts and errors).
- `program_settings.vpage_stock_photos` (on by default).
- The pages' reads (anonymous): `public.vehicle_stock_photo(make, model, year)` (the generation of that model year;
  the newest generation on a model page; the most popular model's on a make page) and `public.stock_photos_generic(slot)`.
- The pipeline (service role): `app.stock_photo_targets`, `app.stock_photo_save` (an Admin's choice, a hidden row and a
  pending request are left alone; returns the files to delete), `app.stock_photo_save_generic`, `app.stock_photo_pending`,
  `app.stock_photo_resolve`, `app.stock_photo_run_begin`/`_finish`, `app.stock_photo_coverage`.
- Admin through the door: `admin_read 'stock_photos'`, `admin_do 'stock_photo'` (hide, show, replace with a Commons
  file, pick again; Admins only). `app.door` wrapped (`door_pre0137`).

## What visitors see

- **Vehicle pages** (`/cars/<make>`, model, year, trim, the trim in a place; the same pages under `/buy` and every
  `/t/<term>` tree): the head shows the generation's photo instead of the studio drawing, with its credit always
  visible under it: "Photo: <author>, <licence>, Wikimedia Commons. Representative photo, not one of our cars" (the
  author links to the Commons file page, the licence to its page). Alt text: "<Make> <Model> of the <from> to <to>
  generation". A responsive srcset (640w, 1280w), the photo's own width and height (no layout shift); the head image
  is the page's main image and loads first (as the drawing it replaces did), every other stock photo is lazy. The
  Product's `image` is an ImageObject (contentUrl, width, height, license, creditText, creator, copyrightNotice,
  acquireLicensePage); og:image is the 1280 px photo at its real size. Colour pages keep the studio drawing in their
  colour (a photo would show another colour). Our cars below always show their own photos.
- **Place pages** (`/car-rental/...`, `/detailing/...`, `/body-shop/...`, the buying help pages) and `/detailing`,
  `/body-shop`: one of the slot's generic photos (a Toronto highway, a car being washed or detailed, a body shop, cars
  for sale), the same one for the same address, credited, lazy, with an ImageObject. The fleet's own cards stay below.
- Before the migration or the first run, and for a model with no photo, the pages are exactly as before.

  service pages" (on), and the card **Stock photos**: models and model years with a photo, the popular models still
