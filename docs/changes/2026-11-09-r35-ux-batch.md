## 1. Sales gets a renter ready (1106)

"sales people should be able to request payment, edit offers, and verify id and basically do everything to make sure a renter is good to go for a rental."

| Tool | Before | Now (Sales) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay link, deposit link, card on the device | Allowed (0129, 0970) | Unchanged, now reachable on a confirmed booking |
| Read the booking | Requests and offers only | Until the handover (payments without processor ids; no refunds, statements, emails) |
| Conversation and activity log | Requests and offers only | Until the handover |
| Save, send, resend (later expiry), withdraw, approved by phone, ask the renter, revise in place | Requests and offers | Also while it waits for payment |
| Licence and selfie | Allowed (0169) | Also on a confirmed booking |
| Proof of coverage | Customer service and Admin | Sales too, approve or reject with the reason |
| Drivers' signing links, agreement regenerate | Agreement only | Both |
| Refunds, cancellations, deposit settle or release, payouts, fee changes, voids, removing a paid add-on | Not allowed | Still not allowed (removing an add-on is refused to Sales in the door) |

How: `app.door` runs the listed calls (`app.sales_ready_stages`) as customer service for that call only (`app.field_as_cs`, `app.sales_as_cs`;
`app.va_fn` and `app.va_active_fn` answer `cs`), the history names Sales (`app.review_role`), and the audit rows carry the Sales person.
Field staff and Fleet are unchanged (tested). The booking page: `bookingCan` gives Sales `editOfferPending`, `resendOffer`, `renterDocs`,
`driverLinks` and `readiness`, never `addonRemove`; the **Ready for the rental** checklist (`features/booking-page/readiness.ts`) shows for
Admin, customer service and Sales.

## 2. The Prepare checklist and its progress (1107)

Field cards and jobs call the add-ons a Prepare checklist with each quantity (the ticks of 0930, audited, still stop the handover).
`ops_read prepare_progress` (booking, or a car's next booking) answers Admin, Sales, customer service, Fleet and Finance: each add-on, ticked
or not, by whom and when, and the pickup or delivery job with its status and field person. Shown on the booking (overview and car) and on the car page.

## 3. Invoices (1108)

Insurer invoices: issued as Blind Spot Car Rental (`bs_billing_name`, `bs_billing_email`, `bs_billing_phone`; a value naming CarmaCars is never printed),
legal entity 18122211 Canada Inc., no CarmaCars logo (a text wordmark), author or footer, file `blindspot-invoice-<number>.pdf`. Fees by name:
a draft's "Fees charged at booking" line becomes the named fee lines when they add up to it (`app.insurer_fee_items`); an issued invoice's stored
line is itemized on the PDF. Layout (`features/documents/invoice-blocks.ts`): issuer, bill-to and details blocks, line items, totals (subtotal,
HST, total, paid, balance), how to pay, terms; every value right-aligned (`valuesRight`), the claim number included; "Draft" only on a draft, never
"Preview" on an issued invoice. Renter statements and receipts and owner and partner invoices use the same issuer block and alignment.

How to pay: `cc_pay_*` (CarmaCars) and `bs_pay_*` (Blind Spot) Program settings, all empty by default: direct deposit (account name, bank,
institution, transit, account) and the Interac e-Transfer email. An insurer invoice uses Blind Spot's, or CarmaCars' when Blind Spot's are all
empty, without any value naming CarmaCars. An empty section is left off the PDF.

Already stored issued PDFs (a final record is stored once, 0032) keep their old file until an Admin re-issues them.

## 4. Add-ons not returned (1108)

Program settings `unreturned_ev_charger_cents` (150), `unreturned_phone_mount_cents` (75), `unreturned_child_seat_cents` (450, baby seats too),
`unreturned_booster_seat_cents` (200), `unreturned_other_times` (3) and `unreturned_other_min_cents` (100). Stated in the agreement's fine print
after the Schedule on a booking with add-ons in the car, and in the FAQ (Payment and deposit: "What if an add-on is not returned?"), not in the
booking flow. The deposit review gains **Add-ons returned** on such a booking, its facts the suggested charge per add-on; it never charges.

## 5. Commission on add-ons (1109)

The commission base adds `app.commission_addon_cents`: each confirmed add-on's own charge line before HST, not voided; prepaid fuel left out;
nothing on a cancelled booking or one whose rental part is fully refunded. Same rate, tiers and rules. `staff_commissions.addon_base_cents` shows
the add-on part on Sales' and the Admin's commission lines ("incl. $X add-ons").

## 6. Sales' offer tools, checked (coordinator item 1a)

"sales people seem to not have the ability to edit offers or withdraw them or extend them". On an offer (status offered), Sales
already had Edit the offer (the review screen, `offer_revise`), Withdraw (`withdraw_offer`) and send again (`resend_offer`) in the
database; the booking page drew Send again, later expiry for an Admin only. It is now drawn for Sales and customer service too
(`bookingCan.resendOffer`), with the "Who found this client?" choice (`sales_choice`) when the booking has none yet; 1106 adds
the same tools while the offer waits for payment. DB tests: section 7 of `99_r35x_ux_sales_prepare.sql` (send, edit, extend, withdraw
as Sales); unit test: `viewer.test.ts` (the three buttons are drawn on `can.offer` / `can.resendOffer`).

## 7. The renter's profile (1110, coordinator item 10)

Email and phone stay self-serve (ChangeEmail, SignInSettings). The name and the date of birth: saved at once while no licence of the
renter is verified (`app.profile_identity_save`: the renter, the account and the account holder's driver); after, a correction
request (0812's `name_change_requests` with `current_dob`, `requested_dob`), shown in the profile as waiting, in customer service's
queue, decided by Sales, customer service or an Admin (`name_change_decide`; the booking page shows it to them). Owners and investors
keep the Admin-only name change. The extension: Extend is on the booking page while confirmed or on rent (`action-extend`).
DB tests: `99_r35x_ux_renter_profile.sql`; unit test: `features/portal/identity.test.ts`.
