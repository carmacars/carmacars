# The check-out without the office, Payments by day, the owner's month to date, October 24, 2026

A simulation of real staff flows on release 6 (problems 10, 14 and the owner part of 15) found: the field Ready check
offered only "Stop: do not release the car", so every handover waited for office work in another portal (identity review,
the paper copy, the deposit, a tracker that is slow to report); the office identity review took about 15 clicks; Fleet
scheduled every handover and return by hand and saw "Place: none"; Finance could not reconcile a day (cash and e-Transfer
by month, cards on another page with raw codes, a refund requested on a booking never reached Finance, the approval on a
second tab); owners saw nothing until an Admin approved the month; raw PostgreSQL errors reached staff screens.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0151_field_finance_owner_mtd.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0151`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0151_gen_guides.mjs`. Tests: `supabase/tests/96_field_finance_owner_mtd.sql`, `web/src/lib/db/friendly.test.ts`,
`web/src/features/money/ledger.test.ts`, `web/tests/e2e/field-finance-owner-mtd.spec.ts` (and `guided-actions.spec.ts`
for the review on one screen). Code: `web/src/features/field/ReadyResolve.tsx`, `web/src/features/field/Checklist.tsx`,
`web/src/server/actions/field-paper.ts`, `web/src/features/admin/DocumentReview.tsx`, `web/src/features/money/`
(`PaymentsView.tsx`, `ledger.ts`), `web/src/app/admin/payments`, `web/src/app/va/payments`, `web/src/app/api/payments/export`,
`web/src/features/owner/MonthToDate.tsx`, `web/src/server/owner/data.ts`, `web/src/lib/db/friendly.ts`, `web/src/server/door.ts`.

## 1. The ready check fixed on the spot (field)

Each red line of the ready check now shows, right under it, what field staff can do there; Stop stays for what cannot be
fixed (coverage and the calendar are the office's; an expired licence, a face that does not match). Everything is decided
again by the database and recorded with the person (`handover_resolutions`, append-only and audited) and in the booking
history.

| Line | On the phone |
| --- | --- |
| Agreement not signed | **Sign now on this device** (0130), or **Signed on paper instead? Scan it**: a photo of each page from the camera, made into one PDF (`server/actions/field-paper.ts`, pdf-lib) and recorded as customer service records a paper copy (`app.field_paper_signed`). A rental added by hand is confirmed before it is signed (0051): its paper copy is now accepted while it waits for the signature, not only while it waits for payment (0044's check, changed only where its text is still there) |
| Identity not verified | For each driver: **Take** the licence front, the back and the selfie (0130's staff upload), then four checks on the original licence in hand (name, photo, valid past the return, class G2 or G and no alteration), the number and expiry typed when the booking has none; **The licence checks out** approves the uploads as customer service would (`app.field_identity_in_person`; the identity verification says `in_person`, the history "checked in person at the check-out by ...") |
| Deposit not taken | **Card hold: hand this phone to the renter** (0129's pay link on this device; a long rental saves the card), or **Paid by e-Transfer** (the reference) or **Paid in cash** when the setting allows them (`app.field_deposit_offline`, 0129's offline deposit). Cash also gives Finance a task to collect it from the field staff member |
| Additional driver not accepted | **Sign now on this device (additional driver)** |
| Tracker not reporting (its own step) | **The car has to go anyway?**: a reason (10 characters or more), **Release without the tracker**: an urgent Admin task at once (escalated), the handover then completes for that job with the condition report keeping "tracker not reporting" (`app.field_tracker_override`, `app.field_complete_overridden`) |

For one call only, the field staff member acts as customer service (approving the licence they checked, recording the
paper copy) or may record the deposit (`app.field_as_cs`, `app.field_collect_as`, transaction settings that name the user
and are cleared at once). An Admin or assistant working a check-out job of their own through the field screens has the same
steps (0130's rule). The deposit card that sat above the checklist (`features/money/FieldCollect.tsx`) is the Deposit line's
fix now.

Settings (Admin > Settings > Program, Field jobs and handovers): `field_offline_deposit_methods` (e-Transfer and cash by
default; the Field Staff Agreement 6.1 says field staff take no cash: an Admin removes cash here until the agreement is
amended), `field_tracker_override` (on).

## 2. Field jobs made from bookings (Fleet)

Every confirmed booking gets its handover (a delivery when it is delivered) and its return (a pickup when it is picked up)
job at the booking's times, at its place: the booking's address, else the airport by name, the owner's location, or our
location (the company address; never "none"), given to the active field staff member who drives with the fewest jobs that
day (then that week). `app.field_jobs_sync` runs on a booking's confirmation and on any change of its times, places, ways
or car; a time or place Fleet changed by hand stays as Fleet set it (`auto_at`, `auto_place` keep what was set); a job Fleet
cancelled is not made again; 0020's delivery and pickup jobs get their place and person. Fleet's "schedule the handover"
task closes once both legs have someone. The jobs of the bookings already confirmed were made by the migration. Settings:
`field_jobs_auto` (on), `field_jobs_auto_assign` (`least_busy` or `none`). The board marks "made from the booking", and a
handover or return still without a job shows its place.

## 3. The identity review on one screen (office)

`DocumentReview.tsx` shows every licence image, selfie, proof of coverage and the Protection Plan side by side, what to check
written once, **Approve all** and, per item, **Reject this one** with the reason the renter sees. `app.doc_review_approve_all`
runs each item through the function that decides it (0019, 0071); one the database refuses is named with its reason and the
rest are approved. The assistants' identity screen (`/va/verify/<booking>`) has the same Approve all. About 15 clicks became 2.

## 4. Payments by day (Admin and Finance)

`/admin/payments` and `/va/payments` (Money, Payments by day): From and To, the methods (card, e-Transfer, cash, cheque,
platform payout, insurer, bank transfer, pre-authorized debit, other), totals by method, each day by method with the cash
drawer expected (cash received less cash given back) against the count Finance records (`cash_drawer_counts`, **Record the
count**: balanced, over or short), every payment with friendly words (a card is "Card Visa ending 4242", holds marked "not
money in"), **Export CSV** (`/api/payments/export`, refunds negative, no formula injection). `app.payments_ledger`, at most
three months at a time.

Refunds waiting: every refund requested anywhere (a cancellation, a booking's Money tab, Finance, an Admin) that is not
finished, with the policy maths from the cancellation (paid, the tier, the percentage back, what is kept, the deposit
returned), **Approve** (Finance within its single limit and the cancellation table, as 0012's guard decides; otherwise "an
Admin approves") or **Decline** with the reason, in the same row (`app.refund_decide`), then **Issue the refund**. Finance's
Payments page and Admin > Payments and refunds show friendly words instead of the codes (purpose, kind, method, card).

## 5. The owner's month to date

`public.owner_portal_month_to_date` builds the statement the month would give today in a savepoint, reads it and rolls it back
(a placeholder row first, so no statement number is used; nothing is kept), marked provisional: Owner Revenue, the Revenue
Share, fees and deductions, the expected payout, the lines per car, and the rentals still under way. An approved statement for
the month is the final one and is linked instead. The loader is `ownerMonthToDate` (`server/owner/data.ts`); the section is
plain markup (`features/owner/MonthToDate.tsx`, class `ow-mtd`) at the top of /owner/statements for the owner portal's design
work to style.

## 6. Raw database errors

Every staff and investor call goes through `server/door.ts`: a raw PostgreSQL error (a constraint, a cast, a missing column,
a timeout) becomes a plain sentence with a reference ("That did not go through and nothing was changed. ... reference
E-1A2B3C") and is logged on the server with the reference, the call and the code, email addresses and phone numbers masked
(`lib/db/friendly.ts`). The database's own refusals pass through as written.
