# R50 hotfix 2: Add old photos (5185 to 5187)

Migrations `5185_r50h2_old_photos.sql`, `5186_r50h2_old_photos_door.sql`, `5187_r50h2_old_photos_guides.sql`. Asked by the owner on Nov 27.

## What the owner asked

Field staff have phone photos from earlier check-ins and check-outs that they could not upload because of bugs, and they cannot see those past bookings. Make it easy: every past booking of the last 60 days (also ones they did not handle), searchable by plate, car or renter; many photos at once for one booking, in the background, resumable, HEIC accepted, capture time kept; a pile of photos matched to bookings by time and plate with one tap per group; the photos on the check-in or check-out report marked "added later by" the person, with no renter or owner notice.

## Changes

- **Who and which bookings (5185).** `app.old_photo_bookings`: an Admin, Fleet, customer service and field staff see every booking (not owner use) picked up or returned within `old_photos_days` (60), whoever handled it, newest first, searched by booking number, renter, car name or plate (any case, spaces and dashes ignored). A car still out offers its check-in only.
- **One photo (5185).** `/api/old-photos` asks `app.old_photo_gate` first (permission, window, and whether the phone's upload key is attached already: a retry stores nothing), checks the bytes (`imageSniff`), stores the JPEG under `bookings/<id>/old/<random uuid>.jpg` (the server's key), then `app.old_photo_attach` puts it on the check-in (pickup) or check-out (return) report: `shot` other (the photo standard and the plate cover never use a photo whose angle nobody checked), `device_taken_at` the phone's EXIF time (none when unreadable, in the future or over 3 years old) and `taken_at` the same time only when it falls inside the booking's own pickup to return dates, 3 days either side (otherwise `taken_at` is the upload time, the report shows no capture time and the phone's claim stays as evidence), `uploaded_by` the person, `added_later_at` now, `client_key` the upload key (unique per report). A report holds at most 200 photos (the next is refused; a retry of one already attached still answers as a duplicate). A booking with no report gets one "details later" as 2815 does, so its odometer and fuel still show as missing. The booking does not move; the log gets an internal `adjusted` line when a report is made and once a day per person and side. No notice to the renter or owner.
- **The bulk suggestion (5185).** The phone groups the photos by capture time (`old_photos_group_minutes`, 30), reads the plate from up to two photos of each group (`/api/old-photos/plate`: the vision chain, purpose `plate_detect`, inside the day's allowance; recorded in `provider_calls` with no photo or plate kept), and `app.old_photo_suggest` returns up to 4 check-ins or check-outs within `old_photos_match_hours` (12), nearest first (ties: a booking the person handled), on that car when the plate matched one.
- **The door and the report (5186).** `ops_read old_photo_bookings, old_photo_suggest, old_photo_gate, old_photo_plate_gate`, `ops_do old_photo_attach, old_photo_plate_record`. `app.handover_report` gives each photo `added_later_at` and `added_later_by`, and shows a check-in or check-out not completed yet when it has photos added later. The report reads "taken <time> · added later by <name>". Photos added later show to staff and Admins only: `server_renter_handover_report` drops them (and any report not completed), and a restrictive row policy on `condition_report_photos` (5185) hides them from an owner's browser session.
- **The screens.** `/staff/field/old-photos` (link on Today and on the missing check-ins card), `/staff/old-photos` (a tab beside Check-in visits: Fleet under Checks, customer service under its Check-in visits link, so its menu keeps five links), `/admin/old-photos` (Cars > Check-in visits, a tab beside it: Cars keeps five items). One booking: Check-in or Check-out, then many photos at once. A pile of photos: groups with thumbnails, the plate read, the suggested booking, **Not this one?** for the others, one tap to add. Uploads run two at a time in the background with one progress bar; a network failure waits and retries by itself (2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 seconds; at once when the phone is back online) with the same key; the key is the SHA-256 of the photo's bytes, so the same photo picked again later (after a reload, or in a second pile) is attached once and shows **Already added**; a refusal shows the reason and **Try again**; leaving the page while photos are still going asks first. HEIC is read by the phone (Safari) and sent as the standard JPEG (`toStandardImage`); the capture time is read from the original file first (`features/old-photos/exif.ts`, JPEG and HEIC).
- **Settings.** `old_photos_days`, `old_photos_match_hours`, `old_photos_group_minutes` under Program settings > Field jobs and handovers. `old_photos_plate_daily` (30): the plate reads one person may ask for in a day (`provider_calls.created_by`), so the shared `plate_detect` allowance is not drained; the gate answers `daily` past it and the phone falls back to choosing the booking by hand.

## Not changed

- Uploads do not survive closing the page or a reload: the photos stay on the phone and the person picks them again; the ones already added are answered **Already added** and stored once. Keeping the files across a reload (IndexedDB) is left out.
- A phone browser that cannot open HEIC (Chrome on some Android phones) refuses that photo with the existing HEIC message, as every other upload does.
