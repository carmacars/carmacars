# 01 Motors' GoHighLevel: our cars in their CRM, their cars here (October 24, 2026)

Migration 0136 (`SCHEMA_MARKER` 0136). The owner, October 24, 2026: "I also need to set up the CRM integration with 01 Motors so I can connect to their GoHighLevel CRM so that they can see all of our cars and inventory available", and earlier: their cars, availability and photos should come to CarmaCars, and ours go to them. Owner's rule (same day): **only CarmaCars' own cars (supply type company) and program owners' cars go to 01 Motors. A partner car (01 Motors' own, or any other partner's) is never sent.**

## What 01 Motors needs to give us

One Private Integration token from their GoHighLevel sub-account, and its location ID. Nothing else is needed to start; we never ask for their password.

1. Sign in to GoHighLevel and open **the 01 Motors sub-account** (not the agency view).
2. **Settings** (bottom left) > **Private Integrations** > **Create new Integration**. Name: `CarmaCars`. Description: `Shares CarmaCars cars and availability; reads 01 Motors cars.` Press **Next**.
3. Tick these scopes, then **Create**:

| Scope | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `locations.readonly` (View Locations) | Test connection reads the location's name |
| `objects/schema.readonly`, `objects/schema.write` (Custom Objects) | Find or create the "CarmaCars Vehicles" object, and list their objects |
| `objects/record.readonly`, `objects/record.write` (Custom Object records) | Write our cars as records; read their cars when they keep them in an object |
| `locations/customFields.readonly`, `locations/customFields.write` (Custom Fields) | Add the object's fields (car code, rates, next free date, photos, booking link...) |
| `products.readonly`, `products.write`, `products/prices.readonly`, `products/prices.write` | The fallback when their plan has no custom objects; reading their cars when they keep them as products |
| `opportunities.readonly` | List their pipelines (for leads) and read the stage that sends leads to us |
| `opportunities.write`, `contacts.readonly`, `contacts.write` | Only for leads (off until the owner turns them on) |

4. **Copy the token** (it starts with `pit-` and is shown once). Send it to the owner by phone or in person, never in a plain email or a group chat.
5. **Location ID**: in the address bar, `app.gohighlevel.com/v2/location/<THIS PART>/...`, or **Settings** > **Business Profile**.
6. Optional: if they keep their fleet in GoHighLevel, tell us where (the custom object, for example "Vehicles", or their products), or give a feed address (JSON or CSV). For leads: which pipeline and stage should receive our leads, and which stage they use to send leads to us (Test connection lists them).
7. Renew the token every 90 days: **Private Integrations** > the integration > **Rotate and expire later** (the old one keeps working for 7 days), then paste the new one in Admin.

If their account cannot create custom objects with this token (HighLevel's current reference lists object creation for agency tokens on some plans), either they create the object once by hand (**Settings** > **Objects** > **Add Custom Object**: singular "CarmaCars Vehicle", plural "CarmaCars Vehicles", key `carmacars_vehicles`, primary field "Vehicle") and the next sync fills in its fields and records, or the link uses products by itself.

## What staff see

**Admin > Settings > Partners** (`/admin/settings/partners`, a new tile; also **CRM link** on each partner's page) lists every partner company and its link. **Settings > Partners > 01 Motors** (`/admin/settings/partners/<partner id>`):

- **Their GoHighLevel**: status, location (and its name from the last test), the token's last four characters and where it is kept ("in the vault"), who saved it and when, the last test, the last sync and its error. **Connect** (password field, never shown again) saves the token, runs Test connection and, when it passes, the first sync. **Test connection** (reads only: their location, objects, products, pipelines) and **Run sync now**. **Pause the link** / **Resume**, **Disconnect** (deletes the token from the vault), **Advanced** (the API version header). A box says exactly what 01 Motors must create (the section above).
- **What 01 Motors sees**: how many cars (CarmaCars', program owners'), how many are in their CRM, "Never sent: partner cars", what is shared, the partner price, the last sync's counts and the first five cars with their partner rate and next free day. **Change what we share**: our cars on or off, program owners' cars, rates, availability, photos, custom object or products (Automatic by default), the object's key. **Partner price**: the site's rates (default) or the site's rates adjusted by a percentage (for example -10), to the nearest dollar.
- **01 Motors' cars coming in**: the source (Automatic: the object named, else their feed; or products), whether a car not found here goes on the site at once (off by default: it waits for review), copying their photos (on), their booked dates as blocks (on); the list of their cars with **Add as their car**, **Link** (to one of their cars here), **Set aside**, **Bring back**.
- **Leads both ways**: off until turned on, with their pipeline, the stage for our leads and the stage that sends leads to us (lists from the last test); the last 20 leads.
- **Feed, widget and booking links**: the JSON and CSV feed addresses and the widget's iframe, each with Copy; **New key** (ends the old addresses at once); the booking link code (`01-motors`).
- **Recent runs**: the last ten runs, their result and counts.
- **Admin > Today**: "The CRM link with 01 Motors has failed since ..." once a link has failed for an hour.

## What 01 Motors gets

- **A "CarmaCars Vehicles" custom object** in their GoHighLevel (made by the first sync, with its fields): one record per car with vehicle, car code, year, make, model, trim, class, seats, body, transmission, colour, partner rate a day, a week and a month (CAD), available today, next free date, free days in the next 30 and 60, booked dates, location, cover photo, photos, **Book on carmacars.ca** (`https://carmacars.ca/p/01-motors/<car>`) and the car page. Kept up to date every 15 minutes and within five minutes of a change; a car that leaves the site (or stops being shared) is removed. Unchanged cars are not sent again (a hash of what was sent).
- **Products instead**, when their plan has no custom objects: one product per car (name with the car code, a description with the rates, availability, location and booking link, the photos) and a "Partner rate a day" price in CAD.
- **A read-only feed** (`/partner-feed/<partner id>?key=...&format=json|csv`, the same cars and prices; the CSV is safe for spreadsheets) and **a widget** (`/partner-widget/<partner id>?key=...`): plain HTML, no script, light and dark, each card opening the car's booking link in a new tab. The widget is the only page other sites may frame (`frame-ancestors https:` on that path only; every other page keeps `frame-ancestors 'none'` and `X-Frame-Options: DENY`). The key is an HMAC of the partner and the key's version with `LINK_SIGNING_KEY`: nothing secret is stored, and New key ends the old one.
- **Booking links** `/p/<code>/<car>`: a known code is kept in a cookie for 30 days and the request to book records it (`partner_referrals`); then the car page.
- **Leads** (when on): a request for one of their cars, or one their link brought, becomes a contact (tags `carmacars`, `carmacars-referral` or `carmacars-booking`) and an opportunity in the chosen stage, with the car, the booking number and the total. An opportunity they move to the chosen stage becomes a lead in Admin > Leads (source "partner"), once.

## Their cars coming in

From the custom object they name, their products (only when chosen), or their feed (JSON: an array or `{vehicles|cars|inventory|data: [...]}`; CSV with a header row). Field names are matched loosely (name, make, model, year, trim, colour, class, seats, body, transmission, daily rate, 3+ day and weekly rates, photos, booked dates). Each car is matched to one of their partner cars here by its remote id, then the sheet name (0093), then make, model, trim and colour; not found, it is added as their partner car on the partner-rate basis (0093's markup and cut), off the site unless the setting says otherwise; without a make, a model and a rate it waits for review. Their rates become the car's sheet rates (the renter's follow); their booked dates become `external_booking` blocks kept in step; their photos (https, JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB, eight a car) are copied into the public bucket for a car with no photos of its own. A car no longer in their list is marked gone and its blocks lifted (the car stays; staff decide). A list that could not be read changes nothing. What comes in never triggers a sync of ours (their cars are never sent anyway).

## How it works

- **The rule, three times**: `app.partner_crm_outbound_cars` selects only `supply_type in ('company', 'program_owner')` with no partner (and only cars on the site, never a sample car or a retired one), whatever the settings; `src/features/partner-crm/mapping.ts` `shareable()` drops anything else again before a call; `public.server_partner_crm_done` refuses a record for any car that is not company or program owner. Suite 85 and the unit tests check no partner car is ever in the payload, the feed, the job or their CRM.
- **The token**: pasted in Admin, sent by the server action straight to `public.server_partner_crm_token` (service role only, the Admin checked again), stored with `vault.create_secret` / `vault.update_secret` in Supabase Vault; the row keeps the vault id and the last four characters. Where the vault is absent (the local and test databases) a stand-in table `app.crm_secrets`, granted to no one, holds it. The server reads it with `public.server_partner_crm_secret` for the call only. It is never in the repository, the door, a page, an error (`redact()`), the run log or the audit log.
- **The job**: the Worker's five-minute cron tick asks `public.server_jobs_due` (now with `crm`): a connected link is due 15 minutes after its last sync, or once a change asked for one (statement triggers on vehicles, rate cards, listings, photos, bookings and calendar blocks set `sync_requested_at`, skipping a busy row, never waiting). `/api/jobs/partner-crm` (the jobs' token) runs up to five links. A run is refused while another runs (10 minutes); every run is logged (`partner_crm_runs`, the last 200).
- **GoHighLevel API v2** (`src/features/partner-crm/ghl.ts`): `https://services.leadconnectorhq.com`, `Authorization: Bearer pit-...`, `Version: 2021-07-28` (custom objects and fields try `v3` by themselves when a version is refused, and keep the one that worked). Rate limits (100 requests in 10 seconds, 200,000 a day per location): the `X-RateLimit-*` headers are read and the client waits before the window runs out; a 429 or a 5xx is tried again three times (Retry-After honoured). Endpoints: locations, objects (schema, records, search), custom fields (folder, field), products and prices, pipelines, opportunities (search, create), contacts (upsert).
- **The stand-in** (`src/features/partner-crm/fake-ghl.ts`): the same endpoints in memory, used by the unit tests and, with `CRM_PROVIDER=fake` (the default outside production), by local work and the end-to-end tests. `CRM_PROVIDER` unset in production means GoHighLevel: nothing to set in the deploy.
