# Field staff may take a cash deposit, October 25, 2026

The owner's decision: "Field staff can take cash deposit". Release 9 (`0151_field_finance_owner_mtd.sql`) already lets field
staff take the deposit in cash at the check-out: the app records it at once (the booking's deposit amount, `payments` and
`handover_resolutions`) and gives Finance the task `field_cash_handin` ("Collect the cash deposit from field staff", due in
1 business day). The Field Staff Agreement 6.1 still said "You take no cash or payment from a customer", so 0151's record
asked an Admin to remove cash from the settings until the agreement was amended. This amends it.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0153_field_cash_deposit_clause.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0153`), its guides block generated by
`supabase/seed/0153_gen_guides.mjs`. Test: `supabase/tests/97_field_cash_deposit_clause.sql`.

The text lives in the Worker: `web/scripts/gen-person-agreements.mjs` regenerated
`web/src/features/person-agreements/source.generated.ts`, whose version is the hash of the three source documents (the
investment and VA agreements did not change; their text is the same under the new version). The 1 business day is the due
time of 0151's Finance task.

**Re-acceptance.** There is no automatic re-acceptance for staff agreements: a signed agreement keeps the version it was
signed under, and new agreements are generated from the new text. Field staff who signed the earlier version are asked to
sign the new one the existing way: on the staff member's page an Admin presses **Send the agreement to sign** (or records a paper
copy). Until then, a cash deposit taken under 0151 rests on the owner's decision and the app's record, not on the signed text.

## 2. Settings

settings row where it was removed (0151's record asked for that only until this amendment); the change is in the audit log.
