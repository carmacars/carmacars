## 1. The catalogue (`addons`)

| Field | What |
| --- | --- |
| Name, short description, photo (optional), order | Shown to renters; the photo is uploaded on Admin > Settings > Add-ons (public bucket, `addons/`) |
| Price and unit | Per trip; per day with an optional most days and most per trip (`cap_cents`, per item); per hour with a minimum (`min_hours`) |
| Price by car | `price_overrides`: lines of classes or body types and a price, the first match wins (prepaid fuel: SUV, premium, luxury, exotic, and minivans/vans) |
| HST | On or off per add-on (the line carries it) |
| Active | Off: not offered; turning one on needs a price |
| Which cars | All by default; only some classes, only some cars, cars left out, fuel types left out (prepaid fuel: electric), months (winter tires October to April) |
| Notice | `lead_time_hours`: renters (website, portal) cannot ask later than this before pickup; staff may |
| Stock | `stock_count` for physical items, counted across every booking holding the car over the same dates (tentative, pending payment, confirmed, on rent); empty means not counted |
| Staff confirmation | A chauffeur asked for by a renter waits (`pending`, its line says "to be confirmed"); staff keeping it on the offer, or sending the offer, confirms it |
| Fleet task | `needs_prep`: a Fleet task "Get ready: ..." due before pickup by the notice (at least 2 hours) |
| Refund | `non_refundable_after_lead`: kept on a cancellation once its notice has started |
| Handover note, prepaid fuel | Shown to field staff; prepaid fuel stops the fuel charge at check-in |

## 2. On a booking (`booking_addons`)

Each add-on is one booking line (`booking_line_items.booking_addon_id`), priced from a snapshot of the catalogue taken
when it was added (refreshed while the booking is a request), so a later price change never moves a booking.

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Car page | Add-ons under Dates and price, priced live in the estimate (`quote_booking` takes `addons`), carried to the request (`?addons=code:count:hours`) |
| Request to book | A step of its own: five steps (Dates and delivery, Driver details, Licence and coverage, **Add-ons**, Review and send); notice and stock checked by the database (`app.addons_resolve`) |
| Offer | The review screen (Admin and assistants) adds, removes and counts add-ons, hours for a chauffeur; the preview re-prices live |
| Add a rental, staff quotes | The add-ons list under The rental (every function and field staff) |
| Renter portal | "Add something to your trip" until `addon_portal_cutoff_hours` (24, Program settings) before pickup or the add-on's notice if longer; charged at once on the Card on File (its own payment, purpose `addon`, and line); with no card the line waits unpaid and Finance gets a task to collect it; a chauffeur is asked for and charged when customer service confirms it |
| Staff after booking | The booking's Add-ons card: Confirm and charge, Decline, Remove (unpaid: the line is voided; paid: a refund for it, reason `addon_removed`, waits for approval) |
| Agreement, offer, receipt, statement | The agreement summary names the add-ons; every document lists each as a charge line |
| Field | The job shows "Put in the car" (each add-on, how many, its note) and the prepaid fuel note; the Car ready step lists them |
| Fleet | Tasks "Get ready" (flowers, chauffeur), "Not enough ..." when a confirmed booking finds no stock left; Cars > Add-ons for stock |

## 3. Money

- Add-on lines are revenue class `company_only`, account **4150 Add-ons** (new): never Owner Revenue, never a partner's
  share, never in the kickback base or a staff commission (the commission base stays the rental charge after discounts;
  a refund of a removed add-on is left out of it too).
- The company statement's income summary has its own **Add-ons** line (the old baby seat and winter tire fees moved there
  from Renter service fees).
- `app.booking_paid_cents` counts add-on payments, so a cancellation refunds them by the booking's tier; an add-on marked
  not refunded after its notice, once the notice has started, is kept in full (the tier applies to the rest; the refund's
  allocation leaves its line out). When CarmaCars cancels, everything is refunded.
- Prepaid fuel: the check-in assessment proposes no fuel charge.
