# A car's weekend rate is always above its daily rate (0271)

Some cars had a weekend rate equal to or below their daily rate. Now a weekend rate at or below the daily rate is raised
to the daily rate plus 15%, rounded up to the dollar, whenever a rate card is saved (staff, bulk rate change, partner
sync), and the current cards that broke the rule were raised once. A weekend rate already above the daily rate is kept
as typed. The 15% is a Program setting (weekend premium). Bookings keep the rates they were made with.

## Guides

No guide change: rates are entered the same way; a too-low weekend rate is simply raised on save.
