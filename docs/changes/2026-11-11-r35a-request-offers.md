# The request as sent and the offer history, the coverage card, the owner's own trip, a secondary contact, rideshare documents, instant booking for repeat renters and two new rewards (Release 35, part A: 1300 to 1309)

Branch `r35-a`. Migrations `1300_r35a_request_history.sql`, `1301_r35a_owner_trip_badge.sql`, `1302_r35a_secondary_contact.sql`,
`1303_r35a_rideshare_docs.sql`, `1304_r35a_instant_book.sql`, `1305_r35a_rewards_photo_rebook.sql`, `1309_r35a_door.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER
left to the integrator). Tests: `supabase/tests/99_r35a_request_offers.sql`; unit tests `web/src/features/request-history/model.test.ts`,
`web/src/features/rideshare/model.test.ts`, `web/src/lib/db/refusal.test.ts`.

| Item | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Original request (1300) | `booking_request_snapshots`: the request exactly as the renter sent it (car, dates, places, coverage choice, add-ons, note, the request details and the price shown with its lines), taken once at the end of the transaction that made the request (a deferred trigger) and never changed. Requests made before r35 get one rebuilt from what the booking kept: the dates, choices and note always; the car and the price only when no offer or staff edit had changed them (else left out, said on screen) |
| Offer history (1300) | Booking page (Admin, customer service, Sales, Fleet; `ops_read request_history`): **Original request, as the renter sent it** and **Offer history**, every version newest first with its status, who sent it and when, the car and dates, the message, what the total did since the version before, and the price breakdown |
| Revert to this offer (1300) | On an old version while the offer is being arranged (customer service, Sales, an Admin; `ops_read offer_revert`): loads that version's car, dates, rate type, rates, rental charge, deposit, km, discount (with its reason), fees, Protection Plan price, promo code and a message into the review as a new offer. Nothing changes until it is sent; the version out now cannot be "reverted to" |
| Coverage card (1300) | At the top of the request and booking: the coverage the renter chose (own insurance OPCF 27, credit card, Protection Plan, included with the car) and its proof (not sent yet, sent and waiting for review, confirmed, rejected with the reason, not needed for the plan), and when it differs from what the renter first asked for |
| Book it now (0770, checked) | Already waits for an Admin when staff press it (alert **Override asked for**, To do, one-tap approve or decline; an Admin's runs at once). New here: staff's checkbox says "an Admin approves first"; the rule is re-tested in 99_r35a |
| Owner's own trip (1301) | **Owner's own trip ($0)** on the booking's title, Requests and offers, All bookings, the calendar bar and its panel, the request's To do title, the review screen, and a new staff alert **Owner booked their own car ($0)** (Admins, customer service, Sales and Fleet on duty) |
| Offers in three steps | Admin and staff alike. Send an offer (composer): 1. Car and dates (renter, dates, car, places, coverage, extras), 2. Price, 3. Review and send, with a step bar; advanced options stay under More options. The request review: a step bar (1. Car and dates, 2. Price, 3. Review and send), billing to an insurer, how it is paid and the calendar under **More options**, what changed from the request shown before Send. Fixes: Send waits while the price is being worked out again for changed dates (no stale price sent), one send at a time (a double tap sent twice), and a refusal or a dropped connection always says something (`src/lib/db/refusal.ts`) |
| Secondary contact (1302) | Renter: **Secondary contact** on Account > Profile and, per trip, under the booking page's **More**: name, relationship, phone and/or email; this trip only or the profile too; an empty name removes it. Staff (every function): a **Secondary contact** card on the booking with call and email, saying where it comes from. Logged on the booking |
| Rideshare documents (1303) | Renter: under the booking page's **More**, **Rideshare or delivery documents** (`/account/bookings/<n>/rideshare`, from the offer accepted until the return; never on an owner's trip): pick Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart or other (named), then the rental agreement copy, a proof of insurance and coverage letter (fleet policy and the booking's coverage and proof) and a vehicle registration and ownership letter (car, plate, VIN, authorized drivers), on the letterhead of the company's legal name only, printable. Staff: alert **Rideshare documents asked for** (Admins, customer service and Fleet on duty), a card on the booking and the same letters at `/admin|staff/bookings/<n>/rideshare` |
| Requirements page | Rideshare and delivery work is allowed with the documents; re-renting and car-sharing listings stay not allowed. **The rental agreement's section 10.2 still prohibits ride-hailing and delivery work: an Admin decides the agreement wording (not changed here)** |
| Instant booking (1304) | A signed-in renter with at least `instant_book_min_rentals` completed rentals (Program setting, 1), a verified licence, no damage or claim on a past rental and nothing owing, booking a standard rental of an available car with the licence on file: the request becomes an accepted offer at once (`public.server_instant_book`), the agreement is made, and the renter goes straight to Sign and pay (payment, agreement and the normal deposit at checkout). Everyone else keeps request to book. The booking shows **Instant (repeat renter)**. `instant_book_repeat_enabled` (on) switches it off. An Admin takes a renter's instant booking away (with why) or gives it back from the booking page; staff see whether the renter qualifies and why not |
| Rewards (1305) | `review_photo` (100 points, a review on carmacars.ca with at least one photo, on top of the review's points, once per rental; photos added from the review page) and `rebook` (150 points, every completed rental after the first). The Rewards page lists every action that is on (follows, reviews, Google, referral, the two new ones), and Google's link falls back to the place id. The return email and the review request email list the ways to earn and link to the Rewards page. Social follow buttons need the links in Settings (Facebook has none yet) |
