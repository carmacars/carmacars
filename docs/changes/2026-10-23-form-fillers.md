# The form fillers: the ServiceOntario forms and the agreements, October 23, 2026

The owner's report: "the serviceontario form filler doesnt seem to be built properly, neither does the agreements filler".
Both were exercised end to end with realistic records: the five official ServiceOntario PDFs filled for a car in each
onboarding method and rendered page by page, and every agreement the product generates rendered from the records. Then
the owner decided four points (quoted below). This record says what was wrong, what the fillers do now, and the tests
that hold it.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0122_form_fillers.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0122`), with its mapping block generated by
`supabase/seed/0122_gen_forms.mjs` and its guides block by `supabase/seed/0122_gen_guides.mjs`. The filler is
`web/src/features/official-forms/pdf-update.ts` (with `fill.ts`). Tests: `web/src/features/official-forms/official.test.ts`
(the publisher's PDFs filled for real and compared with the originals object by object), `fill.test.ts`,
`web/src/features/agreement/render.test.ts` (the agreements read back with pdf.js), `supabase/tests/79_form_fillers.sql`,
and the end to end `web/tests/e2e/form-fillers.spec.ts` and `tolls-plates-forms.spec.ts`. Fixtures:
`web/tests/fixtures/official-forms/` (the five forms exactly as ServiceOntario publishes them, encrypted as published,
SHA-256 as `original_sha256` in `docs/forms/official-forms.json`; and the registry context of four cars read from the
database) and `web/tests/fixtures/agreements/facts.json` (facts read from the database, names made up).

## 2. The ServiceOntario forms: the publisher's PDF, only its text boxes filled

The filler (`pdf-update.ts`) keeps the publisher's PDF byte for byte and writes the values after its last byte, as an
incremental update (the way Adobe Reader saves a filled form):

- each filled text field gets its value and the appearance of that value, drawn with the field's own font, size and
  colour and the form's own font resources; nothing else in the field changes;
- checkboxes, drop-downs, radio buttons and signature boxes are never touched: they are ticked, chosen and signed by
  hand at the counter, in ink; a signature's date box is left for the date it is signed;
- no page, annotation, image, stamp or font is added, nothing is flattened, and no value is drawn over the page;
- the publisher's PDFs are encrypted (they allow filling, nothing else); they stay encrypted, the update is encrypted
  with the file's own key;
- SR-LV-006, SR-LV-012 and 013-0169 also carry an Adobe XFA copy of their fields. Adobe Reader shows that copy, which
  would stay empty, so the update switches it off (the one /XFA entry of the form dictionary; the original revision
  keeps it): Adobe Reader then shows the same PDF fields every other viewer shows, with their values. 013-0169 carries
  Adobe's "extended features" signature; Adobe Reader may say those features are no longer available in the filled
  copy (filling and printing are not affected);
- ON00453 is an Adobe dynamic form (XFA only, no PDF fields: like the download, it opens in Adobe Acrobat Reader and
  other viewers show a blank page). Its values are written in its own XFA data, nothing else. The laid-out copy with
  printed values that 0047 used is retired.

Official forms are no longer signed on screen: the owner's form page says "Print it and sign it in ink", the signing
package (Check and sign) no longer holds them, and the route that saved an on-screen signed form refuses (410). A form
signed in ink is recorded by an Admin (Record paper signature) or filed at the registry, as before.

The templates. The forms stored until now were the publisher's PDFs opened and saved again (decrypted, the XFA copy
removed; ON00453 a laid-out copy). The migration registers each publisher's download with its fields and mapping
(`official_form_originals`); `app.official_templates_use_originals()` makes it the current template of its form once it
is in the documents bucket. The forms-fetch workflow now stores each download byte for byte
(`forms/templates/<code>/official-<sha>.pdf`) and calls that function after storing. Until it is run, the four fillable
forms are filled from the stored copies the same way (only their text boxes), and ON00453 gives owners its data sheet.

What was wrong before, all fixed (the mappings, checked against the real forms):

| Form | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SR-LV-006, SR-LV-012 | CarmaCars' one-line address in the 45-character street box, the postal code cut in half | Street line, unit, city and postal code each in its own box |
| SR-LV-006 | Section 1 named CarmaCars for every method; section 7 without the VIN | The vehicle's registrant by method (the owner's corporation, the owner, the lessor or CarmaCars); the VIN in section 7 |
| SR-LV-012 | Section 1 filled for every method; the owner's signature drawn in sections the owner does not sign | Section 1 only when CarmaCars holds the plates; section 2 only under Co-Registrant; no signature drawn anywhere |
| SR-LV-006, SR-LV-012 | "OLIVIA MARGARET TREMBLAY" where the form asks "Last Name, First Name and Middle Initial"; a person's licence for a corporation | "TREMBLAY, OLIVIA M"; the corporation's RIN |
| ON00453, 013-0169 | "+14165550142"; the street number box empty | "(416) 555-0142"; street number and name apart |
| All | A value longer than its field cut without a word | Reported (`truncated`); the tests assert none is cut |

To tick or choose at the counter (each form's notes in Admin > Settings > Forms list them): SR-LV-006 "Number plates
owned by other than above" (a Lease, a Transfer of a leased car), "Vehicle to be registered to 2 individuals"
(Co-Registrant), the plates and "fit" boxes; ON00453 "New" and "operated out of the owner's place of residence: Yes";
013-0169 the province (Ontario).

## 3. The tests that hold the rule

`official.test.ts` fills the five publisher's downloads for the cars of the four methods and compares each filled file
with the official one: the official bytes come first, unchanged; the same pages in the same order, no page object (its
content streams and annotations) changed; every object the update replaces is a filled text field whose dictionary
differs only by its value and appearance, or the form dictionary with only its XFA entry removed, or (ON00453) the XFA
data with only the filled values changed and every node kept; every new object is an appearance stream of a filled
field; every checkbox, drop-down, radio button and signature is untouched. It also opens the filled forms in another
reader (pdf.js) and reads each value back.

## 4. The agreements

The party is "18122211 Canada Inc." everywhere: the rental agreement ("This Agreement is between 18122211 Canada Inc.
("CarmaCars", "we", "us") and the Renter"), the Enrollment and Title-Holding covers, Forms A to D (the trust
declaration, the lease summary, the powers of attorney), the investment, VA, field staff and partner agreements, the
countersignature ("Signed electronically for 18122211 Canada Inc., by Abbas Al-Mashita, Director, under a standing
authorization dated ...") and the payee of the pre-authorized debit agreement. "CarmaCars" stays the short name the
agreements define and use, and the brand stays in headers and logos. The approved texts in `docs/source` are unchanged
(their version tags too): the party line is replaced when the text is built. Invoices and receipts keep "(operating as
CarmaCars)"; they are not agreements.

Also fixed in the agreements, page by page: phones printed as people write them (416-555-0142); the signature block's
heading over its own columns; a VIN never broken over two lines; a table's heading repeated on the next page and never
left alone at the foot of a page; "Ltd.." with one full stop; a corporation named once; the witness's time in the same
Toronto format as every signature; and "Signed copy" of an investment, staff or uploaded agreement now holds the whole
document as signed (checked against its SHA-256) followed by the signature page.
