# The form fillers: the ServiceOntario forms and the agreements, checked page by page, October 23, 2026

The owner's report: "the serviceontario form filler doesnt seem to be built properly, neither does the agreements filler".
Both were exercised end to end on a local database with realistic records: the five official ServiceOntario PDFs
(downloaded by the forms-fetch workflow, the SHA-256 of each matching `docs/forms/official-forms.json`) filled for a car in
each onboarding method and rendered page by page, and every agreement the product generates rendered from the records,
signed with a drawn signature. The defects found, what was fixed, and the tests that now hold it.

Migration `supabase/migrations/0120_form_fillers.sql` (SCHEMA_MARKER `0120`), with its mapping block generated by
`supabase/seed/0120_gen_forms.mjs`. Tests: `web/src/features/official-forms/official.test.ts` (the official PDFs filled for
real, each value read back from its field and found inside its field's box on the page), `web/src/features/agreement/render.test.ts`
(the agreements read back with pdf.js), `supabase/tests/78_form_fillers.sql`, and the end to end
`web/tests/e2e/form-fillers.spec.ts` (from the Admin and owner portals, and the investor portal). Fixtures:
`web/tests/fixtures/official-forms/` (the official PDFs as stored for owners, and the registry context of four cars read
from the database) and `web/tests/fixtures/agreements/facts.json` (facts read from the database, names made up).

## 1. The ServiceOntario form filler

Until now the filler had only ever been tested with a small PDF made by the tests themselves (`sampleForm()`); the draft
mappings of 0047 were never filled into the real forms. Filled into the real forms, for a car in each method:

| # | Form | What was wrong | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | SR-LV-006, SR-LV-012 | CarmaCars' address went whole into the 45-character street box: "2967 Dundas St W, Unit 208B, Toronto, ON M6P" (the postal code cut in half); the apartment, city and postal code boxes stayed empty | The address is split into its parts (`app.split_ca_address`): street line, unit, city, province, postal code, each in its own box |
| 2 | SR-LV-006 | Section 1 named CarmaCars for every method, and "Number plates owned by other than above" was never ticked | Section 1 is the vehicle's registrant by method: the owner's corporation under a Lease (its RIN, at the owner's address), the owner under Co-Registrant (licence number and birth date), the lessor under a Transfer of a leased car, CarmaCars under a Transfer of an owned car. "Number plates owned by other than above" is ticked when CarmaCars holds the plates as lessee; "Vehicle to be registered to 2 individuals" under Co-Registrant |
| 3 | SR-LV-006 | Section 7 (the insurance certificate) left out the VIN it certifies | The VIN is filled |
| 4 | SR-LV-006 | Section 8 was printed for CarmaCars' signing officer under every method, and no one could sign it on screen | Section 8 names whoever signs for the registrant; under a Lease or Co-Registrant the owner (or their corporation's signing officer) signs it on screen |
| 5 | SR-LV-012 | Section 1 ("only if licence plates are owned by someone other than the vehicle owner") was filled with the owner for every method, Co-Registrant included, with the owner's signature | Section 1 names CarmaCars as the plate owner only under a Lease or a Transfer of a leased car; it is certified by CarmaCars' signing officer, never with the owner's signature |
| 6 | SR-LV-012 | The owner's drawn signature was placed in section 2 even when section 2 did not apply (any method other than Co-Registrant) | A signature box with a condition is drawn only when the database kept it for that car (`fill.ts`); the page offers "Sign on screen" only when the owner signs something on that form (`signs.ts`) |
| 7 | SR-LV-006, SR-LV-012 | An individual was written "OLIVIA MARGARET TREMBLAY" where the form asks "Last Name, First Name and Middle Initial" | "TREMBLAY, OLIVIA M" (`app.registry_person_name`) |
| 8 | SR-LV-012 | A corporation that owns the car got the individual's driver's licence and birth date, and its own RIN was never used | The corporation's RIN; no birth date for a company |
| 9 | ON00453, 013-0169 | The phone printed "+14165550142": the format only knew ten digits | `(416) 555-0142`, with or without the country code (`app.apply_transform`) |
| 10 | ON00453, 013-0169 | The street number box stayed empty, the number written into the street name | The street line is split into number and name (`app.street_parts`) |
| 11 | 013-0169 | The province drop-down stayed empty: the value "ON" is not one of its options ("ON - Ontario") | A drop-down takes the option a value stands for, whatever the dash between code and name (`matchOption`) |
| 12 | ON00453 | "Is the business operated out of the owner's place of residence?" was left unanswered, though the address on the form is the owner's own | Ticked Yes (the corporation's address on record is the owner's) |
| 13 | All | A value longer than its field was cut without a word | The filler reports it (`truncated`); the tests assert no value is cut |

What the forms still leave blank, on purpose, is written in each template's notes (shown in Admin > Settings > Forms):
for example the fleet insurer and policy in SR-LV-006 section 7 and the lessor's RIN under a Transfer of a leased car.

The new mapping replaces the 0047 draft only where no Admin has saved a mapping of their own (`app.official_form_remap`
checks the template's SHA-256 and that `mapped_by` is empty). A form already in use for owners is filled again from it;
a form signed or filed stays as it was. Admin > Settings > Forms lists the new sources (vehicle registrant, plate owner,
the CarmaCars address parts, the owner's street number and name, the registry name) for any mapping an Admin changes.

The forms-fetch workflow can now be run with "store" off: the PDFs and the report are kept as the run's artifact without
writing to the documents bucket (`.github/workflows/forms-fetch.yml`).

## 2. The agreements filler

Every agreement the product generates was rendered from the records and read page by page: the rental agreement, the
Vehicle Enrollment Agreement with Schedules A and B, the Title-Holding and Lease Agreement with Schedule 1, Forms A, B,
C (on screen) and D (printed), the investment agreement and the staff agreements.

| # | Document | What was wrong | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Rental agreement | "This Agreement is between CarmaCars", where the approved text (`docs/source/v2_rental.md`) names "18122211 Canada Inc., operating as CarmaCars" | The legal company is the party, from the settings |
| 2 | Enrollment Agreement, Title-Holding and Lease Agreement | The cover's party row said "CarmaCars (CarmaCars.ca)", where the approved text names "18122211 Canada Inc., operating as CarmaCars and CarmaCars.ca"; the facts did not even carry the legal name | The facts carry it (`app.owner_agreement_facts`, wrapped) and the cover names it |
| 3 | Rental, Enrollment, investment and staff agreements | Phones printed as stored, "+14165550142" | `416-555-0142`, as the company's own number is written |
| 4 | Every agreement with a signature block | The block's heading used four equal columns while its rows used other widths, so "Name" and "Electronic signature" stood over the wrong cells | The heading takes the rows' widths |
| 5 | Form D, Form A, and any table | A three-column table gave the VIN 16% of the width, so a VIN broke over two lines ("1G1ZD5STOE36C / 686C") | A column narrower than its longest word takes the difference from the columns with room to spare: a VIN, a plate or an amount is never broken |
| 6 | Every multi-page table | A table that ran onto the next page continued without its heading, and a heading could stay alone at the foot of a page; a signature row moved to the next page without the block's heading | The heading repeats on the next page and never stays alone; a signature row takes the heading with it |
| 7 | Forms A to D | An owner whose name ends with a full stop ("Northshore Auto Holdings Ltd.") got two: "Ltd.." | One full stop |
| 8 | Enrollment Agreement, schedules | A corporation whose name is its legal name was written twice: "Northshore Holdings Ltd. (Northshore Holdings Ltd.)" | Once |
| 9 | Form C signed on screen | The witness's time was written differently from every other signature ("Oct 23, 2026, 7:37 a.m." beside "Fri, Oct 23, 2026, 7:37 a.m.") | The same Toronto date and time as every signature row |
| 10 | Investment agreement, staff agreements, PDFs an Admin sends | "Signed copy" in the investor, VA and field portals (and in Admin) opened only the signature page: the agreement itself was not in the signed copy | The signed copy is the document as signed (checked against the SHA-256 the signature names) followed by the signature page and the record (`server/sign-requests.ts`, `public.server_sign_request_document`); a document that cannot be read or does not match keeps the signature page alone, as before |

Checked and found right: every merge field filled (no "undefined", "null", "NaN" or "[●]" anywhere), amounts with their
cents where they have them, dates and times in Toronto time, the signature images inside their column on their signer's
row, the three initials of the Enrollment Agreement and the Form C acknowledgments, the countersignature's approved
wording, and the signature record page.

The renderer moved to `web/src/features/agreement/render.ts` (`pdf.ts` re-exports it for the server) and the models import
each other by relative path, so the unit tests render the agreements in Node.

## 4. Before using the forms for owners

The mapping follows the forms' own instructions (who is the registrant, who owns the plates, who signs); an Admin still
presses "Checked: use for owners" for each form after a preview, as before. Points to confirm with the registry office:
whether the lessee signs SR-LV-006 section 8 under a Transfer of a leased car (the mapping names CarmaCars' signing
officer), and whether a new Owner Corporation's RIN (ON00453, the same visit) is written on SR-LV-006 at the counter.

## Guides

No guide change: the screens and their steps are the same (Admin > Settings > Forms, the owner's Documents and the signing
pages); what changed is what the filled PDFs and the signed copies hold.
