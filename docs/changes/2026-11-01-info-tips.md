# The "i" beside non-obvious fields (no migration)

Release 21b, item 7. Code: `web/src/components/InfoTip.tsx` (the component), `web/src/features/info/text.ts` (the words, with
`text.test.ts`), `web/src/features/admin/Act.tsx` and `web/src/features/forms/Field.tsx` (the "i" by a field's name or its
`info`), `web/src/features/portal/Blocks.tsx` (`Dl` rows with an "i"). `web/src/features/forms/Info.tsx` now re-exports it.

- InfoTip: a real button (aria-label "About <topic>", aria-expanded, aria-controls) beside the label, never inside it, so the
  field's name stays its label. Click, tap, Enter or Space opens it; Escape closes it and returns the focus to the button; a
  tap or focus elsewhere closes it. The popover is kept inside the window on a phone; the button's touch target is 44 px.
- The words are short and plain, from the guides and the policy in the database (deposits, the $2,500 deductible, young
  driver fees, coverage options, bill-to and insurer fields, the Revenue Share, pay models, owner fees, commission tiers,
  staff functions and work modes, discount scope, VLF, request claiming, the academy gate, investors' returns).
- Where: every Admin and staff form field whose name is in the catalog (Act, SaveForm, Program settings without a longer
  hint already); the booking review (bill to, claim, adjuster, authorized rate, deposit, coverage, booking type); a rental
  added by hand (insurance replacement); promo codes (what a discount comes off); staff functions and the Sales work mode;
  requests (claiming); commissions; the renter's request (coverage, cancellation), booking (deposit) and Complete your
  details (insurer, policy, card coverage); the owner's car terms and statement; the investor's dashboard and statement.

## Guides

None: the "i" repeats what the guides say.
