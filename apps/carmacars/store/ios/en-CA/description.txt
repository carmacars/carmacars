CarmaCars is car rental across Ontario, from everyday sedans to SUVs and luxury cars, by the day, the week or the month.

Find your car
Every car with its real photos and prices: daily, weekend, weekly and monthly rates, the kilometres included and the deposit.

Request in a minute
Pick your dates, pickup or delivery, and send a request. You pay nothing yet.

A real offer from our team
A person on our team checks the car and your dates and sends you an offer with the full total.

Sign and pay on your phone
Read and sign the rental agreement, add your driver's licence with the camera, and pay by card.

Pickup and return
Your trip, the pickup and return times, and the car's condition photos, all in one place. Extend your rental from the app.

Alerts at every step
Phone alerts for your offer, payments, pickup, return and messages from our team. Turn each kind on or off.

Car owners in our program and our team use the same app: the account you sign in with opens your own portal.

Questions? Message us from your booking, or visit carmacars.ca.
