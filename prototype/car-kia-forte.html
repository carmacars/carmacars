Requirements in full

Coverage

Third-party liability coverage is included in the rate. For damage to the Forte itself, have one of these in place before pickup.

  1. Option 1

    OPCF 27 on your own auto policy

    If your Ontario auto insurance includes the OPCF 27 endorsement, it can cover a rental. Send us the policy page or a letter from your insurer.

  2. Option 2

    Your credit card’s rental coverage

    Many cards include it. The rental has to be paid with that card, and the benefit has to cover the car and the length of the rental.

  3. Option 3

    Coverage bought through CarmaCars

    If you have no policy and no card benefit, add coverage to your booking. It is priced in your offer, for your car and the driver’s age. A third-party provider supplies it on its own terms, which we send you before you buy.

If the car is damaged: under $2,500 you are billed the repair at cost. At $2,500 or more we claim on our fleet policy and you pay the $2,500 deductible. More on coverage.

Questions about the Forte

How much does it cost to rent the Kia Forte?

$64 a day, or $74 for a day that starts on a Friday or Saturday. A week is $380 and 30 days is $1,250. Trips of 1 to 6 days never cost more than the weekly rate. HST and any fees are added on top.

How much is the deposit for the Kia Forte?

$500. It is taken about 1 hour before pickup, not when you book. For rentals of 7 days or less it is a hold on your card. We release the balance within 7 business days after you return the car.

How many kilometres are included?

400 km for each day of the rental, added together. A 3 day rental includes 1,200 km. Extra kilometres cost $0.25 each.

Can a driver aged 18 to 20 rent the Kia Forte?

Yes. Every car accepts drivers 18 and up with a G2 or G licence. For this car the young driver fee is $35 a day for ages 18 to 20 and $20 a day for ages 21 to 25, with no maximum.

Can you deliver the Kia Forte?

Yes, anywhere, including Toronto Pearson and Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport. Each way costs $75 for this car plus the distance from our location, priced in your offer. An airport surcharge may apply.

What is the fuel policy?

Return the car with the same fuel it had at pickup. If it comes back lower, we refuel it at the pump price plus 25%, plus a $25 service fee.

Can I take the Kia Forte outside Ontario?

The car stays in Ontario unless we agree in writing first. Ask in your request and we will tell you before you pay.
