Drivers

Age18 or older at pickup, for every car in the fleet.
LicenceA valid Ontario G2 or G licence, carried for the whole rental. Other licences are reviewed before we confirm.
CardA credit card in your own name. It pays for the booking and stays on file for the rental.
Identity checkBefore pickup, each driver sends a photo of their licence, front and back, and a selfie holding it. If we cannot verify a driver, we refund what you paid.
Additional drivers25% of the car’s daily rate, a day, for each one, with no maximum. Each one meets the same rules, is verified, and signs the agreement.
At pickupYour original licence and the physical card you paid with. We hand the car only to the renter, in person.

Coverage

Liability is included. Damage to the car is on you, so you need one of three options in place before we release it.

Included in every rateThird-party liability coverage at the minimum limits Ontario law requires, and basic 24-hour roadside assistance for mechanical breakdown: call 289-321-5991. Liability coverage does not cover damage to the rental car or your belongings.
OptionWhat it isWhat we need from youWhat we check
1. OPCF 27Rental vehicle coverage on your own active auto policyThe policy page or an insurer letter showing OPCF 27It is active to your return date and covers this car and every driver
2. Credit cardRental car coverage that comes with your credit cardThe card’s benefits certificateThe same card pays for the rental, and the benefit covers this class of car and the length of the trip
3. Through CarmaCarsCoverage you buy with your booking, supplied by a third-party provider on its own termsPayment for it. We send the provider’s terms before you buy.We activate it and confirm. Priced in your offer, by car and driver age.

For options 1 and 2 it is your job to check that the coverage really applies to the car, the rental and every driver. No confirmed option at pickup counts as cancelling at that moment. CarmaCars is not an insurer.

If the car is damaged

Under $2,500

billed at cost

You pay the repair estimate or invoice from our approved shop, plus towing, storage and loss of use if any, and a $50 administration fee.

$2,500 or more

you pay the deductible

We claim on our fleet policy and bill you the $2,500 deductible and a $75 administration fee. You then claim that back from your coverage option.

You are responsible for damage during your rental whoever is at fault, except mechanical failure you did not cause. The limit above does not apply if the insurer denies the claim because of an unapproved driver, impaired driving, a prohibited use or false information.

After an accident, theft or vandalism

  1. Call 911 if anyone is hurt. Report to police or a Collision Reporting Centre where the law requires, and always for theft.
  2. Call our 24-hour line, 289-321-5991, as soon as it is safe, and within 24 hours.
  3. Get everyone’s details, take photos, and do not admit fault or settle.
  4. Send us a written report, your photos and all the keys within 48 hours.
