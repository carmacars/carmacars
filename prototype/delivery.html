How delivery works

The same handover as collecting it yourself, at your door.

  1. Add your address to the request

    Tick delivery, pickup at the end, or both. We confirm the address and the time with your booking.

  2. We message you the day before

    A reminder with the time, the place and what to bring: your original licence and the card you paid with.

  3. Handover at your door

    We take the deposit, check your licence and card, then walk around the car with you and photograph it. You both sign the condition report.

  4. At the end, we come back

    If you chose pickup, meet our driver with the car and all the keys. The return inspection happens there, with you.

What it costs

One fee for delivery, the same again for pickup at the end. Before HST. Collecting and returning the car yourself is free.
