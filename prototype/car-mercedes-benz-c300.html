Requirements in full

Coverage

Third-party liability coverage is included in the rate. For damage to the C300 itself, have one of these in place before pickup.

  1. Option 1

    OPCF 27 on your own auto policy

    If your Ontario auto insurance includes the OPCF 27 endorsement, it can cover a rental. Send us the policy page or a letter from your insurer.

  2. Option 2

    Your credit card’s rental coverage

    Many cards include it. The rental has to be paid with that card, and the benefit has to cover the car and the length of the rental.

  3. Option 3

    Coverage bought through CarmaCars

    If you have no policy and no card benefit, add coverage to your booking. A third-party provider supplies it on its own terms, which we send you before you buy.

If the car is damaged: under $2,500 you are billed the repair at cost. At $2,500 or more we claim on our fleet policy and you pay the $2,500 deductible. More on coverage.

Questions about the C300

How much does it cost to rent the Mercedes-Benz C300?

$119 a day, or $142 for a day that starts on a Friday or Saturday. A week is $700 and 30 days is $2,300. Trips of 1 to 6 days never cost more than the weekly rate. HST and any fees are added on top.

How much is the deposit for the Mercedes-Benz C300?

$1,000. It is taken at pickup, not when you book. For rentals of 7 days or less it is a hold on your card. We release the balance within 7 business days after you return the car.

How many kilometres are included?

200 km for each day of the rental, added together. A 3 day rental includes 600 km. Extra kilometres cost $0.35 each.

Can a driver aged 18 to 20 rent the Mercedes-Benz C300?

No. Drivers aged 18 to 20 can book the cars we mark for them, and this is not one. Drivers aged 21 to 25 can rent it with a young driver fee of $20 a day, up to $200 per rental. See cars open to ages 18 to 20.

Can you deliver the Mercedes-Benz C300?

Yes. Delivery costs $100 for this car, and so does pickup at the end if you want it. Give us the address in your request and we confirm it with your booking.

What is the fuel policy?

Return the car with the same fuel it had at pickup. If it comes back lower, we refuel it at the pump price plus 25%, plus a $25 service fee.

Can I take the Mercedes-Benz C300 outside Ontario?

The car stays in Ontario unless we agree in writing first. Ask in your request and we will tell you before you pay.
