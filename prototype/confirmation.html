What happens next

  1. We check the car and your details

    We look at the calendar, your dates and the place you chose. We reply within 2 hours, day or night.

    CarmaCars

  2. You get the confirmed price

    By email to , with every fee and HST. If we had to adjust anything, the message says what changed. That price is an offer and stays open for 24 hours. It does not hold the car.

    CarmaCars

  3. You approve, sign and pay

    Reply yes and we send the rental agreement to sign online and a secure payment link. Use a credit card in your own name. The car is booked when the payment goes through. If someone else pays for the same car and dates before you do, we tell you and offer another car or other dates.

    You

  4. You send your licence and coverage proof

    A photo of your licence, front and back, and a selfie holding it, at least 24 hours before pickup. For option 1, add the policy page or insurer letter that shows OPCF 27.For option 2, add your card’s benefits certificate, and pay with that card.For option 3, we send the provider’s terms and activate the coverage once it is paid.

    You

  5. Pickup day

    We take the deposit about 1 hour before pickup. Bring your original licence and the card you paid with. We walk around the car with you and hand over the keys.

    Both
