Choose, request, drive.

Booking a car here takes five steps. The first costs nothing. The car is yours once you have approved, signed and paid.

  1. 1

    Choose a car and send a request

    Pick the car, your dates and times, and where you want it: at our location, at the car owner’s location, delivered to an address or at the airport. Any time of day, with at least 2 hours’ notice. No card and no payment at this point.

    You

    What you tell us

    • The car, pickup and return dates and times, any time of day with at least 2 hours’ notice
    • Where you get it and return it: our location, the car owner’s location where offered, an address or an airport
    • The driver’s name, age and contact details
    • Licence class and which coverage option you plan to use
  2. 2

    We confirm the car and the price

    A person checks the car’s calendar and your details, then sends the full price with fees and HST. If something has to change, such as the pickup time or the car, we say what and why. That price is an offer, open for 24 hours. It does not hold the car.

    CarmaCars

    What we check and send back

    • That the car is free and the trip is 30 days or less
    • The rental charge, delivery and pickup fees, any young driver or additional driver fee, extras and 13% HST
    • The deposit amount, shown separately because it is taken about 1 hour before pickup
    • The price of coverage bought through us, if you chose it
    • Anything we need to adjust, and why
    • The price is an offer, open for 24 hours. It does not hold the car
  3. 3

    You approve, sign and pay

    Sign the rental agreement online and pay through our checkout with a credit card in your name. The car is booked once the payment goes through, and not before. If someone else pays for the same car and dates first, we tell you and offer another car or other dates.

    You

    What you do to confirm

    • Sign the rental agreement online and tick the four consents
    • Pay the total through our checkout with a credit card in your own name
    • Choose non-refundable if you want 10% off the rental charge
    • The car is booked at this moment. If someone else pays for the same car and dates first, we tell you and offer another car or other dates
  4. 4

    We check your licence and coverage

    Send a photo of your licence, front and back, a selfie holding it, and proof of your coverage option. We need these at least 24 hours before pickup.

    You, then CarmaCars

    What we need before pickup

    • A photo of each driver’s licence, front and back
    • A selfie of each driver holding their licence
    • Proof of your coverage option, or payment for coverage bought through us
    • All of it at least 24 hours before pickup
  5. 5

    Pick up, or we deliver

    Bring your original licence and the card you paid with. The deposit is taken about 1 hour before pickup, and we photograph the car together.

    Both

    What happens at handover

    • We hand the car only to the renter, in person
    • We check your original licence and the physical card you paid with
    • We take the deposit about 1 hour before pickup: a card hold for 7 days or less, a refundable charge for longer
    • We complete the condition report and photo set together, and you both sign it

Bringing it back

Return the car on time, with the same fuel or charge, and all the keys. We inspect it with you and compare it with the pickup photos.

What you agree to when you sign

Four consents sit beside the signature. Every box starts unticked.

  1. Tracking and disabling

    Every car has a GPS tracker. It records location, speed and distance, not sound or video. A moving car is never disabled.

  2. Identity verification

    We check your licence against a live photo of your face. You can ask for a staff review instead of the automated match.

  3. Electronic signature

    You sign and receive the agreement and notices electronically.

  4. Card authorization

    We may hold or charge your card for the total, the deposit and later charges such as tolls.
